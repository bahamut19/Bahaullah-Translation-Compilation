Lawḥ Shams al-Ḥaqq (Tablet of the Sun of Truth)

BH00011

Translated on 17 Qudrat 183 using GPT 6.1 Sol

1 In the name of Him of whom all are heedless.

2 Praise sanctified above the tongues of contingent beings and exalted beyond the remembrance of created things befits the holy court of the King of Eternity, who, through a sprinkling from the boundless ocean of His unfading glory, has guided every atom of existence to the city of His pure, everlasting life, and through a breath of His holiness has directed the realities of all things to the paradise of the glory of His oneness. The cloud of His transcendent grace has bestowed such mercy that every atom considers itself rich and independent of all else. The sun of His divine providence has shone so brightly from the horizon of all-glorious majesty that, in all the mirrors of contingent existence, within each one’s own essence and through its own self, the image of the radiance of the sun’s light has been imprinted. Exalted is His grandeur, supreme His sovereignty, perfect His beneficence, manifest His Cause, and radiant His face. He is indeed the Sovereign, the Everlasting, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. It is because observers have fixed their gaze on these degrees that some have advanced claims and, intoxicated by the wine of divine mercy and the Kawthar of transcendent generosity, have lost their restraint and ascended to stations above their own—in the realm of mention and words, not in the realm of meaning, reality, and essence.

3 Primal blessing and the pure essence of exalted mercy rest upon the first lofty divine Tree and the first appearance of the Sun of Oneness, through whom the mystery of existence was revealed, the beauty of the Desired One rose, and contingent beings returned to seats of praiseworthy holiness; through whom the beauty of the Unseen shone forth, the nightingale of glory called out, the dove of the Cause sang, and every hidden thing was revealed; and upon His guides and His loved ones until the day on which the Sun of Eternity rises from the horizon of the glory of the Beloved.

4 Now, a question had been asked concerning the Sun of Truth and the mirrors that reflect it. It is known to you that, since the winds of discord have blown from every direction and the breaths of malice and hatred have encompassed every quarter, this servant is unable to speak of the wonders of hidden knowledge and the jewels of treasured wisdom. For if this subject were truly set forth as God—exalted be His glory—has willed, you would see everyone bewildered and thunderstruck. Indeed, if you were to lift the veil and leave the city of illusion in which all people, heedless of God, have now taken up residence and secluded themselves, you would find the forms of those arrayed in ranks and the souls of the exalted to be nonexistent, perishing, and sheer nothingness.

5 But since God, the Exalted, has not wished to turn seekers away deprived from the door of His mercy, a sprinkling from the vast ocean of generosity is bestowed upon you and the people of the contingent world, so that each may attain their allotted share.

6 It is known to those endowed with knowledge and vision that the Sun of Oneness and pure Essence of Eternal Self-Sufficiency has always risen, shone, and shed its light, and will forever do so, upon its seat of sanctified glory and within its holy retreat of transcendence. Thus, all who possess keen vision and subtle perception, those endowed with a divine nature, and those who drink the wine of subtle meanings behold that divine Sun and Essence of sovereign glory in its own abode, with the eye of insight, which is sanctified above these darkened, veiled eyes, in a station where no setting, decline, or eclipse can overtake it.

7 As for those who become veiled by selfish desire and are deprived of beholding the lights of the true dawn, which has always been illumined through the sun’s bounty, this brings no harm to the radiance of the sun’s light. For it shines, gleams, and radiates in its own abode, while this failure of perception has always pertained, and will always pertain, to the sight of the beholder and the vision of the observer. Likewise, all this denial and affirmation, whether from those who turn toward it or those who turn away, pertains to the stations of these weak or strong souls themselves; it can in no way pertain to that Sun of Truth. For that Essence of existence abides in its own lofty exaltation and inaccessible sublimity, sanctified above the affirmation of those who turn toward it and the denial of those who turn away.

8 Yet every eye that perceives that pure Essence of Eternity through its original nature will be reckoned as belonging to Him and will be gathered beneath His shadow for all eternity. Such a soul will enter and dwell in the all-glorious Paradise, the highest of all paradises. Whoever does not will enter the darkness of self and the fire of negation, and will indeed remain there for as long as God wills.

9 Moreover, if that Sun did not shine and this absolute grace were to withhold its bounty from the mirrors of created things, you would find nothing whatsoever existing on the face of the earth. Furthermore, the mirrors of oneness do not exist through themselves or for themselves. This matter has been recorded and inscribed in all the Tablets and Scriptures and is hidden from no spiritually discerning or enlightened, mature soul. Rather, the mirrors exist through the existence of the Sun, whether they are aware of this lofty degree or heedless of it, for the bounty of that Sun shining from the horizon of oneness has always been, and will always be, unconditional. This is the station of God’s all-embracing mercy, which He bestows without entreaty or seeking, recognition or certitude. The only distinction is that those mirrors that have perceived this most exalted degree and all-glorious station will occupy a higher rank and station than the others.

10 Consider further that, if the sun did not exist, the mirrors would have no existence whatsoever and would remain forever behind the veils of nonexistence. When the Sun of Eternity rises from the horizon of holiness, all the mirrors reflect it once they face it and draw near. If the sun sets, the images imprinted in the mirrors, which are reflected likenesses, will certainly set as well. This matter is too evident for anyone of sound understanding to fail to perceive.

11 Let any obstinate denier who rejects this evident subtle truth consider the visible sun and the mirrors that reflect it: as long as a mirror faces the sun, its radiance and appearance can be seen in the mirrors, but after the sun has set, no trace will be found. Rather, all will be seen to be utterly nonexistent, wholly vanished, and entirely absent. In short, O seeker, cleanse the eye of spiritual insight, which is a divine trust, of the dust of dark, false words, so that you may now behold the Sun of Eternal Self-Sufficiency in the loftiness of its own station, at the center of the heaven of justice. By God, your eyes would indeed be gladdened if you beheld it and attained its beauty.

12 Yes, what is recorded in the revealed Tablets of the Bayan is truth in which there is no doubt, and we all believe in it. When that divine Sun and pure Essence of Eternity—may the souls of all else be sacrificed for Him—says that the Suns of Truth rise and appear from the horizon of holiness in every cycle and age, what is intended is universal manifestation: namely, the revelation of verses, the sending down of words, the changing of religious law, and the renewal of the affairs of the faith. It is with reference to this station that such statements have issued from that Essence of pristine nature.

13 I swear by that spiritual Beauty and that unseen holiness of the Eternally Self-Sufficient that, had He found the people mature, He would not have uttered such statements. For that station is sanctified above any mention of universal and particular, manifestation and concealment, since change and alteration occur in the realm of limitations, while that sought-after Essence abides in the seat of holiness. Glorified, then, is God above what you imagine. How many suns of unfading providence have risen without ever making a change or altering the religious order, instead summoning people to the same religious law that had previously been in force! For all are from God, all return to God, and all appear from God.

14 Turn your gaze, then, away from the earth and its inhabitants, and look toward the Most Great Scene and the most manifest Station, so that you may behold the divine Sun clothed in concealment and veiling. Although it has always been as manifest, brilliant, and luminous as the sun in the sky, several years have now passed during which it has remained hidden by the veil of envy and hatred from the eyes of those endowed with recognition, let alone those who are confined and veiled. In every circumstance, then, swim with keen perception in the deep ocean of meanings, so that you may attain the pearls of the ocean of oneness and become independent of all else.

15 Furthermore, do not surrender the entire reins of your vision to others, or content yourself with the outward meaning of certain expressions and allusions. For all these outward words, holy allusions, and exalted indications change in every cycle. This very change, transformation, and renewal establish that beyond every utterance something has lain concealed in divine knowledge, and above every wisdom something will remain stored in the treasury of heavenly wisdom. Thus it was observed among former peoples that the wisdom of the words and the manner of expression in the Torah changed and were transformed after the melodies of the Holy Spirit appeared. So it continued until the advent of the Point of the Bayán and the Countenance of the All-Glorious—may the souls of all in the kingdom be sacrificed for Him. He brought forth all the gems and pearls within the shells of the oceans of the Qur’an from their depths and adorned them with new and exalted forms.

16 Likewise, in the age of the Sovereign of Eternity and the Most Great King, Him Whom God will make manifest, all these exalted words and wondrous expressions will change. It is as though one can behold that pure Essence of Oneness and that Essence of ancient glory appearing from the dayspring of divinity, without how or where, direction or indication, and taking His seat in the highest Paradise and upon the most glorious Throne—that same still earth which is the dwelling place of that Sun of Eternity. He will reveal all the mysteries deposited in the Bayán, and no one but that Ancient Beauty will know, as they deserve to be known, the pearls concealed in this most great Ocean. He is the Revealer and Interpreter of the mysteries and knowledge ordained in the Bayán.

17 Yes, the mirrors that receive His image will perceive according to their ranks and capacities, not as He is in Himself. Therefore no one will be able to draw near to the Tree of Meaning upon the Sinai of the Bayán except those whom your Lord wills. This is why it has been said: do not, of your own accord, content yourself with the outward meaning of certain expressions, for minds fall short of comprehending it. Otherwise, a single letter of these revealed Books is dearer to this servant than all who are in the heavens and on earth. None knows this except those endowed with understanding.

18 Likewise, that holy Being will set aside these expressions that are exalted today. For example, the designation “Sun,” which in the Bayán is the greatest designation and the most sublime expression: He may bestow this designation and rank upon one of His servants. He grants this greatest bounty and most mighty grace without any deserving, but through His own grace, mercy, and generosity. He will designate His own noble Self, which is the place where the fire of the Primal Tree appears and the light of the Last rises, as “Heaven,” for the Sun will rise from His horizon and depend upon His existence. Just as today you find mention of the Sun throughout the Bayán, so in the Tablets of that Heaven of holy meanings you will find mention of Heaven. By that same holy, unseen Essence and that purest, most sacred Glory!

19 If I wished to speak until the end that has no end of what the holy Manifestations will utter at the beginning of their appearance, I could do so through the grace of my Lord. Yet because I have been and remain imprisoned amid false suspicions, I have not deemed it fitting to convey the songs of the Dove of the unseen, eternal realm. For the beauty of the everlasting Rose and the paradise of the glory of Oneness befit the song of the spiritual Nightingale—not the company of these weak, idolatrous souls, who are so utterly lost and effaced within the veils of their imaginings that, even if they beheld the pure unseen Essence with the clearest holy utterances and the most glorious divine revelations, they would turn away and close their eyes. In brief, O questioner, exchange the step of Satan for the tread of the All-Merciful, and walk through the outspread land of understanding by the power of direct vision and spiritual disclosure, so that you may attain the destination of certitude, which is the station of recognizing the Revealer of these words, and gain insight into the City of Oneness, which is the most glorious Paradise itself. Blessed, then, are those who attain!

20 The rising and setting mentioned in connection with the eternal Manifestations are not the rising and setting that these souls understand. For this rising and setting fall within the compass of sight, whereas that sought-after Essence stands beyond vision in the Most Great Scene. Rather, “setting” means that the eyes of the veiled and the vision of those confined by limitations fail to perceive. For example, if the outward sun passes behind a cloud, outward eyes are prevented from beholding it, and to their sight the sun is absent, although in its own station it remains radiant and bright. But clouds do not obstruct the vision of those endowed with spiritual sight; they are not shut out by veils of glory, and they behold it from beyond a hundred thousand veils and coverings.

21 Consider how most of the people of the earth, despite the lofty ascent of the Sun shining in the Bayán, have paid no attention at all, and have remained and will remain in darkness, concealed and veiled. Likewise, consider the people of the Bayán: despite the lofty elevation and surpassing grandeur of this exalted, most sublime Cause, which is manifest and resplendent in the heaven of most glorious holiness, no one has perceived it, and all have followed and will follow their own desires. Yet this lack of attention is no proof that that Sun of Eternity does not exist. That Most Exalted Pen, that Most Glorious Essence, that purest and most resplendent Being, has enjoined in all the revealed Books and dispatched Scriptures that, should a soul or a being, a light or a revelation, rise from the horizon of the divine Will, they should allow nothing to veil them and should enter beneath that blessed shadow, which is the very shadow of God. He has made the whole Bayán, and the prescribed deeds and ordained acts within it, culminate in the recognition of that holy Soul. Nevertheless, they have remained so thoroughly veiled by their suspicions, selves, and imaginings that they have never sought the divine River, the Ocean of Eternity, or the everlasting heavenly Paradise. Indeed, they have not sought to recognize that holy Being, recognition of whom is the very recognition of God. They all consider themselves established in the abode of faith and the seat of certitude, unaware that today they have not been and will not be remembered before God. God will never look upon them, speak with them, or turn toward them, although the proof in its entirety has appeared in its fullest manifestation and at its highest ascent, leaving no one any room to turn away or close their eyes unless they turn away completely from all the revealed Books and all the Messengers sent forth.

22 O unworthy, immature servants! If a single drop from the cloud of mercy were bestowed upon the whole earth, you would behold all the forms of the trees of existence bearing the divine fire and the utterance “I am God.” If a sprinkling from this most great Ocean were poured upon all the mirrors of created things, you would behold all beings adorned with holy glory. In brief, the flight of this divine Falcon is too high for earthly birds to reach, and the melody of this Dove of holy glory is too subtle to be sullied by the ears of these heedless inhabitants of the perishable earth. God speaks the truth, but the people do not hear.

23 Yes, through these unceasing outpourings of bounty, all contingent existence and all beings have been made ready. It remains to be seen at what time and hour they will present themselves, whether in heart or in body, in the station of nearness before the All-Merciful, which is the highest rank of the throne of manifestation and concealment. Perhaps the holy breeze of the unknowable will blow from the paradise of inaccessible glory and draw those distant souls, who dwell in the lowest degree of limitation, to this most exalted abode and this loftiest retreat, so that they may not be deprived of this wondrous bounty and exalted grace. God provides for whomever He wills, but I am not one who provides for the dead. He, indeed, is the Provider, the Bestower, the Generous.

24 Furthermore, you had asked about the plurality of the Daysprings of Oneness and the holy Manifestations. You have seen that the Cause of God, the divine Will, and the heavenly Purpose have never been, and are not, confined within limits. In some ages there has been a single Person, while at other times multiple Figures have appeared from the dayspring of the Cause, as is recorded, demonstrated, and evident in all the Books, earlier and later. Were this servant to wish to set forth the details, an extensive book would have to be written.

25 If in some utterances of the Manifestations of the Cause and the holy Daysprings you find a restriction to one, do not let it veil you. Look instead to the root of the Tree of the Cause and the holy Tree, for the Cause of God has always appeared and will forever appear without limit or number. Rather, these restrictions upon the reflecting mirrors are intended to safeguard and conceal the Cause of God from rebellious souls and pharaonic figures, so that they may not act presumptuously, or defile the stronghold of the Cause’s inviolability with the treachery of their gaze and hearts, and so that satanic manifestations may not set foot upon Solomon’s seat of holiness.

26 As can be seen today, every soul has a desire in its head, and every demon of the self that commands evil has fallen into delusion amid its own imaginings, while a cry has risen from every corner. Many dead bodies and hearts have risen up with the braying of their own desires and set foot in the land of Pharaoh. Yet for those endowed with understanding who have attained maturity in the city of oneness, truth has always been and will remain distinct and separate from falsehood, just as the two have never been, and will never be, confused.

27 In short, God, in His holy Essence, has not, in any manifestation among the Manifestations or in any affair among His affairs, been confined within the limits of writing that appears in ink, or veiled by the veils of words. The Cause is in His hand; He brings forth whatever He wills, and He indeed does whatever He wills. Nothing other than Him will ever be mentioned in His presence or attain to Him. He will never be numbered by any reckoning or circumscribed by any limit. No gesture will ever point to Him, nor will He be joined to any indication, concealed by any veil, or hindered by any words. He is indeed the Unique, the All-Sufficing, who has always willed through His own will and will forever exercise power through His own purpose. All act at His command.

28 It is because the people of the Book have not attained that station that, in every appearance of the divine Manifestations, they have remained veiled from the Source and Originator of the Cause, cast into the darkness of delusion—indeed, reduced to nonexistence and nothingness. Glory be to God! How can the will of anyone other than Him take precedence over His will, when all besides Him have received life through His word and have been gathered, at the resurrection of their being, in their appointed places? Thus, in the Persian Tablet written some years ago, most of the matters that had been hidden from the eyes of the mystic knowers were mentioned. If anyone considers it with the eye of the heart, they will grasp all sacred knowledge and the meanings of the consummate words—indeed, they will behold them directly.

29 In short, leave the frailest of houses—the imaginings of perishable, rejected souls—and enter the holy, pure, fortified city. Do not exchange the melodies of the Dove of the glory of oneness for the words of the rabble of the earth, who have been formed and fashioned in darkness. Do not forsake the heavenly Salsabīl of holiness flowing from the right hand of the throne of the All-Merciful and content yourself with a fleeting, murky draught. Kindle in your heart the flame of Moses, and breathe into your inmost heart the breath of the Holy Spirit of Jesus. Like Abraham, the Friend endowed with the glory of submission and serenity, enter this blazing, ancient fire and this burning flame. Efface your heart, your inmost being, and your will in the heart, the inmost being, and the will of the Beloved, so that God may grant you light and mercy. He is indeed the guardian of those who do good.

30 Be watchful with the utmost attention, lest you remain veiled from the first name revealed in the prayer for the Night of Destiny, and lest the rejection of those who turn away and the denial of the deniers prevent you from reaching and entering the shore of the Most Great Ocean. For this blessed name, which precedes all names, has always been illumined by the radiance of the Sun of holiness and will forever be brightened by the flashing splendors of the unseen Presence. An illumined eye is needed to behold the manifestations of the fire of the Lote Tree of Sinai from the place of revelation on the holy, pure Mount. The Most Exalted Point and the All-Glorious Beauty have spoken at length and in detail of this name and its precedence over all names. Turn, then, to the Bayān, if you wish to know.

31 Although the revealed verses and manifest proofs are too evident for anything else to be used as evidence, the people of delusion and conjecture, who speak only from desire, have looked to arguments of this kind, and today they will remain as they were before. If anyone today claims that the realm of the divine Cause has been confined within a limit or indicated by an allusion, such a person is the furthest of all people from the straight path of justice. For the realm of the Absolute and of the Cause has, from all eternity, been sanctified above restriction and limitation. If pure, stainless hearts were to turn, for less than an instant, toward the Sun of oneness, which today remains imprisoned in people’s conjectures and confined within the imaginings of His servants, all the mysteries of what has been and what is to come would be imprinted upon those hearts, according to the degree of their turning and their capacity.

32 Yet now, besides failing to turn toward so great a bounty, most souls display malice and hatred. Nevertheless, they count themselves among the believers in the Bayān and rejoice, unaware that the whole Bayān, in every tongue, curses those who turn away from this station. In short, all who are veiled have now taken up their abode in associating partners with God, in the fire of denial and remoteness, in the dungeon of torment, and in the abyss of wrath. It is as though not one of these heedless people has ever possessed, or now possesses, any awareness. They have become so utterly dead and reduced to nothing that they have not been stirred by the breeze of loving-kindness, which at every moment blows in manifest, evident breaths from the hidden sanctuary of the bounty of the Lord of Glory—much less have they pitched their tent in the realm of eternity or raised their pavilion on the shore of the Most Great Ocean.

33 All these utterances are recorded in the Tablets in consideration of the expectations and requests of His servants. Otherwise, the radiant, dawning, resplendent Sun is sanctified above being described by any description, characterized by any attribute, or mentioned in any remembrance. All remembrances and descriptions are illumined and made manifest by a single ray from Him. Hear the call of the Holy Spirit speaking in this luminous, lofty Tree, through a single melody of which perishable, nonexistent bodies enter the everlasting, enduring realm of might, so that you may behold the whole earth and its people, high and low, in the shadow of a shadow. Thus may you leave the overturned cities of conjecture and delusion and, through the firm foundations of these pearl-like words and clear proofs, enter the paradise of reality, where the heavenly throne is established, and partake of the ripe fruit of the garden of oneness.

34 In the divine springtime, the days when the furthermost Tree appeared and the Most Exalted Countenance rose in splendor, was it not witnessed how many holy souls and glorious figures shone forth from that ancient Sun and were illumined by it, each claiming exalted stations and limitless ranks, as all these people now living have seen with their own eyes? His honor Quddūs, for example, who was the highest and most glorious fruit of the Tree, appeared and was witnessed with such sovereignty that it was as though the tree of fire had called out through a single revelation of his splendor. So too did the Countenance of Ṭā, and the one below those two who is present among you.

35 Yet although all these people have witnessed, heard, and understood all these degrees with their own eyes and ears, they have nevertheless clung to certain sayings and words and remained deprived of the One who sends down and bestows all-embracing mercy. Although only a few days have passed since what they saw with their own eyes, they have cast such doubt and uncertainty upon their own observations that they ask about them again. They are so immersed in conjecture that, if someone were to advance, out of personal desire, two arguments frailer than a spider’s house and declare all the infinite outpourings of God to be finite and limited, they would all accept them willingly, without compulsion, and regard what they themselves had heard and witnessed as though it had never been. A hundred thousand expressions of astonishment and regret for such weak, wavering, inconstant souls, who at the slightest breeze turn from the right hand of knowledge and steadfastness to the left hand of conjecture and error!

36 They are like the peoples of the past, who, through their deluded conjectures, have brought all the successive outpourings and unending manifestations of God to an end, and have been, and will remain, worshippers of their own desires rather than God. Yet in all the books, tablets, scriptures, pages, scrolls, and epistles, it is explicitly stated and set forth at length, not merely hinted, that the grace of the All-Bountiful can never be suspended, that the favor of the Bestower can never be delayed or cut off, and that the infinite manifestations can never end. Nevertheless, the condition of all who dwell on earth can now be seen: they acknowledge and confess that grace and favor are limited and have ceased—indeed, they are content and rejoice in this. Since these people strove to manifest this revelation within themselves and turned toward this Sun, the hand of grace did not bar them from this station. This is a token of His outspread grace, which has encompassed all things, but most people do not understand.

They stopped at that point and then devoted themselves to it according to their own desires, not according to what God desired for them. Therefore their names were recorded among the worshippers of names, because of their devotion to them. For God’s intention in this name was that they should order their religious affairs in the primary reality, yet they veiled themselves from this, as you know. Since they did not believe in the manifestations of justice, it is established that they did not attain the true ordering of affairs that proceeds from this blessed and beloved name.

77 Then consider the revelation of this name in the soil: whatever is entrusted to its depths is not betrayed there. Likewise, behold the revelation of this Sun through this name in everything, so that you may become acquainted with the suns of the names and be among those who are acquainted with the mysteries of the Cause.

We have contented Ourselves with what We have explained to you; you too should be content with what has been apportioned to you by this hidden Pen. We have opened before your face the doors of knowledge and wisdom through what We have imparted to you in truth, so that you may draw from this ocean whatever pearls of concealed knowledge you desire. Should you wish to bring forth a hundred thousand doors from every door, you will be able to do so if you turn toward this veiled and treasured Name, who has been afflicted at the hands of those who have not believed in God for the blink of an eye, though they claim for themselves the very essence of faith.

For the knowledge that has always been accounted knowledge in God’s sight is recognition of Him and recognition of the Manifestations of His Cause, if you look into the Books of God.

80 You will find all this in the Bayān, if you read it. My pen will move only as the Pen of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting, has moved. Say: O people of the Bayān, do not hold Me to account because verses have descended upon Me. By God, this fault is not Mine; rather, the Spirit raises Me up at every moment, if you listen. Were you to purify yourselves, you would find the fragrances of God wafting from this diffusing musk. But since you have deprived yourselves of this paradise, you will find nothing, just as the peoples of the Qur’an did not find the fragrance of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

81 As for you, O questioner, reflect on what We have imparted to you and take for yourself whatever you desire. If by the sun you meant these shining suns, We have disclosed their names and mysteries to you and shown you their revelations in the mirrors of created things and their radiance upon all that has been and will be. But if you meant the Sun of suns, the Sovereign of existence and the King who is the object of all desire, around whom these suns and their realities revolve, say: Glory be to the Lord of grandeur, before whom all who possess grandeur and power bow down! Glory be to the Lord of might, before whom all who possess might and eminence submit! Glory be to the Lord of glory, before whom all who possess glory and invulnerability humble themselves! Glory be to the Lord of strength, before whom all who possess strength and splendor lower themselves!

82 Then know that none other than Him can attain to Him, and all besides Him is nonexistent in His presence. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Beloved. All names are called into being by His word, and all attributes appear at His command, if you know. Beware of ascribing this station to anyone other than Him. Mention no one when mentioning Him, and associate with Him neither allusion nor expression. For He, exalted and mighty is He, cannot be indicated by the allusion of another, nor can He be encompassed by the understanding of His creatures, if you understand. The highest understanding of those who know and the attainment of those who attain refer only to His creation, brought into being by His saying, “Be,” and it is. Everything besides Him is created at His command, fashioned by His will, prostrate before His countenance and submissive in His presence. All is with Him in a preserved Tablet.

83 He will appear in truth, as God has promised you in the Book. Fear God, and do not speak concerning His Cause. Await His day, just as all who are in the heavens and on earth await His days, though they themselves are unaware. Then know that I have believed in Him before His appearance, after His appearance, and at the time of His appearance. Await His days in accordance with what has been inscribed upon the Tablets of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Beware of mentioning this name, and entertain no evil thought concerning His Cause. Be among those who seek forgiveness every day.

84 We have forbidden you to mention this name lest anyone imagine himself to have drawn near to this hidden Tree, and lest you ask about anyone as you have asked. Do not designate anyone by this name, for it belongs exclusively to Him, if you listen to what this Dove teaches you through the words of God, the Almighty, the Self-Subsisting, which the Spirit has imparted to it. O people, do not be remiss concerning the Book, do not distort the word of God, and do not be among those who speak of what they do not know.

85 By God, the dust beneath His feet is more exalted than all that has been or will be created, and than all that has been and will be. Were I assured that souls would not be thrown into turmoil, I would say that it is the throne of God on earth, for upon it rests His mighty and beloved foot. Remain so steadfast in what We have imparted to you that your feet will never slip. Do not be among those who do not understand and ascribe this station to whomever they wish. By God, this is an error without parallel in the world of being. Fear God, O people, and do not transgress the bounds set in the Book of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

86 Glory be to You, O God, my God! You are the One who inspired Me with the wondrous expressions of Your remembrance, then prevented Me from disclosing them among Your creatures. You have brought Me to a station where I am prevented from disclosing the all-embracing mysteries of Your wisdom because the signs of heedlessness have appeared among Your creation. For, O my God, if I conceal from the people what You have bestowed upon Me through Your generosity, I would then be ungrateful for Your favor; and if I tell them of what You have graciously conferred upon Me, the clamor of Your servants and the outcry of Your worshippers will rise. Yet I see all this as coming only from You, for You have raised Me up and made Me manifest in days the like of which You have never chosen for raising up any of Your chosen ones. I do not know what You have intended thereby through the manifestations of Your decree and ordainment, and the workings of Your enactment and ordering.

87 By Your might, O my Beloved, I testify that Your knowledge has never encompassed a day greater or more severe than these days, for in them all Your servants, both lowly and eminent, are put to the test. Yet despite my helplessness, poverty, affliction, and destitution, I will remain steadfast in Your Cause through Your power and strength, even if no one turns toward this Face seeking to meet You. I thank You for the steadfastness You have bestowed upon me and with which You have honored me, which no one can dislodge from its station, even if the swords of the deniers and the rejection of those who associate partners with You are raised over my head. I testify that a particle of this steadfastness is better than the worship of both humankind and jinn. Through it my heart finds rest and my soul becomes calm, and I regard all who are in the heavens and on earth as no more than a handful of clay—indeed, as less than that—except those who have entered beneath Your shadow in this most perfect, most great Name, through which all names have been transformed and all attributes changed in the dominion of the revision of divine decree. For those who do not recognize You have no standing with You, nor are they mentioned in Your presence. I therefore ask You, O my Beloved, not to let my foot slip from Your Cause, alongside which nothing can stand. Make me steadfast through Your generosity and grace, and detach me from all besides You. You are indeed the Almighty, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise, the Self-Subsisting.

88 We had intended to write these Tablets in Persian, but God held the Pen back from it and returned it to a clear Arabic tongue. Thus We have imparted to you some of the mysteries of knowledge and wisdom in this tongue. Now We return to the tongue of the Persians, so that God’s testimony, His proof, [corrupted original text], and His signs may be complete for you, that you may be among those who turn toward God, who created Me and you. We are indeed among His worshippers. Praise be to God, the Lord of all worlds.

89 From these utterances shining from the horizon of exposition, it has become clear and demonstrable that those endowed with wisdom have applied, and continue to apply, the name “sun” to each of the manifestations of these radiant names already mentioned. Thus, if mention of the sun is found in the utterances of the Manifestations of divine unity and the Daysprings of eternal holiness, what is intended will be these shining suns and radiant lights, except as your Lord may will.

90 Furthermore, it has been clear and demonstrable to those possessed of knowledge and wisdom, sound dispositions and illumined natures, that in every age and era when the veils of glory are lifted from the face of beauty, and that concealed, veiled, and hidden Countenance emerges visibly from the veils of the unseen, that Tree of divine Reality and ripe Fruit of God must be recognized through Himself. For all besides Him has been, and will remain, beneath His shadow, and the hidden stations of that Treasure of unity are apprehended through His radiant utterances.

91 A penetrating sight, sanctified from perceiving flaw and limitation, does indeed behold the traces of God’s greatness in all that proceeds from Him. For the splendors of that radiant, all-encompassing Sun are too manifest to remain hidden from the eyes of those who possess true knowledge, and the surging of that Most Great Ocean is too evident to be concealed from the sight of those brought near. Yet because people differ in their degrees of knowledge and certitude, solely to manifest grace and generosity and to extend bounty and favor to all existence, He has appointed the verses sent down from the heaven of His will and the clear evidences shining from the horizon of His grace as His testimony, so that all people, high and low alike, may recognize that eternal Essence and most perfect Word through this manifest and conclusive testimony and these radiant, resplendent verses.

92 All this has been, and will remain, a token of His grace to His servants, without their deserving it, so that they may not be deprived of divine outpourings and God’s holy favors. Otherwise, the rejection of all beings and the acceptance of all created things would be alike in that most holy court. Furthermore, on the day of the appearance of that Most Great Luminary, no excuse from anyone will be accepted. For example, if everyone on earth were to draw upon all the heavenly scriptures and revealed books to establish a matter, and that Essence of being were to appear contrary to that firmly established and acknowledged matter, no one would have the right to object. For every command and prohibition is conditional upon and dependent on His will and acceptance, whereas the utterances issuing from that Heaven of unity have never been, and will never be, conditional upon or dependent on anything.

93 O seeker, enter this Sinai of the spirit with an illumined heart, so that you may behold the splendor of the Sun of meanings upon the Sinai of the heart, shatter the idols of fancy and conjecture with the power of Abraham, and, freed and sanctified from imitation, restriction, and limitation, ascend to the heights of certainty, constancy, and steadfastness.

94 Consider also: if all the people of the Bayán were to draw upon everything in the Bayán to establish a condition for the next Revelation, and were to take it as certain that the promised Beauty and Sun of existence must appear with this stated sign, but that Essence of free choice were to appear otherwise, what would you do then? Would what you had understood make you independent of recognizing that Sun of knowledge? No, by the One who taught this Pen the mysteries of eternity! Nothing will suffice you except holding fast to the hem of His loving-kindness.

95 Have you not observed how the peoples of the past, through their devotion to the idols of conjecture and desire, have remained deprived of the grace of the Day of Decision? In every condition and circumstance, take refuge in God, so that nothing you see in the heavens and on earth, whether established utterances or outward events, may keep you from the Tree of desire and from recognizing Him, which—which is itself the recognition of the divine Manifestations who have neither beginning nor end. Strive not to remain veiled from the pouring rain of God’s uplifted clouds.

96 Although the cloud of grace is raised at all times and never ceases, not every cloud has been, or will be, the manifestation of all-encompassing bounty. Consider the sun and its ascent and descent. Although in its own essence it is sanctified from ascent and descent, with the changing of its stations and zodiacal signs it appears and shines in different degrees. In Leo, for example, it appears with the fullness of its radiance and heat, while elsewhere it is seen otherwise. Now that, by God’s grace, the cloud of mercy has encompassed the whole earth and its inhabitants, and the sun of loving favor shines visibly with the fullness of its splendor and heat, do not occupy yourselves with the whisperings of the devils of self and the embodiments of hatred and envy.

97 In every condition and circumstance, know God to be a freely choosing Agent who does whatever He wills, and remain firm and unwavering in this all-embracing statement, the greatest of revealed words, so that you may remain steadfast in God’s religion and His Cause. This is pure truth and unalloyed veracity, and what is there beyond truth but error? Just as God’s sanctified Essence is His own proof, and all besides Him falls short and is powerless to recognize Him as He truly deserves, so the verses sent down from the heaven of His Cause bear witness to His Revelation. All besides Him is sheer nonexistence, absolute nothingness, and unmentioned in that court. God is sufficient as a witness.

98 One must devote the fullest measure of understanding and reflection to these all-embracing revealed words, so that from each of them gates of endlessly infinite knowledge may open before the hearts of seekers and aspirants. This is a station into which no one can set foot except with the essence of a pure nature and an unalloyed disposition. If the mirrors of the heart are stained, even by less than a hair’s breadth, with the dust of anything besides Him, the splendor of this exalted Light and wondrous Cause will never be imprinted, reflected, or manifested in those mirrors. How lofty and inaccessible is this station, which admits nothing but pure sanctity, and how high and exalted, accepting nothing short of utter purity!

99 Ah, a sigh above every sigh! Ah, a sigh beneath every sigh! Ah, a sigh that fills the heavens with the lovers’ lamentation! Ah, a sigh that fills the earth with the cries of those who yearn! Ah, a sigh that makes radiant crimson tears flow from the eyes! Ah, and again ah, and yet again ah, from the beginning that has no beginning to the end that no learned scholar can reckon! Ah, a sigh whose fire appears in existence, whose anguish arises in manifestation, and which grieves all created things and, beyond them, the members of the assembly of those arrayed in ranks! Ah, a sigh by which hearts are burned and lands are shaken! Ah, a sigh that none can know but God, the Almighty, the All-Beautiful!

100 For the people have veiled themselves from the Beauty of God and His manifestation, from the Countenance of God and His dawning, and He has fallen into the hands of these people in a humiliation beyond comparison and a sorrow unequaled among either former or later generations. Through this, the life of every conscious being has been altered, and the pillars of a mighty throne have been shaken. Yet the people remain heedless and happy, in manifest delight.

101 Thus God brings to nothing the deeds of those who have neglected His remembrance and disbelieved in the verses of God, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Omnipotent, that are in their possession. And may the Spirit rest upon the people of the Spirit from this dawning, ancient Glory.

102 In these days of the supreme testing, guard yourself lest you slip from the straight path, for before long you will see all people agitated and shaken—indeed, turning away—except those whom your Lord wills. Two years ago, a sheet was revealed in the City, and all the events that have since occurred were recorded in it. If you would turn toward recognition today, consult that blessed sheet, so that you may perhaps rescue yourself from the abyss of extinction and enter the abode of everlasting life, pass beyond the city of names and its kingdom, and enter a noble station of holiness, the station of pure sanctity and utter, absolute transcendence. Through the wealth of God, you may then be freed from the abasement of asking. For asking has always been, and will remain, in itself evidence of the questioner’s wavering and lack of steadfastness, although the exalted Truth has loved, and still loves, to be asked, so that He may bestow grace and bounty. Yet true recognition has another station: it pertains to sight, heart, and inmost being, not to what pen and ink set down. Thus does the Pen of rectitude convey to you on this Day, to which the decree of the beginning and the return reverts.

103 You also asked what rank and station this servant has claimed. I am only a servant who has believed in God, His Messengers, His Books, and everything sent down from Him; in what was sent down to ʿAlī in truth; in what has been sent down at this time; and in what will be sent down in the time of the Mustaghāth, when everyone in the heavens and the earths will be made anew. Then the Hour will come, the Balance will be set up, the Bridge will be raised, and God will come with His Cause, if you are among those who recognize. Everything God has ordained within Me bears witness to this, if you are among those who bear witness.

104 For a number of years, every moment has passed like fifty thousand years, owing to successive afflictions and relentless adversities that have not ceased for an instant. Besides these outward afflictions—by Him through whose sovereignty and power the very being of all contingent things has appeared!—there have at every moment been inward afflictions, hidden from every eye, mind, and soul, of which no one but God has been aware. Apart from what has been mentioned, you have surely heard how many fabrications born of their lying suppositions the people of the Bayān have ascribed and attributed to this blessed, pure Tree of oneness. As though what the enemies had inflicted were not enough, this faction too has held back from nothing within its power and ability, although the existence of this servant has at all times been the cause of the exaltation of the good Word, as all of you have known and seen.

105 If, however, conjectural falsehoods should come between some people’s hearts and their sincerity, and they should deny this exalted grace, that is another matter; to God all affairs return. Consider the inward to be like the outward as well, if you look with the eye of the spirit. Amid all these afflictions, trials, and calamities, hidden and manifest, nothing but pure servitude has appeared in any act or utterance of this imprisoned servant. As has been observed, throughout His stay in the City of Peace this servant associated with all the people of that land and its surroundings, learned and otherwise. To the extent of His ability—indeed, beyond it—not a moment was neglected in aiding and exalting the Cause, until the exaltation of the Word reached such a degree that it encompassed all the people of the earth.

106 Never was any compromise made with the enemies of God. With the utmost earnestness and diligence, strenuous effort and resolute exertion were devoted to proclaiming the Word of remembrance. In the end, matters led to departure from the City. Every discerning person has also come to know how the might and exaltation of this Cause were manifested in that departure and migration, until We became imprisoned and confined in this place. In all this We thank God, praise Him, remember Him, and bear patiently with His decrees. He indeed pays in full the rewards of the patient. Praise be to God for all this. There is no refuge except in Him and no one whose help can be sought but Him. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Merciful.

107 Indeed, throughout all these past years, praise be to God, oceans of meaning have surged and divine lights have shone within the heart, so that, without any act of will, their sprinklings were bestowed upon contingent beings. The favor of the Lord of might has so encompassed Us that to number or delimit it is beyond the contingent world and its inhabitants. If this servant has made any mention of these degrees in the revealed Tablets, it has been in obedience to this exalted utterance: “As for the bounty of your Lord, proclaim it.” You, however, should look to the essence of the matter, which has never been, and will never be, confused with anything other than itself.

108 If you cannot apprehend the exalted Truth through His own Self and gain insight, but look instead to the words sent down from the heaven of the holiness of oneness, turn to the Books sent down from the heaven of the divine Will. In most of them you will find mention, both explicit and implicit, of this all-embracing, concealed Word. Examine the Great Epistle and other writings. Likewise, most of the verses of the Qayyūm al-Asmāʾ indicate the all-sustaining sovereignty of this lofty, exalted Tree. I swear by God that I am ashamed to mention these statements, for only a few days have passed since the appearance of that dawning Sun of oneness, and all have witnessed with their own eyes the manner of that appearance. Yet all have become veiled.

109 What mention or statement is more manifest than the Revelation itself, that it might be used as proof of it? Consider now how greatly the concealment and delay of this Cause indicate and attest the exaltation of this Revelation, as though its delay had no purpose other than to test and try everyone in the heavens and on earth. He likewise says: “In the year nine you will be granted the meeting with God.” Although some have sought to refer this manifestation of eternal holiness and this dawning of divine light to something other than His own Tree, and strive with all earnestness to distort this all-embracing utterance, the matter is in God’s hands: He guides whomever He wills to a straight path.

110 People of the Bayān! Make every effort not to be like the people of the Qurʾān, who have referred all attributes and names, from the highest station of the glory of dominion to the lowest degree of the human realm, to unseen countenances, while remaining heedless of the Dawning-Places of oneness and Manifestations of eternity who actually exist.

111 Now, servants, look with the eye of justice and fairness: I swear by the Revelation of Him whom God will make manifest that, throughout this servant’s residence in the Abode of Peace, not for a day or a night did outward circumstances give Me reason to expect to remain alive. For enemies on the right and left, to the south and north, were intent upon their purpose with the utmost determination, as all the inhabitants of that place know. At last divine destiny prevailed over satanic deception, and the sovereignty of the heights overcame the forms of the depths. With such exaltation and renown, this servant departed with a company of emigrants. The exaltation of this Name, its fame, and its manifestation came about without any act of will.

112 What more can I say of what this servant has endured and what has befallen Him? By God, no one possessed of love, a heart, vision, or awareness could bear to hear it.

113 In any case, the divine mysteries have always been concealed in the treasuries of protection and will forever remain secure and guarded within sheltering veils. Were the mountain of the servants’ souls not to be crushed, I would mention a hint of the veiled, shrouded, hidden mysteries. But God reveals whatever He wills, and I will not reveal what is concealed. All of you, and the people of the earth, have known how much servitude and humility this servant has shown toward all the guides and loved ones, from the first appearance of the Sun of Truth until today. Yet how many false accusations have been directed against this servant, and no one has sought to dispel them. To God I have committed My affair, and in Him I have placed My trust. In Him let His detached servants place their trust.

114 Another question was asked: how can the words of the exalted be changed into Sijjin, and the letters of affirmation be counted among the manifestations of negation? You know that the Sun of divine grace and the Ocean of heavenly bounty shone upon all the mirrors of existence. In some that have turned to face it, its disk is imprinted, while upon the others only the brightness of its rays appears. Thus, after that Sun of eternity has been imprinted in the forms of the mirrors, whatever description it gives of the mirrors, with attributes beyond number, is a description of itself, for it beholds the lights of its own face in the mirrors. All these exalted attributes in reality refer to it alone. This bounty exists in the mirrors through their facing it; once they turn away, these attributes will vanish and cease to exist.

115 Now cleanse your vision of the dust of all else, so that you may recognize a Sun such that, were all the mirrors of existence to face it for the briefest instant, the lights of truth and the signs of the glory of eternal and everlasting unity would be imprinted and reflected in them all. Otherwise, “God effaces whatever He wills and establishes whatever He wills, and with Him is the Mother Book.” The freely choosing Agent is free to choose. He effaces after establishing, and likewise the reverse. It is in this connection that He says, “Do not rejoice in what has come to you, nor despair over what has escaped you.” This station is the point of justice, at which all else has ever stood, and will ever stand, in fear and hope. Everyone who occupies a lofty position fears effacement, and everyone in a lowly position hopes for the station of affirmation. By Him in whose hand is the soul of Husayn! Were I to explain this station according to what has been ordained within it, you would see most people shaken. Recall His words—exalted be His mention—“If We willed, We would surely take away what We have revealed to you.” By My life! Were more than this imparted to you, those who follow falsehood would surely be filled with doubt.

116 I do not wish to say anything further on this subject, for people are immersed in sheer illusion. As for some of the events about which you asked, they occurred in this servant’s absence, and their good and evil rest with those who carried them out. In any case, everything is clear and manifest. If the veils of people’s illusions were torn apart and they examined the Cause of God with their own eyes, they would plainly behold all its mysteries. God willing, We hope that a bounty may appear from the source of the generosity and grace of the Lord of might and grandeur, drawing all who are veiled from the veils of darkness into the pavilions of unity. God bestows whatever He wills, and He is indeed the Most Bountiful, the Ancient. Praise be to God, the Lord of all worlds.

117 Further, this servant counsels everyone not to be heedless for a moment, but to watch over the Cause of God and exercise the utmost diligence and effort in preserving and protecting it, for this is the whole of goodness and its root. The first concern has been, and will ever be, the recognition of God, and everything that has been heard and witnessed revolves around this matter, for everything proceeds from Him and returns to Him. After recognition of the one divine Essence, following His command and enforcing His laws are among the greatest concerns. The breezes of remembrance have always blown, and will forever blow, from the city of His Cause, and through this holy, eternal breath the signs of God will remain established among His creatures. God willing, may all turn toward their Source with pure and sincere hearts at every moment and at all times, detaching themselves from everything but God, so that in these darkened days they may not remain veiled from recognizing that Sun of oneness.

118 This servant asks the Lord of might to bestow on everyone sight and hearing sanctified from limitation and imitation. God bestows whatever He wills, and He is indeed the Ever-Bestowing, the All-Knowing, the Generous. I conclude the melodies of the Spirit with the remembrance of God, the Most Great, the Supreme, the Mighty, so that their close may be musk. Truly, through this the souls of the sincere are drawn at every moment. Praise be to God, the Lord of all worlds.
