1 In the name of our Lord, the Most Holy, the Most Great, the Most Exalted, the All-Glorious.

2 Glory be upon whoever has torn through the veils on the Day of Return, shattered the idols of vain imaginings in the name of the Lord of Days, and arisen to serve the Cause in such a way that the might of the tyrants of the earth has not frightened him—those who have adorned their heads with turbans and seized the reins of the people in the name of their Lord, the Lord of creation. They used to enter mosques and places of worship to remember God and recite what He had sent down to His Beloved and Chosen One. Yet when the horizon of the world was illumined and the Sun of His Most Great Name rose above it—the Name through which the cause of every prophet was established and the mission of every messenger fulfilled—they turned away from Him, denied Him, cast aside His Cause, broke His covenant, and committed deeds that made the atoms lament and the heavenly maidens swoon in their chambers.

3 By the life of God! Their deeds have brought tears to the eyes of the inhabitants of the most exalted Paradise and the highest Garden. In His name they vaunted themselves among the people, yet after His appearance they inflicted on Him what the kingdom of utterance is powerless to describe. They have denied the Day of God, whose mention adorns the books, scriptures, scrolls, and tablets. This servant and all things bear witness to their heedlessness, ignorance, and error. These are the ones against whom the sentence of punishment pronounced in the Book has become binding. What God sent down in the Qur’an suffices them. Exalted is His utterance: “We gave them hearing, sight, and hearts, but neither their hearing, nor their sight, nor their hearts availed them in the least, since they denied the signs of God; and what they used to ridicule engulfed them.” They have taken up the book of their own selves and cast the Mother Book behind their backs. By the life of God! They are the idols, the sources of conjecture and the dawning places of vain imaginings; they are the tyrants of the earth and its pharaohs. They have clung to self and desire, turning away from the Lord of humanity, relying on their own pens and turning away from what has descended from the Most Exalted Pen.

4 Glory be to You, through whose verses those in the graves have stirred, and through the fragrances of whose shirt the Mount took flight when the light shone from the horizon of revelation. I ask You, by the lamp of Your Cause and the waves of the ocean of Your utterance, to strengthen Your loved ones in remembering and praising You and in arising to serve You. Then protect them, my God, by Your power and might, from those who have denied Your truth, rejected Your proof, and disputed Your verses. Make them, my God, the standards of Your victory in Your lands and the banners of Your glory in Your dominions. I further ask You, Beloved of the world, who are manifest through the Most Great Name, to grant success to him who has turned toward Your most exalted horizon and arisen to serve Your Cause among humanity. Lord, You see him championing Your religion, standing firm on Your path, and speaking Your name among Your servants. Strengthen him with a strength that neither the affairs of the world nor the clamors of the nations can arrest. You are the One who holds in His grasp the reins of strength and power, and around whose will revolve the manifestations of majesty and sovereignty. There is no God but You, the All-Powerful, the Most Exalted, the Almighty, the All-Seeing.

5 To continue, beloved of my heart and darling of my soul: the call of the bird of your utterance has brought me tidings, the song of the nightingale of your praise has drawn me, and the intoxication of the choice wine of your remembrance has so seized me that I wished to fly on wings of longing to a station God has sanctified beyond the mention of all who dwell in the world. When I entered the presence and attained the radiance of the Sun of Glory, I presented the contents of your letter before Him who sent down the Mother Book through a movement of His Most Exalted Pen in the kingdom of creation. After its contents had received the attention of the Lord of humanity, the Tongue of Grandeur spoke these words—glorified be His grandeur:

6 “Varqá, you who gaze toward the most exalted horizon, say:

7 Praise be to You, my God, who are sanctified beyond my remembrance and expression and beyond whatever Your creatures have spoken in Your kingdom and Your servants have uttered on Your earth, for having enabled me to journey through the realms of inner meaning until I reached their center—that station called the Luminous Spot and the White Land, which in another place You named the Land of Resurrection. There You showed me Your beauty, gave me the choice wine of Your utterance to drink, and made known to me what carried me beyond myself and caused me to forget all but You. I ask You, by this greatest favor and this supreme gift, to preserve for me this most exalted station. You are indeed the Lord of the next world and of this world. This mortal being, my God, bears witness to Your eternity; this weak one, to Your power and might; and this destitute lover, to the streaming radiance of the sun of Your beauty. You, my God, my Master, my Support, and my Desire, are the One who revealed Yourself through a letter of Your most exalted Word upon Mount Sinai. Then the branches and leaves, and beyond them the atoms, uttered what the Lote Tree uttered on the Mount of recognition from the All-Merciful. Through another letter You revealed Yourself to the kingdom of utterance, and then You sent down the Torah, the Gospel, and the Qur’an. Through a drop from the sweet stream of Your mercy flowing from Your Most Exalted Pen, the river of life appeared in the world of existence. Exalted is Your power, and great is Your sovereignty.

8 I ask You, who set the pulse beating in the body of the world with Your Most Great Finger, to draw me near to You in every condition and remind me of the days when I drank the river of reunion with You from the vein of Your grace and bounty. You, my God, are the One who called me to Yourself, admitted me beneath the canopies of Your majesty, and established me before Your face to serve, remember, and praise You. Lord, You see and know that my joy lies in remembering You and turning toward You, and my delight in Your grace and loving care. Send down upon me from the clouds of Your mercy what will keep me smiling in Your days. You are indeed the Lord of generosity and bounty, and in Your grasp is the kingdom of names. There is no God but You, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.” End.

9 May it bring you joy, beloved of my heart! You have drunk the water of loving care and life from the cups of the favors of my Beloved, your Beloved, and the Beloved of all who are in the heavens and on earth, in such a measure that the atoms should say, “May it bring you joy and refreshment, you who have attained this great bounty!”

10 You had written that Áqá Mírzá ‘Abdu’lláh, 9 66 [the glory of God], had borne the burdens of that beloved one, and you asked God—glorified be His glory—to reward him. These matters were presented at the most inaccessible, most holy, most exalted Court. This is what the Tongue of God uttered—exalted be His majesty: “Varqá, the Lord of Names declares: A favor has flowed from the Most Exalted Pen concerning him that nothing in the world can equal. This is the essence of recompense, which has ever been and remains beloved in the sight of God—glorified be His glory. Nevertheless, outward favors will also reach him, God willing. Give him, on My behalf, the glad tidings of what My tongue has uttered. I am the Ordainer, the All-Wise.” End.

11 What you wrote concerning the delight of the eye, Mírzá Rúḥu’lláh, was presented. This is what descended from the kingdom of will and utterance—glorified be His grandeur: “Varqá, you are to recite three times in his right ear: ‘You have come by the command of God, appeared to remember Him, and been created to serve Him, the Almighty, the Beloved.’ We made mention of him before, when [his mother] called for help, and at this time as well. I am the Most Bountiful, the All-Manifest. Remember her mother on My behalf, and give her the glad tidings of what the tongue of the Wronged One has uttered in His mighty prison.” End.

12 You had also written that “and make him” occurs in some revealed prayers and supplications, asking whether it would be considered an alteration if, when supplicating, one recited “and make me,” which refers to the speaker. The reply is that this is not an alteration, and both forms are permissible. Likewise, in the blessed expressions “Say: O God” and “Say: Praise be to You,” both omitting the word “Say” and retaining it are correct. Retaining it honors the blessed word that has issued from the mouth of the Lord of Oneness, while omitting it is permissible because the expression of servitude and personal application is more evident in this case.

13 What you wrote concerning Áqá Mírzá Ghulám-‘Alí—upon him be 9 66 [the glory of God]—and the particulars of his devotion, steadfastness, sincerity, and service was presented before the Throne. He said—glorified be His majesty: “Praise be to God, he is aflame with the heat of love and adorned with the light of recognition. The river of reunion is the most precious of the world’s jewels and more costly than the water of life. Praise be to God, he has succeeded in preserving it and has manifested its fruit. God willing, these signs will always appear through him. Blessed is the soul who has attained the meeting and arisen to do what befits this station. From this station We send Our greeting of glorification upon him and upon My loved ones there, who have cast aside the world and embraced what they were commanded by God, the Lord of eternity.” End.

14 What you wrote concerning Áqá Yúsuf (of Naráq)—upon him be 9 66 [the glory of God]—was presented at the most inaccessible, most holy, most exalted Court. This is what descended from the heaven of the will of our Lord, the All-Wise.

15 He is the Most Great, the Most Holy, the All-Glorious.

16 O Yūsuf, the Wronged One remembers you in a melody which, if all existence, unseen and visible, were to hear it, would cause all to forsake life and continued existence, hastening to the abode of sacrifice in this Path. When it appeared, the Straight Path called out: “The Promised One has come with His manifest, clear, and unmistakable Path. By God, I was created for this Day, to circle around this Path and, in its presence, announce to humanity this Revelation, through which God took His covenant from the Prophets and Messengers.” O Yūsuf, the shirt has stirred in the breeze of your Lord’s will, and through it the breeze of God has wafted over the lands and the fragrance of the All-Merciful has spread between the heavens and the earths. We have remembered you before, and at this time when the Wronged One has ascended the throne of His mighty Name. We counsel you and those who believe to observe that through which the sun of character rises from the horizon of a sound heart. O My loved ones, aid the All-Merciful through praiseworthy character. This is a word We have sent down in various Tablets. To this bears witness the One who has declared in every circumstance: “There is no God but Me, the Ordainer, the All-Knowing.” Glory be upon the people of Bahāʾ who have drunk the choice wine of eternity from the cup of the bounty of their generous Lord. End.

17 God willing, may all attain what is fitting. The very being of the world is seen as a single tongue, proclaiming at the highest pitch the words, “The Lord of heaven has come,” yet no one has attained a hearing ear except those whom God wills. The wonder is that it is itself speaking and itself heedless. Glorified be the One who has commanded me to remain silent and conceal His mighty and exalted Cause, even though its disclosure has reached a degree that tongues and pens are powerless to describe. Again, glorified be the One who has appeared and revealed whatever He desired, who has spoken and caused all things to declare that He is the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Aware. The Sun of Truth is manifest and shining in the fullness of its revelation, and the divine lamp is bright and radiant in the fullness of its light. Nevertheless, all are veiled and deprived. This lowly servant asks God—and you, too, should ask—that He heal, with the antidote of His loving-kindness, the chronic illnesses that have afflicted people through ignorance and lack of understanding. He is indeed the Healer, the All-Wise. Praise be to God, for He is the All-Powerful, the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful.

18 As to what you wrote concerning Āqā ʿAlī-Muḥammad, son of Ḥājī Aḥmad Mīlānī—upon them both be the glory of God—what you requested was graciously granted especially for him and was sent through his brother. Now, at this moment when this servant is engaged in writing this letter, His Holiness the Greater Branch of God—may my spirit, my being, and my very existence be sacrificed for the dust beneath His most pure feet—is not here; He has gone to the outskirts of this city. God willing, after His return, when this lowly servant is honored to enter His presence, I will bring the new letter that had been sent to His blessed attention and present what you had written.

19 As to your mention of Āqā Faraj Āqā and the degree of his sincerity, God willing, may he be enabled at all times to serve the Beloved of all existence and be assisted in remembering and praising Him. As to the inquiry concerning the entrusted goods, they arrived in full and intact. This servant bears witness to this, and behind him, the One who created him, fashioned him, and caused him to voice His beautiful praise. Since you mentioned him, and this servant also wished that he should be mentioned at the most inaccessible, most holy, most exalted Court, these particulars were presented before His face on the seventh night of the month of Ṣafar, and these resplendent verses and perfect words descended from the heaven of the loving-kindness of the Revealer.

20 His words—exalted be His grandeur: He is the Manifest, the Hidden.

21 O Faraj, We have remembered you before with a remembrance that the books of the world cannot equal. Your Lord is indeed the Reckoner, the All-Knowing. Rejoice that you have been remembered time after time and that the face of the Wronged One has turned toward you from this mighty fortress. Were you to become aware of what has been ordained for you by My Most Exalted Pen, you would soar on the wings of joy and gladness in this air, from the stirring of whose winds can be heard what the Countenance of Revelation heard on Sinai. Your Lord is indeed the Truthful, the Trustworthy. Blessed are you for having turned toward Him and heard the call of God as it rose between the heavens and the earths. By My life, whatever eyes see and minds comprehend will pass away, while what has descended from this perspicuous Pen will remain for you. Say: “Praise be to You, who have enabled me to turn toward Your horizon, caused me to hear Your call, and adorned me with the vesture of certitude when the learned of the age were lost in profound delusion.” Be content with what has been ordained for you, and remain steadfast in this Cause, at whose appearance the feet of those of mature understanding slipped.

22 And We remember the one named Muḥammad, who crossed land and sea until he attained the Most Great Ocean in this land where the call of the Prophets and Messengers has been raised. He came, stood at the door, heard the call of the Wronged One, and drank His sealed wine from the hand of His bounty. Thus have We remembered him as a grace from Our presence, and I am the Most Bountiful, the Generous. Glory be upon you, upon him, and upon whoever has perceived this precious and wondrous fragrance. End.

23 Yes, Ḥājī Āqā Muḥammad—upon him be 9 66 [the glory of God]—arrived and attained the honor and distinction of His presence. Time after time, outside the land of the Prison, he was granted what was desired, namely, the privilege of visiting the Beloved of all the worlds.

24 As to what was written concerning Ḥājī ʿAlī-Aṣghar and his apprentice—upon them both be the glory of God—after it was presented, the sun of loving-kindness shone forth and a most holy, most great Tablet descended. God willing, may they be granted the honor of receiving it. His words—exalted be His majesty:

25 In My name, the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

26 O ʿAlī-Aṣghar, the Lord of eternity is manifest, yet the nations are veiled from Him; the Sun of Truth is shining, yet eyes are deprived of Him. The Euphrates of mercy is flowing, yet people are kept from it. The Most Exalted Pen moves and speaks, yet all are heedless of its voice except those whom God wills. Whoever has attained recognition of the Lord of existence today is among those whose mention was inscribed by the divine Pen in the Books of old. Give thanks to the Beloved of all the worlds, who has assisted you to accept His grace and bounty. All the people of the world—scholars, mystics, and rulers—longed with all their souls for this most glorious, most luminous Day. Yet when the Sun of Revelation shone and the call was raised, they failed to turn toward Him and listen. The chief cause that has held the nations back has been, and remains, the learned of the age. The Messenger laments the oppression of those heedless souls, while that ignorant rabble clings to its own delusions and rejoices. The heedlessness of these souls has caused the heedlessness of the people of the world, and their oppression has caused the oppression of the nations. Yet the finger of power will rend the veils, even as it has already done.

27 And We remember Muḥammad-Ḥusayn, that he may rejoice in the remembrance of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. God willing, may you be adorned with the vesture of trustworthiness and manifest divine character. Praiseworthy attributes are like lights for the horizon of the heaven of humanity. Whoever attains them will shine with their light, and his relationship to God—exalted be His glory—will become clear and manifest. God willing, may all attain what will exalt the Cause. He ordains that which will elevate the station of the human being in the world of existence. To this every fair-minded and discerning person bears witness. End.

28 Praise be to God, the fresh divine banquet and the enduring, everlasting bounty flow and descend without interruption from the heaven of oneness. Anyone who reflects even a little will become aware of the ocean of God’s compassion—exalted be His glory. By night and day, the Tongue of Grandeur is occupied with remembering His friends and speaking counsel to His enemies. With the utmost compassion and mercy, He has summoned all the people of the world to the Most Exalted Horizon. Yet blessed is the one who has heard and turned toward Him, and woe to the heedless. Praise be to God, the Lord of all the worlds.

29 What you mentioned concerning the son of the honored one who has ascended to God, who was named Riḍā al-Rūḥ in the Book of Names, was presented, and this most inaccessible, most holy Tablet descended especially for him from the heaven of the will of the Beloved of all existence. His words—exalted be His majesty:

30 In My name, shining from the horizon of utterance.

31 A remembrance sent down by the All-Merciful for the one who has turned toward the Face on a day when the unity of the world has been shattered and lamentation has risen between the heavens and the earths, that he may rejoice in My remembrance, which God has made the choice wine of life for the people of existence, the Most Great Spirit among the nations, and the supreme gift to humanity. Exalted is the Lord of religions, who has come from the heaven of grace with manifest justice.

32 O Hādī! We sent forth the Qāʾim, but all the scholars of the age denied Him, except those whom God, the Lord of the worlds, willed otherwise. When successive years had passed, We manifested Our own Self in Our name, the Self-Subsisting. Yet the people turned away, until they issued rulings against Us with such injustice that the Faithful Spirit lamented over it. They seized Us time after time and imprisoned Us again and again: once in the land of Ṭāʾ, another time in Mīm, once again in Ṭāʾ, and in the Land of Mystery and this mighty prison. Through all this We thank God, and in His path We have accepted whatever befell Us at the hands of those who were heedless of God, the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent.

33 We remember your father, through whom the mouth of recognition smiled in the world of existence, and who attained the presence of God, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. We have remembered him with that which nothing on earth can equal, and We will remember him as long as the dominion and [corrupted original text] endure. I am the Compassionate, the All-Bountiful.

34 O Riḍā! Rejoice that the Lord of Names remembers you from the direction of the prison with a remembrance that enraptures the heavenly Concourse, the inhabitants of Paradise, and those who circle the Throne at morning and evening. Blessed is whoever turns toward you, visits you with what has been sent down from My Most Exalted Pen, and stands before your noble grave. You are the one who turned toward the horizon of God and directed yourself toward Him on a day when everyone heedless and far removed turned away from Him.

35 May the first remembrance uttered by the tongue of the Lord of eternity among the nations rest upon you, O you who speak in remembrance of God and turn toward the Face after all things have passed away. I bear witness that you drank the sealed wine from the hand of His name, the Self-Subsisting, cast vain imaginings behind you, and held fast to certitude through a strength from God, the Strong, the All-Subduing, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. Blessed are you, for your head has been adorned with the crown of acceptance and your person with the ornament of God’s loving care in His wondrous Day. Glory, remembrance, spirit, peace, and the fragrant breezes wafting from the garment of your Lord’s mercy rest upon you, O you who attained your Lord’s loving care when He came with manifest authority. End.

36 You mentioned the people of Sīsān and those enkindled souls. God willing, may they all attain the loving care of the True One. This prayer has at this very moment been answered. A most exalted, most holy Tablet has been sent down especially for those souls who have turned toward Him with certitude, and they have previously also received divine Tablets. His words—exalted is His station and glorious His majesty:

37 In the name of the One, the All-Seeing.

38 I give the friends in Sīsān the glad tidings of the loving care of the All-Merciful. All have been, and remain, under His merciful gaze, and have made their home beneath the shade of the lofty, divine Lote Tree. Reflect on the grace of God. The scholars of the world, who considered themselves the most learned, the most discerning, the most versed in religious law, and the most exalted of the world’s people, did not attain a single drop from the ocean of utterance, nor were they honored with a mention in the presence of the All-Merciful. Yet through the loving care of the True One—glorious is His glory—those souls have attained a station such that the Most Exalted Pen is occupied with their remembrance in the Most Great Prison. Exalted is this loftiest, most radiant station, and exalted is this greatest, most sublime remembrance! Who can adequately thank God? I swear by the Sun of Truth shining from the horizon of the prison: if a soul possessed as many tongues as there are atoms and offered thanks and praise from the beginning that has no beginning to the end that has no end, it would assuredly not equal a single “O My servant” issuing from the Dawning-Place of utterance. And now the tongue of the All-Merciful is proclaiming, “O My loved ones in Sīsān!” God willing, may all regard this station as great and strive to become aware of its greatness. If they truly attain this station, they will see themselves freed from the world and all its peoples.

39 O friends! The Beloved of all existence remembers you and has assigned a particular station to each of you in the kingdom of grace. Rejoice in the glad tidings God gives you. He is indeed the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful. We have remembered you before in verses of which none but God, the All-Reckoning, the All-Knowing, is aware. We enjoin wisdom upon you and guide you to His straight path. Hold fast to the cord of wisdom and deal tactfully with people, so that no clamor may arise from those who are heedless of My mighty Cause. Glory rest upon you and upon My handmaidens there who have heard and answered what has risen from My luminous horizon. End.

40 There has previously been sent down especially for the friends of God in Sīsān that whose fragrance will never cease to pervade the world. Praise be to God, the cloud of grace has risen in such a way that it has encompassed every atom—how much more those assured souls who have turned toward Him, drunk from the cup of certitude, and attained the station of assurance. They have a station with their Lord, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. I ask You, by the ocean of Your utterance and the radiance of the sun of Your face, to [corrupted original text] my remembrance and my call effective in the hearts of Your servants and a life-giving Kawthar for the dead among the people of Your kingdom. You are the One whose grace has preceded all, whose bounty has encompassed all, and whose mercy has embraced all. Take the hands of Your servants in Your loving care, then rescue them from the depths of self and desire. You are indeed the Lord of all creation and the Ruler of the world to come and this world. There is no God but You, the Ever-Forgiving, the All-Bountiful. End.

101 God willing, through this most inaccessible, most holy Tablet, the dead in the valley of heedlessness will come to life and attain a new life. Whoever speaks today with moderation for the sake of God will assuredly find that their utterance is effective, their remembrance commanding, and their purpose triumphant. Every undertaking is difficult for us, but nothing is difficult for God. I convey to him the greeting “God is most great” and express my utmost sincerity. I ask the exalted True One to bring forth from him that which will adorn his remembrance with immortality and preserve it forever in the record of the world. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent. There is no God but Him, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

102 Concerning what he had written, that on Friday the house of the honored Āqā Mīrzā ʿ-B, upon him be the glory of God, was blessed by the presence of the friends: bliss be upon the people of Bahā, whom the warmth of the love of God has so seized that those who are near to Him see the radiance of the All-Merciful in their faces and hear His beautiful praise from their tongues. This matter was presented in the most inaccessible, most holy presence, and this firm and blessed utterance descended from the heaven of grace. These are His words, exalted be His majesty:

103 Upon those who today speak in remembrance of God and remain firm and steadfast in the divine Cause, the Concourse on high invokes blessings at evening and dawn. Blessed is a house illumined by the lights of My remembrance, in which the call of My loved ones has been raised—those who have cast aside all but Me and followed what they were commanded in My luminous Book. The honored ʿ-B, upon him be My glory, has, praise be to God, attained remembrance of the True One, His favor, and service to Him. In God’s sight, this station surpasses whatever has been seen. God willing, may he always attain it, but with wisdom.

104 O Varqā! O you who gaze toward the most exalted horizon! Hold fast to wisdom in every circumstance and cling to it. A gathering of the friends that exceeds the bounds of moderation will cause people anxiety, for the inhabitants of the earth, its religious scholars, and its rulers have not yet become aware of the will of the True One, glorified be His glory. Misled by certain ignorant people who consider themselves the religious scholars of the earth, they have regarded, and still regard, the Dawning-Place of revelation and the Dayspring of inspiration as the cause of sedition and the source of disorder. For this reason, they have reckoned gatherings of the friends of God to be a cause of division among people and the disruption of what they possess. Thus, in all matters, moderation must be kept in view because of the weakness of heedless souls. I swear by the reality of the Tree beyond which there is no passing: if they were to gain awareness and recognition to a degree smaller than the eye of a needle, they would all soar on the wings of yearning and longing and set out for the farthest destination, the loftiest summit, and the ultimate goal.

105 Greed has deprived the people of the world of the Self-Sufficient One. They do not know what has happened or what is taking place, for they have not sought, and do not seek, awareness. In one of the Tablets We said: “An eyelid, thin as it is, deprives the physical eye of seeing the world and all that is in it. What, then, will happen if the veil of greed descends upon the eye of the heart?” The fire of vain imagining has consumed the world; conjectures and delusions have darkened and obscured the realm of human consciousness. The rulers of the earth today have not, in truth, interfered with anyone’s religion or creed, nor do they do so now. Yet they have been, and remain, extremely wary of any place they see as a cause of people gathering and associating, for in the past all manner of sedition and disorder have arisen on earth from such things. Now, seeing people outwardly turn toward this Cause, they draw an analogy with the past and arise to obstruct and prevent it.

106 Ask the True One to grant those souls a drop from the ocean of knowledge and illumine them with a ray from the sun of wisdom, so that all may become sanctified from their own desires and act according to the divine will, neither comparing this Cause—which is the dawning-place of the world’s life and the dayspring of [corrupted original text]—with the affairs of the past, nor failing to distinguish corruption from rectitude. The True One, glorified be His glory, is able to raise up one of the rulers to become informed and make the others aware. The Tongue of Wisdom says: “Give each day what accords with it; consider the requirements of every moment.” He indeed does what is right and guides the way. Authority belongs to God, the Almighty, the All-Beautiful.

107 O Varqā! I have said before: “Do not believe everyone who speaks, nor place your trust in everyone who arrives,” and other such admonitions. Nevertheless, some of the friends accept a person merely on the strength of a claim and regard that person as a confidant. This has been, and will remain, a mistake. Although, praise be to God, the assemblies, meetings, and gatherings of the loved ones are sanctified and free from sedition and disorder, knowledge of the gatherings and the coming and going of the friends of God stirs the hearts and minds of the heedless.

108 Since they are unaware of the true nature of the Cause, these things make them anxious, and they strive with all their might to extinguish the divine light, although they have never been able to do so and remain unable. Thus have We imparted to you that which tells of My grace, My mercy, My loving care, My compassion, and My gifts, so that you may thank your Lord, the Compassionate, the All-Bountiful. End.

109 God willing, through the attention of that loved one, these matters will be set right in those regions. Great care and earnest effort are required to safeguard the divine Tablets. Likewise, every possible effort must be made to keep concealed the letters and Tablets sent from the Most Holy Court to various places, for if a weak or hypocritical person learns of them and speaks of them, the resulting harm may reach the Most Holy Court or the loved ones of the True One elsewhere. In all circumstances, keeping these matters hidden and confidential is necessary and is considered prudent. God willing, through the grace of the Beloved of all the worlds, that loved one will be assisted and confirmed in service to the Cause. He is indeed the Guardian of the sincere.

110 Your question concerning the passage on interpretation revealed by the Most Exalted Pen in the Most Holy Book was presented in the most inaccessible, most holy presence. This is what the Tongue of Grandeur uttered:

111 What is meant concerning interpretation is that people should not deprive themselves of the outward meaning or remain veiled from the intended meaning. For example, if “Wash your faces” descends from the heaven of the divine will, they should not interpret it to mean that washing signifies washing the inner face, which must be washed and purified with the water of spiritual knowledge, and so forth. It often happens that, through such interpretations, a person’s face remains thoroughly foul-smelling and dirty, while that person imagines they have carried out the very essence of God’s command. Yet it is clear and evident that what is meant here is washing the face with physical water.

112 Some divine words may be interpreted—that is, through interpretations that do not give rise to conjectures and delusions or deprive one of the divine intent. Reflect on what the All-Merciful revealed in the Qur’an, exalted be His words: “Whoever is given wisdom has indeed been given abundant good.” Some hold that this wisdom means the divine laws revealed in the Book, while others hold that it is the science of medicine, and that whoever attains it has attained abundant good, since it concerns the human being and is the science of bodies, and this science is nobler than the other sciences. Thus, in the past, the tongue of wisdom uttered this exalted saying: “Knowledge is of two kinds: knowledge of bodies and knowledge of religions.” In this blessed saying, knowledge of bodies was given precedence, and in truth it has precedence, for the revelation of the True One and the divine laws have all been, and will remain, for the education and advancement of humanity, the protection of the people of the world, and similar purposes. Thus, whatever brings about the preservation, health, and well-being of the human body has had, and will continue to have, precedence.

113 This matter is clear and evident. Some hold that wisdom is knowledge of the realities of things, and that whoever attains this station will possess exalted stations. Another group holds that wisdom is acting upon what benefits humanity: whoever succeeds in attaining this station possesses abundant good. A great many hold that wisdom is what protects a person from whatever abases them and guides them to whatever exalts them. A small group holds that wisdom is the knowledge of how to associate with people and treat God’s servants with forbearance, without exceeding the bounds of moderation. Some have said that wisdom is the science of theology, which is among the established disciplines of earlier philosophers. They have also said that wisdom is the knowledge of substances and accidents, as well as astronomy and similar sciences. Others have said that wisdom is the knowledge of how to earn a livelihood in this world. A small group maintains that wisdom is knowledge of first principles. Some hold that wisdom consists in the manifestations of justice, which has always meant, and will continue to mean, giving everyone their due. Others hold that it is the science of the elixir: whoever attains it has attained abundant good. Some hold that it is geometry and similar sciences. Each group, according to its own understanding, has also offered proofs and arguments for each of the views mentioned. Were these matters to be set out in detail, it would only increase weariness. Glorified be He who uttered this exalted saying: “The beginning of wisdom is the fear of God.” For the fear and awe of God restrain a person from whatever brings abasement and degradation to the human station, and strengthen them in whatever brings exaltation and elevation. A wise person avoids shameful deeds, for they see the punishment that follows them. The tent of order in the world stands firmly upon two pillars: reward and punishment. Some time ago, this blessed verse shone forth from the dawning-place of the divine mouth: “Justice has a host, namely the punishment of deeds and their reward. Through these two, the tent of order has been raised in the world, and every transgressor has taken hold of the reins of his own self for fear of retribution. Thus has the Lord of Names spoken. He is indeed the Speaker, the All-Knowing.”

114 In the past, some people called themselves followers of the mystical path, and their works still exist in the world today. Various sects each adopted a different path. Through these people, Islam became divided, and they drew channels away from the Most Great Ocean, until at last there befell the religion of God what befell it. Islam, with all its immense power, was reduced to utter weakness through the deeds and actions of those who claimed allegiance to it, as people have witnessed and continue to witness. Some who call themselves dervishes have interpreted away all the divine laws and commandments. If they are told that obligatory prayer is one of God’s firmly established laws, they say, “Obligatory prayer means supplication, and at birth we came into the world supplicating, and have thus performed the true obligatory prayer.” Yet this hapless person is deprived of the outward observance, let alone the inward reality. The delusions of heedless souls have always been, and remain, beyond reckoning.

115 In short, out of laziness and indolence, they have interpreted away all the divine commandments, which are like a mighty barrier for the protection and security of the world. Secluding themselves in dervish lodges, they have adopted no occupation but eating and sleeping. In matters of spiritual knowledge, they assent to whatever is said, yet no trace of the warmth of the love of God has so far been seen in them, except for a very few who have tasted the sweetness of the utterance and have gained some awareness of the purpose of the Revelation. Some of the interpretations mentioned in the discussion of wisdom are each valid in their own context, for they do not conflict with the principles of the divine laws. As for verses concerning divine commandments and prohibitions, such as acts of worship, blood money, criminal offenses, and the like, the intention has always been, and will remain, that the outward meaning of the verses be put into practice.

116 However, most of the divine verses revealed concerning the Resurrection and the Hour, whether in the earlier scriptures or in the Qur’an, are subject to interpretation: “None knows its interpretation but God.” These matters are clear and demonstrated in the Book of Certitude. Whoever reflects upon it will become aware of what has been hidden from everyone’s sight. Consider what the All-Bountiful has revealed in the Book, exalted be His words: “It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor does the night outstrip the day.” Scholars, both earlier and later, have explained and interpreted this blessed verse, and each believes that he has grasped its meaning. But how far the station of knowledge is from the levels of conjecture and delusion! The man they call the sovereign of commentators, namely Qāḍī Bayḍāwī, has said that this verse was revealed as a refutation of sun worshippers. Observe how far he is from the source of knowledge, despite his vast learning in the estimation of the people. The reins of the sciences are held in the grasp of divine power. He bestows them upon whomever He wills.

117 Likewise, in the earlier scriptures, most of what flowed from the Most Exalted Pen concerning this Revelation is subject to interpretation. For example, He says, “The valleys melt like wax before the Lord.” He also says, “His tent will be raised upon the mountains,” as has indeed happened. Elsewhere He says, “He has a tent whose cords have not been, and will not be, cut, whose pegs have not been, and will not be, uprooted, and which will not be moved from place to place.” These passages are subject to interpretation. A fair-minded person understands where interpretation is called for and where the outward meaning of the revealed text is binding.

118 In short, today, whatever cleanses people of defilement and brings them true peace is the way of God, the religion of God, and the Cause of God. Thus has the rain of utterance poured down from the heaven of divine knowledge as a bounty to you. Give thanks and say, “Praise be to You, O Beloved of those who know You! To You be all acclaim, O Desire of the worlds! To You be thanks, O You in whose grasp is the dominion of the kingdom of the heavens and the earth!” End.

119 From what has been revealed from the kingdom of the All-Merciful’s utterance, it is clear and evident that, in the case of certain divine verses revealed concerning social relations, manners, conduct, transactions, legal penalties, and the like, if their rulings are removed from their outward meaning, the Cause of God will be brought to a standstill and hindered. Every discerning scholar bears witness to this. Beseech the True One to adorn the people of the world with the ornament of fairness. They profess faith on the basis of trifling matters, a limited number of extraordinary occurrences, and a handful of signs, yet are seen to turn away from oceans of them. One is bewildered. Exalted be He who has concealed from them and disclosed to us.

120 Your question concerning unused water and the point at which it is considered used was submitted before the Throne. This is what God has spoken: “With a small quantity of water, such as a cupful or twice or three times that amount, washing the hands and face in it renders it used. But if it reaches the volume of a kurr, washing one or two faces does not alter it, and there has been and is no objection to using it. If one of the three properties appears in it—that is, if the color of the water changes to some degree—it is considered used. O you who drink from the cup of My loving care! The purpose is cleanliness. You are familiar with the pools of [Persia]. However much effort is devoted to cleanliness is pleasing in the sight of God.” End.

121 Concerning remembrance, you wrote that some of the friends of God rise before sunrise and engage in remembrance of the True One, glorious is His majesty, whereas this remembrance belongs to the day, and during the days of fasting the day has been defined as extending from sunrise to sunset. This is what the True One, glorious is His majesty, has spoken: “In the Bayān, the day is indeed from sunrise to sunset. However, when souls rise before sunrise out of love for God and engage in remembrance, this is pleasing and acceptable before the Throne; there is no need to repeat it. Likewise, in connection with the Mashriqu’l-Adhkār, the friends of God, holding fast to what has been revealed in the Most Holy Book, make their way to the Mashriqu’l-Adhkār in the hours before dawn and engage in remembrance of the Beloved of all the worlds. If they too engage in remembrance at sunrise or at an hour after sunrise, it is acceptable. In all circumstances, the True One has desired the ease of His servants, and if remembrance of Him proceeds from anyone with complete joy and radiance, it has been and will remain pleasing and acceptable. Blessed is the soul that has breathed with love for its Lord, and blessed is the servant whom the breezes of the love of God have awakened.” End.

122 Concerning what you wrote about Āqā Khalīl ʿAṭṭār, may the glory of God rest upon him, and the ascension of his brother, this is what God has revealed for him, glorious is His grandeur in His words:

124 A Book sent down in truth for one whom the rapture of the All-Merciful’s utterance has seized, and who has answered his Lord when He came with a mighty Cause, while the Day of God calls out to all besides Him. The sun, despite its radiance, draws light from His light, yet most of the people are heedless. Everything concealed within the veils of the unseen has appeared, and what was inscribed in the Books of God, the Lord of the worlds, has come forth. How many a learned man has broken God’s covenant and pledge, and how many a heedless one has heard the call from the Most Exalted Horizon and turned his heart toward God, the All-Knowing, the All-Aware.

Do not grieve over what has befallen you. He who believed in God, the Master of this luminous Day, has attained the companionship of the Most Exalted. We have remembered him as a grace from Us to you and to him, and I am the Most Bountiful, the Generous. Blessed is he for having turned toward Me when most of My servants, whom We created to serve My firm and steadfast Cause, turned away from Me. Your Lord forgave your brother at the time of his ascension, purified him of sins, and admitted him to a station that the tongues of those who know are powerless to describe. From this station We send greetings to My loved ones there who have drunk the choice wine of My utterance and attained what has flowed from My Most Exalted Pen. Your Lord is indeed the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful. May glory from God, the Lord of Names, rest upon you and upon those whose names have been sent down in My preserving Tablet. End.

126 Praise be to God, the sun of generosity is shining, the ocean of loving care is manifest, and the Euphrates of utterance flows and spreads without interruption. This mortal asks and hopes that God will deprive no one on earth of the fragrant breezes of this most exalted and most holy Day, and will enable everyone to do what He loves and approves. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent.

127 Convey greetings from this mortal to all the friends, and say: “These days are unlike other days, and this time is unlike the times before and after it. Strive to gain a share of the light of the divine dawn.” He indeed has power over whatever He wills. There is no God but Him, the Almighty, the Most Great.

128 May glory rest upon you, upon those with you, and upon those who have turned with radiant hearts toward God, our Lord and your Lord, our heart’s desire and your heart’s desire, and the heart’s desire of all who are in the heavens and on earth.

Wisdom and its meanings have been explained to some extent. Today, however, wisdom consists of conduct, deeds, and words that guide people to the Most Exalted Horizon without raising an uproar or giving rise to turmoil and discord.
