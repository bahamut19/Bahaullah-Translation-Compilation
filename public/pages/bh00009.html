1 In the name of our Lord, the Most Holy, the Most Great, the Most High, the Most Glorious.

2 Today the rustling of the Tree beyond which there is no passing rises high, and the sun of utterance shines resplendent from the horizon of the heaven of meanings. The Path speaks with the most eloquent expression, and the Balance makes mention in the most perfect words. Yet the banner of “There is none in the realm but Him” is planted and raised aloft, for the world is lifeless and despondent—indeed, dead. There is no ear to listen and no eye to behold, except for a few whom the veils of names and the coverings of allusions and words did not hold back. They left behind all but the True One and joined themselves to Him. They heard the call, drank the choice wine, and arose for the Cause with such resolve that it caused the hearts of the aggressors to tremble and the feet of those who associate partners with God to falter. In truth, not every ear is fit to hear, nor are diseased eyes, lacking clear vision, capable of beholding. Today is the day of remembrance and praise. For whom? For the friends, those who stand firm in the Cause with perfect steadfastness and speak in remembrance of the Desire of the world. Every remembrance and every praise befits those assured souls, content with God and pleasing to Him.

3 Glory be to You, O You in separation from whom those brought near have lamented, and at whose absence the sincere have cried out. I ask You, by the lights of the dawn of Your revelation, through which You have brought Your servants and Your creation to life and given those in every land the glad news of union with You and attainment to Your presence, to assist Your friends in that through which the manifestations of Your might and the evidences of Your power may be exalted. O Lord, adorn the records of the world and the books of the nations with their names. You indeed have power over whatever You will. There is no God but You, the Helper, the Bestower, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. O my God and the God of the world, my Desire and the Desire of the nations! I ask You, by Your sweetest call raised between earth and heaven, and by the radiance of the lights of Your face from the most exalted horizon, to protect whoever has arisen to serve Your Cause and spoken Your praise. Then strengthen him with the hosts of the unseen and the seen, and aid him with a company of angels and the Spirit. You are indeed the All-Powerful, whom neither the power of rulers nor their might, nor the doubts raised by the learned nor their insinuations, can thwart. There is no God but You, the All-Protecting, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Most Great.

4 O beloved of my heart! I was looking, reflecting, grieving, and pondering the manifestations of our Lord’s power, what had befallen Him, and what had proceeded from the factions. Then the door opened, and the bearer of your letter entered—a letter from which everyone endowed with a sense of smell perceived the fragrance of your sincerity, your love, your turning toward God, our Lord and your Lord, and your resolve to serve the Cause in days when the might of the heedless had seized the inhabitants of the cities of names, except those whom God, the Lord of all and the Sovereign of the next world and this one, had protected. When I read it, I found in each of its letters that which acquainted me with what was in your hearts and made known to me what had drawn your heart, your very veins, your outward and inward being: the Cause of God, to whose cord you have held fast and to whose hem you have clung, to exalt His Word and guide His servants and His creation. When I understood this, I flew on the wings of longing toward God, the Lord of the Covenant. I entered His presence and presented it to the end. Then the gentle rustling of the Tree beyond which there is no passing arose, and the Lord of all spoke words that this servant cannot fittingly recount. I ask Him to sustain me through His grace and generosity. Can any writer number the waves of the sea as they appear and rise? No, by His own true Self, except through assistance from Him and grace from His presence. There is no God but Him, who records all things in a clear Book.

5 And at the close of His utterances—may the souls of all in the kingdoms of command and creation be sacrificed for Him—He said, exalted be His utterance and glorious His proof:

6 He is the Watchful, the Witness, the Knowing, the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

7 The Tree of Utterance at the pivot of the contingent world calls out: “There is no God but Me, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.” The Tree beyond which there is no passing, upon the luminous earth beyond the ocean of grandeur: “By God, the Hidden One has come with manifest sovereignty!” The Tree of Certitude, planted in the land of saffron, calls out: “The Sovereign has come! Sovereignty belongs to God, the Lord of existence.” The Tree of Proof upon the white spot declares: “The fragrance of the garment has spread, and the light has shone from the horizon of revelation. Hasten, people of the earth, and do not follow anyone who is ignorant and veiled.” This is a day on which the mountains have bowed down, for the Self-Sufficient, the Most Exalted, has come with the banner of power and the standard of “There is no God but Me, the Lord of the unseen and the seen.” Say: “People of Iran, fear the All-Merciful and do not be among those who do not know. We have blown the trumpet, which is My Most Exalted Pen, and through it We have brought to life those who were in My preserved knowledge.”

8 Say: “People of the Bayān, have you heard and yet denied? Have you seen and yet turned away? Fear God and judge fairly concerning this Cause, at whose appearance souls were shaken, except those whom God took hold of, rescued from the darkness of vain imaginings, and raised to a station where they heard the call of the Kingdom.” O Warqāʾ, hear the call as you have heard it before and since.

We have revealed for you that which is a light to the books of the world, a straight path for the followers of religions, and a mighty pillar for whoever acknowledges this momentous announcement. Say: “You who turn away, listen and then judge fairly. By the life of God, the call draws you to a station where you lay aside what you possess in the hope of what is with God, the Almighty, the All-Loving.” Say: “This is the arena of proof; bring forward what you have. By the life of God, none ride in it but the horsemen of certitude, who have cast the world aside and turned toward God, the Lord of the promised Day.” Thus has the sea brought forth the shells of wisdom and utterance, and the Tree its fruits, but the people do not know.

10 Hear the call of the Wronged One in the Persian tongue. What was hidden has become manifest, and what had ever been stored in the heaven of the divine will and preserved in the Preserved Tablet has been sent down. Yet the recompense for their deeds deprived all of the outpourings of the Day of Return, just as the deeds and their recompense were seen among the people of the Qurʾān, particularly the Shiʿi community. O Warqāʾ, reflect on the lack of discernment of those deluded souls among the people of the Bayān. Although the veils were torn apart, and they saw with their outward eyes the conjectures, vain imaginings, unbelief, hypocrisy, oppression, and tyranny of the former community, they are now once again occupied in organizing a Shiʿi community; they wish to make a people into Shiʿis. In short, vain imaginings and names have deprived people of the ocean of meanings and denied them a share in true recognition of divine unity. In truth, in one respect names are idols, as has been and continues to be witnessed. Even now the former community has not taken heed or risen from the sleep of heedlessness. Each day the fire of oppression blazes more fiercely, and the flame of malice and hatred burns more brightly. Today it is necessary and incumbent upon all to arise to aid the Cause, which consists in protecting weak souls from conjectures and vain imaginings.

11 Convey to the friends in that land, on behalf of the Wronged One, the greeting “God is Most Great.” Say: “Strive, that you may attain the lights of the sun of true recognition of divine unity. Whoever attains it will find himself free and released from the world.” Blessed are those who act, and blessed are those who recognize. May the glory manifest and shining from the horizon of the heaven of My Kingdom rest upon you and upon whoever listens to your words concerning the Cause of God, the Lord of the worlds. End.

12 O beloved of my heart! The sun of the All-Merciful’s utterance shines with such radiance that all the utterance of the world, from the beginning of creation until now, bows in humility and reverence at its appearance and revelation. What, then, can this lost one submit, and what can he express? This mortal servant is bewildered by what those clamorous voices are saying and what they cling to. The worth and station of certain people are now becoming clear. Although all have seen with their outward eyes the audacity of the former community and witnessed the idolatry, heedlessness, hypocrisy, and oppression of those souls, they nevertheless speak now of a mirror, now of a successor, now of a deputy, a noble one, a chief, and the like. Glory be to God! They have denied whatever was previously revealed that runs counter to the desires of those who turn away. They have utterly denied the Persian Bayān, which is in truth among the greatest books of the Primal Point—may the soul of all besides Him be sacrificed for Him. They have said it has been altered. We must all lament and cry, “Alas!” The lies of the past have been resurrected, and the former fabrications have reappeared. We belong to God, and to Him we return.

The fair-minded and discerning know and recognize the greatness of the Revelation, its all-encompassing power, and likewise what has been sent down from the heaven of His will. They behold, as plainly as the sun, the lights of the revelation of the Lord of all. Nevertheless, this tribe of apish creatures, defiled, dust-covered, and filthy, are seen to resemble the divines of the Shiite community, who, in the corners of their seminaries, made a filthy show of asceticism and wickedly claimed knowledge and faith. They have never been, and are not now, worthy of this court. Praise be to God, who has sanctified His court from meeting them, His light from their sight, His Revelation from their recognition, and His call from their hearing. They have resorted to scheming, deceit, and trickery. Some deeds of those heedless, ignorant souls have been recounted that leave every rational person bewildered. May God protect us and you from the evil of these people.

Yet, O beloved of my heart, letters have arrived and continue to arrive from all quarters in such numbers that this servant has truly had, and still has, no opportunity to express to the friends what is in his heart. Two years have passed, and replies to some of the gentlemen—may God’s glory and loving care rest upon them—have been and remain overdue. I ask and hope that God, exalted be His glory, will grant assistance and success. He is powerful and mighty. Most of your letters, however, have been answered. The one God is my witness: whenever a letter from that beloved friend has arrived, it has brought fresh joy and delight. Besides this, for some time certain discussions have arisen in this land as well, and prudence must sometimes be observed.

This statement is adorned with the testimony of the Desired One of the worlds. [corrupted original text] to you. He is indeed the All-Bountiful, the Generous, and He is the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful. We ask Him for that in which lies the good of this world and the next. Likewise, a Tablet had previously been revealed especially for him and sent. This servant asks his Lord to aid His loved ones in what He loves and approves, and to ordain for them the best of what has been revealed in His lucid Book.

The point is that few ears are found ready to hear the call of God, and few eyes keen enough to behold what has appeared from the treasuries of the Pen. We have ordained abundant good for you in the Book.” Here it ends.

161 O beloved of my heart, most are nursing infants: they seek milk and have never been, nor are they now, capable of taking anything beyond it. We ask God, exalted is He, to aid His servants in remembering and praising Him, turning toward Him, and placing their trust in Him. He is indeed the All-Hearing, the Responsive, and He is the Unique, the One, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy.

May they devote themselves to the betterment of the world with radiant hearts purified of rancor and hatred, so that the light of concord may encompass the world and the enmities concealed in human hearts may subside and disappear.

I say this, but I hear, “Far from it, far from it!” For some souls have treated the Cause of God lightly. They play and do not understand, and say things of which they have no awareness.

165 As for the matter of the two kinds of script, His Holiness the Greater Branch of God—may my soul and my being be sacrificed for the dust beneath His feet—has written concerning it. It is very good. He Himself will write out the details. Regarding your statement that one of the friends has asked about backbiting, these matters are as manifest and clear as the sun in the divine Tablets. In these days, all should be ablaze with the fire of the Word and engaged in service to the Cause. They should seize the opportunity the time affords and, through wisdom and utterance, awaken the people of the world. Every soul who today succeeds in acknowledging what God has sent down in the Book is named and counted among the inhabitants of the highest Paradise.

166 Today is the lord of days, and the Lord of Revelations is established upon the throne of utterance. Blessed are those who have attained! In one sense, the days of the Manifestations of God are called the Day of God, but in all the Books these days are specifically associated with the revelation of the hidden Unseen and the treasured Mystery, who speaks with the loudest call before the faces of the world. The greatness of these days is mentioned and recorded in the former Books and in the Qurʾān.

167 The Herald, namely the Primal Point—may the soul of all besides Him be sacrificed for Him—attained the honor of meeting, outwardly concealed from everyone, during the days of His journey toward Mākū. And Quddūs—upon him be My glory and My loving care—attained repeatedly. During the journey to the land of Khā, We expressly summoned him, and for several months he was in Our presence and attained the meeting. The Point—may the soul of all besides Him be sacrificed for Him—named him and the Most Great Bāb the First and the Last, and also referred to them as the first to arrive and the last to alight. He gave each of them the glad tidings of this Revelation, and specifically told Sayyāḥ—upon him be My glory—“Go; perhaps you will find.”

168 The universal Manifestations were aware before their appearance. They are the dawning places of divine knowledge. We ask God to assist His friends in recognizing what He has sent down in His firm and lucid Book. Certain discussions cause veiling and also extinguish the fire of the divine Lote-Tree within one’s being. Glory rest upon whoever confines his gaze to turning toward the Most Exalted Horizon, detached from conjectures and vain imaginings. O beloved of my heart, certain discussions truly cause heedlessness and listlessness, especially the mention of what God does not approve. It is hoped that all will drink of the choice wine of the All-Merciful’s utterance and appear in the gathering of the world adorned with distinction. The people of God must assuredly be distinguished from others. Greetings of praise and peace have been and continue to be conveyed to the friends in that land, and this mortal servant asks and hopes that the abiding God will assist everyone to do what is fitting.

169 Regarding the matter in question, it is very clear and evident that, in God’s sight, anything that harms someone or brings affliction upon a soul—indeed, anything that causes people sorrow and distress—is rejected and unacceptable in this Most Great Revelation. Whatever brings advancement, elevation, and exaltation, and causes joy and gladness in the hearts of the people of the world, is acceptable and beloved. Yet, in the interests of wisdom, one should act in such a way that no hint of despair arises. Our Lord, the All-Merciful, has forbidden everyone corruption and conflict and commanded them to show love, compassion, and affection. Wisdom, however, has always required and still requires forbearance. I ask God to assist the person mentioned in protecting the flock. In that case, divine assistance will assuredly reach him.
