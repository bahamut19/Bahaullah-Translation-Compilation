Lawh-i-Ibn-i-Dhi’b (Epistle to the Son of the Wolf)

Translated on 9 Qudrat, 183 BE using GPT 6.1 Sol

1 In the name of God, the Unique, the One, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

2 Praise be to God, who remains without perishing, endures without decline, and abides without change; who rules through His sovereignty, is manifest through His signs, and is hidden through His mysteries; at whose command the banner of the Most Exalted Word was raised in the world of creation, and the standard of “He does whatever He wills” was planted among humankind. He is the One who revealed His Cause to guide His creatures, sent down His verses to make His testimony and proof manifest, and adorned the opening page of the book of humanity with utterance through His words: “The All-Merciful taught the Quran, created the human being, and taught him utterance.” There is no God but Him, the Unique, the One, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Bestower.

3 May the light shining from the horizon of the heaven of bounty, and the blessing radiating from the dawning place of the will of God, the Master of the kingdom of names, rest upon the Supreme Intermediary and the Most Exalted Pen, which God has made the dawning place of His most beautiful names and the dayspring of His most exalted attributes. Through it the light of divine unity has shone from the horizon of the world, and divine oneness has prevailed among the nations who have turned with radiant faces toward the Most Exalted Horizon and acknowledged what the Tongue of Utterance has proclaimed in the kingdom of knowledge: “Sovereignty and dominion, grandeur and might belong to God, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the All-Bountiful.”

4 Distinguished scholar, listen to the call of the Wronged One. He counsels you for the sake of God and exhorts you to what will draw you near to Him in every circumstance. He is indeed the Self-Sufficient, the Most Exalted. Know that ears were created to hear the call on this Day, which was mentioned in the Books, the Scriptures, and the Tablets. First, cleanse yourself with the water of detachment, adorn your head with the crown of piety, and your body with the vesture of trust in God. Then rise from your place, turning toward the Most Great House, appointed by the Lord of Eternity as the place around which all who are in the world should circle, and say:

5 My God, my God, my Desire, my Adored One, my Master, my Support, the ultimate object of my hope and expectation! You see me turning toward You, holding fast to the cord of Your generosity, clinging to the hem of Your bounty, acknowledging the sanctity of Your Being and the transcendence of Your Essence, and affirming Your oneness and uniqueness. I testify that You are the Unique, the One, the Single, the All-Sufficing. You have taken for Yourself no partner in sovereignty and no equal on earth. All created things have testified to what the Tongue of Your grandeur testified before them: that You are God; there is no God but You. You have always been sanctified above the mention of Your servants and exalted beyond the description of Your creatures. O Lord, You see the ignorant one seeking the ocean of Your knowledge, the thirsty one the river of Your utterance, the abased one the pavilion of Your glory, the poor one the treasury of Your riches, the petitioner the dayspring of Your wisdom, the weak one the dawning place of Your power, the destitute one the heaven of Your generosity, and the weary one the kingdom of Your remembrance.

6 I testify, my God and my Sovereign, that You created me to remember and praise You and to aid Your Cause, yet I aided Your enemies, who broke Your covenant, cast aside Your Book, and disbelieved in You and Your signs. Alas, alas for my heedlessness, my shame, my sin, and my wrongdoing, which kept me from entering the vast ocean of Your oneness and the fathomless sea of Your mercy! Alas, alas, and again alas, alas for my wretched condition and the enormity of my rebellion! You brought me into being, my God, to exalt Your Word and make Your Cause manifest, but my heedlessness hindered and encompassed me, so that I arose to efface Your signs and shed the blood of Your loved ones, the dawning places of Your verses, the daysprings of Your revelation, and the repositories of Your mysteries.

7 O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord, O Lord! I testify that through my injustice the fruits of the tree of Your justice have fallen, and through the fire of my rebellion the hearts of those among Your creatures who are near to You have burned, and the innermost beings of Your sincere servants have melted. Alas, alas for my wretchedness! Alas, alas for my injustice! Alas, alas for my remoteness, my heedlessness, my ignorance, my abasement, my turning away, and my opposition! How many days You commanded Your servants and Your loved ones to protect me, while I commanded them to harm You and Your trusted ones! How many nights You remembered me through Your grace and guided me to Your path, while I turned away from You and Your signs! By Your glory, Hope of those who affirm Your unity and Desire of the hearts of the detached, I find no helper but You, and no sovereign, refuge, or shelter other than You. Alas, alas! My turning away has burned the covering of my protection, and my opposition has torn the veil of my dignity. If only I had been beneath the layers of dust and the evil of my deeds had not appeared among Your servants! O Lord, You see the disobedient one turning toward the dawning place of Your pardon and bounty, and the mountain of injustice seeking the heaven of Your mercy and forgiveness. Alas, alas! My grievous wrongdoings have kept me from approaching the court of Your mercy, and my great sins have removed me far from the precincts of Your presence. I am the one who failed in my duty toward You, broke Your covenant and Your pledge, and committed what caused the inhabitants of the cities of Your justice and the dawning places of Your grace in Your lands to lament. I testify, my God, that I abandoned Your commands and followed the commands of my own self, cast aside the laws of Your Book, and took up the book of my own desire. Alas, alas! The greater my wretchedness grew, the greater Your forbearance became, and whenever the fire of my rebellion blazed, Your pardon and grace concealed it. By Your glory, Desire of the world and Beloved of the nations, Your patience deluded me and Your endurance emboldened me. You see, my God, the tears I shed from shame and the sighs I heave over my heedlessness. By Your grandeur, I find no resting place except the shelter of the court of Your generosity, and no escape except beneath the domes of Your mercy. You see me in the ocean of hopelessness and despair after You have caused me to hear the words “Do not despair.” By Your glory, my injustice has severed the cord of my hope, and my rebellion has blackened my face before the throne of Your justice. O Lord, You see the dead one cast down at the door of Your bounty, ashamed to ask for the river of Your pardon from the hand of Your grace. You gave me a tongue to remember and praise You, yet it uttered what melted the innermost beings of those among Your chosen ones who are near to You and burned the hearts of the sincere among the inhabitants of Your holy courts. You gave me sight to behold Your works and contemplate Your signs and the manifestations of Your handiwork, yet I cast aside Your will and did what caused the sincere among Your creatures and the detached among Your servants to lament. You gave me hearing with which to hear Your remembrance and praise and what You sent down from the heaven of Your generosity and the atmosphere of Your will. Alas, alas! I abandoned Your command, ordered Your servants to revile Your trusted ones and Your loved ones, and acted before the throne of Your justice in a way that caused the sighs of those who affirm Your unity and the sincere among the people of Your kingdom to rise. I do not know, my God, which act of my rebellion to recount before the waves of the ocean of Your generosity, or which of my errors to speak of before the effulgent lights of the suns of Your gifts and favors.

8 I ask You at this moment, by the mysteries of Your Book, by what lies hidden in Your knowledge, and by the pearls concealed in the shells of the ocean of Your mercy, to make me one of those whom You have mentioned in Your Book and described in Your Tablets. Have You ordained for me, my God, any joy after this sorrow, any relief after this anguish, any ease after this hardship? Alas, alas! You established pulpits for Your remembrance, the exaltation of Your Word, and the proclamation of Your Cause, yet I ascended them to promote the breaking of Your covenant and delivered to Your servants what caused the people of the pavilion of Your grandeur and the inhabitants of the cities of Your knowledge to lament. How often You sent down the feast of Your utterance from the heaven of Your bounty, while I rejected it! How often You summoned me to the sweet waters of Your mercy, while I turned away from them through following self and desire! By Your glory, I do not know for which sin to seek Your forgiveness and turn to You in repentance, or from which injustice to return to the court of Your generosity and the precincts of Your bounty. My wrongdoings and sins have reached such a degree that those who count are unable to number them, and those who write are unable to record them. I ask You, O You who replace darkness with light and reveal mysteries on the Mount, to strengthen me in every circumstance to trust in You and entrust all affairs to You. Then make me, my God, content with what has been inscribed by the pen of Your decree and the reed of Your ordaining. You indeed have power to do whatever You will, and in Your grasp are the reins of all who are in the heavens and the earth. There is no God but You, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

9 O Shaykh, know that people’s slanders, their turning away, and their opposition cannot harm one who holds fast to the cord of providence and clings to the hem of the mercy of the Lord of creation. By the life of God, Bahá has not spoken from desire. He has been made to speak by the One who caused all things to voice His remembrance and praise. There is no God but Him, the Unique, the One, the All-Powerful, the Unconstrained.

10 Those with keen sight, attentive ears, radiant hearts, and open breasts recognize and distinguish truth from falsehood. Read this prayer that has flowed from the tongue of the Wronged One, and reflect on it with an unburdened heart and a pure, sanctified hearing. Perhaps you may perceive the breezes of detachment and have mercy on yourselves and on God’s servants.

11 O God, O Adored One, O Desired One, O Generous One, O Merciful One! Souls come from You, and all power lies within the grasp of Your might. Whoever You exalt rises above the angels and attains the station of “We raised him to a lofty station,” and whoever You cast down is lower than dust; indeed, nothingness is better than him. O Lord, despite our wrongdoing, sinfulness, and lack of piety, we seek a seat of truth and long to meet Your chosen ones. The command is Yours, the judgment is Yours, and the realm of power is subject to Your command. Whatever You do is pure justice—indeed, sheer grace. A single revelation of Your name, the All-Merciful, would efface and obliterate every trace of rebellion from the world, and a single breeze from the breezes of the day of Your revelation would adorn the world with a new robe. O Mighty One, grant strength to the powerless and bestow life upon the dead, so that they may find You, gain access to the ocean of Your knowledge, and remain steadfast in Your Cause. If the fragrance of Your praise diffuses from the various languages of the world, all are beloved of the soul and desired by the spirit, whether Arabic or Persian. If they are deprived of it, neither words nor meanings are worthy of mention. O Lord, I ask You to show everyone the way and guide them. You are the Powerful, the Mighty, the Knowing, the Seeing.

12 We ask God to assist you in justice and fairness and to acquaint you with what has been concealed from eyes and sight. He is indeed the Almighty, the Unconstrained. The request is that you reflect on what has appeared and speak with justice and fairness, so that the radiance of the sun of truthfulness and purity may shine forth, deliver people from the darkness of ignorance, and illumine the world with the light of knowledge. This Wronged One has attended no schools and witnessed no scholarly debates. By My life, I did not reveal Myself; rather, God revealed Me as He willed. In the Tablet to His Majesty the Shah—may God, blessed and exalted, assist him—these words flowed from the tongue of the Wronged One:

13 “O King, I was like any other servant, asleep upon My couch. The breezes of the All-Glorious passed over Me and taught Me the knowledge of all that has been. This is not from Me, but from One who is Almighty, All-Knowing. He commanded Me to raise My call between earth and heaven. Because of this, there befell Me what has caused the tears of those who know to flow. I have not read the learning current among people, nor have I entered the schools. Ask the city in which I lived, so that you may be certain that I am not among those who lie. This is a leaf stirred by the winds of the will of your Lord, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. Can it remain still when tempestuous winds blow? No, by the Master of names and attributes! Rather, they move it as they wish. Nothingness has no existence before the Eternal. His irrevocable command has come and caused Me to speak His remembrance among all peoples. Before His command I was only as one dead. The hand of the will of your Lord, the All-Merciful, the Merciful, turned Me about.”

14 It would now be best for you to purify yourself with the water of detachment that has flowed from the wellspring of the Most Exalted Pen and, for the sake of God, reflect on whatever has appeared or been revealed before and since. Then, through wisdom and utterance, strive to the extent of your ability to extinguish the fire of rancor and hatred concealed in the hearts of the peoples of the world. The purpose of sending the Messengers and revealing the Books has been the knowledge of God and fellowship and unity among His servants. Yet now we see that they have made the divine law a cause of hatred and hostility. What sorrow and regret that most people cling to and occupy themselves with what they possess, heedless of, and veiled from, what is with God.

15 Say: “My God, my God, adorn my head with the crown of justice and my body with the ornament of fairness. You are indeed the Master of gifts and favors.”

16 Justice and fairness are two guardians for the protection of the people, and from these two emerge firm and blessed words that bring about the well-being of the world and the preservation of nations.

17 In one of the Tablets these words flowed from the Pen of the Wronged One: “God, exalted be His glory, has come to bring forth the gems of meaning from the mine of humanity—that is, the dawning places of His Cause and the treasuries of the pearls of His knowledge—for He, exalted is He, is an unseen mystery, concealed and hidden from sight. Consider what the All-Merciful has revealed in the Qur’an: ‘Eyes do not perceive Him, but He perceives all eyes, and He is the Subtle, the All-Aware.’”

18 Today the religion of God and the faith of God require that the diverse religions and various paths not be made a cause of hatred. These principles, laws, and firm, well-established paths have appeared from a single source and shone from a single dawning place. Their differences have been due to the needs of the times, centuries, and ages.

19 O people of Bahá, gird yourselves with determination, so that religious contention and strife may be removed from among the people of the world and effaced. Out of love for God and His servants, arise to undertake this great and momentous task. Religious rancor and hatred are a world-consuming fire, and extinguishing it is exceedingly difficult, unless the hand of divine power delivers people from this devastating affliction. Consider the war that took place between the two states. Both sides sacrificed possessions and lives. How many villages were seen reduced to nothing!

20 This utterance is like a lamp in the niche of speech: “O people of the world, you are all the fruit of one tree and the leaves of one branch. Conduct yourselves with perfect love, unity, affection, and concord. By the Sun of Truth, the light of concord will brighten and illumine the world. The all-knowing God has been, and remains, witness to these words.”

21 Strive to attain this lofty and most exalted station, the station of safeguarding and preserving the human world. This purpose is the sovereign of all purposes, and this hope the king of all hopes. Yet until the horizon of the sun of justice is freed from the dark clouds of oppression, the realization of this station appears difficult. These dark clouds are the embodiments of conjectures and vain imaginings—that is, the religious scholars of Iran. At times We have spoken in the language of religious law, and at others in the language of mystical truth and the spiritual path. Our ultimate purpose and highest aim have been the realization of this lofty and most exalted station. God is sufficient as a witness.

22 O people of Bahá, associate with all the people of the world in joy and fellowship. If you possess a word or a gem of which others are deprived, impart it and make it known in the language of love and compassion. If it is accepted and takes effect, the purpose is achieved; otherwise, leave the person to himself and pray for him, rather than mistreat him. The language of compassion attracts hearts and is nourishment for the spirit. It is like meaning to words and like the horizon for the shining of the sun of wisdom and knowledge.

23 By religious scholars in the instances mentioned here are meant those who outwardly adorn themselves with the garb of learning but are inwardly deprived of it. In discussing this subject in the Tablet to His Majesty the Shah, several passages are cited from the Hidden Words, which appeared from the Most Glorious Pen under the title of the Scroll of Fátimih, may God’s blessings rest upon her.

24 “O faithless ones, why do you outwardly claim to be shepherds, while inwardly you have become wolves preying on My sheep? You are like the star before dawn, which appears radiant and bright, but in reality leads the caravans of My city and lands astray and to destruction.”

25 And He also says: “O you who are outwardly adorned but inwardly deficient, you are like clear but bitter water, which outwardly appears perfectly delicate and pure, yet when tested by the divine Assayer’s taste, not a drop of it is accepted. The sun’s radiance is present in both dust and mirror, but know that the difference between them is as great as that between the two Pole Stars and the earth—indeed, the difference is infinite.”

26 And He also says: “O son of the world, many a dawn the revelation of My loving-kindness came from the dawning place of the Placeless to your dwelling, found you on your bed of ease occupied with another, and returned like a spiritual flash of lightning to its seat of radiant glory. In the retreats of nearness, before the hosts of holiness, I did not disclose it, nor did I wish for your shame.”

27 And He also says: “O you who claim to love Me, at dawn the breeze of My loving-kindness passed over you and found you asleep upon the bed of heedlessness. It wept over your condition and returned.”

28 But those scholars who are truly adorned with learning and good character are like the head to the body of the world and like eyes to the nations. The guidance of the people has always depended, and continues to depend, upon those sanctified souls. We ask God to enable them to do what He loves and approves. He is indeed the Master of creation and the Lord of the next world and this one.

29 O Shaykh, We have heard that you have turned away from Us and opposed Us, commanding people to revile Me and issuing a ruling for the shedding of the servants’ blood. God bless the one who said: “Willing obedience to a judge whose judgment is a wonder: he has decreed that my blood be shed both outside the sanctuary and within it.” Truly I say, whatever befalls one in the path of God is beloved of the soul and the heart’s desire. Deadly poison in His path is surpassing honey, and His torment a fitting sweetness. In the Tablet to His Majesty the Shah it is stated: “By His own Self, the Truth! I do not bewail afflictions in His path or calamities endured for His love. God has made affliction a morning rain for this verdant field and a wick for His lamp, through which earth and heaven have been illumined.”

30 Turn your heart toward the Kaaba of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Then raise your hands with such steadfastness that the hands of all created things may be raised through it toward the heaven of the grace of God, the Lord of the worlds. Then turn toward Him with such devotion that all beings may thereby turn toward His radiant, luminous horizon, and say: “My Lord, You see me turning toward the heaven of Your generosity and the ocean of Your bounty, and turning away from all besides You. I ask You, by the splendors of the Daystar of Your revelation upon the Mount and by the rays of the sun of Your grace shining from the horizon of Your name, the Ever-Forgiving, to forgive me and have mercy on me. Then ordain for me, through Your Most Exalted Pen, what will exalt me through Your name in the earthly realm of creation. My Lord, enable me to turn toward You and to hear the call of Your loved ones, whom the power of the world has not weakened nor the might of the nations restrained. They turned toward You and said: ‘God is our Lord and the Lord of all who are in the heavens and on earth.’”

31 O Shaykh, truly I say, the seal of the sealed wine has been removed in the name of the Self-Subsisting. Do not deprive yourself. This Wronged One speaks for the sake of God; you too, for the sake of God, reflect on what has been sent down and made manifest, so that you may receive a share of the outpourings of the true Bestower of grace on this blessed Day and not remain deprived. This is not difficult for God. Through the Word of God, earthly Adam became heavenly, and a fisherman became endowed with divine wisdom. His Honor Abū Dharr was a shepherd of sheep; he became a leader of nations.

32 O Shaykh, this day has not been, and is not, the day of outward learning and arts, for it has been seen that a person unacquainted with a single word of such learning is seated upon a throne of pure gold at the head of the gathering of spiritual knowledge, while one possessed of learning and versed in the arts remains deprived. By this learning is meant learning that begins with words and ends with words. But sciences that yield results and bear fruit, and bring comfort and well-being to the servants of God, have always been and remain acceptable to God. If you hear My call, you will abandon what you possess and turn toward a station where the ocean of wisdom and utterance surges and the fragrance of the loving care of your Lord, the All-Merciful, wafts.

33 At this point it seemed fitting to mention briefly some past events, in the hope that this might bring forth justice and fairness. In the days when His Majesty the Shah—may God, his Lord, the All-Merciful, assist him—had resolved to travel to Isfahan, this Wronged One obtained permission and set out to visit the sacred, luminous shrines of the Imams, may God’s blessings be upon them. After returning, because of the intense heat in the capital, We went to Lavasan. After Our departure, the incident involving His Majesty the Shah—may God, blessed and exalted be He, assist him—occurred. In those days affairs were thrown into turmoil and the fire of anger blazed. A number of people were arrested, among them this Wronged One. By the life of God, We had no involvement whatever in that reprehensible affair, and Our innocence was also established in the investigative proceedings. Nevertheless, they arrested Us and took Us in chains from Niyavaran, then the seat of the royal court, to the prison in Tehran, bareheaded and barefoot, on foot. For a mounted oppressor accompanying Us snatched the hat from Our head, and with a band of executioners and attendants they hurried Us along at full speed. For four months they assigned Us quarters in a place without parallel or likeness. As for the prison that housed this Wronged One and others who had been wronged, a narrow, dark tomb would in truth have been preferable to it. When We entered the prison, they led Us into a dark corridor. From there We descended three steps and reached the quarters they had assigned Us. The place was dark, and Our companions were some hundred and fifty people: thieves, murderers, and highway robbers. Despite this crowd, the place had no opening except the passage by which We had entered. Pens are powerless to describe it, and its foul odors are beyond expression. Most of those people had neither clothing nor bedding. God knows what befell Us in that most foul-smelling, most dark place.

34 During the days and nights in that prison, We reflected on the deeds, circumstances, and conduct of the Bábí community: despite that community’s loftiness, nobility, and understanding, what had happened that such an act should have issued from them—namely, that community’s audacious action against the person of the Shah? This Wronged One then resolved, upon leaving prison, to arise with all His strength to refine the character of those souls.

35 One night, in a dream, these exalted words were heard from every direction: “We will indeed make You victorious through Yourself and through Your Pen. Do not grieve over what has befallen You, and do not fear: You are among those who are secure. God will raise up the treasures of the earth: men who will aid You through Yourself and through Your name, by which God has brought the hearts of those who recognize Him to life.”

36 When this Wronged One left prison, at the command of His Majesty the Shah—may God, exalted be He, protect him—We set out for Arab Iraq, accompanied by an attendant of the exalted government of Iran and of the illustrious government of Russia. After Our arrival, through divine assistance and the grace and mercy of the Lord, verses were sent down like pouring rain and dispatched to the regions of the earth. We counseled all the servants of God, and especially this community, with wise exhortations and compassionate advice, and forbade corruption, strife, contention, and warfare, until, through God’s grace, heedlessness and ignorance were changed into righteousness and understanding, and weapons into reform.

37 During Our confinement in the prison of the Land of Ṭā, although sleep was scarce because of the torment of the chains and the foul odors, on those occasions when it did come, it felt as though something poured from the crown of Our head onto Our breast, like a mighty river descending to the earth from the summit of a lofty, towering mountain. As a result, every limb showed signs of fire, and at that moment Our tongue recited what no one could bear to hear.

38 Some of the utterances revealed in Tablets addressed specifically to this community are quoted here, so that all may know with clear certainty that this Wronged One has acted in ways beloved and acceptable to people of understanding and to those who embody justice and fairness.

39 O friends of God in His lands and His loved ones in His realms! The Wronged One exhorts you to trustworthiness and piety. Blessed is the city that has attained their light. Through them the station of the human being is exalted and the door of assurance is opened to all in the contingent world. Blessed is the one who holds fast to them and recognizes their worth, and woe to the one who denies their station.

40 Elsewhere these words were revealed: “We command the servants and handmaidens of God to be chaste and God-fearing. Let them arise from the slumber of desire and turn toward God, the Creator of earth and heaven. Thus did We command the servants when the Daystar of the horizons shone from the direction of Iraq. What harms Me is not My imprisonment, My affliction, or what has been inflicted on Me by the tyrants among the people, but rather the deeds of those who ascribe themselves to Me and commit what causes My heart and My Pen to lament. Those who spread corruption on earth, take possession of people’s property, and enter houses without their owners’ permission—I disavow them unless they repent and return to God, the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful.”

41 And elsewhere: “O peoples of the earth! Hasten toward the good pleasure of God, and strive with all your strength to manifest His irrevocable and firmly established Cause. We have ordained that striving in the path of God be undertaken with the hosts of wisdom and utterance, and with good character and deeds. Thus has the decree been issued by One who is Strong and All-Powerful. There is no glory for one who spreads corruption on earth after it has been set right. Fear God, O people, and do not be among the wrongdoers.”

42 And elsewhere: “Do not revile anyone among you. We have come to bring unity and concord to all who dwell on earth. What has appeared among the servants from the ocean of My utterance bears witness to this, yet most of the people remain manifestly far away. If anyone reviles you, or harm touches you in the path of God, be patient and place your trust in the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. He indeed witnesses and sees, and does what He wills through an authority of His own. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty. You have been forbidden to engage in strife and contention in the Book of God, the Mighty, the Great. Hold fast to what benefits yourselves and the people of the world. Thus commands you the Lord of Eternity, manifest through the Most Great Name. He is indeed the Ordainer, the All-Wise.”

43 And elsewhere: “Beware of shedding blood. Draw the sword of the tongue from the sheath of utterance, for through it the cities of hearts are conquered. We have abolished the decree of killing among you. Mercy has indeed preceded all created things, if you only knew.”

44 And elsewhere: “O people! Do not spread corruption on earth, do not shed blood, do not wrongfully consume the property of others, and do not follow every outcast who raises a clamor.”

45 And elsewhere: “The sun of divine utterance will never set, nor will its decline follow. Today this exalted utterance was heard from the Lote Tree beyond which there is no passing: ‘I belong to whoever loves Me, holds fast to My commandments, and casts aside what has been forbidden in My Book.’”

46 And elsewhere: “Today is the day of remembrance and praise, and the day of service. Do not deprive yourselves. You are the letters of the words and the words of the Book, and you are saplings planted by the hand of loving care in the soil of mercy, which have grown through the rains of bounty. He protected you from the tempests of associating partners with God and the shattering winds of unbelief, and nurtured you with the hands of compassion. Now is the time for fruit and leaves, and the fruits of the human tree have always been, and remain, good deeds and pleasing qualities. Do not withhold these fruits from the heedless. If they accept them, the purpose is achieved and life is made manifest; otherwise, leave them to amuse themselves in their idle talk. O people of God! Strive, so that the hearts of the diverse peoples of the world may, through the water of your forbearance and compassion, be cleansed and purified of malice and hatred, and become fit and worthy to receive the splendors of the Sun of Truth.”

47 In the fourth Ishráq of the Ishráqát We stated: “Every cause requires a helper, and the victorious hosts in this Revelation are commendable deeds and qualities. The leader and commander of these hosts is the fear of God. It is the sovereign of all and rules over all.”

48 In the third Tajallí of the Book of Tajallíyát We stated: “The sciences, arts, and crafts are the means by which existence is exalted and elevated. Knowledge is like wings for existence and a ladder for ascent. Its acquisition is incumbent upon everyone, but it must be knowledge from which the people of the earth benefit, not knowledge that begins with words and ends with words. Those versed in the sciences and crafts have a great claim upon the people of the world. To this the Mother of Utterance bears witness in this manifest station.”

49 In truth, a human being’s real treasure is knowledge. It is the source of honor and prosperity, joy and delight, gladness and cheer. Blessed is whoever holds fast to it, and woe to the heedless.

50 You should, in all circumstances, summon the servants to whatever brings forth spiritual qualities and good deeds, so that all may become aware of what exalts human existence and, with full resolve, seek the most exalted station and the loftiest summit. The foremost means of educating humanity has been the fear of God. Blessed are those who attain it.

51 The first word set down and inscribed by the Most Glorious Pen on the first leaf of Paradise is this: “Truly I say, the sure protection and strong fortress for all the people of the world is the fear of God. It is the greatest means of protecting humanity and the supreme cause of safeguarding humankind. There is indeed a sign within human beings that restrains and guards them from what is unseemly and unworthy, and it has been named a sense of shame. Yet this is confined to a few; not everyone has possessed or possesses this station. The rulers of the age and the learned among the people must hold fast to religion, for it is the means by which the fear of God appears in all besides Him.”

52 The second word We mentioned on the second leaf of Paradise is this: “At this moment the Pen of Utterance counsels the manifestations of power and the dawning places of authority—that is, the kings and sovereigns, may God aid them—and exhorts them to uphold religion and hold fast to it. It is the great means of establishing order in the world and tranquility among all who dwell in it. The weakening of the pillars of religion has strengthened the ignorant and made them bold and insolent. Truly I say, whatever has diminished the lofty station of religion has increased the heedlessness of the wicked, and the eventual outcome is chaos and confusion. Listen, O people of insight, and take heed, O people of discernment.”

53 It is hoped that you will listen with an attentive ear to what has been stated, so that you may turn the servants’ awareness from what they possess to what is with God. We ask God to free the light of fairness and the sun of justice from the dark clouds of heedlessness and make them manifest. No light can equal the light of justice. It is the means of establishing order in the world and bringing tranquility to the nations.

54 In the Scroll of Utterance this exalted word is written and inscribed: “Say: O friends! Strive, so that the afflictions that have befallen the Wronged One and you in the path of God may not be wasted among the people. Hold fast to the hem of chastity, and likewise to the cord of trustworthiness and piety. Consider the welfare of the world, not your own selfish desires. O people of the Wronged One! You are the shepherds of the world. Keep the sheep safe from the wolf of self and desire, and adorn them with the ornament of the fear of God. This is the firm decree that has flowed at this moment from the Pen of Eternity. By the life of God! The sword of good character and conduct is sharper than swords of iron. Human nature is at this moment calling out and saying: ‘O people! The Day has come, and my Lord has made me manifest with a light whose dawning has eclipsed the suns of utterance. Fear the All-Merciful, and do not be among the heedless.’”

55 The third word We mentioned on the third leaf of Paradise is this: “O son of man! If you look to grace, set aside what benefits you and take up what benefits the servants; and if you look to justice, choose for others what you choose for yourself. At one time, humility raises a human being to the heaven of glory and power; at another, pride brings one down to the station of abasement and humiliation.” The Day is momentous, and the call is mighty. In one of the Tablets We stated this exalted word: “If the entire world of the spirit were transformed into the faculty of hearing, it could be said to be worthy of hearing this call that has risen from the Most Exalted Horizon. Otherwise, these ears, defiled by false tales, have never been and are not worthy of it. Blessed are those who listen, and woe to the heedless.”

56 We ask and hope that God, exalted be His glory, will aid the dawning places of wealth and power and the sources of honor and authority—that is, the kings of the earth, may God aid them—to establish the Most Great Peace. This is the greatest means of bringing tranquility to the nations. The sovereigns of the world, may God grant them success, must jointly uphold this cause, which is the greatest means of protecting the world. It is hoped that they will arise to do what brings ease to the servants. They must convene a great assembly, and the kings or their ministers must attend it, enforce unity and concord, and turn from arms to reconciliation. If one sovereign rises against another, the other sovereigns must arise to restrain him. In that case, armies and the instruments and equipment of war would be unnecessary, except for a limited measure to protect their countries. If they attain this greatest good, all the people of the realm will pursue their affairs in comfort and happiness, and the lamentation and mourning of most of the servants will be stilled. We ask God to aid them in what He loves and approves. He is indeed the Lord of the throne and the earth, and the Master of the next world and this one. It would be better and more fitting for the great sovereigns themselves to attend that assembly and issue its decrees. Whichever sovereign arises to undertake this cause and carry it out is, in God’s sight, the foremost of sovereigns. Blessed is he, and bliss be his.

57 In these lands, whenever the time for military conscription comes, the greatest alarm appears on every side. Each state increases its forces every year, for the ministers of war are eager in this matter and keep saying, “Is there more?” According to reports, the government of Iran, may God aid it, also intends to increase its army. In the view of the Wronged One, one hundred thousand well-organized and disciplined troops would suffice at present. It is hoped that His Majesty will increase the light of justice. By the life of God! Justice is a mighty host. It is, first and foremost, the conqueror of hearts and souls; it reveals the mysteries of existence and bears the banner of love and generosity.

58 And in the treasuries of divine knowledge there is a knowledge whose application dispels fear, though only to the extent possible. It is a method that must be practiced from childhood. It makes a great difference, and whatever it diminishes of fear it adds to courage. If the divine will lends its aid, a detailed explanation of what has been mentioned will flow from the pen of utterance. Perhaps there will also appear, in the various branches of the sciences and arts, that which will bring about the renewal of the world and its nations. Likewise, a word has been inscribed and recorded by the Most Exalted Pen in the Crimson Scroll. That word will reveal in their entirety the powers latent in human beings; indeed, it will add as much again to them. We ask God, blessed and exalted be He, to aid His servants in what He loves and approves.

59 These days enemies have appeared on every side, and the fire of hatred is ablaze. People of the earth! By my life and yours, this Wronged One has never entertained, nor does he now entertain, any ambition for leadership. His purpose has been and remains to remove whatever causes discord among the peoples of the world and division among its nations, so that all may be free and at ease and attend to their own affairs. The friends are asked not to soil this hem with the dust of falsehoods, or to diminish his dignity, station, sanctity, and purity by recounting the miracles they ascribe to him.

60 Glory be to God! This is the day when the wise should seek the counsel of this Wronged One and ask God for what brings honor and well-being. Yet all, on the contrary, are striving and laboring to extinguish this radiant, shining light, and everyone seeks to establish some fault or raise some objection. Matters have reached the point where they have said things about every aspect of this Wronged One’s conduct that are unworthy of mention. One of the friends reported that a person among those gathered in the Great City had said that every year a sum of fifty thousand tumans goes from the homeland to Acre. He mentioned this with the deepest regret. It is not known who the accountant was, or who the bookkeeper.

61 In short, this Wronged One has remained patient and silent in the face of everything they have inflicted and said, for our intention is, through the grace of God, exalted be His glory, and His all-preceding mercy, to efface from the world the rule of contention, conflict, and bloodshed through the power of utterance. In all circumstances, faced with what they have said, we have held fast to gracious patience and left the matter to God. In reply to this statement, however, we said: If he is indeed truthful in what he has stated, he should thank the Lord of existence and Sovereign of the unseen and the seen for having raised up a person from Iran who, while in prison, without helper or supporter, has taken possession of Iran and collects a tax from it every year. This calls for praise, not blame, if he is fair-minded. And if anyone wishes to learn about the affairs of this Wronged One, the answer is that on some nights and days the captives of the world and the wronged ones among the nations have passed from night to morning and from day to night without food. We have never liked, nor do we now like, mentioning such things, and we have neither complained nor do we complain of the person who made that statement. In this prison, an honored person earned his livelihood for a time by cutting stone, and at times some have been sustained by the food of God—that is, hunger. We ask God, blessed and exalted be He, to aid everyone in justice and fairness and enable them to turn to Him and return. He is indeed the All-Hearing, the Responsive.

62 Glory be to You, O God, my God! You see what has befallen the Wronged One at the hands of those who have never associated with me and have risen to harm and abase me, to such a degree that pens are powerless to record it, tongues to describe it, and tablets to bear it. You hear the lament of my heart and the cry of my being, and what has befallen Your trusted ones in Your lands and Your chosen ones in Your realm at the hands of those who have broken Your covenant and pledge. My Lord, I ask You by the cries of Your lovers throughout the world, by their lamentation in their remoteness from the court of Your presence, by the blood shed for love of You, and by the hearts consumed in Your path, to protect Your loved ones from the oppression of those who are heedless of the mysteries of Your name, the All-Choosing. My Lord, strengthen them through Your power, which has prevailed over all things, and enable them to be patient and steadfast. You are indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Ever-Bestowing. There is no God but You, the Generous, the Bountiful.

63 These days some have abandoned justice and fairness and have attacked with the sword of malice and the spear of hatred, although it befits the fair-minded to assist one rejected by the world and cast out by its nations, and to hold fast to righteousness and piety. Most have not yet understood what this Wronged One intends or why he has borne countless afflictions. In short, the tongue of the heart speaks the words, “If only my people knew!” And the Wronged One, detached from all, utters these exalted words: “The waves have surrounded the ship of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Mariner, do not be alarmed by the winds! The One who causes the dawn to break is with you in this darkness, which has troubled the hearts of all people except those whom God, the Almighty, the All-Choosing, has willed to spare.”

64 Shaykh! By the Sun of Truth that rises and shines from the horizon of the heaven of this prison, this Wronged One has had no purpose but reform. Every discerning knower and every well-informed scholar bears witness to this. In times of affliction he held fast to the cord of patience and steadfastness and was content with what befell him at the hands of his enemies. He says: “My God, I have relinquished my will for Your will, and my desire for the manifestation of Your desire. By Your might, I desire my life and its continuance only to arise in service to Your Cause, and I cherish my existence only to sacrifice it in Your path. My Lord, You see and know that those from whom we sought justice and fairness have risen against us with oppression and tyranny. Outwardly they were with me, but inwardly they aided my enemies, who arose to violate my honor. My God, my God! I bear witness that You created Your servants to aid Your Cause and exalt Your Word, yet they have aided Your enemies. I ask You, by Your Cause, which has encompassed existence, and by Your name, through which You have subdued the unseen and the seen, to adorn the people of the earth with the light of Your justice, and illumine their hearts with the radiance of Your knowledge. My Lord, I am Your servant and the son of Your servant. I bear witness to Your oneness and uniqueness, to the holiness of Your essence and the transcendence of Your being. My Lord, You see Your trusted ones in the hands of the treacherous among Your creatures and the slanderers among Your people. You know what has befallen us at the hands of those whom You know better than we do. They have committed what has torn the veil of those among Your creatures who are near to You. I ask You to aid them to arise and make good what they have missed in the days of the Dawning Place of Your revelation and the Dayspring of Your inspiration. You indeed have power over whatever You will, and in Your grasp are the reins of all who are in the heavens and the earth. The call of innate nature and its lament have risen, and it cries at the top of its voice, saying: ‘People! By God, I have attained the presence of the One who manifests me and sends me down. This is the day when the Mount has smiled at the One who converses with it, Carmel at the One who descends upon it, and the Lote Tree at its Teacher. Fear God and do not be among those who deny. Do not deprive yourselves of what has appeared through grace. Take the Kawthar of everlasting life in the name of your Lord, the Lord of Names, and drink from it in remembrance of Him, the Almighty, the Incomparable.’”

65 In all circumstances we have enjoined people to do good and forbidden them to do evil. The Lord of existence bears witness that this Wronged One has asked God, on behalf of humanity, for what fosters fellowship, unity, affection, and concord. By the life of God, this Wronged One has been unable to conceal it. He has manifested what He willed, and He is the All-Powerful, the All-Choosing.

66 We repeat some of these exalted words revealed in the Tablet to His Majesty the Shah, so that you may know with clear certainty that what has been stated came from God: “King! I was like any other person, asleep upon my couch. The breezes of the All-Glorious passed over me and taught me the knowledge of all that has been. This is not from me, but from One who is Almighty, All-Knowing. He commanded me to raise my call between earth and heaven. Because of this, there befell me what has caused the tears of those who recognize Him to flow. I have not studied the sciences current among the people, nor have I entered their schools. Ask the city in which I lived, so that you may be certain that I am not one of those who lie. This is a leaf stirred by the winds of the will of your Lord, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. Can it remain still when tempestuous winds blow? No, by the Lord of Names and Attributes! Rather, they move it as they will. Nonexistence has no existence before the Eternal. His irrevocable command has come and caused me to speak His praise among the peoples of the world. Before His command I was as one dead; the hand of the will of your Lord, the All-Merciful, the Compassionate, turned me about. Can anyone speak of his own accord words for which all people, both lowly and exalted, assail him? No, by the One who taught the Pen the mysteries of eternity—unless he is aided by One who is All-Powerful, All-Capable.”

67 “King! Look upon this Wronged One with the eye of justice, then judge truthfully what has befallen him. God has made you His shadow among His servants and a sign of His power to those who inhabit the lands. Judge between us and those who have wronged us without proof or an illuminating Book. Those around you love you for their own sake, while this Youth loves you for your own sake. He has wished only to draw you near to the seat of grace and turn you toward the right hand of justice. Your Lord is witness to what I say.”

68 “King! If you were to hear the sound of the Most Exalted Pen and the cooing of the Dove of Eternity upon the branches of the Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary, as they remember God, the Maker of names and Creator of earth and heaven, it would bring you to a station where you would see nothing in existence but the revelation of the One who is worshipped. You would regard sovereignty as the most insignificant thing, relinquishing it to whoever desired it and turning toward a horizon radiant with the lights of His face. You would never bear the burden of sovereignty except to aid your Lord, the Exalted, the Most High. Then the Concourse on high would bless you. How excellent is this most sublime station, if you would ascend to it through a sovereignty known by the name of God!”

69 You, or someone else, have said that the Surah of Divine Unity should be translated so that it may become clear and evident to everyone that God “neither begets nor is begotten,” whereas the Bábís profess lordship and divinity.

70 Shaykh! This station is that of dying to self and abiding in God, and if this word is mentioned, it denotes sheer and utter nothingness. This is the station of “I have no power to bring myself benefit or harm, life or resurrection.”

71 Shaykh! What do the scholars of this age say about the revelations of the Lote Tree of Utterance to the son of ʿImrán on the Mount of recognition? He heard the Word from the Tree and accepted it. Yet most are deprived of understanding this station, for they are occupied with what they themselves possess and heedless of what is with God. Sayyid Findirisk has spoken well on this subject: “No outward understanding can grasp these words, even if you are Abú Naṣr or Abú ʿAlí Síná.” What do they say about this passage uttered by the Seal of the Prophets—may the soul of all else be sacrificed for him? He says: “You will see your Lord as you see the full moon on the fourteenth night.” And the Commander of the Faithful, peace be upon him, says in the Ṭatanjiyyih sermon: “Await, then, the appearance of the One who spoke to Moses from the Tree upon the Mount.” Likewise, Ḥusayn son of ʿAlí, peace be upon him, says: “Can anyone other than You possess a manifestation that You do not possess, so that he should be the one to make You manifest? Blind is the eye that does not see You.”

72 Such sayings from the utterances of the holy ones, God’s blessings be upon them, are well known and widely cited, and are found in authoritative books. Blessed is the one who examines and speaks with unalloyed truthfulness. Bliss awaits the soul who, through the heavenly stream of the utterance of the Desire of all peoples, has purified himself of conjectures and vain imaginings, torn through the veils of glory in the name of the All-Sufficing, the Most Exalted, passed beyond the world and its peoples, and set out for the Most Great Prison.

73 Shaykh! The fragrances of revelation are distinct from all else, and divine utterance shines resplendent among books like the sun. Blessed is the one who has found and recognized it and said: “Praise be to You, Desire of the world, and thanks be to You, Beloved of the hearts of the sincere!”

74 People have not understood what is meant by the mention of divinity and lordship. If they understood, they would rise from their places and utter the words, “We have turned in repentance to God.” The Seal of the Prophets—may the soul of all else be sacrificed for him—says: “We have states with God in which we are He and He is we, yet He is He and we are we.”

75 Even setting this station aside, why have they not mentioned the other stations revealed by the All-Glorious Pen? On most days and nights the tongue of this Wronged One utters these exalted words: “My God, my God! I bear witness to Your oneness and singleness, and that You are God; there is no God but You. You have always been sanctified beyond the mention of anyone besides You and the praise of anyone other than You, and You will forever remain as You have been, before and hereafter. I ask You, Lord of eternity, by the Most Great Name, by the radiance of the Sun of Your Revelation upon the Mount of Utterance, and by the waves of the ocean of Your knowledge throughout contingent existence, to assist me in what will draw me near to You and detach me from all besides You. By Your might, God of all beings and Desire of all created things! I long to lay my face upon every spot of Your earth, that it may perhaps be honored by a place honored by the footsteps of Your holy ones.”

76 By the life of God! Vain imaginings have deprived people of the horizon of certitude, and conjectures have kept them from the sealed wine. I speak truthfully, and I speak for the sake of God: this servant, this Wronged One, is ashamed to attribute being and existence to himself, let alone stations beyond them. Whenever an insightful person walks upon the earth, he finds himself abashed, for he knows with clear certainty that, by God’s leave, the source of his well-being, wealth, honor, elevation, distinction, and power is the earth beneath the feet of all the world. A soul who becomes aware of this station is assuredly sanctified and freed from arrogance, pride, and conceit. What has been said has come from God. He has borne and continues to bear witness, and He is the All-Knowing, the All-Informed.

77 Ask God for attentive ears, keen eyes, receptive breasts, and hearts turned toward Him, so that people may perhaps grasp the purpose and turn toward the Friend. Afflictions unlike anything eyes have witnessed befell this Wronged One, yet he never hesitated to proclaim the Cause. He conveyed to their majesties the kings and sovereigns—may God, exalted be He, assist them—what would bring about the peace, unity, concord, and prosperity of the world and the well-being of its peoples. Among other things, two statements by Napoleon III were heard, and these prompted us to send him a Tablet from Adrianople. He did not reply. After our arrival in the Most Great Prison, a letter with a handwritten note arrived from his minister. Its first part was written in Persian script and its final part in his own hand. He expressed his kindness and stated: “As requested, I delivered the letter, and thus far he has given no reply. We have, however, instructed our minister plenipotentiary in the capital and our consul in those regions that whatever request you may have, you should make it known, and it will be carried out.”

78 From this statement of his, it became clear that he had understood this servant’s purpose to be the improvement of outward circumstances. Therefore, verses addressed to him were revealed in the Surah of the Temple. Some of them are quoted here so that you may know that the Cause of this Wronged One has been for God and from God.

79 “King of Paris! Tell the priest to ring the bells no longer. By God, the True One! The most majestic Bell has appeared in the temple of the Most Great Name, and the fingers of the will of your Lord, the Exalted, the Most High, ring it in the dominion of eternity in His All-Glorious name. Thus have the most great verses of your Lord been sent down once again, so that you may arise to remember God, the Creator of earth and heaven, in these days when all the tribes of the earth have lamented, the foundations of the countries have been shaken, and the dust of unbelief has enveloped His servants, except those whom your Lord, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise, has willed to spare. Say: The Chosen One has come beneath canopies of light to quicken all beings with the fragrances of His name, the All-Merciful, so that the world may be united and all may gather around this table sent down from heaven. Beware of denying God’s bounty after it has been sent down. This is better for you than what you possess, for that will perish, while what is with God will endure. He, indeed, ordains whatever He wills. The breezes of forgiveness have blown from the direction of your Lord, the All-Merciful. Whoever turns toward them will be purified by them from disobedience and from every disease and ailment. Blessed is the one who turns toward them, and woe to those who turn away!”

80 “If you turn toward all things with the ear of your innate nature, you will hear them say: ‘The Ancient One, possessed of great glory, has come.’ Everything glorifies its Lord with praise. Among them are those who have recognized God and remember Him, and those who remember Him without recognizing Him. Thus have we set forth the matter in a clear Tablet.”

81 “King! Listen to the call from this Fire blazing in this verdant Tree upon this lofty Mount, on the holy, snow-white Spot beyond the ocean of eternity: ‘There is no God but Me, the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful.’ We sent the one whom We strengthened with the Holy Spirit to tell you of this Light, which has shone from the horizon of the will of your Lord, the Exalted, the All-Glorious, and whose signs have appeared in the West. Turn toward Him on this Day, which God has made the crown of days, and on which the All-Merciful has revealed Himself to all who are in the heavens and on earth. Arise to serve God and aid His Cause. He will support you with the hosts of the unseen and the seen, and make you sovereign over all upon which the sun rises. Your Lord is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty.”

82 The breezes of the All-Merciful have wafted through creation. Blessed is whoever has caught their fragrance and turned toward them with a pure heart. Adorn your person with the ornament of My name, your tongue with My remembrance, and your heart with My mighty, unassailable love. We have desired for you only what is better for you than all you possess and all the treasures of the earth. Your Lord is indeed the All-Knowing, the All-Aware. Arise among the people in My name and say: “People of the earth! Turn toward Him who has turned toward you. He is indeed the face of God among you, His proof in your midst, and His guide for you. 265 It is related of the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, that he said: “Blessed is the one who visits ʿAkkā, and blessed is the one who visits a visitor to ʿAkkā. Blessed is the one who drinks from the Spring of the Cow and bathes in its water, for the wide-eyed maidens of Paradise drink the camphor of Paradise from the Spring of the Cow, the Spring of Silwān, and the Well of Zamzam. Blessed is the one who drinks from these springs and bathes in their waters, for God has forbidden the fire of hell to touch him or his body on the Day of Resurrection.”

266 It is related of the Prophet, may God bless him and grant him peace, that he said: “In ʿAkkā there are bounties and favors that God bestows on whomever He wills. Whoever says in ʿAkkā, ‘Glory be to God, and praise be to God; there is no god but God, and God is most great; there is no power and no strength except through God, the Most High, the Almighty,’ God will record for him a thousand good deeds, erase a thousand evil deeds, raise him a thousand degrees in Paradise, and forgive his sins. Whoever says in ʿAkkā, ‘I ask God for forgiveness,’ God will forgive all his sins. Whoever remembers God in ʿAkkā in the mornings and late afternoons, in the evenings and at daybreak, will be better in the sight of God than the carrying of swords, spears, and weapons in the path of God, exalted be He.”

267 And the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: “Whoever gazes at the sea at noon and proclaims God’s greatness at sunset, God will forgive his sins, even if they are like the sands of ʿĀlij. Whoever counts forty waves while proclaiming the greatness of God, exalted be He, God will forgive his past and future sins.”

268 And the Messenger of God, may God bless him and grant him peace, said: “For whoever gazes at the sea for an entire night, this is better than two full months between the Corner and the Station. Whoever is brought up on the shores is better than one brought up elsewhere, and one who sleeps on the shores is like one who keeps vigil elsewhere.” End of quotation.

269 The Messenger of God, may God, exalted be He, bless him and grant him peace, has spoken the truth.
