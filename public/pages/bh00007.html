Surih-i-Haykal (Surah of the Temple)

BH00007

Translated on 16 Qudrat 183 using GPT 6.1 Sol

1 This is the Surah of the Temple. God has made it the mirror of His names between the heavens and the earth, and the sign of His remembrance among all peoples.

2 He is the Most Wondrous, the All-Glorious.

3 Glory be to Him who has sent down the verses for people who understand. Glory be to Him who sends down the verses for people who perceive. Glory be to Him who guides whomever He wills to His path. Say: I am indeed the path of God for all who are in the heavens and on earth. Blessed are those who hasten toward it.

4 Glory be to Him who sends down the verses for people who know. Glory be to Him who speaks from the dominion of command; none recognize Him except His honored servants. Glory be to Him who brings whomever He wills to life through His word, “Be,” and it is. Glory be to Him who raises whomever He wills to the heaven of grace and sends down from it whatever He desires in a predetermined measure.

5 Blessed is He who does whatever He wills through a command from His presence. He is indeed the Truth, the Knower of all knowledge. Blessed is He who inspires whomever He wills with whatever He desires through His irrevocable, hidden command. Blessed is He who aids whomever He wills with the hosts of the unseen. He indeed accomplishes whatever He desires, and He is the Almighty, the Self-Subsisting. Blessed is He who exalts whomever He wills through the sovereignty of His might and strengthens whomever He wills however He desires. Blessed are those who recognize Him.

6 Blessed is He who has ordained a measure for everything in a treasured Tablet. Blessed is He who has sent down to His servant that which illumines hearts and minds. Blessed is He who has sent down upon His servant such affliction as has burned the innermost beings of those established in the pavilion of eternity, and the hearts of those brought near. Blessed is He who has sent down upon His servant the arrows of affliction from the clouds of decree; then He sees Me enduring with gracious patience. Blessed is He who has ordained for His servant what He has ordained for none of His other servants. He is indeed the Unique, the Almighty, the Self-Subsisting.

7 Blessed is He who has sent down upon His servant the spears of decree from the clouds of hatred raised by those who turn away; then He sees Him filled with profound gratitude. Blessed is He who has laid upon His servant the weight of the heavens and the earth. We praise Him for this, though none understand it except those endowed with knowledge. Glory be to Him who has placed His Beauty beneath the claws of malice wielded by the perpetrators of indecency. We are content with this, though none comprehend it except those endowed with understanding. Glory be to Him who has placed Husayn among the hosts of enemies, while at every moment the spears of oppression and hatred strike His body. We thank Him for what He has decreed for His sorrow-laden servant who turns to Him.

8 When I saw Myself at the very center of affliction, I heard the most wondrous, sweetest voice above My head. When I turned, I saw the Maiden of the remembrance of My Lord’s name suspended in the air, level with My head. I saw that she was filled with joy, as though the adornment of divine pleasure shone from her face and the radiance of the All-Merciful appeared upon her cheek. Between the heavens and the earth she spoke with a call that enraptured hearts and minds, bringing to every part of My outward and inward being glad tidings that rejoiced My soul and filled the honored servants with delight.

9 She pointed her finger toward My head and addressed all who are in the heavens and on earth: “By God! This is the Beloved of the worlds, but you do not understand. This is the Beauty of God among you and His sovereignty in your midst, if only you would recognize Him. This is the mystery of God and His treasure, the Cause of God and His might, for all who are in the kingdom of command and creation, if only you would understand. This is indeed the One whom those in the dominion of eternity, and those established behind the pavilion of the All-Glorious, long to meet, yet you turn away from His Beauty.”

10 People of the Bayan! If you will not aid Him, God will aid Him with the hosts of the heavens and the earth, and the hosts of the unseen, through His command, “Be,” and it is. By His will He will raise up a people known to none but Himself, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. He will purify them of the defilement of delusion and desire, raise them to the station of holiness, and manifest through them the signs of His sovereign might on earth. Thus has it been decreed by God, the Almighty, the All-Loving.

11 People of the Bayan! Do you deny the One you were created to meet, then rejoice in your seats? Do you oppose the One a single hair of whom is better in God’s sight than all who are in the heavens and on earth, and then mock Us? People of the Bayan! Bring forward what you possess, so that I may know by what proof you believed in the Manifestations of the Cause in the past, and by what evidence you now act so arrogantly.

12 By Him who created Me from the light of His beauty, I have found none more heedless or more blind than you. You offer the Tablets in your possession as proof of your faith in God. Yet when the verses were sent down and the lamp shone forth, you denied the One through whose Pen all affairs were decreed in a preserved Tablet. You read the verses and deny their source and the One who reveals them. Thus has God taken away your sight in recompense for your deeds, if only you perceived. You transcribe the verses at evening and at daybreak, yet remain veiled from their Revealer.

13 The Concourse on high sees you engaged in your evil deeds and disowns you, though you do not hear. They ask one another, “What are these asses saying, and in what valley are they grazing? Do they deny what their own beings testify to? Do they shut their eyes while they can see?” By God, people! Your actions have bewildered the inhabitants of the cities of names, while you wander in a barren valley, unaware.

14 Pen of the Most High! Listen to the call of your Lord from the Tree of the Utmost Boundary in the radiant realm of oneness, so that the melodies of your Lord, the All-Merciful, may fill you with joy and fragrance, and these breaths wafting from the direction of My name, the Ever-Forgiving, may free you from sorrow. Then raise up within this Temple the temples of oneness, so that in the kingdom of creation they may tell of their Lord, the Most High, the All-Glorious, and be among those illumined by the lights of their Lord.

15 We have ordained this Temple to be the origin of existence in the wondrous creation, so that all may know with certainty that I have the power to accomplish whatever I will through My word, “Be,” and it is. Beneath the shadow of every letter of this Temple We raise up a people whose number none knows except God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. God will create from it a people whom the allusions of those who have rebelled against God will not veil, and who will drink at all times from the river of life. They, indeed, are the ones who attain success.

16 These are the servants established beneath the shadow of their Lord’s mercy, whom none seeking to hinder them has held back. The radiance of the All-Merciful is seen upon their faces, and the remembrance of My name, the Almighty, the Hidden, is heard from their hearts. Were their lips to open in glorification of their Lord, all who are in the heavens and on earth would glorify Him with them, yet few among the people hear. When they remember their Maker, all things remember Him with them. Thus has God favored them above creation, but the people do not know.

17 They move around the Cause of God as the shadow moves around the sun. Open your eyes, people of the Bayan, so that you may see. Through their movement all things move, and through their stillness all things are still, if only you were certain. Through them those who affirm God’s unity turn toward the focal point of all horizons, and tranquillity and dignity have appeared among the righteous, if only you knew. Through them the earth has been made firm, the clouds have poured down rain, and the table of holiness has been sent down from the heaven of grace, if only you understood.

18 These are the guardians of the Cause of God on earth. They protect the beauty of the Cause from the dust raised by every detested idolater. They have no fear for their lives in the path of God, but expend them in the hope of meeting the Beloved and of His exaltation through this name, the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent, the Almighty, the Most Holy.

19 Temple! Arise in your own person in such a way that all contingent beings may arise through your arising. Then aid your Lord with the power and might We have bestowed upon you. Beware of dismay when all things are dismayed. Be the manifestation of My name, the Self-Subsisting; then aid your Lord to the utmost of your ability, and regard created beings and whatever comes from their mouths as no more than the hum of a gnat in a boundless valley. Drink from the river of life in My name, the All-Merciful, then give those brought near among the people of this paradise a draught that will detach them from every name and bring them beneath this blessed, outstretched shadow.

20 O Temple! We have gathered within you all things created between earth and heaven, and questioned them about that concerning which We took their covenant in the primordial realm of eternity. We found most of them faltering in speech, their eyes staring fixedly, and only a few radiant in countenance and fluent in utterance. From these We brought forth the creation of all that has been and will be. God has honored their faces above turning toward the faces of those who associate partners with Him, sheltered them beneath the shade of the Lote-Tree of His own Self, sent down upon them the tranquility of His Cause, and strengthened them with the hosts of the unseen and the visible.

21 O Eye of this Temple! Do not turn toward heaven and what it contains, or toward earth and those upon it. We created you for My beauty. Here it is before you: gaze as you wish, and do not withhold your glances from the beauty of your Lord, the Almighty, the Beloved. Through you We will raise up keen eyes and discerning vision that will behold the signs of their Creator and turn their gaze away from all that those endowed with perception can perceive. Through you We bestow the power of sight upon whomever We will, and seize those who have been deprived of this bounty. Indeed, they drink deeply from the cup of delusion and do not understand.

22 O Hearing of this Temple! Purify yourself from the clamor of every rejected clamorer, then listen to the melodies of your Lord. From the direction of the throne He reveals to you: “There is no God but Me, the Almighty, the All-Powerful, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.” Through you We will raise up purified ears to listen to the Word of God and to what has appeared from the dawning-place of the utterance of your Lord, the All-Merciful. Indeed, they will hear the melodies of revelation from this blessed and praiseworthy quarter.

23 O Tongue of this Temple! We created you through My name, the All-Merciful, taught you what was treasured in utterance, and caused you to speak so that I might be greatly remembered throughout creation. Speak this wondrous remembrance and do not fear the manifestations of Satan, for you were created for this by My all-protecting, self-subsisting command. Through you We have opened the tongue to utterance in all that has been, and through My sovereignty We will do so in all that is to come. Through you We will raise up speaking tongues, all moving in praise among the company of eternity and the company of creation. Thus have the verses been sent down and the decree issued by the Possessor of names and attributes. Your Lord is indeed the Truth, the Knower of all unseen things. Nothing will prevent them from praising their Creator. Through them all things will arise to proclaim the Possessor of names: “There is no God but Me, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Beloved.” No tongue of those who remember speaks without being sustained by this Tongue from this paradise, yet few among the people recognize this. There is no tongue that does not glorify its Lord and speak His remembrance. Some people understand and remember, while others remember but do not understand.

24 O Maiden of inner meanings! Come forth from the chambers of words by the permission of God, the Possessor of the earths and the heavens. Appear adorned with the vesture of divinity, then with ruby fingers serve the wine of the realm of power, so that the inhabitants of the mortal realm may become aware of the splendor with which the Sun of eternity has shone from the horizon of the heavenly kingdom, and arise in praise between earth and heaven, remembering this Youth who has taken His seat upon the throne of His name, the Bestower, at the heart of the gardens of paradise. From His face has appeared the radiance of the All-Merciful, from His glance the glances of the All-Glorious, and from His ways the ways of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

25 If you find no one to take the crimson wine from the snow-white hand in the name of your Lord, the Exalted, the Most High, who has appeared once again in His name, the All-Glorious, do not grieve. Leave these people to themselves, then return behind the pavilion of grandeur and majesty. There you will find a people whose faces shine with light like the sun at its zenith. They proclaim the oneness of their Lord and glorify Him through this Name, which has taken its place upon the seat of independence with the sovereignty of might and glory. You will hear from them nothing but My remembrance. Your Lord bears witness to what I say. None of those created by the Word of God from the eternity of eternities has known these people. Thus have We explained the matter to you and variously set forth the verses, so that people may reflect upon the signs of their Lord.

26 They were not commanded to bow down before Adam, nor have they turned their faces away from the face of your Lord. At every moment they enjoy the bounty of holiness. Thus has the Pen of the All-Merciful inscribed the mysteries of all that has been and will be, so that people may understand. God will bring these people forth upon the earth, and through them exalt His remembrance, spread His works, establish His words, and proclaim His signs, despite those who have disbelieved, denied, and rejected His signs.

27 O Countenance of oneness! If you find them and attain their presence, recount to them what the Youth tells you of His own story and of what has befallen Him, so that they may learn what is inscribed in a preserved Tablet. Tell them the tidings of the Youth and the adversity and affliction that have touched Him, so that they may take heed through My sufferings and be among those who are mindful. Then tell them that We chose one of Our brothers and sprinkled upon him a spray from the surging ocean of knowledge. We then clothed him in the garment of one of the names and raised him to a station at which all arose to praise him. We protected him from the harm of everyone intent on harm, in a manner beyond the ability of the powerful.

28 We stood alone against the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth in the days when all people arose to kill Me. Among them We continued to proclaim the remembrance and praise of God and to uphold His Cause, until the Word of God was established among His creatures, His signs became widely known, His power was exalted, and His sovereignty shone forth. Honored servants bear witness to this. When My brother saw that the Cause had risen, pride and conceit arose within him. He then emerged from behind the veils, made war against Me, disputed My verses, denied My proof, and rejected My signs. The belly of the covetous one was not satisfied until he desired to eat My flesh and drink My blood. Those servants who migrated with God bear witness to this, and beyond them, servants who have been brought near.

29 He consulted about this with one of My servants and enticed him into it. Then God aided Me with the hosts of the unseen and the visible, protected Me through the truth, and sent down upon Me what prevented him from carrying out his intent. The scheming of those who disbelieved in the signs of the All-Merciful came to nothing. Indeed, they are a people given to denial. When news of what his self had prompted him to do was spread, and those who had migrated learned of it, an outcry arose among them, reaching such a pitch that it almost became known throughout the city. We then restrained them and imparted to them the word of patience, so that they might be among those who endure patiently.

30 By God, besides whom there is no other God! We endured this patiently and commanded the servants to show patience and forbearance. We departed from among them and took up residence in another house, so that the fire of hatred in his breast might subside and he might be among the rightly guided. We did not interfere with him or see him afterward, but remained alone in the house, awaiting the grace of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. When he learned that the matter had become known, he took up the pen of falsehood, wrote to the servants, and attributed everything he had done to My peerless and wronged Beauty, seeking within himself to stir up discord and to instill hatred in the hearts of those who believed in God, the Almighty, the All-Loving.

31 By Him in whose hand is My soul! We were bewildered by his scheming; indeed, all existence, unseen and visible, was bewildered by it. Even so, he found no rest within himself until he committed what the pen cannot recount, violating thereby My honor and the honor of God, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. Were I to recount what he did to Me, the oceans of the earth could not complete the account, even if God made them ink, nor could all things exhaust it, even if God turned them into pens. Thus do We relate what has befallen Me, if only you knew.

32 O Pen of eternity! Do not grieve over what has befallen you. God will raise up a people who will see with their own eyes and recount what has befallen you. Withdraw the pen from mentioning these people, then move it in remembrance of the Possessor of eternity. Leave contingent things behind, then drink of the sealed wine of My remembrance. Beware of occupying yourself with the mention of those from whom you will find nothing but the odors of hatred. Love of leadership has seized them to such a degree that they destroy themselves to exalt their own renown and perpetuate their names. God has inscribed them among the worshippers of names in a preserved Tablet. Recount what you intended for this Temple, so that its signs may appear on earth, the lights of this radiance may fill the horizons, and it may cleanse the earth of the defilement of those who have disbelieved in God. Thus have We sent down the verses and explained the matter for a people who understand.

33 O Temple! Extend your hand over all who are in the heavens and on earth, then grasp the reins of the Cause in the grip of your will. We have placed the kingdom of all things in your right hand. Do whatever you wish, and do not fear those who do not know. Then raise your hand to the Tablet that has shone from the horizon of your Lord’s finger, and grasp it in such a way that, through your grasp, the hands of all in creation may grasp it. This is what befits you, if you are among those who understand. As your hand rises toward the heaven of My grace, the hands of all things rise toward God, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the All-Loving. From your hand We will raise up hands of strength, power, and might, and through them manifest My power to all in the kingdom of command and creation, so that the servants may know that there is no God but Me, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Through them We give and take, and none knows this except those who see with the eye of the spirit.

34 Say: People! Do you flee from the power of God? By God, today you have no escape, nor does anyone have a protector except those to whom God shows mercy through His grace. He is indeed the Merciful, the Ever-Forgiving. Say: People! Leave what you possess, then enter the shadow of your Lord, the All-Merciful. This is better for you than whatever you have done or will do. Fear God, and do not deprive yourselves of the breezes of the days of God, the Possessor of names and attributes. Do not alter the word of God or displace it from its proper place. Be mindful of God and be among those who fear Him.

35 Say: People! This is the hand of God that has always been above your hands, if you understand. Within it We have ordained the good of the heavens and the earth, so that no good appears that does not issue from it. Thus We have made it the dawning place and treasury of good in all that has been and will be. Say: All the rivers of meaning and utterance that have flowed in the Tablets have joined this Most Great Ocean, if you perceive. Whatever has been set forth in detail in the Books has reached its culmination in this most exalted Word, which has shone from the horizon of the mouth of the will of the Most Glorious in this Revelation, through which the unseen and the visible have broken into a smile.

36 God will bring forth from the sheaths of power hands of strength and triumph. They will assuredly aid the Youth, cleanse the earth of the defilement of every rejected idolater, arise for the Cause, and conquer the lands through My name, the All-Powerful, the Self-Subsisting. They will enter the midst of the dwellings, and dread of them will seize all people. This is an act of God’s might; His grasp is indeed severe in its justice. He encompasses all who are in the heavens and on earth and sends down whatever He wills in a determined measure.

37 Were one of these to stand against all that has been brought into being in creation, he would prevail through the ascendancy of My will. This is of My power, yet My creatures do not know. This is of My sovereignty, yet My creation does not understand. This is of My command, yet My servants do not perceive. This is of My ascendancy, yet people give no thanks, except those whose eyes God has illumined with the light of His recognition, whose hearts He has made treasuries of His revelation, and whose selves He has made bearers of His Cause. They discern the fragrances of the All-Merciful from the garment of His name, and at all times they rejoice in the verses of God. But those who have disbelieved and associated partners with God have incurred His wrath. They will be dragged into the fire and then cry out in anguish in its depths. Thus We set forth the verses in detail and make the truth clear through evident proofs, so that people may reflect on the verses of their Lord.

38 O Temple! We have made you the sign of My glory amid all that has been and will be, and We have made you the sign of My command between the heavens and the earth through My saying, “Be,” and it is.

39 O H of Essence in this name! We have made you the treasury of My will and the hidden repository of My purpose for all in the kingdom of command and creation, as a grace from One who is All-Protecting, Self-Subsisting.

40 O Y of My name, the Omnipotent! We have made you the manifestation of My sovereignty and the dawning place of My names, and I have the power to accomplish whatever I say.

41 O K of My name, the Generous! We have made you the dawning place of My generosity among My creatures and the wellspring of My bounty amid My creation. I am the All-Powerful through My sovereignty. Nothing created between the heavens and the earth will escape My knowledge, and I am the Truth, the Knower of all unseen things.

42 Send down from the clouds of your generosity what will enrich all contingent beings. Do not withhold your grace from existence. You are the Generous in the dominion of eternity and the possessor of immense grace for all in the kingdom of names. Do not look at people and what they possess; look instead at the beauty of your beneficence and the wonders of your gifts. Bring the servants beneath your outstretched shadow. Extend the hand of bounty over all contingent beings and the fingers of generosity over all created things. This is what befits you, yet people do not understand. If anyone turns toward you, this is through your grace; and if anyone turns away, your Lord is indeed independent of all that has been created in the contingent world. His sincere servants bear witness to this.

43 Through you God will raise up triumphant hands and conquering arms. They will come forth from behind the veils, aid the All-Merciful Himself amid the contingent world, and raise a cry through which hearts will be distinguished from one another. Thus has it been inscribed in a written Tablet. They will appear with such might that fear will seize the inhabitants of the earth and all will tremble.

44 Beware of shedding blood. Draw the sword of the tongue from the scabbard of utterance, for through it the cities of hearts are conquered. We have abolished the ordinance of killing among you. My mercy has preceded all contingent beings, if you know. Then aid your Lord, the All-Merciful, with the sword of clear exposition. It is sharper than utterance and more precious than it, if you reflect on the words of your Lord. Thus have the hosts of revelation been sent down from God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting, and the hosts of inspiration appeared from the dawning place of the Cause, from God, the Almighty, the Beloved.

45 Say: The measures of all things have been ordained in this hidden yet visible Temple, and within it have been treasured the knowledge of the heavens and the earth and the knowledge of all that has been and will be. The finger of your Lord’s handiwork has inscribed in this Book what those endowed with knowledge are powerless to comprehend. Within it have been created temples of which no one but God Himself has knowledge, if you are certain. Blessed is the one who reads it, reflects on it, and is among those who understand.

46 Say: Nothing is seen in My temple but the Temple of God, nor in My beauty but His beauty, nor in My being but His being, nor in My essence but His essence, nor in My movement but His movement, nor in My stillness but His stillness, nor in My pen but His Pen, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. Say: Within My Self there has been none but the Truth, and in My essence none is seen but God.

47 Beware of speaking of two signs in My Self. The atoms proclaim that there is no God but Him, the One, the Unique, the Almighty, the All-Loving. From all eternity I have proclaimed in the dominion of eternity, “I am God; there is no God but Me, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.” And I will forever proclaim in the kingdom of names, “I am God; there is no God but Me, the Almighty, the Beloved.” Say: Lordship is My name, for which I have created manifestations in the world. We have ever been sanctified above it, if you bear witness. And Divinity is My name, for which We have appointed dawning places that encompass the servants and make them servants of God, if you are certain. Understand all the names in this way, if you understand.

48 O L of grace in this name! We have made you the manifestation of grace between the heavens and the earth. From you We began to bestow grace among contingent beings, and to you We return it; then from you We manifest it once again by Our command. I do whatever I will through My saying, “Be,” and it is. Every grace that has appeared in the world originated from you and returns to you. This is what has been ordained in a Tablet that We have preserved behind the pavilion of grandeur and protected from the sight of eyes. How blessed is the one who has not deprived himself of this grace, sent forth in unbroken succession!

49 Say: Today the fertilizing winds of grace have blown over all things, and everything has conceived according to its nature, yet people turn away from it. The trees have conceived wondrous fruits, the seas radiant pearls, the human being meanings and understanding, created things the effulgences of the All-Merciful, and the earth what no one knows but the Truth, the Knower of all unseen things. Soon they will each bring forth their burden. Blessed is God, who sends forth this grace that has encompassed all things, both manifest and hidden. Thus We have created all things anew on this day, yet most people do not perceive. Say: The grace of God can never be known as it truly is; how much less His own Self, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting!

50 O Temple of the Cause! If you find no one turning toward your gifts, do not grieve. You have been created for My own Self. Occupy yourself with My remembrance among My servants. This is what has been ordained for you in a preserved Tablet. Since We found the hands on earth unclean, We have kept the hem of your robe pure from their touch and from the touch of those who associate partners with God. Be patient in the Cause of your Lord. God will raise up pure hearts and radiant eyes that will hasten from every direction toward your all-encompassing, outspread grace.

51 O Temple of God! When the hosts of revelation were sent down with the banners of verses from the Sovereign of names and attributes, those versed in allusions fled, rejected the clear proofs of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting, and rose up in hypocrisy. Among them was one who said, “These verses are not clear proofs from God and were not revealed through innate inspiration.” In this way those who associate partners with God treat the wound in their breasts. For this, all who are in the heavens and on earth curse them, yet they themselves remain unaware.

52 Say: The Holy Spirit was created through a single letter of what has been sent down from this Most Great Spirit, if you understand. Innate inspiration, in its very essence, was created from the verses of God, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the Beloved. Say: It takes pride in its relationship to Our own Self, the Truth, but We take no pride in it or in anything besides it, for all that is other than Me was created through My word, if you would understand.

53 Say: We have revealed the verses in nine modes, each of which attests to the sovereignty of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. A single one of these modes is sufficient as proof for all who are in the heavens and on earth, yet most people are heedless. If We wished, We would reveal them in other modes whose number no reckoners could count.

54 Say: O people, fear God, and do not move your lying tongues to utter what God does not love. Feel shame before the One who created you from a drop of water, as you know. Say: We created all who are in the heavens and on earth according to God’s pattern. Whoever turns toward this Face will manifest the nature in which he was created, while whoever veils himself will be veiled from this all-encompassing, hidden grace. We have withheld grace from nothing. We created all things on an equal footing and, through a word from Us, offered them the trust of Our love. Whoever bore it was saved and secure and numbered among those who are safe from the terror of this Day. Whoever turned away disbelieved in God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Through it We have distinguished between the servants and separated them from one another. We are indeed the One who separates.

55 Say: The Word of God can never be confused with the words of His creatures. It is the sovereign of words, just as His own Self is the sovereign of all souls, and His Cause rules over all that has been and all that will be. Enter, O people, the city of certitude, the seat of the throne of your Lord, the All-Merciful. This is what the Pen of the All-Glorious commands you, as a grace bestowed upon you from His presence, if you will not dispute His Cause.

56 Among those who associate partners with God is one who disbelieved in his heart, rose up in opposition, and said, “These verses are fabrications.” So spoke the people who passed away before them, and now they cry for help in the Fire. Say: Woe to you for what comes from your mouths! If the verses are fabrications, by what proof have you believed in God? Bring it forth, if you understand. Whenever We sent down clear verses to them, they rejected them, and when they saw that the like of which all humanity was powerless to produce, they said, “This is sorcery.”

57 What is wrong with these people that they say what they do not know? So spoke the people of the Qur’an when God came with His Cause. They are indeed a people who deny. They prevented people from entering the presence of the Ancient Beauty and eating with His loved ones. One of them said, “Do not approach these people. They bewitch people and lead them astray from the path of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.” By God, the Truth! The one who cannot speak in Our presence says what those of former times never said and has committed what no one who disbelieved in the All-Merciful in any age ever committed.

58 Their words and deeds bear witness to this, if you judge fairly. Whoever attributes the verses of God to sorcery has not believed in any of God’s Messengers. His efforts have gone astray in this vain life, and he is among those who say what they do not know. Say: O servant, fear God, who created and fashioned you, and do not neglect your duty to God. Then judge fairly within yourself and be among those who act justly. Those who have been given knowledge by God will surely find in their objections powerful proofs that refute them and establish this manifest Light. Say: Do you say what even those who associated partners with God did not say when a Reminder came to them from their Lord? Woe to you, company of the ignorant! How evil is what you do!

59 O Ancient Beauty! Leave those who associate partners with God and all they possess, and perfume all contingent beings with the remembrance of your Beloved, the Most High, the Most Great. Through His remembrance all beings are brought to life and the temples of the worlds are renewed. Say: He has taken His seat upon the throne of grandeur and majesty. Whoever wishes to behold His beauty—here He is! Blessed is God, who has appeared in this radiant, luminous Beauty. Whoever wishes to hear His melodies—they have risen from this wondrous, pearl-like mouth. To whoever wishes to be illumined by His lights, say: Come before the throne. This is what God has permitted you, as a grace from His presence to all the worlds.

60 Say: O people, We ask you for a word spoken with the utmost truthfulness, and We take God as a witness between Us and you. He is the protector of those who do good. Place yourselves before the throne, then speak fairly and be among the fair-minded. Does God have power over His Cause, or are you the ones who possess power? Is He free to choose for Himself, as you say, “He does whatever He wills and is not questioned about what He wills,” or are you the ones who choose, uttering these words merely in imitation, as your fathers uttered them in the days of the Messengers?

61 If He is free to choose for Himself, He has indeed made manifest the Manifestation of His Cause with verses that nothing in the heavens or on earth can withstand. He has appeared in a manner whose like has never appeared in creation, as you have seen and heard, when the Luminary of the horizons shone from the horizon of Iraq with manifest sovereignty. All matters ultimately rest upon the verses, and these are the verses of God, the King, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the All-Powerful. Apart from these, He has appeared with a Cause to whose sovereignty all contingent beings have acknowledged submission. None denies this except every sinful one who associates partners with God.

62 Say: O people, do you wish to conceal the beauty of the sun with the veils of your own selves, or prevent the Spirit from singing in this polished, luminous breast? Fear God, do not wage war against His own Self, and do not dispute with the One at whose command the letter Kaf was created and joined to its mighty pillar. Believe in the emissaries of God and His sovereignty, and then in God’s own Self and His grandeur. Do not follow those who disbelieved after believing and claimed for themselves a station according to their own desires. They are indeed among those who associate partners with God. Bear witness to what God has witnessed, so that the company of those brought near may be illumined by what comes from your mouths. Say: “We believe in what was sent down to the Messengers of God before, in what was sent down in truth to Ali, and in what is being sent down from the direction of a mighty throne.” Thus God teaches you through His bounty and grace. His grace has encompassed all the worlds.

63 O Foot of this Temple! We have created you from iron. Stand firm in the Cause of your Lord in such a way that the feet of the detached may stand firm on the path of your Lord, the Almighty, the All-Wise. Beware lest you be shaken by the storms of hatred and the tempests of these wretched ones. Remain steadfast in the Cause and be among the steadfast. We have raised you up through Our name, through which everyone endowed with steadfastness has stood firm, and through every one of Our most beautiful names between the heavens and the earth. From you We will raise up steadfast feet that will stand upon the path and never slip from it, even if armies equal to the hosts of the first and the last wage war against them. All grace is in Our grasp; We bestow it upon whomever We will among Our servants who are brought near. Thus We have favored you time and again, so that you may thank your Lord with a thanksgiving that will open the tongues of all contingent beings to give thanks to My own Self, the All-Merciful, the Merciful.

64 Arise for the Cause with power from Us and authority from Our presence, then convey to the servants what the Spirit of God, the King, the Unique, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, has conveyed to you. Say: O people, do you leave the True One behind you and invoke the one We created from a handful of clay? This is an injustice you do to yourselves, if you reflect on the signs of your Lord. Say: O people, purify your hearts and then your eyes, so that you may recognize your Creator in this holy, radiant robe. Say: This is indeed a divine Youth who has established Himself upon the throne of glory and appeared with the sovereignty of power and independence. He calls out between earth and heaven with His wondrous, sweetest call: “O people of all realms of being, why have you disbelieved in your Lord, the All-Merciful, and turned away from the beauty of the All-Glorious? By God, this is the concealed Unseen, risen from the dawning place of contingent being, and this is the beauty of the Beloved, shining from the horizon of this Paradise through the sovereignty of God, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the All-Conquering, the All-Powerful.”

65 O Temple of Holiness! We have made your breast smooth and free of the allusions of contingent things and sanctified it from the indications of created beings, so that the lights of My beauty may be imprinted upon it and the mirrors of the worlds may be reflected from it. Thus We have chosen you above all that has been created in the heavens and on earth, preferred you above all that has been ordained in the kingdom of revelation and creation, and set you apart for Myself. This is God’s grace to you, from this day until the day that will never end in His dominion and will endure as long as God, the King, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, endures. For the Day of God is His own Self when He appears in truth; night will never follow it, nor will any description define it, if you are among those who understand.

66 O Breast of this Temple! We have made all things mirrors of yourself and made you the mirror of My own Self. Shine, then, upon the breasts of contingent beings with the lights of your Lord that have shone upon you, so that He may purify them of limitations and allusions. Thus has the sun of wisdom shone from the horizon of the Pen of the Lord of Eternity. Blessed are those who discern. From you We have brought forth polished breasts, and to you We will surely return them, as a mercy from Us to you and to those brought near. Through you We will raise up pure breasts and radiant bosoms that will reflect nothing but My beauty and indicate nothing but the revelations of My face. They are the mirrors of My names among all created beings.

67 O Temple of Holiness! We have made your heart the treasury of the knowledge of all that has been and all that will be, and the dawning place of Our knowledge that We have ordained for the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth. All beings will surely draw abundantly from you and, through the wonders of your knowledge, attain recognition of God, the All-Powerful, the Most High, the Most Great. But My knowledge that pertains to My Essence has been known by no one; no soul knows it, and no one in all the worlds can bear it. Were a single word of it to appear, souls would be thrown into turmoil, the foundations of all things would vanish, and the feet of those who have attained maturity would slip.

68 We possess a knowledge of which, were We to impart a single word to created beings, all would become certain of the manifestation of God and His knowledge, discover the mysteries of all the sciences, and reach a station at which they would see themselves as having no need of the knowledge of the first and the last. We possess other branches of knowledge of which We cannot mention a single letter, nor can people bear to hear any mention of them. Thus have We informed you of the knowledge of God, the Knowing, the All-Aware. Were We to find receptive vessels, We would impart to them the treasures of inner meanings and teach them that of which a single letter encompasses all the worlds.

69 O Heart of this Temple! We have made you the dawning place of My knowledge and the manifestation of My wisdom between the heavens and the earths. From you We have brought forth the branches of knowledge, and to you We return them; then We will bring them forth from you once again. This is a promise from Us; We will assuredly fulfill it. From you We will raise up one endowed with wondrous sciences and another endowed with powerful arts, and through them We will reveal what has never entered the heart of any of Our servants. Thus We give whatever We will to whomever We will, take back from whomever We will what We have given him, and decree by Our command whatever We desire.

70 Say: Were We to shine upon the mirrors of all beings with the sun of Our loving care at one moment and withdraw from them the lights of Our revelations at another, We would have the power to do so. No one has the right to say “Why?” or “By what means?” For We do whatever We will, and We are not to be questioned about what We have done. None doubts this except those who associate partners with God and are steeped in doubt. Say: Our power cannot be restrained, nor Our decree suspended. We raise whomever We will to the dominion of glory and might, then, if We will, return him to the lowest of the low.

71 Do you imagine, O people of the earth, that if We raise someone to the Lote Tree beyond which there is no passing, My power and sovereignty over him are then removed? No, by My own Self! Rather, should We will, We would return him to dust in less than an instant. Consider the tree: We plant it in the gardens and water it with the water of Our loving care. Then, when it has grown tall, put forth green leaves, and borne the finest fruits, We send against it the shattering winds of Our command and leave it lying upon the ground. Thus have We acted, and thus do We deal with all things. This is among Our wondrous ways with all things, before and hereafter, if you are among those who see. No one knows the wisdom of this except God, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the All-Wise.

72 Do you deny, O people, what you see? Woe to you, O company of deniers! The One who will never change is His own Self, the All-Merciful, the Merciful, if you are among those endowed with insight. All besides Him changes through His will, and He is the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the All-Wise.

73 O people, do not speak concerning Me, for you cannot attain the wisdom of your Lord, nor reach His mighty, all-encompassing knowledge. Whoever claims to know His Essence is among the most ignorant of people. Every atom gives the lie to him, and My truthful, trustworthy tongue bears witness to this. Make mention of My Cause, then speak of it and of what you have been commanded by Us. Anything beyond this does not befit you, nor does anyone have a path to it, if you are among those who listen.

74 O Temple! We have made you the dawning place of each of Our most beautiful names, the manifestation of each of Our most exalted attributes, and the source of every mention of Us for those on earth and in heaven. Then We raised you up in My image between the heavens and the earth and made you the sign of My glory for those in the dominion of revelation and creation, so that My servants may be guided by you and be among the rightly guided. We have made you the Lote Tree of bounty for those in the heavens and on earth. Blessed is the one who takes shelter in your shade and draws near to your Self, which keeps watch over all the worlds.

75 Say: We have made every name a wellspring, and from them We have caused rivers of wisdom and recognition to flow through the gardens of the Cause. No one knows their number except your Lord, the Most Holy, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. Say: We originated all the letters from the Point and returned them to it, then raised them up in a human temple. Exalted is the Maker, the Most Wondrous, the Incomparable! Once again We will unfold them through My name, the All-Glorious, as a grace from Me. I am the Most Bountiful, the Ancient.

76 We caused the lights to shine from the sun of Our name, the True One, returned them to it, and manifested them in the human temple. Exalted is the Powerful, the All-Compelling, the All-Powerful! No one can prevent Me from accomplishing My Cause, nor can any soul bar Me from My sovereignty and power. I am the One who brought contingent beings into existence through My word, and I have power to do whatever I desire.

77 Say: Were We to desire to withdraw the spirits from all things in a single breath and bring them forth from them once again, We would have the power to do so. No one possesses knowledge of this except God, the Knowing, the All-Knowing. Were We to desire to bring forth from an atom suns that have neither beginning nor end, We would have the power to do so, and would manifest them all at My command in less than an instant. Were We to desire to bring forth from a drop the oceans of the heavens and the earth, and unfold from a single letter the knowledge of all that has been and all that will be, We would have the power to do so. This is indeed easy and simple. Thus have I been powerful from the beginning that has no beginning to the end that has no end. Yet My creatures have remained heedless of My power, turned away from My sovereignty, and disputed with My own Self, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

78 Say: Nothing moves between the heavens and the earth except with My permission, and no soul ascends to the kingdom except at My command. Yet My creatures have been veiled from My power and sovereignty and have remained among the heedless. Say: Nothing is seen in My manifestation but the manifestation of God, and nothing in My power but the power of God, if you are among those who understand. Say: My creatures are like the leaves upon a tree: they are manifest through its existence and sustained by its very being, yet remain heedless of their root. Thus have We set forth a likeness for Our servants who understand, so that they may rise above the rank of plants and attain the station of maturity in this firmly established, steadfast Cause. Say: They are like a fish in the water: its life depends upon it, yet it does not recognize what sustains its life from the Almighty, the All-Wise. It is so veiled from it that, if asked about the water and its qualities, it would not know. Thus do We set forth likenesses, so that people may turn toward the Point of Adoration of all who are in the heavens and the earths.

79 People, fear God and do not disbelieve in Him whose mercy has encompassed all contingent things, whose grace has preceded all created things, and the sovereignty of whose Cause has embraced your outward and inward being, your first and your last. Be mindful of God and be among the God-fearing. Beware of being like those who fail to recognize the verses of God as they pass before them. They are indeed among the heedless.

80 Say: Do you worship one who neither hears nor sees and who is the most contemptible and most astray of servants? Why, then, do you not follow Him who has come from the dawning-place of the Cause with tidings of God, the Most High, the Most Great? People, do not be like those who entered the presence of the Throne without perceiving it. They are indeed among the abased. We were reciting to them the verses that enraptured the people of the realm of divine power and the inhabitants of the Kingdom, yet they returned veiled from them, awaiting the call of one of the servants whose life depended on a will proceeding from God. Thus We impart to you what will guide you to the path of those brought near.

81 How many servants entered the precinct of Paradise, the seat of the Throne, into the presence of their Lord, the Most High, the Most Great, and asked about the four Gates or one of the Imams of the Qur’an! Such was their conduct, if you are among those who know. Likewise, you see in these days that those who have disbelieved and associated partners with God have clung to one of the names and remained veiled from the One who brought them into being. We testify that they are among the people of the blazing Fire. They ask the Sun what the shadow has said, and the Truth what creation has uttered, if you are among those who witness. Say: People, the Sun has nothing but its radiance and what appears from it; everything else has been illumined by its light. Be mindful of God and do not be among the ignorant. Among them are those who have asked darkness about the light. Say: Open your eyes so that you may see the radiance that has encompassed the horizons. It can indeed be seen with the eye. This is a light that has risen and shone from the horizon of the dawn of inner meanings with manifest splendor. Do you ask the Jews whether the Spirit was truly from God, or the idols whether Muhammad was a Messenger, or the people of the Qur’an about the Remembrance of God, the Most High, the Most Great?

82 Say: People, set aside what you possess when this Revelation shines forth, and take hold of what you have been commanded. This is God’s command to you; He is indeed the best of those who command. By My beauty! In those words I did not mean Myself, but the One who will come after Me, and God is indeed a knowing witness to this. Do not treat Him as you have treated Me. When the verses of God are sent down to you from the direction of My grace, do not say, “They have not been revealed spontaneously.” Innate nature itself was created by My word and circles around Me, if you are among those who are certain. Inhale from the utterance of your Lord, the All-Merciful, the fragrances of the garment of inner meanings. They have spread through all worlds, and the realm of existence has been perfumed by them. Blessed is the one who has perceived their fragrance and turned to God with a radiant heart.

83 O Temple! We have made You a mirror of the kingdom of names, so that You may reflect My sovereignty among all created beings, summon people to My presence and My beauty, and guide them to My clear and straight path. We have exalted Your name among the servants as a grace from Us, and I am the Ever-Bountiful, the Ancient. We have adorned You with the ornament of My own Self and imparted My word to You, so that You may rule throughout creation as You wish and do whatever You desire. We have ordained for You the good of the heavens and the earth, so that no one may have any share of good unless they enter Your shadow—a decree from Your Lord, the All-Knowing, the All-Aware. We have given You the rod of authority and the criterion of judgment, so that You may distinguish every matter of wisdom. We have caused the oceans of inner meanings and utterance to surge within Your breast in remembrance of Your Lord, the All-Merciful, so that You may give thanks to Your Lord and be among the thankful. We have singled You out among My creatures and made You the Manifestation of My own Self throughout the heavens and the earths.

84 Raise up, by Our permission, reflecting mirrors and exalted letters, so that they may reflect Your sovereignty and power, bear witness to Your might and grandeur, and be manifestations of Your names among all peoples. We have made You the source of the mirrors and their originator, just as We brought them forth from You the first time; and We return You to My own Self, just as We brought You forth. Your Lord is indeed the All-Conquering, the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent. Tell the mirrors, when they appear, not to exalt themselves over the One who brought them into being and created them when He appears, and not to let leadership beguile them into failing to humble themselves before God, the Almighty, the All-Beautiful.

85 Say: You mirrors have been created by My command and raised up by My will. Beware of disbelieving in the verses of My Lord and becoming like those who have done wrong and are among the losers, or of clinging to what you possess and boasting of the exaltation of your names. You should detach yourselves from all who are in the heavens and on earth. Thus has it been ordained by One who is All-Powerful, Omnipotent.

86 O Temple of My Cause! Say: If I wished to make all things mirrors of My names in less than an instant, I would have the power to do so. How much more, then, My Lord, who created Me through His irrevocable and mighty command! And if I wished to transform all contingent things in less than the blink of an eye, I would have the power to do so. How much more, then, the purpose treasured within the Will of God, My Lord and the Lord of all worlds!

87 Say: Manifestations of My names! If you were to strive in the path of God with your possessions and your lives, and worship God as many times as there are grains of sand on earth, drops of rain, and waves of the seas, yet oppose the Manifestation of the Cause when He appears, your deeds would have no standing before God. But if you were to leave such deeds undone and believe in Him in those days, God might pardon your sins. He is indeed the Almighty, the All-Bountiful. Thus God teaches you what is intended, so that you may not exalt yourselves over Him through whom all that was sent down in the eternity of eternities has been confirmed. Blessed is the one who draws near to the Most Great Countenance, and away with those who turn aside!

88 How many servants spend their wealth in the path of God, yet at the time of His appearance We find them among those who turn away! How many servants fast by day, yet oppose Him by whose command the law of fasting was established! They are indeed among the ignorant. How many servants eat barley bread, sit upon what grows from the earth, and bear hardships to preserve their positions of leadership! Thus We have set out their deeds for you, so that this may be a reminder to others. They bear hardships to be seen by people, to perpetuate their names, although these will endure only through that for which those in the heavens and the earths will curse them.

89 Say: If your names endure as you imagine, will this benefit you in any way? No, by the Lord of all worlds! Has ‘Uzza gained honor through the perpetuation of its name among those who worship names? No, by the very Self of God, the Almighty, the Omnipotent! And if no one on earth mentions you, but God is pleased with you, then you are within the treasuries of His name, the Hidden. Thus We have sent down the verses to draw you toward the dawning-place of lights and enable you to know what your Lord, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise, has intended. Restrain yourselves from what you have been forbidden in the Book, eat of the lawful things God has provided for you, and do not deprive yourselves of His blessings. He is indeed the All-Bountiful, the Possessor of immense grace. Do not impose hardships upon yourselves. Do what We have made clear to you through evident proofs and shining verses, and do not be among the heedless.

90 Company of learned ones! If you abstain from wine and similar things forbidden to you in the Book, this is no cause for pride, for indulging in them would destroy your standing among people, alter your circumstances, and expose what you conceal. Rather, honor lies in your submission to the Word of Truth and your detachment, in private and in public, from everything but God, the Almighty, the Omnipotent. Blessed is the learned one who has not made knowledge a veil between himself and the Object of knowledge, and who, when the Self-Subsisting comes, turns toward Him with a radiant face. He is indeed among the learned; the people of Paradise will seek blessings from his breath, and those in the heavens and the earths will seek illumination from his lamp. He is among the heirs of the Prophets. Whoever has seen him has seen the Truth, and whoever has turned toward him has turned toward God, the Almighty, the All-Wise.

91 Dawning-places of knowledge! Beware of allowing yourselves to become corrupted, for through your corruption most people will become corrupted. This is an injustice you commit against yourselves and against the servants, as everyone with knowledge and understanding will testify. You are like a spring: when it is polluted, the rivers branching from it become polluted. Be mindful of God and be among the God-fearing. Likewise, when a person’s heart becomes corrupt, the limbs become corrupt; and when a tree’s root becomes corrupt, its branches, twigs, leaves, and fruits become corrupt. Thus We have set forth these comparisons for you, so that what you possess may not veil you from what has been ordained for you by One who is Almighty, All-Bountiful.

92 If We were to take a handful of dust and adorn it with the ornament of the names, We would have the power to do so. This would be My grace bestowed upon it, without any merit on its part. Thus has it been sent down in truth by an All-Knowing Revealer. Consider the Black Stone, which God has made the point toward which all peoples turn. Does this distinction come from the stone itself? No, by My own Self! Does this honor come from its own essence? No, by My Essence, which all who are in the worlds have been powerless to comprehend!

93 Likewise, consider the Farthest Mosque and the places We have made centers of pilgrimage for those in every region and land. Their honor does not come from themselves, but from their association with Our Manifestations, whom We have made the dawning-places of Our revelation among the servants, if you are among those who know. In all this there is a wisdom known only to God. Ask Him to explain to you what He has intended; He knows all things. Detach yourselves, people, from the world and its glitter, and pay no attention to those who have disbelieved and associated partners with God. Rise above the horizon of utterance to remember your Lord, the All-Merciful. This is what God has desired for you. Blessed are those who recognize Him.

94 Say: People, We commanded you in the Tablets to sanctify yourselves, at the time of the Revelation, from names and from everything created between earth and heaven, so that the radiance of the Sun of Truth, shining from the horizon of the will of your Lord, the Almighty, the Magnificent, might be imprinted upon your souls. We commanded you to purify your souls of love for those on earth and hatred of them, lest anything hold you back from one direction and compel you toward another. This was among My greatest counsels to you in a clear Book. Whoever clings to either cannot recognize the Cause as it truly is. Every fair-minded and discerning person bears witness to this.

95 You forgot God’s pledge and broke His covenant, until you turned away from the One whose appearance brought joy to the eyes of those who affirm His unity. Purify your vision of veils and coverings, then consider the proofs of the Prophets and Messengers, so that you may recognize the Cause of God in these days when the Promised One has come with mighty authority. Be mindful of God and do not deprive yourselves of the Dawning-place of the verses. This is what will benefit your souls. Your Lord has no need of any of the worlds. He has always existed, and nothing existed with Him. In His name the banner of divine unity has been raised upon the Sinai of existence, both unseen and visible, proclaiming: “There is no God but Me, the One, the Almighty, the Peerless.”

96 Those who were created through a will proceeding from Him and raised up at His command have turned away from Him and taken for themselves a lord other than God. Truly, they are among those who are cast far away. They used to remember the All-Merciful at all times, yet when He appeared in truth, they waged war against Him. Shame on them for breaking the covenant when the Luminary of the horizons shone from the horizon of the will of God, the Holy, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. They drew the swords of hatred against the face of God, yet have no awareness within themselves, as though they were dead in the graves of their desires, even after the breeze of God had wafted through the lands. Truly, they are wrapped in a mighty veil. When the verses of God are recited to them, they persist in arrogance, as though they knew nothing and had not heard the melody of God, the Most High, the All-Knowing.

97 Say: Alas for you! Do you claim faith for yourselves while disbelieving in the verses of God, the Almighty, the All-Knowing? Say: People, turn your faces toward your Lord, the All-Merciful. Beware lest what was sent down in the Bayān veil you. It was sent down only for My mighty, unassailable remembrance, and its sole purpose was My Beauty. The horizons have been filled with My proof, if only you were fair-minded.

98 If the Primal Point were, as you suppose, other than Me and were to attain My presence, He would never part from Me and would find companionship in My Self, and I would find companionship in His self in My days. He lamented His separation from Me. He preceded Me to announce to the people the glad tidings of My Kingdom. Thus it was sent down in the Tablets, if you are among those who look. If only there were someone with hearing to hear His lament in the Bayān over what has befallen Me at the hands of these heedless people, and to recognize His yearning in separation from Me and His ardent desire to attain My mighty, wondrous presence. Then he would behold His Beloved among the servants who were created for His days and to bow down before Him, in such abasement that the Pen has confessed its inability to describe it, because of what has befallen Him at the hands of these wrongdoers.

99 Say: People, in the first Revelation We called you to the Most Great Vision, this most pure station, and announced to you the glad tidings of the days of God. Yet when the greatest veil was torn apart and the Ancient Beauty came upon the clouds of destiny, you disbelieved in the One in whom you had believed. Woe to you, you who associate partners with God! Fear God and do not refute the truth with what you possess. When the Sun of the verses shines upon you from the horizon of the finger of the Sovereign of names and attributes, fall on your faces in prostration before God, the Lord of the worlds. Your prostration at the threshold of His gate is indeed better than the worship of both humankind and jinn, and your humility at His appearance is better for you than whatever has been created in the heavens and the earths.

100 Say: People, I admonish you for the sake of God and desire no reward from you. My reward rests only with God, who created Me, raised Me up in truth, and made Me a remembrance for all creatures. Hasten to the place of God’s manifestation and His dwelling, and do not follow Satan within yourselves. He commands you to commit injustice and indecency and bars you from the path established in the world through this irrevocable, wise Command.

101 Say: Satan has appeared in a manner whose like has never appeared in the realm of existence. Likewise, the Beauty of the All-Merciful has appeared in an adornment the like of which the eyes of earlier generations have never beheld. The call of the All-Merciful has been raised, and behind it the call of Satan. Blessed is whoever has heard the call of God and turned toward the Throne, a noble seat of holiness. Anyone whose heart contains even less than a mustard seed of love for anyone other than Me will never be able to enter My Kingdom. My proof is that with which the brocade of the Book of Existence has been embroidered, if you are among those who understand. Say: Today is the day when the greatest grace has appeared, and there is nothing, either in the heavens above or in the earths below, that does not speak My remembrance and sing My praise, if you are among those who hear.

102 Temple of Revelation, blow the trumpet in My name. Then, Temple of Mysteries, breathe into the flute in remembrance of your Lord, the Chosen One. Then, Maiden of Paradise, come out from the chambers of the gardens and announce to the inhabitants of all realms: “By God, the Beloved of the worlds has appeared, the One sought by those who recognize Him, the One worshipped by all who are in the heavens and the earths, before whom the first and the last bow down.”

103 Beware of hesitating over this Beauty after He has appeared with the sovereignty of might, power, and majesty. He is indeed the Truth, and everything besides Him is as nothing before even one of His servants and vanishes at the appearance of His lights. Hasten to the river of grace and do not be among those who wait. Whoever hesitates for less than a moment, God will surely nullify his deeds and return him to the abode of wrath. How wretched is the dwelling of those who hesitate!
