The Kitab-i-Aqdas (The Most Holy Book)

Translated on 9 Qudrat, 183 BE using GPT 6.1 Sol

1 In His name, the Ruler over all that has been and all that will be.

2 The first duty God has prescribed for His servants is to recognize the Dayspring of His revelation and the Dawning Place of His Cause, who represents His own Self in the world of command and creation. Whoever attains this has attained all good, and whoever is deprived of it is among those who have gone astray, even if he performs every deed. Once you have attained this most exalted station and highest horizon, every soul must follow what has been commanded by the Desired One, for these two belong together: neither is accepted without the other. This is what the Dawning Place of inspiration has decreed.

3 Those endowed by God with insight see God’s laws as the greatest means of ordering the world and safeguarding its peoples. Whoever is heedless belongs among the ignorant rabble. We have commanded you to break the bounds of self and desire, not what has been inscribed by the Most Exalted Pen. It is indeed the spirit of life for all who dwell in the realm of existence. The oceans of wisdom and utterance have surged as the breeze of the All-Merciful has stirred. Seize this opportunity, people of understanding. Those who have broken God’s covenant concerning His commandments and turned back on their heels are among those who have gone astray in the sight of the Self-Sufficient, the Most Exalted.

4 People of the earth, know that My commandments are the lamps of My loving care among My servants and the keys to My mercy for My creatures. Thus has the command been sent down from the heaven of the will of your Lord, the Master of religions. If anyone were to taste the sweetness of the utterance that has issued from the mouth of the will of the All-Merciful, he would expend all he possesses, even if it were all the treasures of the earth, to establish a single one of His commandments that shine from the horizon of loving care and favor.

5 Say: From My laws comes the fragrance of My garment, and through them the banners of victory are raised upon peaks and hills. The Tongue of My power has spoken in the dominion of My grandeur, addressing My creatures: “Observe My laws out of love for My beauty.” Blessed is the lover who has caught the fragrance of the Beloved from this utterance, from which the breezes of grace have wafted in a manner beyond description. By My life, whoever drinks the choice wine of fairness from the hands of loving favor will circle around My commandments that shine from the horizon of creation.

6 Do not think that We have revealed mere laws to you. Rather, with the fingers of power and might, We have broken the seal of the sealed choice wine. What has been sent down by the Pen of revelation bears witness to this. Reflect, people of thought.

7 Obligatory prayer has been prescribed for you, consisting of nine units, offered to God, the Revealer of verses, at noon, in the morning, and in the late afternoon. We have exempted you from another number, as a decree in the Book of God. He is indeed the Ordainer, the All-Powerful, who freely chooses. When you wish to perform obligatory prayer, turn your faces toward My Most Holy presence, the sacred Station that God has made the center around which the Concourse on high circles, the point toward which the inhabitants of the cities of eternity turn, and the source of command for all in the earth and the heavens. At the setting of the Sun of truth and explanation, turn toward the place We have appointed for you. He is indeed the Almighty, the All-Knowing.

8 Everything has come into being through His irrevocable command. When the Sun of laws shines from the horizon of utterance, everyone must follow them, even if a command should cause the heavens of the hearts of religions to split apart. He does whatever He wills and is not questioned about what He wills. Whatever the Beloved decrees is indeed beloved, by the Lord of creation! Whoever has caught the fragrance of the All-Merciful and recognized the Dawning Place of this utterance will face arrows with his own eyes to establish the laws among humanity. Blessed is the one who has turned toward Him and attained the decisive word.

9 He reads what was revealed before it; otherwise, God has pardoned him. [corrupted original text] We have set out the obligatory prayer in detail on another sheet. Blessed is the one who acts according to what he has been commanded by the Master of all people. Six repetitions of “God is Most Great” have been revealed for the prayer for the dead by God, the Revealer of verses, and the one who knows how to read [corrupted original text] the Almighty, the All-Forgiving.

10 Hair does not invalidate your prayer, nor does anything devoid of life, such as bones and the like. Wear sable as you wear silk, squirrel fur, and other materials. He did not forbid this in the Qur’an, but the scholars misunderstood. He is indeed the Almighty, the All-Knowing.

11 Prayer and fasting have been made obligatory for you from the onset of maturity, as a command from God, your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers. Whoever is weakened by illness or old age has been exempted by God as a grace from Him. He is indeed the All-Forgiving, the Generous. God has permitted you to prostrate yourselves on any clean surface, and We have removed from the Book the restriction that applied to it. God knows, and you do not know. Whoever cannot find water is to repeat five times, “In the name of God, the Most Pure, the Most Pure,” and then proceed with the act. This is what the Lord of the worlds has decreed. In lands where nights and days are long, they are to pray according to clocks and instruments by which the times are determined. He is indeed the Expounder, the All-Wise.

12 We have exempted you from the prayer of the signs. When they appear, remember God with grandeur and power. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. Say: “Grandeur belongs to God, Lord of all that is seen and unseen, Lord of the worlds.”

13 You have been required to perform obligatory prayer individually. The ordinance of congregational prayer has been abolished, except for the prayer for the dead. He is indeed the Ordainer, the All-Wise.

14 God has exempted women from fasting and obligatory prayer during menstruation. They are to perform ablutions and repeat ninety-five times, from one noon to the next, “Glory be to God, the Possessor of radiance and beauty.” This is what has been ordained in the Book, if you are among those who know.

15 For you and for women, when traveling, upon stopping and resting in a safe place, a single prostration is to replace each obligatory prayer. During it say, “Glory be to God, the Possessor of grandeur and majesty, bounty and grace.” Anyone unable to do this says, “Glory be to God”; this will indeed suffice. He is indeed the All-Sufficing, the Everlasting, the All-Forgiving, the Merciful. After completing the prostrations, you and women are to sit in the posture of unity and say eighteen times, “Glory be to God, the Possessor of the earthly and heavenly kingdoms.” Thus God makes clear the ways of truth and guidance, and they have converged in a single way: this straight path. Thank God for this great grace. Praise God for this bounty that has encompassed the heavens and the earth. Remember God for this mercy that has preceded all the worlds.

16 Say: God has made My hidden love the key to the treasure, if only you would recognize it. Without the key, it would have remained concealed for all eternity, if only you were certain. Say: This is indeed the Dawning Place of revelation and the Dayspring of radiance through which the horizons have been illumined, if only you knew. This is indeed the established decree, and through it every irrevocable decree has been established.

17 Most Exalted Pen, say: People of creation, We have prescribed fasting for you for a specified number of days and have made Naw-Rúz a festival for you upon their completion. Thus has the Sun of utterance shone from the horizon of the Book, from the Lord of the beginning and the return. Place the days in excess of the months before the month of fasting. We have made them manifestations of the letter Há among the nights and days; thus they have not been bounded by the limits of the year and the months. During them, the people of Bahá should provide food for themselves and their relatives, as well as the poor and needy, and with joy and gladness proclaim the oneness and greatness of their Lord, praise Him, and glorify Him. When the days of giving that precede abstinence have ended, let them enter the fast. Thus has the Lord of humanity decreed. Travelers, the sick, pregnant women, and nursing mothers are under no obligation. God has exempted them as a grace from Him. He is indeed the Almighty, the Bestower.

18 These are the laws of God inscribed by the Most Exalted Pen in the scriptures and Tablets. Hold fast to God’s commandments and ordinances, and do not be among those who have adopted principles of their own and cast God’s principles behind them, following conjectures and vain imaginings. Refrain from eating and drinking from sunrise to sunset. Beware lest desire deprive you of this grace ordained in the Book.

19 Everyone who believes in God, the Judge, has been required each day to wash his hands and then his face, sit facing God, and repeat ninety-five times, “God is Most Glorious.” Thus has the Creator of heaven decreed as He sat upon the thrones of names in grandeur and power. Likewise, perform ablutions for obligatory prayer, as a command from God, the One, who freely chooses.

20 Murder, adultery, backbiting, and slander have been forbidden to you. Avoid whatever you have been forbidden in the scriptures and Tablets.

21 We have divided inheritances according to the number of Zāʾ. Of these, your offspring have been allotted from the Book of Ṭāʾ according to the number of maqt; spouses, from the Book of Ḥāʾ according to the number of Tāʾ and Fāʾ; fathers, from the Book of Zāʾ according to the number of Tāʾ and Kāf; mothers, from the Book of Wāw according to the number of rafīʿ; brothers, from the Book of Hāʾ, the number of Shīn; sisters, from the Book of Dāl, the number of Rāʾ and Mīm; and teachers, from the Book of Jīm, the number of Qāf and Fāʾ. Thus decreed My Herald, who remembers Me in the night and at dawn. When We heard the clamor of the offspring still in their fathers’ loins, We doubled their share and reduced those of the others. He indeed has power over whatever He wills; through His sovereignty He does as He wishes.

22 If someone dies without offspring, their shares revert to the House of Justice, so that the trustees of the All-Merciful may spend them on orphans, widows, and whatever benefits the general public, that they may give thanks to their Lord, the Almighty, the Ever-Forgiving.

23 If someone has offspring but none of the other heirs specified in the Book, two-thirds of what he leaves revert to the offspring and one-third to the House of Justice. Thus has the Self-Sufficient, the Most Exalted, decreed in majesty and glory.

24 If someone has none to inherit from him but has relatives among the sons and daughters of his brother or sister, two-thirds go to them; otherwise, to his paternal and maternal uncles and aunts, and after them to their sons and daughters. One-third reverts to the Seat of Justice, as an ordinance in the Book from God, the Master of all people.

25 If someone dies leaving none of those whose names have been sent down by the Most Exalted Pen, all the property reverts to the aforementioned Seat, to be spent on what God has ordained. He indeed is the All-Powerful, the Supreme Ordainer.

26 We have assigned the residence and personal clothing to the male offspring, to the exclusion of the female offspring and other heirs. He indeed is the Giver, the Most Bountiful.

27 If someone dies during his father’s lifetime and leaves offspring, they inherit their father’s share as prescribed in the Book of God. Divide it among them with pure justice. Thus has the ocean of utterance surged and cast forth the pearls of ordinances from the Lord of all people.

28 If someone leaves offspring who are minors, entrust their property to a trustworthy person to trade on their behalf until they reach maturity or the age of partnership. Then assign the trustee a share of the proceeds from trade and earnings.

29 All this follows payment of the Right of God and any debts owed by the deceased, provision for shrouding and burial, and the conveyance of the deceased with dignity and honor. Thus has the Lord of the beginning and the return decreed.

30 Say: This is indeed the hidden knowledge that will never change, for it began with Ṭāʾ, which points to the treasured Name, the Manifest, the Inaccessible, the Unassailable. What We have set aside for the offspring is a grace bestowed on them by God, that they may thank their Lord, the All-Merciful, the Compassionate. These are God’s limits; do not transgress them at the prompting of your own desires. Follow what you have been commanded by the Dawning-Place of utterance. The sincere see God’s ordinances as the water of life for the followers of religions and the lamp of wisdom and salvation for all in the earth and the heavens.

31 God has prescribed that a House of Justice be established in every city, in which souls numbering the numerical value of Bahāʾ shall gather; if the number is greater, there is no objection. They should regard themselves as entering the presence of God, the Most High, the Most Exalted, and as seeing Him who cannot be seen. They should be the trustees of the All-Merciful among created beings and God’s representatives for all who dwell on earth. They should consult, for God’s sake, on the interests of His servants as they consult on their own affairs, and choose what is best. Thus has your Lord, the Almighty, the Ever-Forgiving, decreed. Beware of abandoning what is expressly stated in the Tablet. Be mindful of God, you who possess insight.

32 People of creation! Build houses throughout the lands in the name of the Lord of religions, as perfectly as is possible in the world of being, and adorn them with what befits them, not with images and statues. Then remember within them your Lord, the All-Merciful, with joy and gladness. Truly, through remembrance of Him hearts are illumined and eyes are cheered.

33 God has ordained pilgrimage to the House for those of you who are able, except women. God has exempted them as a mercy from Him. He indeed is the Giver, the All-Bountiful.

34 People of Bahāʾ! Every one of you is required to engage in an occupation, such as a craft, a trade, or the like. We have made your engagement in such work the very worship of God, the True One. Reflect, My people, on God’s mercy and favors, then thank Him in the evening and at daybreak. Do not waste your time in idleness and laziness, but occupy yourselves with what benefits you and others. Thus has the matter been decreed in this Tablet, from whose horizon the sun of wisdom and exposition has shone. The most detestable of people in God’s sight is one who sits and begs. Hold fast to the cord of means, placing your trust in God, the Originator of all means.

35 Kissing hands has been forbidden to you in the Book. This is what your Lord, the Almighty, the Supreme Judge, has prohibited you from doing. No one may seek forgiveness in the presence of another. Turn to God in repentance on your own. He indeed is the Forgiver, the Giver, the Almighty, the Ever-Relenting.

36 Servants of the All-Merciful! Arise to serve the Cause in such a way that those who disbelieve in the Dawning-Place of the verses cannot afflict you with sorrow. When the promise was fulfilled and the Promised One appeared, the people fell into disagreement, and every party clung to its own conjectures and imaginings.

37 Among the people is one who sits among the shoes in pursuit of the seat of honor. Say: Who are you, heedless deceiver? Among them is one who claims knowledge of the inner meaning and its innermost meaning. Say: Liar! By God, what you possess is only husks that We have left to you, as bones are left to dogs. By God, the True One, even if someone were to wash the feet of the whole world and worship God in thickets, ravines, mountains, peaks, and lofty summits, and beside every stone, tree, and clod of earth, yet the fragrance of My good pleasure did not waft from him, he would never be accepted. This is what the Lord of all people has decreed. How many a servant has withdrawn to the islands of India, denied himself what God has made lawful for him, and borne austerities and hardships, yet has not been remembered before God, the Revealer of the verses. Do not make your deeds a snare for your hopes, and do not deprive yourselves of this ultimate goal, which has been the hope of those brought near to God from all eternity. Say: The spirit of deeds is My good pleasure, and everything depends upon My acceptance. Read the Tablets, that you may know what is intended in the Books of God, the Almighty, the All-Bountiful. Whoever has attained My love is entitled to sit on a throne of pure gold in the seat of honor in the world of being. But if one who has been deprived of it should sit upon the dust, the dust would seek refuge from him with God, the Lord of religions.

38 Whoever claims a revelation before the completion of a full thousand years is a lying impostor. We ask God to help him turn back. If he repents, God is indeed the Ever-Relenting; but if he persists in what he has said, He will raise up against him one who will show him no mercy. He is indeed severe in punishment. Whoever interprets this verse or explains it contrary to its revealed, outward meaning is deprived of the Spirit of God and His mercy, which has encompassed all worlds. Fear God and do not follow your own imaginings. Follow what your Lord, the Almighty, the All-Wise, commands you. Clamor will soon arise from most lands. Shun it, My people, and do not follow any wicked, base person. This is what We told you when We were in Iraq, in the Land of Mystery, and in this radiant place.

39 People of the earth! When the sun of My beauty has set and the heaven of My person has been concealed, do not be troubled. Arise to champion My Cause and exalt My Word among the peoples of the world. We are with you in all circumstances and will assuredly aid you. We are indeed powerful. Whoever has recognized Me will arise to serve Me with such resolve that the hosts of the heavens and the earth cannot hold him back.

40 People are asleep. Were they to awaken, they would hasten in their hearts to God, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise, and cast away all they possess, even if it were all the treasures of the world, so that their Lord might remember them with a word from Him. Thus does He who possesses knowledge of the unseen inform you in a Tablet that has not appeared in the world of being and of which none but His own Self, the Guardian over all worlds, has knowledge. The intoxication of desire has so seized them that they do not see the Lord of creation, whose call has risen from every direction: “There is no God but Me, the Almighty, the All-Wise.”

41 Say: Do not rejoice in what you possess in the evening, for at dawn someone else will possess it. Thus the All-Knowing, the All-Informed informs you. Say: Have you found any permanence or faithfulness in what you possess? No, by My own Self, the All-Merciful, if you judge fairly. The days of your lives pass like the winds, and the carpet of your glory will be rolled up as the carpet of those before you was rolled up. Reflect, people: where are your past days, and where are your bygone ages? Blessed are the days spent remembering God and the hours devoted to remembering Him, the All-Wise. By My life, neither the glory of the mighty, nor the finery of the rich, nor the power of the wicked will endure. All will perish at a word from Him. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty, the Omnipotent. People’s possessions do not benefit them, while they neglect what does benefit them. Soon they will awaken and will not find what they missed in the days of their Lord, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. If they understood, they would spend all they possess so that their names might be mentioned before the Throne. Yet they are indeed among the dead.

42 Among the people is one whom learning has deluded, and through it [corrupted original text] My name, the Self-Subsisting. When he hears the sound of sandals behind him, he considers himself greater than Nimrod. Say: Where is he, you who have been rejected? By God, he is in the lowest depths of hell. Say: Scholars! Do you not hear the scratching of My Most Exalted Pen, and do you not see this Sun shining from the most glorious horizon? Why have you remained devoted to the idols of your desires? Abandon your imaginings and turn toward God, your ancient Lord.

43 Endowments dedicated to charitable purposes have reverted to God, the Revealer of signs. No one may dispose of them without permission from the Dawning Place of Revelation. After Him, authority passes to the Branches, and after them to the House of Justice, if it has been established in the lands, so that they may spend them on the places exalted in this Cause and on whatever they have been commanded by One who is All-Powerful and Omnipotent. Otherwise, they revert to the people of Bahá who speak only with His permission and judge only according to what God has decreed in this Tablet. These are the champions of victory between the heavens and the earth. They are to spend them as prescribed in the Book by One who is Almighty and Generous.

44 Do not despair in adversity, nor rejoice. Seek a course between the two: remembrance in that condition and awareness of what will come to you in the end. Thus the All-Knowing, the All-Informed instructs you.

45 Do not shave your heads. God has adorned them with hair, and in this are signs for anyone who considers the requirements of nature ordained by the Sovereign of creation. He is indeed the Almighty, the All-Wise. Hair should not extend beyond the ears. This is what the Lord of the worlds has decreed.

46 Exile and imprisonment have been prescribed for the thief. On the third offense, place a mark on his forehead by which he may be recognized, so that the cities and lands of God will not admit him. Do not let pity restrain you in carrying out God’s religion. Do what you have been commanded by One who is Compassionate and Merciful. We have trained you with the lashes of wisdom and laws to protect you and elevate your stations, just as fathers bring up their children. By My life, if you understood what We have intended for you through Our sacred commandments, you would sacrifice your lives for this holy, mighty, unassailable Cause.

47 Anyone who wishes to use vessels of gold and silver may do so. Take care not to plunge your hands into bowls and platters. Adopt what is most in keeping with refinement. He wishes to see you observe the manners of the people of Paradise in His inaccessible, unassailable Kingdom. Adhere to refinement in all circumstances, so that no eyes fall upon anything repugnant to you or to the people of Paradise. Whoever departs from it will have his deeds immediately brought to nothing; but if he has an excuse, God will pardon him. He is indeed the Almighty, the Generous.

48 The Dawning Place of the Cause has no partner in the Most Great Infallibility. He is indeed the manifestation of “He does whatever He wills” in the kingdom of creation. God has reserved this station for Himself and has allotted no one a share in this exalted, unassailable rank. This is God’s decree, formerly concealed behind the veils of the unseen. We have disclosed it in this Revelation, and through it We have torn apart the veil of those who did not understand the ruling of the Book and were among the heedless.

49 Every father is required to educate his son and daughter in knowledge and writing, and in the other subjects prescribed in the Tablet. If anyone neglects what he has been commanded, the trustees may take from him what is needed for their education if he is wealthy; otherwise, the matter devolves upon the House of Justice. We have made it a refuge for the poor and needy. Whoever educates his own child or another child is as though he had educated one of My children. Upon him rest My glory, My loving care, and My mercy, which has encompassed all worlds.

50 God has decreed that every adulterer and adulteress pay a fine to the House of Justice of nine mithqals of gold. If they repeat the offense, double the penalty. This is what the Sovereign of Names has decreed in this world; in the world to come He has ordained a humiliating punishment for them both. Anyone who commits a sin may repent and return to God. He forgives whomever He wills and is not questioned about what He wills. He is indeed the Ever-Forgiving, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy.

51 Do not let the veils of glory keep you from the pure water of this flowing spring. Take up the cups of salvation this morning in the name of Him who causes the dawn to break, then drink in remembrance of Him, the Almighty, the Incomparable.

52 We have made it lawful for you to listen to voices and melodies. Take care that listening does not lead you beyond the bounds of propriety and dignity. Rejoice in the joy of My Most Great Name, through which hearts have been enraptured and the minds of those brought near have been drawn in attraction. We have made it a ladder for souls to ascend to the highest horizon. Do not make it a wing for self and desire. I seek refuge from your being among the ignorant.

53 We have assigned a third of all fines to the seat of justice, and We counsel its members to practice pure justice, so that they may spend what has accumulated with them as they have been commanded by One who is All-Knowing and All-Wise. Members of the House of Justice! Be shepherds of God’s sheep in His kingdom, and protect them from the wolves that have appeared in disguise, just as you protect your own children. Thus the faithful Counselor advises you.

In My appearance there is a wisdom, and in My absence there is another wisdom, known to none but God, the One, the All-Informed. 55 People of the earth! By God, the Truth, sweet, refreshing rivers have burst from the rocks, so seized have they been by the sweetness of the utterance of your Lord, the Unconstrained; yet you remain heedless. Beware of excess in this matter. Follow the path of justice and fairness in all things. Thus the Dawning-Place of Revelation commands you, if you are among those who understand.

God has power over all things.

Otherwise, if he has a genuine excuse, he must inform his wife and make every effort to return to her. Follow My commandments, and do not follow any who associates partners with God and is recorded as sinful in the Tablet. If news arrives during her waiting period, she may do what is right. He desires reconciliation between His male and female servants. Beware of doing anything that creates hostility among you. Thus has the matter been decreed, and the promise is fulfilled. If news of his death or killing reaches her and is established by widespread report or by two just witnesses, she is to remain in the house. When the appointed months have passed, she is free to choose as she wishes. This is what He who is mighty in command has decreed.

69 If estrangement or aversion arises between them, he may not divorce her, but must wait a full year, so that perhaps the fragrance of love may spread between them. If the year is completed and that fragrance has not spread, there is no objection to divorce. He is wise in all things. In a Tablet inscribed by the Pen of Command, God has forbidden you what you formerly practiced after three divorces, as a favor from Him, so that you may be thankful. A man who has divorced his wife may choose to return to her at the end of any month, with affection and mutual consent, as long as she has not remarried. If she has remarried, their separation is confirmed by another union, and the matter is settled, unless a manifest change occurs. Thus has the decree been inscribed with majesty by the Dawning-Place of Beauty in the Tablet of Glory.

70 If a man travels and his wife travels with him, and disagreement then arises between them, he must provide her with maintenance for a full year and return her to the place from which she departed, or entrust her to a trustworthy person, together with whatever she needs for the journey, to convey her to her home. Your Lord decrees as He wills, with an authority encompassing all the worlds.

71 A woman divorced because of proven misconduct has no right to maintenance during her waiting period. Thus has the Sun of the Cause appeared above the horizon of justice. God loves union and harmony and detests separation and divorce. Associate with one another, O people, in joy and gladness. By My life, all who exist will pass away; what remains is the good deed. God bears witness to what I say. O My servants, set right your relations with one another, then listen to the counsel of the Most Exalted Pen, and do not follow any wretched tyrant.

Those who cast aside wrongdoing and waywardness and embrace reverence for God are among the finest of His creatures in the sight of the True One. The Concourse on high remembers them, as do the inhabitants of this station that has been exalted in God’s name.

No one may exalt himself above another. All are His bondservants and bear witness that there is no God but Him. He is wise in all things.

Would you kill someone whom God has brought to life through a spirit from Him? This is a grievous wrong before the Throne. This is indeed the crown of deeds, if you are among those who understand.

If what has now been sent down from God is engraved upon them, it is better for both men and women. We have indeed decreed: “I came from God and have returned to Him, detached from all but Him and holding fast to His name, the All-Merciful, the Compassionate.” Thus God singles out whomever He wills for grace from His presence. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty.

131 And wrap the deceased in five lengths of silk or cotton. Whoever cannot afford this may make do with one of either. Thus has the ordinance been decreed by One who is All-Knowing, All-Aware. You are forbidden to transport the deceased more than an hour’s journey from the city. Bury him with joy and fragrance in a nearby place.

132 God has lifted what the Bayán ordained concerning the restriction of [corrupted original text]. He is indeed free to choose: He does whatever He wills and ordains whatever He desires.

133 People of creation, hear the call of the Lord of Names. He calls to you from His Most Great Prison: “There is no God but Me, the All-Powerful, the Supremely Great, the One in service, the Most Exalted, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.” There is no God but Him, who has power over all worlds. If He wills, He can seize the world with a word from Himself. Beware of hesitating in this Cause, before which the Concourse on high and the inhabitants of the cities of names have bowed. Be mindful of God, and do not be among those who are veiled. Burn away the veils with the fire of My love, and the veils of glory with this Name through which We have subdued all worlds.

134 Raise up the two Houses in the two places, and the sites where the throne of your Lord, the All-Merciful, was established. Thus commands you the Lord of those who know.

135 Beware lest earthly affairs keep you from what you have been commanded by One who is Strong and Trustworthy. Be manifestations of steadfastness among humanity, so that the doubts of those who disbelieved in God when He appeared with mighty sovereignty cannot deter you. Beware lest what was revealed in the Book keep you from this Book, which declares the truth: “There is no God but Me, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy.” Look with fairness upon the One who has come from the heaven of will and power, and do not be among the unjust.

136 Then remember what flowed from the pen of My Herald concerning this Revelation, and what the tyrannical committed in His days. Truly, they are among the greatest losers. He said: “If you attain the presence of the One We shall manifest, ask of God’s grace that He may favor you by taking His seat in your innermost hearts, for that is an inaccessible, exalted honor. For Him to drink a cup of water in your presence is greater than for every soul—indeed, every thing—to drink the water of its own existence, if you understand, My servants.”

137 This is what was sent down from Him in remembrance of Me, if you only knew. Whoever reflects on these verses and discovers the treasured pearls concealed within them will, by God, perceive the fragrance of the All-Merciful from the direction of the prison and hasten toward Him in his heart with a longing that the armies of heaven and earth cannot restrain. Say: This is indeed a Revelation around which proof and testimony circle. Thus has the All-Merciful sent it down, if you judge fairly. Say: This is the spirit of the scriptures, breathed into the Most Exalted Pen, at which all creation has fallen thunderstruck, except those whom the breezes of My mercy and the fragrances of My loving-kindness, which reigns over all worlds, have embraced.

138 People of the Bayán, be mindful of the All-Merciful, and consider what He revealed elsewhere. He said: “The Qibla is none other than the One whom God will manifest. Whenever He moves, it moves, until He comes to rest.” Thus was it sent down by the Lord of Destiny when He wished to speak of this Most Great Vision. Reflect, people, and do not be among those who wander aimlessly. If you deny Him because of your desires, toward what Qibla will you turn, heedless ones? Reflect on this verse, and then, by God, judge fairly, so that you may find the pearls of mysteries in the ocean that has surged through My name, the Almighty, the Inaccessible.

139 Today no one may hold fast to anything but what has appeared in this Revelation. This is God’s decree, before and hereafter, and with it the scriptures of the ancients were adorned. This is God’s remembrance, before and hereafter, with which the brocade of the Book of Existence has been embroidered, if you perceive. This is God’s command, before and hereafter. Beware of being among the abased. Nothing will avail you today, and no one has any refuge except God, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. Whoever has recognized Me has recognized the One who is sought; whoever has turned toward Me has turned toward the One who is worshipped. Thus has it been set out in the Book, and the matter decreed by God, the Lord of all worlds. For anyone to read a single one of My verses is better than reading the books of the ancients and those who came after them. This is the utterance of the All-Merciful, if you listen. Say: This is true knowledge, if you understand.

140 Then consider what was revealed elsewhere, so that you may relinquish what you possess and turn toward God, the Lord of all worlds. He said: “Marriage is not lawful unless it is within the Bayán. If one of the two enters it, whatever that one possesses becomes unlawful to the other, unless the other returns, after the Cause of the One We shall manifest in truth, or what has appeared in justice, has been raised up. Before that, you must draw close, so that through this you may exalt the Cause of God.” Thus has the Dove sung upon the branches in remembrance of her Lord, the All-Merciful. Blessed are those who listen.

141 O people of the Bayán! I implore you by your Lord, the All-Merciful, to look with fairness upon what has been revealed in truth, and not to be among those who see the proof of God yet deny it. They are indeed among those who perish. In this verse, the Point of the Bayán explicitly declared that My Cause would be exalted before His own. Every fair-minded, knowledgeable person bears witness to this, just as you see today that it has been exalted to a degree that none deny except those whose sight is bewildered in this world; in the next, a humiliating punishment awaits them.

142 Say: By God, I am indeed His Beloved, and He now hears what descends from the heaven of revelation and laments what you have committed in His days. Fear God, and do not be among the transgressors. Say: O people! If you will not believe in Him, do not oppose Him. By God, the hosts of oppressors already arrayed against Him are enough.

143 He revealed certain laws so that, in this Revelation, the Most Exalted Pen would move only to mention His highest stations and His most radiant appearance. Yet when We desired to bestow Our bounty, We set them out in truth and lightened what We had intended for you. He is indeed the Bountiful, the Generous.

144 He previously informed you of what this wise Remembrance would proclaim. He said—and His word is the truth—“He proclaims in every circumstance: ‘There is no God but Me, the Single, the One, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed.’” This is a station God has reserved for this inaccessible, wondrous Revelation. This is from the bounty of God, if you are among those who recognize. This proceeds from His irrevocable command, His Greatest Name, His most exalted Word, and the Dawning-Place of His most beautiful names, if you are among those who know. Indeed, through Him the dawning-places and daysprings appear. Reflect, O people, on what has been revealed in truth, ponder it, and do not be among the transgressors.

145 Associate with the followers of all religions in joy and fellowship, so that they may perceive from you the fragrance of the All-Merciful. Beware lest the zealotry of ignorance seize you among the people. All have originated from God and return to Him. He is indeed the source of creation and the One to whom all worlds return.

146 Beware of entering a house in its owner’s absence without first obtaining permission. Adhere to what is right in all circumstances, and do not be among the heedless.

147 You are required to purify your provisions and other possessions through the payment of zakát. This is what the Revealer of the Verses has decreed in this exalted parchment. We will set out its minimum assessment for you when God wishes and wills. He sets out whatever He wishes through knowledge from His own presence. He is indeed the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

148 Begging is not permitted, and giving to one who begs is forbidden. Everyone is required to earn a living. For anyone unable to do so, it is for the trustees and the wealthy to provide what is sufficient. Observe God’s laws and ordinances, and guard them as you guard your own eyes. Do not be among those who suffer loss.

149 In the Book you have been forbidden to engage in argument, conflict, violence, and similar acts that bring sorrow to hearts and souls. Whoever causes another sorrow is to expend nineteen mithqáls of gold. This is what the Lord of all worlds has decreed. In this Revelation He has relieved you of that obligation and enjoins goodness and piety upon you as a command from Him in this luminous Tablet. Do not wish for anyone what you would not wish for yourselves. Fear God, and do not be among the arrogant. You were all created from water and will return to dust. Reflect on your ultimate fate, and do not be among the wrongdoers. Listen to the verses of God that the Lote Tree recites to you. They are indeed the standard of guidance from God, the Lord of the world to come and of this world. Through them souls soar to the Dawning-Place of revelation, and the hearts of those who turn toward Him are illumined. These are the laws of God that have been made binding upon you, and these are the commandments of God that you have been enjoined to follow in the Tablet. Observe them with joy and gladness. This is better for you, if you are among those who recognize.

150 Recite the verses of God every morning and evening. Whoever does not recite them has not fulfilled God’s covenant and pledge, and whoever turns away from them today is among those who have turned away from God from all eternity. Fear God, My servants, each and every one of you. Do not let abundant recitation and deeds by night and day delude you. For someone to read a single verse with joy and gladness is better than to read with weariness the scriptures of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Recite the verses of God to such an extent that weariness and sorrow do not overtake you. Do not burden souls with what wearies and weighs them down, but rather with what lightens them, so that they may soar on the wings of the verses to the Dawning-Place of clear proofs. This is nearer to God, if you understand.

151 Teach your children what has been revealed from the heaven of grandeur and power, so that they may recite the Tablets of the All-Merciful in beautiful melodies in the chambers built within the Dawning-Places of Remembrance. Whoever is seized by the rapture of love for My name, the All-Merciful, recites the verses of God in such a manner that the hearts of those who sleep are enraptured. Blessed is the one who has drunk the choice wine of life from the utterance of his Lord, the All-Merciful, through this Name by which every lofty, towering mountain has been scattered.

152 You are required to renew your household furnishings after nineteen years have elapsed. Thus has the matter been decreed by One who is All-Knowing, All-Informed. He desires refinement for you and for what you possess. Fear God, and do not be among the heedless. Whoever is unable to do so is excused by God. He is indeed the All-Forgiving, the Generous.

153 Wash your feet every day in summer, and once every three days in winter.

154 Meet anyone who is angry with you with gentleness, and do not rebuke anyone who rebukes you. Leave that person alone, and put your trust in God, the Avenger, the Just, the All-Powerful.

155 You have been forbidden to ascend pulpits. Anyone who wishes to recite the verses of his Lord to you should sit on a chair placed upon a platform and remember God, his Lord and the Lord of all worlds. God has wished you to sit on platforms and chairs, in honor of the love you bear for God and for the radiant, luminous Dawning-Place of His Cause.

156 Gambling and opium have been forbidden to you. Avoid them, O people of creation, and do not be among those who overstep the bounds. Beware of using anything that makes your bodies sluggish and harms them. We have desired for you only what benefits you. All things bear witness to this, if you listen.

157 When you are invited to feasts and festive gatherings, respond with joy and delight. Whoever keeps a promise is safe from the threat of punishment. This is a day on which every wise decree has been set out.

158 The mystery of the inversion in the symbol of the Chief has been revealed. Blessed is the one whom God has enabled to acknowledge the six raised up by this upright alif. He is indeed among the sincere. How many a devout worshipper has turned away, and how many a neglectful one has turned toward Him and said, “Praise be to You, O Desire of all worlds!” The matter is in God’s hands: He gives to whomever He wishes whatever He wishes, and withholds from whomever He wishes whatever He wills. He knows what hearts conceal and what moves the eyes of those who find fault. How many a heedless person who turned toward Us with sincerity have We seated upon the throne of acceptance, and how many a learned person have We consigned to the fire, in justice on Our part. We have indeed been the judge. He is indeed the manifestation of “God does whatever He wishes” and the One established upon the throne of “He decrees whatever He wills.”

159 Blessed is the one who has perceived the fragrance of inner meanings from the traces of this Pen, which, when it moves, spreads the breeze of God through all that is other than Him, and, when it rests, manifests the very essence of tranquillity throughout the world of being. Exalted is the All-Merciful, who manifests this great bounty. Say: Because He bore oppression, justice appeared throughout all that is other than Him, and because He accepted humiliation, the glory of God shone among all worlds.

160 You are forbidden to carry weapons except when necessary, and you are permitted to wear silk. As a bounty from Him, God has removed the restrictions on clothing and beards. He is indeed the Ordainer, the All-Knowing. Do what sound minds do not disapprove of, and do not make yourselves the playthings of the ignorant. Blessed is the one adorned with good manners and character. Such a person is among those who have aided their Lord through clear and manifest deeds.

161 Develop God’s cities and lands, and remember Him there in the melodies of those brought near to Him. Hearts are built up through the tongue, just as houses and cities are built through the hand and other means. We have appointed a means for everything from Our own presence. Hold fast to it, and put your trust in the All-Wise, the All-Informed.

162 Blessed is the one who acknowledges God and His verses and recognizes that He is not to be questioned about what He does. This is a statement that God has made the adornment and foundation of all beliefs, and through it the deeds of those who act are accepted. Keep this statement before your eyes, so that the insinuations of those who turn away do not cause you to stumble.

163 Were He to make lawful what He had forbidden from all eternity, or the reverse, no one would have the right to object to Him. Whoever hesitates for less than an instant is among the transgressors.

164 Whoever has not attained this most exalted principle and this highest station is shaken by the winds of doubt and tossed about by the assertions of those who associate partners with God. Whoever has attained this principle has attained the greatest steadfastness. How excellent is this most glorious station, with whose mention every exalted Tablet is adorned! Thus God teaches you what will free you from doubt and bewilderment and save you in this world and the next. He is indeed the All-Forgiving, the All-Bountiful. He is the One who sent the Messengers and revealed the Books, declaring: “There is no god but Me, the Almighty, the All-Wise.”

165 O land of Kāf and Rāʾ! We see you in a condition that God does not love, and We see from you what no one knows but God, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed. We perceive what passes within you in the deepest secrecy. With Us is the knowledge of all things in a clear Tablet. Do not grieve over this. God will soon raise up within you people of mighty strength who will remember Me with a steadfastness that neither the insinuations of the learned can hinder nor the doubts of those who sow doubt can veil. They will see God with their own eyes and aid Him with their own selves. They are indeed among those firmly established.

166 O company of the learned! When the verses were revealed and the clear proofs appeared, We saw you behind veils. This is truly an astonishing thing. You took pride in My name but neglected My own Self when the All-Merciful came with testimony and proof. We have torn through the veils; beware lest you veil the people with another veil. Break the chains of vain imaginings in the name of the Lord of all people, and do not be among the deceivers. When you turn to God and enter this Cause, do not spread corruption within it or measure the Book of God against your own desires. This is God’s counsel, before and hereafter. God’s witnesses and His chosen ones bear witness to this; We all bear witness to it.

167 Remember the shaykh whose name was Muḥammad followed by Ḥasan, who was among the most learned scholars of his age. When the Truth appeared, he and others like him turned away from Him, while a sifter of wheat and barley turned to God. Night and day the shaykh wrote what he imagined were the laws of God, yet when the Chosen One came, not a single letter of them benefited him. Had they benefited him, he would not have turned away from the Face through which the faces of those brought near to God were illumined. Had you believed in God when He appeared, the people would not have turned away from Him, nor would We have suffered what you see today. Fear God, and do not be among the heedless.

168 Beware lest names keep you from their Lord, or any remembrance veil you from this wise Remembrance. Seek refuge in God, O company of the learned, and do not make yourselves a veil between Me and My creation. Thus God admonishes you and commands you to act justly, lest your deeds come to nothing while you remain unaware. Can one who has turned away from this Cause establish any truth in creation? No, by the Lord of all that has been brought into being! Yet the people are plainly veiled. Say: Through Him the sun of testimony has shone and the luminary of proof has dawned for all who dwell in the realm of existence. Fear God, you who have insight, and do not deny. Beware lest mention of the Prophet keep you from this Most Great Announcement, or guardianship from the guardianship of God, which encompasses all the worlds. He has created every name through His word and made every matter dependent on His irrevocable, mighty, wondrous command. Say: This is the Day of God, in which none is to be mentioned but His own Self, who holds sway over all the worlds. This is a Cause that has shaken the imaginings and idols you possess.

169 We see among you those who take the Book and use it to argue against God, just as every religious community has used its own Book to argue against God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Say: By God, the Truth! Today neither the books of the world nor any of its scriptures can avail you except through this Book, which proclaims at the heart of creation: “There is no god but Me, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.”

170 O company of the learned! Beware lest you become a cause of discord throughout the lands, just as you were the cause of people turning away at the beginning of the Cause. Gather the people around this word, through which the pebbles have cried out: “Sovereignty belongs to God, the Dawning-Place of the verses.” Thus God admonishes you as a bounty from His presence. He is indeed the All-Forgiving, the All-Bountiful.

171 Remember Karīm when We called him to God. He grew arrogant, following his own desires, after We had sent him that which gladdened the eye of proof in the realm of existence and completed God’s testimony to all who dwell in the heavens and the earth. We commanded him to turn toward Us, as a bounty from the All-Sufficient, the Most Exalted. He turned his back until the wardens of torment seized him, as an act of justice from God. We were indeed witnesses.

172 Tear through the veils so that the inhabitants of the Kingdom hear the sound of their tearing. This is God’s command, before and hereafter. Blessed is the one who acts as commanded; woe to those who neglect it.

173 In the earthly realm, We have desired only the manifestation of God and His sovereignty, and God is sufficient as a witness over Me. In the Kingdom, We have desired only the exaltation of God’s Cause and His praise, and God is sufficient as My guardian. In the realm of power, We have desired only the remembrance of God and what has been sent down from His presence, and God is sufficient as a helper.

174 Blessed are you, O company of the learned in Bahā! By God, you are the waves of the Most Great Ocean, the stars of the heaven of bounty, and the banners of victory between the heavens and the earth. You are the dawning-places of steadfastness among creation and the daysprings of utterance for all who dwell in the realm of existence. Blessed is the one who turns toward you; woe to those who turn away. Today, whoever has drunk the choice wine of life from the hand of the loving kindness of his Lord, the All-Merciful, should pulse like an artery in the body of existence, so that through him the world and every crumbling bone may be stirred to life.

175 O people of creation! When the Dove has flown from the grove of praise and made its way to the farthest, most hidden destination, refer whatever you have not understood in the Book to the Branch that has sprung from this firmly established Root.

176 O Most Exalted Pen! Move across the Tablet by permission of your Lord, the Creator of heaven. Then recount when the Dawning-Place of Divine Unity set out for the school of detachment, so that the free may glimpse, to the extent of a needle’s eye, the mysteries of your Lord, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, that lie behind the veils. Say: We entered the school of inner meanings and exposition while those in the realm of existence were heedless. We beheld what the All-Merciful had sent down and accepted what He offered Me as a gift of the verses of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. We heard what He testified to in the Tablet; We were indeed witnesses. And We answered Him with a command from Our presence; We were indeed the One who commanded.

177 O people of the Bayān! We entered the school of God while you lay sleeping, and We examined the Tablet while you slumbered. By God, the Truth! We read it before it was revealed, while you were heedless. We encompassed the Book while you were still in the loins of your fathers. This account of Mine is according to your capacity, not according to God’s measure. What is in the knowledge of God bears witness to this, if only you knew, and the Tongue of God bears witness to it, if only you understood. By God! Were We to lift the veil, you would be struck senseless.

178 Beware of disputing about God and His Cause. He has appeared in such a manner as to encompass all that has been and all that will be. Were We to speak of this matter in the language of the inhabitants of the Kingdom, We would say: God created that school before creating the heavens and the earth, and We entered it before the Kāf was joined to its counterpart, the Nūn. This is the language of My servants in My Kingdom. Reflect on what the tongue of the inhabitants of My realm of power utters through the knowledge We have taught them from Our presence, on what has been concealed in God’s knowledge, and on what the Tongue of grandeur and power utters in its praiseworthy station.

179 This is not a Cause with which you may toy through your imaginings, nor is this a station that every deluded coward may enter. By God! This is the course of unveiling and detachment, the arena of vision and ascent. None may ride within it but the horsemen of the All-Merciful who have cast aside the realm of existence. They are God’s helpers on earth and the daysprings of power among the worlds.

180 Beware lest what is in the Bayān keep you from your Lord, the All-Merciful. By God! It was sent down to make mention of Me, if only you knew. The sincere find in it only the fragrance of My love and of My name, which holds sway over all who witness and all that is witnessed. Say: O people! Turn toward what has been sent down from My Most Exalted Pen. If you find in it the fragrance of God, do not object to it or deprive yourselves of God’s bounty and loving kindness. Thus God counsels you. He is indeed the All-Knowing Counsellor.

181 Whatever you have not understood in the Bayān, ask God, your Lord and the Lord of your forefathers. If He wishes, He will explain to you what has been sent down in it and the pearls of knowledge and wisdom concealed in the ocean of His words. He is indeed the Guardian over all names. There is no god but Him, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

182 The established order has been shaken by this Most Great Order, and its arrangement has been transformed by this wondrous new order, the like of which the eye of creation has never witnessed.

183 Immerse yourselves in the ocean of My utterance, so that you may discover the pearls of wisdom and mysteries within it. Beware of hesitating in this Cause, through which God’s sovereignty and might have been revealed. Hasten to Him with radiant faces. This is the religion of God, before and hereafter. Whoever wishes, let him turn toward it; and whoever does not wish—God is indeed independent of all the worlds.

184 Say: This is indeed the balance of guidance for all who are in the heavens and on earth, and the greatest proof, if only you knew. Say: Through it every proof throughout the ages has been established, if only you were certain. Say: Through it every poor person has been enriched, every learned person has gained knowledge, and whoever has desired to ascend to God has risen. Beware of differing over it. Be like firmly rooted mountains in the Cause of your Lord, the Almighty, the All-Loving.

185 Say: O source of rejection! Give up your willful blindness, then speak the truth among the people. By God! My tears have flowed down My cheeks because I see you turning toward your own desires and away from Him who created and fashioned you. Remember the bounty of your Lord, when We nurtured you through the nights and days for service to the Cause. Fear God and be among those who repent. Suppose your true condition is unclear to others: is it unclear to yourself? Fear God, then remember when you stood before the Throne and wrote down the verses of God, the All-Protecting, the All-Powerful, the Almighty, that We imparted to you. Beware lest pride keep you from turning toward the Divine Unity. Turn to Him and do not fear because of your deeds. He forgives whomever He wishes through His grace. There is no god but Him, the Ever-Forgiving, the Generous. We counsel you only for the sake of God. If you turn toward Him, it is for your own good; and if you turn away, your Lord has no need of you or of those who have followed you in manifest delusion. God has seized the one who led you astray, so return to Him in submission, humility, and lowliness. He will indeed efface your misdeeds. Your Lord is indeed the Ever-Relenting, the Almighty, the Merciful.

186 This is God’s counsel, if only you would listen. This is God’s grace, if only you would turn toward Him. This is God’s remembrance, if only you would perceive. This is God’s treasure, if only you would recognize it.

187 This is a Book that has become the lamp of eternity for the world and His straightest path among all peoples. Say: It is indeed the dawning place of God’s knowledge, if only you knew, and the dayspring of God’s commandments, if only you recognized it.

188 Do not load an animal with more than it can carry. We have most emphatically forbidden you to do so in the Book. Be manifestations of justice and fairness throughout the heavens and the earth.

189 Whoever kills a person by mistake must pay compensation to that person’s family, amounting to one hundred mithqāls of gold. Act according to what you have been commanded in the Tablet, and do not be among those who transgress.

190 O members of assemblies in the countries of the world! Choose one language from among the languages for everyone on earth to speak, and likewise one script from among the scripts. God makes clear to you what will benefit you and make you independent of others. He is indeed the Most Bountiful, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed. This is a means of unity, if only you knew, and the greatest means of harmony and civilization, if only you perceived. We have made these two matters signs of the world’s maturity. The first, which is the greatest foundation, We have sent down in other Tablets, and the second has been sent down in this wondrous Tablet.

191 Consuming opium has been forbidden to you. We have most emphatically forbidden you to do so in the Book, and whoever consumes it is not of Me. Fear God, O people of understanding.
