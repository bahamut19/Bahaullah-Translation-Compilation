1 In the name of our Lord, the Most Inaccessible, the Most Holy, the Most Mighty, the All-Glorious.

2 Glory be to You, O You through whom the Essence was established in the Essence, and the being of eternity clothed itself in the robe of names and attributes. Through Your name, the Manifest, manifestations have appeared from the beginning that has no beginning; and through Your name, the Hidden, hidden realities have lain concealed in the unseen of the unseen, so that they have not appeared from the eternity of eternities and will never appear until the end that has no end. With the rising of the sun of Your all-glorious beauty, the Luminary of exaltation rose from the horizon of origination, bringing glad tidings to those in the divine realm of the cloud of unknowing, to the Concourse of eternity, to the inhabitants of worlds that the pen of creation cannot describe and the realities of those endowed with understanding cannot comprehend, and then to those in the lowly human realm: “This is indeed the All-Glorious Beauty and the most resplendent Countenance. He has appeared in a manner sanctified beyond my mention and allusion, my recognition and indication, my hallowing and praise, my declaration of His transcendence and glorification, my affirmation of His unity and uniqueness, and whatever lies in the essence of my essence and the purest core of my being before His remembrance and splendor.”

3 Then, O you who were created in the Bayān through My verses to recognize Him, do not be veiled from the One in whose finger of will the whole Bayān is but a ring, and but a leaf in the paradise of His Cause. Glory be to You, O You whom the Most Great Remembrance can never indicate by mentioning You, and in whose praise the Pen of eternity can never move. How, then, could this perishable one, the lowliest of servants at the door of Your grace that has been opened to the nations, dare to mention You, except through a sprinkling from the oceans of Your generosity that have surged over all created things, and an overflow from the vast ocean of Your providence that has surged over those in the kingdom of the first and the last? Thus the tongues of all things have been opened with the most wondrous words of remembrance in praise of Your sovereign Self, and the call has risen from the heart of the solid rock in the depths of the seas and from all that is on the heights of the mountains: “You are indeed God, the Lord of all that is seen and unseen.”

4 Then, my God, I ask You by this providence that has encompassed all things and this mercy that has preceded all creation to send down now upon this perishable one a breeze from the breezes of Your bountiful generosity and a breath from the breaths of Your wondrous creation, through which decayed bones have stirred and dead bodies have arisen, for You are indeed God, the Everlasting, the Abiding, the Ancient. May I thereby arise to praise Your loved ones, remember those whom You have made lights of guidance among Your creatures and torches of unity among Your servants and Your creation, and speak as You strengthen and inspire me from Your presence. For I know nothing, and You are indeed the All-Knowing, the All-Aware.

5 You know, my God, that my love for them and my mention in their presence are solely for Your sake and purified of any regard for anyone but You. I desire, my God, that they become fully aware of the true circumstances of affairs in Your days and truly recognize what is concealed from them, so that their beings, their realities, their spirits, and their likenesses may thereby remain, in every world of Your worlds, aware of Your truth and acquainted with what has appeared from You and what You have intended for them within the tabernacle of Your providence and grace. For, my God, if they were aware of all things but remained veiled from one thing, to that extent they would be veiled from Your Most Great Countenance and would not have turned toward You with the fullness of their sight. This can come about only by sanctifying that sight from all that You have created and will create, and have manifested or will manifest. Thus Your true and lucid Tongue has declared: “Were the eye as vast as the heavens and the earth, and were it to turn even the tip of its gaze toward any other direction by an amount too small to be reckoned, it would be unable to behold this Most Exalted Countenance with the true vision that befits it.”

6 Exalted, exalted is Your Cause, my God, and glorious, glorious is Your Revelation, my Beloved! Beyond all hardship, it has been grievous to this servant that anyone should remain veiled in days such as these, whose like the eyes of creation have never seen, nor have those brought into being ever beheld. In these days You have appeared in the purity of Your beauty, the camphor-white radiance of Your countenance, and the unalloyed essence of Your belovedness. The manifestations of Your providence and the expressions of Your loving-kindness have encompassed the heavens and the earth, so that every hidden matter, every stored-up treasure, and every guarded mystery has come to light. The Sun of inner meanings has shone from the horizon of the heaven of exposition and shed its splendor, through the manifestations of Your most beautiful names and most exalted attributes, upon all who dwell in the realms of possibility and existence.

7 Those who wish to enter this inaccessible, unassailable court and this exalted, lofty abode should have hearts sanctified from the mention of the allusions of the past and the words established by the ignorant who have become renowned for learning and excellence. Praise be to You, my God, for having appointed for those who enter this most exalted and lofty court a station that neither the birds of the hearts of the people of eternity nor the realities of those in creation can attain. Thus has the Manifestation of Your revelation, the Dawning-Place of Your inspiration, and the Point of Your will declared: “A drop of seed in the year of that Manifestation is more potent than the whole Bayān.” Likewise, in the Qayyūm of the Books and other Tablets, words have been revealed concerning this most exalted station in a manner that leaves the minds of those endowed with understanding bewildered. How exalted is the loftiness of these creatures whom You have created from the pure essence of Your Cause and brought into being from the very substance of Your creation! Then, my God, how is it fitting that this wondrous creation and this most exalted and lofty handiwork should be

8 I am bewildered, my God, by the deafness of some ears, the covering over eyes, and the veil upon some hearts. At Your call the thrones have trembled, the heavens have been rent asunder, the veils of endlessness have been torn apart, and the mountains of the realities of contingent beings have been crushed by a single effulgence of the radiance of the lights of Your face. Yet, despite all this, some of Your servants and creatures have not awakened, although at every moment You call out above their heads with the loudest call and stand before their eyes in the fullness of light. You have drawn forth for them the hand of will from the bosom of purpose and say with the sovereignty of strength and grandeur: “Here is the hand of My might, which I have stretched over the divine realm of My grandeur, the dominion of My power, the kingdom of My sovereignty, the realms of My eternity, and the regions of My Cause. I have folded them all in My hand, together with all that I have ordained within them through My all-encompassing knowledge. It is white with the lights of My face, radiant with the splendor of My countenance, and gleaming with the lights of My brow. Through My grace and generosity, I bestow it upon whoever enters My kingdom and My dominion.” Yet, while the oceans of light are surging, they make mention of the narrators of the people of darkness and delusion.

9 My God, all existence has been ashamed of their words of remembrance, yet they are not ashamed. All contingent beings feel shame at their sayings, yet they feel none. Could one who has awakened at Your call, arisen through the spirit of Your Cause, been quickened by the breath of Your mercy, and drunk from the cup of Your providence allow his tongue to utter the vain imaginings current on the tongues of the heedless among Your creatures and the ignorant among Your creation? No, by Your sovereign Beauty! None could do so except one for whom You have made hearing a sign of punishment from You, and sight, by Your command, a pit of hell in his face.

10 My God, I have indeed ventured to speak these words before the sovereignty of Your grandeur. Yet You know that fire blazes from every hair of mine when I hear the sayings of those who have not become acquainted with the mysteries of Your Cause and the hidden things within Your knowledge, who have forsaken exalted stations and contented themselves with lowly ranks. It is as though they have forgotten what was sent down from the dominion of Your Cause. You said, and Your word is truth: “O son of the most exalted vision! I ordained for you the purest fruits from the all-glorious tree. How have you turned away from them and been content with what is inferior? Return, then, to what is better for you in the most exalted horizon.”

11 I see some of them, my God, veiled by a statement in the Book, even though it was revealed explicitly, not by allusion, that the whole Bayān does not equal a single word from Him. Whatever was mentioned of proof and testimony, of evidence, verses, clear signs, and tokens, was solely for this most great, most great Revelation. No word was mentioned in it, no thing was found in it, and no letter was uttered except as a sign of Your Cause and a proof of Your sovereignty. Thus the Tongue of Will has spoken from Your presence: “Everything that has been or will be created is a sign of Him, created by His utterance from Him, to be His proof for Himself on the day of His manifestation.” Indeed, my God, He made the whole Bayān dependent upon Your permission and acceptance. Say—and His word is truth: “By Your might! If You forgive the Bayān and all who are in it, nothing is diminished from Your dominion; and if You do not accept the Bayān and all who are in it, nothing is added to Your dominion. Rather, if You accept it, You bring those enraptured hearts beneath the sheltering shade of Your love; and if You reject it, it perishes as though it had never been mentioned before.”

12 Then I ask You, O Possessor of existence and King of the unseen and the seen, to strengthen everyone to turn toward the horizon of Your grace and bounty, sanctified from the allusions of past and future, and to acquaint them with what You have sent down in Your Book, so that they may hold fast to the essence of its contents. For this is Your purpose in what has been sent down in truth, and Your desire in what You have revealed in Your Books and Tablets. You are indeed the All-Powerful, the All-Protecting, the Most Exalted, the Almighty, the All-Wise.

13 O eternal God, You bear witness and testify that this servant has at all times sought nothing but Your good pleasure and, God willing, through Your grace will never do otherwise. This servant’s eyes have always been fixed on the Most Great Scene, and his heart turned toward the Source of the Cause. Whatever he has seen and come to know he has laid before Your friends solely for Your sake, so that receptive souls may drink from the Kawthar of life that has flowed from the finger of grace and bounty in the days of the Revelation, and may turn toward the Dawning-place of the Cause and the Dayspring of revelation.

14 For some time this servant had chosen silence over speech, until in these days he observed a dark, gloomy dust rising from the direction of heedless souls, and feared that it might cloud some weak eyes. Therefore, trusting in You and detached from all besides You, this servant considered it his duty to lay some of the events that have occurred before Your loved ones, who rest upon the carpet of steadfastness and have drunk the choice wine of Your grace, so that they might protect the weak from the darkness of selfish desires and the doubts cast by satanic souls. I swear by Your grandeur, O Lord, that at this moment every limb trembles and every part of my frame is shaken. How could this servant be worthy of having his words accepted or his utterance mentioned? How can sheer nothingness raise the banner of existence, and how can pure nonexistence assert its being before the Revelation of Him who is eternal? For presenting these very matters is an assertion of existence, and that is reckoned among the greatest transgressions.

15 But since this is presented so that Your servants may draw near to the shore of nearness and reunion, and souls may turn toward the Point of Adoration of all in earth and heaven, I therefore seek pardon from the ocean of forgiveness. Yet this servant knows with certainty that You have always been, and remain, independent of people’s remembrance, recognition, and turning toward You. Their turning toward You does not benefit You, nor does their turning away harm You. No description reaches Your most holy court, and no mention enters the precincts of Your oneness. Indeed, to mention oneness in Your most holy court is sheer sin and utter heedlessness, for its nobility lies in its relation to You, not in Your relation to it. If You banish it entirely, who can prevent You from doing so? And if You exalt it, as You have exalted it, who can object to Your action? You are indeed the One praised in all that You have willed and will, in all that You have said and say, and in all that You have manifested and manifest.

16 By Your might, my God, I wish to choose for myself only what You have chosen for me, and I desire to speak only what You have commanded me to say. The eye of my being looks toward the horizon of Your commands and the dayspring of Your laws. If You roll up the carpet of remembrance spread far and wide across the earth, I will say: You are sovereign in Your command. And if You command me to do what all in the world would reject, I will say: You are to be obeyed in Your decree. I ask You, O God, to make me one of those who speak only with Your permission and move only by Your will. You are indeed the All-Powerful, the Most Exalted, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

17 I submit that, when the Ancient Beauty entered the prison of ‘Akkā, conditions were exceedingly harsh for a time. Their details were reported to some of the friends in those very days, and need not be repeated. All were in extreme hardship and distress, until one day a Tablet was revealed from the Source of the Cause while this servant was transcribing it in His presence. A verse descended from the heaven of the divine Will in which He named this prison the Most Great Prison. This servant supposed that it had been given this name on account of these severe afflictions, until one day verses were revealed whose purport was this: “Reflect on why We have named this prison the Most Great.” When this utterance appeared from the Dawning-place of the revelation of the All-Merciful, this servant found himself deeply troubled and shaken. Yet, trusting in God, I remained still and did not venture to ask any question. Then one day the love and devotion of one of the inhabitants of this city were mentioned before the throne. A Tablet addressed specifically to him descended from the heaven of oneness. In that Tablet He spoke in detail of the events occurring in this land, and that Tablet is still extant. After its revelation this servant understood that the reason for this prison’s surpassing greatness was the all-encompassing afflictions befalling the Beauty of Oneness. Then one day one of the companions began to quarrel and dispute with some of the others. Although he himself was at fault and was the aggressor, he submitted a petition to the Most Holy Court in which he claimed to have been wronged. In response to him a Tablet in Persian descended from the cloud of divine sorrow. Its text is as follows.

18 He is God.

19 A lover may not assert his own existence or regard himself in the presence of the beloved. If he strays even one step from this path, he is not counted among the lovers. Love does not accept a living self; a falcon does not hunt a dead mouse.

20 Indeed, some lovers have recounted the blows of the afflictions [corrupted original text] before the Beloved of all existence. Their purpose was to commune with the Beloved and gain the attention of Him who is the Object of all desire, not to speak of self and desire. Now a messenger has descended from the loftiest retreats of the glory of the Desired One and is speaking these words. Understand who is speaking and what he says. By God, if you recognized and knew the mysteries of your Lord, the Exalted, the Most High, that lie beyond the veil of grandeur, you would sacrifice your lives out of love for God, the Lord of Names.

21 In brief, the messenger in question related this account: “Once we were on the Sinai of illumination, and the Beloved of the world set out for the mountains. With the utmost humility He entreated those who professed love: ‘Although this journey and migration seem outwardly easy and simple, inwardly they are arduous and a doorway to testing. Though outwardly they appear as sovereignty and power, inwardly they conceal untold suffering and distress. Do not forfeit your honor or bring hardship upon yourselves. Let Me migrate alone.’ Whatever words of love and compassion He spoke, whether indirectly or explicitly, proved of no avail. This servant and a number of others claimed that we sought no provision but the Friend’s good pleasure and had no aim but the face of the Beloved. Devoted, as we imagined, yet heedless of the Friend’s counsel and certainty, we resolved to travel and became the companions and fellow travelers of the Beloved Countenance. We had traversed only a little of the wilderness when the fire of love died down and the beauty of longing was veiled. Then we went beyond that stage: we saw good deeds as evil and counted evil deeds as goodness itself.

22 Then we entered [corrupted original text] the verdant. In that spiritual land the divine Ark came to rest upon the Jūdī of the Cause. Thereafter, mounted on the steeds of desire, we traveled morning and evening in the wilderness of conjecture and delusion. Sometimes together and sometimes apart, sometimes in love and sometimes in heedlessness, we passed our days and nights. At all times the Salsabīl of utterance flowed from the Kawthar of the mouth of the All-Merciful, yet thirst was absent; the lights of His face shone from the horizon of majesty, yet no one turned toward them. Each day we added to our delusions and conjectures and diminished our attention to God. With these unworthy conditions and unbecoming ways, we sank so deeply into the floods of heedlessness and desire that we became heedless even of our own condition.

23 In every circumstance we saw the Beloved Countenance showing the utmost compassion and tenderness. Some of us were bewildered: if God’s all-encompassing knowledge existed, how could we be accepted despite these vile deeds? In the end we considered God heedless and ourselves wise and knowing, unaware that His supreme mercy prevented the tearing away of veils. And if we rendered any service, we followed it with reminders of our favor and with hurt.

24 Afterward He resolved to migrate from that land and again forbade everyone to accompany Him. We neither perceived nor took heed of the reason for this prohibition or its cause. Once more we migrated and traversed wildernesses with Him who is the Object of all desire, until [we] entered another land. We dwelt in one place with the Friend and secretly occupied ourselves with selfish desires, until the fire of desire gained the upper hand and we were barred from the Most Glorious Presence. Through the frequency of our meetings, we lost sight of the value of reunion, and the veil of shame was torn apart.

25 Now we have confined the Beloved in a place and at every moment spare Him none of the arrows of conjecture and spears of delusion that our capacity and strength allow, for we have found Him confined, imprisoned, and alone. Heedlessness has reached such a point that, in that blessed place toward which all the inhabitants of the heavenly Concourse look and from which they seek assistance, we openly utter the foulest words and commit the most misguided and ruinous deeds. Woe to us! The words of God have had no effect upon us. Yet we have not awakened. At the very least, if we have shown no faithfulness, let us not inflict cruelty. Not one night do we engage in remembrance or occupy ourselves with turning toward Him.

26 It is related that Fuḍayl of Khurāsān was among the most wretched of servants and practiced highway robbery. He fell in love with a young woman and came to her one night. He climbed the wall and then heard someone reciting this verse: “Has the time not come for the hearts of those who believe to be humbled at the remembrance of God?” It moved his heart, and he said, “Yes, my Lord, the time has come; the hour has arrived.” He turned back, repented, and set out for the Sacred House of God, where he remained for thirty years, until his spirit ascended to the most exalted horizon. It is astonishing that he heard the Word of God from the lips of one of His servants, and it affected him so deeply that in a single instant he turned from the depths of contingent existence toward the horizon of the All-Merciful. Yet we servants have heard, day after day and night after night, the melodies of the All-Merciful Himself from the Tongue of power and grandeur, and they have not affected us enough for us even to recognize the ugliness of our own deeds and actions, suppositions and delusions.

27 When the account reached this point, the ark of utterance lay stunned and bewildered upon the Judi of the tongue, and the pen fell senseless from the fingers. “Nothing will befall us except what God has ordained for us.” We ask God to open our eyes and acquaint us with ourselves and our deeds. If we are not enabled to know His own Self—glory be to Him—may we be enabled to know our heedless selves. May He lift the obstructing veils from our faces, that we may see Him shining from the horizon of grace and loving-kindness, detach ourselves from all who dwell in the realm of possibility and the worlds of being, and turn toward Him with our whole being. He is indeed our Lord, the All-Merciful. We ask Him to enable us to repent and turn back to Him every morning and evening, and to protect the Dawning-place of the radiance of His countenance from the spears of His loved ones, for He has desired no helper but Him, and He is His origin and His abode; and He says, “There is no God but God.” End.

28 Although it was revealed in general terms, He said that it applied specifically to those who had turned away, who now outwardly profess love, but whose inner secrets would later be revealed. It was read to a number of the loved ones, but was kept from the man himself. Everyone has known and knows that some who professed love for the All-Merciful were engaged in unworthy deeds in Iraq and other cities. Yet the mercy of the All-Merciful prevented the veil from being torn and their deeds from being exposed. Thus, during the sojourn in Iraq, that unjust soul secretly drank wine and engaged in certain other deeds, and toward the end of the days in Iraq he sometimes passed from secrecy to open conduct. Nevertheless, those who circled around Him concealed it, even though words of counsel were continually being revealed from the Dawning-place of divine verses, as recorded in most of the revealed Tablets.

29 The deeds and actions of some of the people of the Bayān at the beginning of the Cause were not, and are not, hidden from anyone. Yet they imagined that such things were acceptable and that they were authorized by God, exalted be His glory, even though the domain of God’s commandments has been sanctified from vile and unacceptable deeds, and His most holy court is free of repugnant and unworthy conduct. By His own Self, the True One! During the years of the sojourn in Iraq and the days of migration, clear and explicit verses forbidding the servants to do anything other than what God had decreed were revealed day and night from the heaven of His will and sent to every region. At last, praise be to God, some of the servants came to recognize what God intended and acted upon it, and the breeze of sanctity and purity belonging to God’s Cause passed among His creatures. In view of the people’s lack of knowledge of the divine commandments, this utterance was heard from the Tongue of Oneness as a gracious favor to them: “God has forgiven what is past.” We ask God to enable them to attain sanctity and purity and to act according to what they have been commanded by One Who is All-Knowing, All-Informed.

30 In short, that unjust soul and Riḍā-Qulī were continually engaged in shameful deeds in secret. This servant repeatedly counseled them both, whether in Iraq, in the Land of Mystery, or in the Most Great Prison, in the hope that they might take heed and awaken from the sleep of heedlessness. It had no effect whatsoever. Neither the words of counsel nor the openly proclaimed healing utterances had any effect upon them, until Riḍā-Qulī openly engaged in drinking and shameful deeds with some Christians. Therefore God expelled him by His authority, and he joined the two wicked men, namely Sayyid Muḥammad and Āqā Jān.

31 Some time passed. One day he sent a petition to the most holy court expressing repentance for his past sins, and a few days later another petition of the same kind. But since he had repeatedly repented and then broken God’s Covenant and Testament, the petitions were not accepted. Certain deeds also came to light that were without parallel or likeness and brought the Cause of God into disrepute among His servants. This mortal servant has not wished to mention them; perhaps the angels who spread abroad will disseminate the deeds of that wicked outcast throughout the earth. Truly, God’s curse rests upon the unjust! When that wicked man joined the other two, the fire of discord and hatred blazed to a degree that pen and utterance cannot recount. But the All-Merciful subdued and extinguished it. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Almighty.

32 Everyone endowed with sight inhales the fragrance of the garment of sanctity and purity from the Ancient Beauty’s expulsion of those souls, for the deeds of Riḍā-Qulī, the wicked Āqā Jān, and his follower are clear and manifest to all. We ask God to aid His servants in fairness and in effacing the vain imaginings they hold and what was related among former generations.

86 The wonder lies in those who followed the foul-smelling ones after the fragrance of the Beloved had spread among the peoples of the world. Thus, through Sayyid Muhammad of Isfahan and his words, they have remained veiled from the most unapproachable, most holy Truth, although he has never known the essence of the Cause and has always been occupied with corruption. Though it is a common saying, it suits this occasion: “See how one blind man becomes the guide of another.” By God, by God, anyone with sight and a sense of smell who has seen him even once has found in his face a trace of hellfire and in his breath the odor of the people of Sijjin. Every day he engages in some deception. When we entered the Most Great Prison, he named himself Quddus Effendi, imagining that a meaningless name would exalt its bearer. Woe to him and to those who follow him! Likewise, the Chief of the Associators named Aqa Jan “Sword of Truth” and promised him Iraq. A letter from Aqa Jan himself still exists, written to the Chief of the Associators, in which he pleaded, “How much longer will it be before the promise you made is fulfilled?” In short, in this land he told everyone, “I am the Sword of Truth, and soon I will conquer most of the lands.” Indeed, God’s curse rests upon the liars!

87 At this point, verses were revealed from the heaven of the divine will. His words—exalted be His majesty:

88 The Most Holy, the Most Great, the All-Glorious.

89 In the affliction of the Lord of all existence at every moment there are signs for all created beings. He has accepted hardship for the prosperity of humanity and suffering for the comfort of all who dwell in the world. May my soul be sacrificed for His grace, my being for His mercy, and my spirit for His loving care, which has encompassed the horizons. No morning has dawned upon Him without His being surrounded by the darkness of insinuations from those who disbelieve in God, the Revealer of verses. Yet nothing prevents Him from accomplishing what He has willed in the Cause of God, the Master of the Day of Summoning. At one time He calls out with His lucid tongue, and at another He points with the finger of certainty, summoning everyone to God, the Lord of all. Were We to recount what has befallen Us, heaven would be rent asunder and the mountains would collapse.

90 Those who disbelieved took pride in the titles they bore. The mute one named himself “the Most Holy” and made the same claims for himself as did the stealthy whisperer. The other named himself “Sword of Truth” and said, “I am indeed the conqueror of lands.” God raised up someone who struck him on the mouth, so that all might know with certainty that he was the tail of Satan, severed by the Sword of the All-Merciful. He had been awaiting the days of his glory and ascendancy, in accordance with the promises made to him by one who disbelieved in God, the Cleaver of the dawn. Thus does God seize whoever turns away from Him and arises to undermine His Cause among His servants.

91 When they perished, the winds of spring began to blow, the gates of heaven were opened, and the clouds poured down rain. Blessed is whoever has attained the recognition of God in His days and detached himself wholly from every direction. Say: Has the Lord of the heavens and the earth not sufficed you? He has indeed come with the truth in His name, the All-Protecting over all creation. Illuminate your heart with the Most Great Lamp, which the Lord of eternity has kindled. Then stand firm in the Cause through the sovereignty of your Lord, the All-Powerful, the Unconstrained.

92 End of quotation. There was a man in this city who had been one of the religious scholars of the Great City. Aqa Jan used to associate with him and had told him the details as well: “I am the Sword of Truth. Soon most of the lands will be conquered at my hands.” Though far removed from the path of Truth, that ignorant scholar made a good remark, as though the Holy Spirit had spoken through his tongue. Immediately after the killing of the associators, he came with the governor of the town to Aqa Jan’s body and said, “This accursed man used to say, ‘I am the Sword of Truth.’ Now it is clear that the Sword of Truth was the one that struck his back and consigned him to the lowest depths of hell.”

93 In short, praise be to God, after this event and the return of the associators to the lowest depths of hell, the rains of mercy have fallen night and day, after mercy had been withheld for several years and the people had suffered the torment of famine and high prices.

94 In short, when we were in Edirne, that vile man had written a letter to someone. Among other things, it was noticed that he had stolen a passage from a Tablet revealed in Iraq and written it in his own name. The passage is this: “We shine like the sun and gleam like the moon.” That heedless, foul man had not understood even this much: the realm of the Simurgh has never been, nor will it ever be, a playground for flies, and darkness has no right to claim to be the sun. By God, because he saw that some people were weak and foolish, he set about fabricating claims of his own. Thus, some have taken up his forged writings and read them. Both the writer and the reader have indeed lost. Through various whisperings and intrigues he kept people from the Lord of humanity, and through former delusions he led some astray.

95 For example, a man met this servant in the marketplace and said, “I would like to meet with you for an hour, provided that no one is there but you and me.” I had never seen him before. I said, “Very well.” A time was arranged, and he came. Various matters came up in conversation, and it became clear that he associated with the one who joined partners with God. He said, “I have been told that you are a Shia.” At this, such laughter overcame this servant that I lost all self-control. The man was astonished and asked, “Why are you laughing?” I said, “You poor, helpless man! Your Shias, who are scattered like pebbles throughout Persia—what station have they attained, or what standing have they had before God, that you should now wish to follow in the footsteps of those rejected, deluded people? Have you not seen that they were all in error and utter misguidance, so much so that they martyred the exalted Truth with their own hands? Indeed, God’s curse rests upon the people who do wrong!”

96 After these words were spoken, he became very thoughtful. Then, for God’s sake, I conveyed to him some of the exalted words I had heard from the Source of the Cause. It became apparent that he was not without capacity. Among other things, I said, “Consider: of all that the Shias held and counted among the foundations of their religion and faith, which was true, that you should now wish to enter the ocean of truth and divine wisdom by following those delusions and lying words? Had these Shias you speak of understood the meaning of the Resurrection?” He said, “No.” I asked, “Had they recognized the Balance?” He said, “No.” I asked, “Had they understood the gathering and rising of the dead?” He said, “No.” I asked, “Was what they related concerning the Qaim true in the way they understood it?” He said, “No.” Then I said, “You yourself bear witness that it was all false. Now, how have you ascertained that this thing they have told you is true?”

97 Then I sat upright facing him and recited what I had memorized of the verses of God, the King, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. I conveyed to him what I had been commanded by God, the Almighty, the Beloved. I said, “O servant! Have you not heard what the Tongue of Grandeur uttered when He took His seat upon His throne, which holds sway over every witness and all that is witnessed? He said—and His word is truth—‘Abandon the delusions you hold, then listen to what the tongue of your Lord, the Almighty, the All-Knowing, proclaims. How long will you follow desire? The Sun of guidance has risen. Turn toward it, sanctified from what your religious scholars have said in the past. By God, this is indeed the manifestation of God and the sign of His concealment to all besides Him. It cannot be compared with anything mentioned from all eternity, nor can it be known through the words of the disputatious that you possess. Draw near to Him with radiant eyes and shining faces. Thus have you been commanded in the Tablets by God, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed.’ And His words—glorious be His majesty—addressed to someone: ‘We see you immersed in the pool of those who are veiled and in the waters of the delusions of the heedless. Come out of it through My name and My sovereignty, then immerse yourself in this Most Great Ocean, every drop of which proclaims: There is no god but Me, the All-Powerful, the Most Exalted, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the All-Bountiful.’”

98 I said, “Man, I speak for the sake of God and make mention of this purely for God: cast away these delusions and rise above these foul, limited, imaginary stations, so that on the wings of the mercy of the All-Merciful you may ascend to the heavens of divine wisdom and attain your goal. Today is the day of ‘Say: God! Then leave them to play in their vain discourse.’ This blessed verse was revealed in the past specifically for this day, for in this Revelation it has become clear and manifest that God Himself alone is sovereign over all and has never taken, nor will ever take, a partner or a likeness. Although the followers of religions previously acknowledged and professed this statement, most were lying, for every day they assigned a partner to God.”

99 Then, on this subject, this servant submitted what was fitting. He asked, “What is our duty today?” I said, “To act according to this very verse that I have cited. Leave these deluded souls and their sayings behind, and enter the delightful, wondrous expanse, so that you may drink the everlasting wine from the verse of the mercy of the All-Merciful, which says: ‘Through My manifestation the rule of new creation has been established, and this is indeed the Originator of the heavens and the earths.’ Pass beyond before and after, and look upon the Manifestation Himself with the eye of the Manifestation, for all are commanded to do this in this Most Great Revelation, and this is peculiar to this Revelation.” Then I mentioned that that wicked soul had used such statements to keep some weak souls away from Him who acts as He chooses, just as Yaḥyā also writes to some, “You are among my followers,” and the purpose of this expression is clear. Every lying scribbler has indeed gone astray. Now consider how far apart are the station of the statements of that wicked soul and the station that the Lord of Lords has desired for His servants. Brother, cast away all the limited, unworthy expressions of the past, and soar on wondrous wings in the delightful, wondrous expanse, so that through divine favor you may drink the wondrous wine and discover the secret of this wondrous Cause. Furthermore, some servants have complained about certain deeds of the weak souls who circle around Him, and have attributed them to God, exalted and glorified be He. For example, someone has said, “How is it possible that, despite the declaration of the truth and the exaltation of this Cause, which has encompassed the world, some souls who claim a connection to God should occupy themselves with unworthy deeds?” This remark, however, arises from that heedless person’s heedlessness. It seems he has not heard this verse, which is well known among the people: “Were all created things to become unbelievers, no dust would settle on the hem of His grandeur.”

100 One day I was present in the court of the throne. The Face of God turned toward me and said, “Have you heard that the sincere are in great danger? O servant present before the throne! The outwardly learned have disagreed about the companions of the Messenger, as to whom the designation ‘companion’ applies. Some have said that companions are those souls who were in His presence for a year or more and arose to wage holy war in the path of God. Others have said that whoever professed the declaration of God’s oneness and met the Messenger is counted among the companions, even if this happened only once. Others have said that this designation applies to those souls whom the Messenger Himself specifically addressed by this designation. Still others have said that they are those souls who were trusted by Him and were present both on journeys and at home. Most of the learned, however, have said, ‘Whoever embraced Islam [corrupted original text] the Prophet, may God bless Him, and accompanied Him, even for the shortest time, is one of the companions.’ Accordingly, at the Farewell Pilgrimage forty thousand souls were with Him, and on the day of His passing one hundred and twenty-four thousand people gathered in Medina. According to the last view, the designation ‘companion’ applies to all these souls. Nevertheless, only a few had drunk of the limpid wine of certitude and attained the Source of the outpourings of [corrupted original text]. In short, such words have arisen, and continue to arise, from heedlessness. We ask God to assist everyone to do what He loves and approves. O friends of God, do not content yourselves with hearsay, and do not set your hearts on the delusions of the past. By God, today wicked souls have kept the weak away from the divine watering place through the delusions of former times.”

101 Listen to the appeal of this servant who serves before the throne, and stand with steadfast feet upon the path of oneness. God has appeared in such a manner that no one has any room left to turn away. All the divine books point to this and attest to it. Some time ago, for example, one of the friends who had belonged to the various religious communities submitted a letter, and in that letter presented two traditions translated from Greek into Arabic in the ancient books.

102 The first passage: “Satan will appear on the island of Qāf and keep people away from the All-Merciful. When that time comes, turn toward the Holy Land; from it the breeze of God passes.” End of quotation. Qāf is Cyprus, and this is well known, for all the Turks call Cyprus “Satan’s island.” The Holy Land is also known: it is where the seat of the throne is now established.

103 And the second passage: “Ḥubāb will appear on the island associated with him. He is short in stature, thick-bearded, narrow of forehead and chest, with yellow eyes and hair. On his back is down like a camel’s, and on his chest hair like a goat’s. When that time comes, draw near to Carmel, even on your breast; then turn toward the holy valley, the land of the gathering, the white spot.” End of quotation. It has been known that Ḥubāb is a name for Satan and a serpent. It says that Satan will appear on the island associated with him, which is Cyprus, since it is known as Satan’s island. And it says, “When that time comes, draw near to Carmel”—and Carmel is a mountain facing Acre—“even on your breast,” that is, even if it be on your chest; “then turn toward the holy valley, the land of the gathering, the white spot.” These are three designations of the land of Acre, as is well known to all and recorded in the books. Would that someone would go to Cyprus and observe with his outward eyes all these stated characteristics in that person.

104 In short, O servants, God has no need of these statements. Such statements are offered solely as a favor, for this lowly servant has thought that through such things some might awaken from the sleep of heedlessness. Otherwise, God is sanctified above the mention of this person or that. He is known only through Himself and described only by that with which He has described His own Self to His own Self. All besides Him is created by His command and returns to its places in the worlds of His origination and creation.

105 We hope that, since this servant’s appeals have been submitted purely for the sake of God, they may become a healing for hearts and a light for breasts, so that all may arise in the love of God in such a manner that no one has any room left to turn away or object, and may regard all that the idolaters have said or may say as sheer nothingness. Then I say: I turn to You in repentance, O my God, for what I have committed in Your court. Forgive me, then, through Your sovereignty and Your grace. You are indeed the Most Generous of the generous, and praise be to You, O God of the worlds.
