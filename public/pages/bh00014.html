1 In the name of our Lord, the Most Holy, the Most Great, the Most Exalted, the All-Glorious.

2 Praise be to God—a praise through which the clouds rain down and those brought near perceive the fragrance of the robe of our Lord, the All-Bountiful; a praise through which the lovers seek the countenance of God, the Lord of the Covenant; a praise through which the sincere hasten to the station of unveiling and reunion, and those who affirm His unity to the loftiest summit; a praise through which the rivers flow and the trees bear fruit. I testify that there is no God but Him, alone, without partner. Exaltation, glory, remembrance, and praise be upon His loved ones and His chosen ones, whom no blame from any blamer has deterred in the path of God, whom no tyrant’s might has held back, and whom no ignorant person’s clamor has frightened. They are honored servants; no fear will come upon them, nor will they grieve.

3 To proceed: your letters, beloved friend, have indeed been pouring down in succession, like rain from the clouds of love. This servant thanks Him who has strengthened you, granted you success, and bestowed recognition upon you, and asks Him to raise you up in the world and honor you among His servants. He is powerful over all things.

4 One of your letters, beloved friend, dated the twelfth of the month of Ṣafar, adorned and illumined the heart with the breezes of joy and the eyes with the lights of love. A hundred thousand thanks to the Desire of all peoples, for He has granted success, and His assistance arrives at every moment; otherwise, existence would return to nothingness. After examining, considering, and reading it, I proceeded to the supreme station and the most exalted, most sublime abode. Once permission was given, it was presented before the face of the Lord of Names. This is what was revealed in reply—His words, exalted be His glory and all-embracing His bounty:

5 He is the One who shines from the horizon of utterance.

6 O Amīn, upon you be My glory, My loving care, and My mercy, which has preceded all who are in the heavens and the earths. Praise the Lord of Eternity, who has strengthened you to turn toward Him, to remember and praise Him, and to serve His Cause. He has made His path known to you, guided you to His way, enabled you to serve His Cause, and raised you to a station in which the Most Exalted Pen remembers you from the All-Glorious Horizon—a station that all humanity has acknowledged its inability to describe. Rejoice in this most great bounty and say: “Praise be to You, O Desire of all peoples.”

7 O Amīn, in most of the Tablets to the loved ones, mention of you has flowed and been revealed from the Most Exalted Pen. Blessed are you and He who has guided you, enabled you to see and hear, and directed you aright. Praise be to Him who has strengthened you and enabled you to recognize the One whom most people have been kept from recognizing. To this the Lord of creation bears witness in this exalted station. Convey greetings of exaltation to the loved ones of God, exalted be His glory, and remember them on behalf of the Wronged One, that they may be set ablaze with the fire of remembrance and engage, with wisdom and utterance, in teaching the Cause of the All-Merciful. The people are deeply heedless. At every time and moment, this Wronged One calls them, in the most exalted utterance, to the horizon of the All-Merciful; yet all are deprived of hearing and sight, except those whom God wills. Veils have kept their eyes from seeing, and the cotton of vain imaginings has stopped their ears from listening. May God have mercy on justice and fairness! Nothing but their names remains: the names exist, but their traces are lost. The matter rests in the hands of God, the Lord of the unseen and the seen.

8 O Amīn, the ocean of utterance has encompassed the world of being, and the call of God has risen from the highest horizon of the world. Nevertheless, no one has become aware of it except a few. Yet this has not been, and is not, a cause for concern. Soon what has flowed from the Pen of God in the Tablets will become manifest. End.

9 The rains of our Lord’s mercy have poured down upon you and upon us, and the sun of His generosity has shone before our faces. Who can remember Him as He deserves to be remembered, describe Him as He deserves to be described, or praise Him as He deserves to be praised? I turn to Him in repentance and ask His forgiveness for whatever has crossed my mind, been spoken by my tongue, or been written by my pen.

10 You have always been remembered, beloved friend, as is clear and evident from the shining verses of God’s loving care, exalted be His glory. A hundred thousand praises and thanks that, in these days, the outcome of the exalted, blessed words He spoke when news arrived of the imprisonment of the honored Ismu’llāh and those with him—upon them be the glory of God, the All-Glorious—is plain and manifest. His words, blessed and exalted is He: “They seek victory over Us through the strength of troops and cannons, while We seek victory over them through the Strong, the All-Subduing, the All-Powerful.” End.

11 Today the horizons of the human world have been graced and adorned with a new splendor. The prayers of those who affirm God’s unity, the sincere, the hopeful, the suppliants, and the seekers have been answered. All had asked for the release of the imprisoned and the confined, especially the honored Ismu’llāh—upon him be the most glorious of all glory—and those with him—upon them be the glory of God, the All-Glorious. Praise be to God, what brings joy and gladness has come to pass.

12 O beloved of my heart, the people are heedless. If they reflected for a single moment, they would see that the enemies do not endure, and would likewise see the manifestations of the strength and power of God, exalted be His glory. Despite imprisonment, confinement, and a hundred thousand outward afflictions, He seized the corruptors of the land of Ṣād with the grip of One mighty and all-powerful, yet not a soul took heed. Likewise He seized the hard-hearted souls of the land of Ṭāʾ, from the liar named Ṣādiq to the one who intended the greatest calamity for the people of Bahāʾ. Now both dwell in hellfire. Nevertheless, the people have not gained awareness even to the extent of a needle’s eye. If this servant wished to recount such matters, he would have to compose a book. What is astonishing is the people’s stupor and heedlessness. What has become of awareness, and where has understanding gone? It is bewilderment upon bewilderment.

13 In truth, one must offer thanks concerning the affair of the land of Ṭāʾ. The religious scholars, who regard themselves as leaders and count themselves as guides and commanders, are at every moment ablaze with the fire of hostility. Each day its flame grows greater, its blaze more apparent, and its harm more intense. They have attacked the people of Bahāʾ a thousand times, yet their rancor has not subsided in the slightest, nor has their hatred abated. If one of those wicked souls obtained temporal power or became a ruler, what would he do? Now reflect on the leaders of the people and His Majesty the Sovereign. Although he was not pleased with this community, and the enemies, seeking to display their service, were occupied night and day with fabrications, His Majesty the Sovereign nevertheless, of his own noble inclination and without an intermediary, granted the prisoners freedom and bestowed release from confinement. The sincere, however, would not exchange a single moment of imprisonment and affliction suffered in the path of God for an entire world. I swear by the Educator of both worlds: every moment of imprisonment in the path of God is a blessing that has never had, and will never have, a likeness or an equal. In every circumstance, one must regard matters with justice and speak with fairness. This passing servant asks and hopes of God, exalted be His glory, that He may strengthen His Majesty the Sovereign at every moment and grant him success. He is powerful over all things.

14 What you wrote concerning Tablets for the sons of the clamorous one of the land of Kāf and Rā was presented in the Most Great Scene before the face of the Lord of destiny. This is what the Tongue of Grandeur spoke—His words, exalted and mighty is He: “The arrangement, before and since, has been that whenever anyone requests wondrous, exalted Tablets, they are revealed from the heaven of divine grace, so that perhaps the heedless may become aware and the sleeping may awaken. We previously left the decision as to its advisability to the Hands of the Cause in each locality, and gave them permission to act as wisdom and expediency require, so that the rule of consultation may become established and firmly rooted in the world.” End.

15 And He said: “How well they have both acted!”—meaning the honored Afnān and you, beloved friend. The request for a Tablet specifically for the friends of Bahrām (AB) was presented in the most inaccessible, most holy, most exalted court, and these resplendent verses shone from the horizon of the will of the Lord of names and attributes. His words, mighty be His utterance and exalted His proof:

16 He is the Speaker, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

17 O people of Bahrām, the Lord of humanity remembers you from His Most Great Prison and calls you to Himself. He is the One who holds sway over whatever He wills, and He is the Unique, the One, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed. He has come with the kingdom of verses and the banners of clear proofs, yet most people are heedless. The call has been raised, and all things have been drawn to it, yet most people are asleep. This is a day on which every seeker has sought the Desire of earth and heaven, every fair-minded soul the Most Exalted Horizon, and every sincere soul the presence of his All-Glorious Beloved. Blessed is this day, and blessed are those who have recognized it and arisen to serve the Cause of God, the Almighty, the Praiseworthy. We exhort you to remain steadfast in this Cause, so that the clamor of those who have disbelieved in God and turned away from His straight path may not deter you. We exhort you to remember and praise God, to turn toward Him with radiant hearts, and to do whatever will exalt His firm and steadfast Cause. Amīn—upon him be My glory—has mentioned you, and We have remembered you in truth. He is indeed the Most Bountiful, the Ever-Forgiving, the Generous.

18 O friends, time and again We informed the inhabitants of the divine cities of the emergence of clamorous voices, the denial of the deniers, and the schemes of the transgressors. Now, in some cities, what the Most Exalted Pen foretold is manifest and evident, and they are busy leading souls astray through a hundred thousand tricks and schemes.

19 Those souls who are illumined by the radiance of the Sun of Truth and have drunk from the Kawthar of meanings and the Salsabīl of utterance have attained a steadfastness that all the peoples of the world have never been, and are not now, able to overcome. Blessed is the soul who has held fast to the cord of loving-kindness and clung to the hem of divine grace.

20 For twelve hundred years, the learned of those regions and the jurists of the surrounding lands called the people to the Qāʾim and, with the utmost longing, implored the Lord of all horizons to grant them reunion with Him. Yet after all their striving, seeking, longing, and waiting, they denied His appearance and, with utter cruelty, cut down that blessed Tree, neither of the East nor of the West. Surely, the curse of God rests upon the people who do wrong.

21 Even now, some of those same souls have firmly girded themselves for wickedness in leading people astray. O people of God, arise to serve the Cause with wisdom and utterance, with such resolve that the winds of dissension can neither shake it nor cause it to falter. Appreciate the worth of these days and occupy yourselves with what is fitting. This day has never had, and does not have, an equal or a likeness. In the divine Books it is known as the Day of God. The throne of the Lord, exalted be His glory, has been established upon the white Spot.

22 With luminous hearts and radiant faces, occupy yourselves with the remembrance of the Lord of humanity, detached and free from the world and all that is in it. We ask God, exalted be His glory, not to deprive you of the ocean of Revelation or keep you from the sun of loving-kindness, and to assist everyone in a deed that will endure and remain as long as the earthly and heavenly kingdoms endure. He has power and might over all things. End.

23 This evanescent one asks God, exalted be His glory, to grant eyes a new vision and bestow upon ears a fresh power of listening, so that, with new eyes and fresh ears, they may hear the divine verses and behold His clear proofs. Today the enemies of God—that is, those who count themselves among the people of the Bayān and are the cause of His servants being led astray—assume every guise and appear in every form. Some, with turban, staff, robe, asceticism, and piety; others, with the utmost love and affection; and still others, by other means, have set themselves upon the helpless servants of God.

24 All the learned of Iran, except a few, despite all their asceticism, piety, sermons, and austerities, issued a ruling for the execution of the Lord of the world—may the souls of all who dwell in the kingdoms of revelation and creation be sacrificed for Him. The square in Ādharbāyjān bears witness, and beyond it everyone endowed with sight and hearing, and beyond them all, God, exalted be His glory. In short, on the ruling of those unjust souls, they suspended that Countenance of divine unity and put Him to death. Now, too, some of the same kind have appeared with insidious whisperings and satanic deceptions. May God strike them down, and may God protect us and you from their evil.

25 A Tablet has also been revealed specifically for the handmaidens of that land and region. His words—exalted be His majesty and glorious His utterance:

26 In the name of God, the All-Knowing, the All-Powerful.

27 O handmaidens of God in the land of Bāʾ and Hāʾ and its surroundings, reflect upon the waves of the ocean of God’s grace. He dwells in prison and, for the sake of God, calls you to the Most Exalted Horizon and guides you to the straight path. He has come from the unseen world into the visible so that He may enable all to partake of the outpourings of the true Bestower. Hold fast to the strong cord of God and turn toward the horizon of His Revelation. Whoever today attains recognition of the Beloved has attained all good. Pass beyond the court of the world and, with the utmost joy and gladness, enter the court of God. Whoever today looks toward the Most Exalted Horizon and remembers the Lord of Names is accounted by God among those admitted to His court. Whatever is seen will soon pass away. Strive to perform a deed from which the fragrance of eternity may be perceived. Give thanks to the Desire of the world, who has enabled you to recognize a Cause from which the world is veiled. He is indeed the Most Bountiful, the Generous. Praise be to Him, for He is the Lord of the worlds and the Desire of those who know Him. End.

28 Praise be to God, the rays of the sun of divine grace have embraced His handmaidens. The whole world longs to hear the words “O My servant,” and now the handmaidens of God have been favored with the blessed words “O My handmaiden.” This is a mighty and great bounty. Blessed is the soul who has understood and recognized it, and woe to the heedless.

29 The mention that was made of Ustād Aḥmad (K-F), upon him be the glory of God, was submitted at the most inviolate, most holy court of the Lord of all. This is what was revealed in reply. His words—exalted be His glory and all-embracing His bounty:

30 He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

31 O Aḥmad, the Desire of the world remembers you and gives you the glad tidings of the loving-kindness of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Blessed is the face that has turned toward Him, the heart that has inclined in His direction, the eye that has beheld the Most Exalted Horizon, and the tongue that has proclaimed before all faces, “There is no god but Him, the Unique, the One, the Almighty, the All-Loving.” We have indeed renewed the world through the breaths of revelation and brought forth the springtime of meanings, yet most of the people do not understand. The ocean of utterance has surged, and the All-Merciful has ascended the throne of proof, yet the people do not recognize Him. They have cast aside their God and taken hold of their own desires. Surely, they do not know. Blessed is the one who has shattered the idols of idle suppositions and believed in God, the Lord of all that has been and all that will be. O Aḥmad, a letter has arrived from Amīn, in which you and those who have believed in that land were mentioned. We have remembered you from this praiseworthy station. Recognize this most exalted station. By the life of God, nothing witnessed or seen can equal My remembrance. To this bears witness the One with whom is the sealed wine.

32 O Siyyid, whose name is followed by Hāshim, listen to the call of God. It rose once in Ṭāʾ, another time in Zawrāʾ, and again in the Land of Mystery and in this station, which has been designated by the most beautiful names by God, the Lord of humanity. To this bears witness the One who has testified that there is no god but Him, the Almighty, the Beloved. Rejoice in My remembrance, then speak the praise of your Lord at evening and at dawn.

33 And We remember the one named Siyyid followed by Muḥammad, that he may rejoice and be among the thankful. We have remembered those who have turned toward the Most Exalted Horizon, and We remember them as a mercy from Us, so that through it their remembrance may endure as long as God’s most beautiful names and most exalted attributes endure. There is no god but Him, the All-Powerful, the All-Knowing, the All-Informed. God has made His remembrance a treasure for His loved ones who have turned toward Him with radiant faces and acted according to what they were commanded in His perspicuous Book. O loved ones of the All-Merciful, We exhort you to safeguard your treasures. Thus admonishes you the One who possesses the knowledge of all things and around whom the Concourse on high circles in His noble station. And We remember My loved ones in that land who did not break the Covenant when the clamor arose. Surely, they are among the steadfast. Blessed is the servant whom the doubts raised by the wicked have not caused to slip, nor the insinuations of the spiteful deterred—those in whose breasts the cauldron of hatred boils and upon whose faces the dust of hell can be seen. The glory shining from the horizon of the heaven of My loving-kindness rest upon you, O people of God in that land, and upon those who circle the throne in My exalted station. End.

34 And this is what was revealed for Ustād Bāqir, upon him be the glory of God. His words—exalted be His glory, glorious His majesty, and great His station:

35 In My name, the Compassionate, the Generous.

36 Glory be to Him who has subjected the earthly and heavenly kingdoms to a command from Himself. He is the Strong, the All-Subduing, the All-Powerful. He does whatever He wills and ordains whatever He desires, and He is the Unique, the One, the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. O Bāqir, We have remembered you before and at this time. By My life, if you knew the station of My remembrance, such joy would seize you that neither human affairs nor the onslaught of the heedless could grieve you. Be steadfast in the Cause of your Lord and speak His glorious praise. This is what will benefit you and My loved ones in the world to come and in this world. To this bears witness the One who has ascended His mighty throne. Arise to serve My Cause and turn your heart toward My horizon. He indeed assists whoever turns toward Him with a luminous heart. My Most Exalted Pen has borne witness in your favor. Recognize this and be among the thankful. Beware lest the doings of the wicked veil you—those who speak according to their own desires and keep people from My straight path. Glory rest upon you and upon whoever has heard the call and answered the Lord of the lofty throne. End.

37 And this is what was revealed for Abū al-Qāsim, upon him be the glory of God. His words—glorious is His majesty and exalted His station:

38 In My name, the Speaker, the Trustworthy.

39 O Abū al-Qāsim, the Wronged One remembers you in a station around which the heavenly Concourse circles, and counsels you to be mindful of God. Your Lord is indeed the Counselor, the All-Knowing. He remembers whoever turns toward Him and desires whoever desires Him. He created faithfulness and made it one of His most beautiful names in His clear Book. Blessed is whoever holds fast to it; in the presence of the All-Merciful, he is among the horsemen in the arena of utterance. Every discerning knower bears witness to this. Rejoice in My remembrance of you, then thank your Lord, who created you, strengthened you, and guided you to His luminous horizon. We remember My loved ones there and give them the glad tidings of My mercy, which has preceded all who are in the heavens and the earths. The Trustworthy One has mentioned you, and We have remembered you. Give thanks to Him who rescued and saved you and revealed for you that from which those brought near perceive the fragrance of the mercy of your Lord, the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful. End.

40 And this is what was revealed for Mullā Ḥasan, upon him be the glory of God. His words—great are His signs and glorious His sovereignty:

41 He is the straight path.

42 A Book revealed by the Wronged One, in which He remembers whoever has turned toward God, the Lord of the world, and answered when His call arose from this station, which He has made one of the loftiest stations. Eyes were created to behold My horizon, ears to hear My call, and the tongue to remember Me, through which the horizons have been illumined. O Ḥasan, listen to the call of Him who spoke on Sinai in this Revelation, then thank your Lord for enabling you to recognize this Cause, through which feet have slipped. We named this day the Day of God in the Books of the past, yet most people remain heedless and veiled. The veils of glory have prevented them from looking toward the Most Exalted Horizon, and doubts have kept them from Him who has come from the heaven of power with the banners of the verses. Speak in remembrance of your Lord and act according to what you have been commanded in the Scriptures and Tablets. Blessed is the thirsty one who has sought the Euphrates, and the one who has turned his face toward God, the Sovereign of all religions. Say: Set aside conjectures and vain imaginings, hastening toward the kingdom of certitude with joy and gladness. Thus have the rains of My mercy poured from the heaven of My loving care. Bliss to whoever has attained, and woe to every heedless doubter. Glory from Our presence rest upon those whom the veils of the learned have not veiled and whom the might of those who have turned away from the Origin and the Return has not frightened.

43 And this is what was revealed for Ghulām Riḍā from God, the Lord of Names. His words—glorious is His majesty and exalted His utterance:

44 In My name, the Guardian over all names.

45 O Ghulām Riḍā, the smoke of doubt and the dust of vain imaginings have kept the world from the Sovereign of humankind. The veils of self and desire have so seized the people that they cannot distinguish good from evil or right from wrong. They associate partners with God yet consider themselves believers in His unity; they worship idols yet count themselves among those of spiritual rank. Heedlessness has reached such a degree that they have exchanged the everlasting blessing for fleeting favors. They have forsaken whatever God—glorious is His majesty—has desired for all, and are occupied with the cravings of self and desire. Shame on them and on what their hands have earned! Praise the Desire of all peoples, who has enabled you to turn toward Him. Appreciate this great favor, and in His name guard the pearls of divine love from treacherous eyes and thieving souls. This is the counsel of the Most Exalted Pen in this dark night. Listen and be among the thankful. Praise be to God, the Lord of all worlds.

46 And this is what was revealed for ʿAlī Akbar (s-m-s) from God, the Sovereign of destiny. His words—blessed and exalted is He:

47 He is the Hearer, and He is the Answerer.

48 O people of God, today it is incumbent on everyone to remember God—glorious is His majesty—and to serve the Cause. Through centuries and ages the people awaited this blessed Day, yet when the door of the All-Merciful’s loving care was opened with the key of utterance, and the promised Beauty, who spoke on Sinai, revealed Himself to save the world and guide the nations, all were seen to be veiled—indeed, thunderstruck, indeed, dead—except for a small number who are mentioned among the letters of the Face in the divine Book. 103 You had also mentioned the honored lady, the handmaid of God, Bíbí Ṣáḥib, upon her be the glory of God. This is what was sent down for her from the kingdom of the utterance of our Lord, the All-Merciful. His words, exalted be His glory and all-embracing His bounty:

104 In the name of the One, the All-Knowing.

105 O My handmaid! God, exalted be His glory, is kind. His bounty encompasses all, as does His knowledge. No voice is raised without an answer descending from the kingdom of the All-Merciful’s utterance. He has adorned women with the blessed words “O My handmaid” and clothed men in the wondrous and exalted words “O My servant.” How many among the peoples of earlier and later times have laid down their lives to hear these words! These words are like a sapling planted by the hand of power. It must be watered with the water of love and steadfastness and kept safe, in the name of God, from scorching winds. Appreciate the worth of these words. By the life of God, if the whole world were to understand, all would be set aflame and turn toward the most exalted horizon. We convey Our greetings to all the handmaids in that land and exhort them all to holiness, purity, and action in accordance with what We have sent down in the Book. Blessed are the men and women who act accordingly. End of quotation.

106 You had mentioned Ḥájí Muḥammad Ḥusayn, upon him be the glory of God. The mention of him was honored with a hearing in the most holy, most inaccessible court. This is what was sent down for him by the All-Wise Revealer. His words, exalted be His remembrance and glorious His utterance:

107 In My name, shining from the most exalted horizon.

108 Every soul who has attained recognition today is remembered before the All-Merciful in the kingdom of utterance. The entire world was created for the Day of God, yet when the Most Great Name rent the veil through His all-subduing power, all were found heedless and veiled, except those whom God willed. Today is the day when whatever is concealed within every soul becomes manifest and plain. The day when secrets are laid bare is today, and the day when the mountains pass away is today. Every hidden thing is revealed, and everything concealed is made plain. Blessed is the soul who has been set ablaze with the fire of divine love, with such a flame that neither the might, power, and majesty of the people of creation nor the doubts and insinuations of the learned have kept that soul from the Lord of Names or deprived it of the ocean of meanings. Every deed performed today in the path of God has an influence that endures as long as the earthly and heavenly kingdoms endure. O people of God, strive to attain what befits the Day of the True One, exalted be His glory. Appreciate the utterance of the All-Merciful and guard it as you would your own life. Glory from Us be upon you and upon My loved ones there, whom the might of the aggressors has not frightened and the power of those who turn away has not weakened. End.

109 You had mentioned Áqá Siyyid Ṣádiq, Áqá Siyyid Yúsuf, Áqá Abu’l-Qásim, and Áqá Siyyid ʿAlí-Riḍá, upon them be the glory of God. This was presented in His presence, and for each one in particular there was revealed that which will endure as long as the most beautiful names endure. This is what was sent down for Siyyid Ṣádiq. His words, exalted be His majesty:

110 He is the Everlasting, the Enduring, the Eternal, the One.

111 A remembrance from Us for the one whom Amín has mentioned, that he may rejoice in the loving care of his Lord and be among the thankful. Whoever has attained My verses today has indeed attained all good. To this bears witness the One with whom is a mighty Book. O Ṣádiq, We have turned toward you from the direction of the prison and have sent down for you what guides the servants to My straight path. Say: O people, set aside what you possess. What was concealed in knowledge and inscribed in the Book of God, the Lord of the worlds, has now appeared. Thus have We sent down the verses, expounded them in truth, and sent them to you as a gift from your Beloved, the All-Beauteous.

112 And We remember Yúsuf as a token of Our grace, and I am the Most Bountiful, the All-Generous. O Yúsuf, leave aside what the people possess and take what you have been given by your Lord, the Most Bountiful, the Ancient. Do not reckon the treasures of the earth equal to a single letter of My verses. Every discerning soul endowed with insight bears witness to this. Glory from Us be [upon those who] have not broken God’s covenant and His pledge. Truly, they are numbered among the steadfast in a clear Book.

113 And this is what was sent down for Abu’l-Qásim. His words, glorified and exalted be He:

114 He is the Compassionate, the Merciful.

115 O Abu’l-Qásim, appreciate the value of these days and arise to do what is fitting. Today, good character and pure deeds are the means of exalting the Word of God. All the friends must assist the wronged True One through these and, in every circumstance, seek to exalt the Cause. This is Our counsel, before and hereafter. We ask God to set everyone ablaze with a fire that neither the oppression of the oppressors nor the clamor of the ignorant can restrain. Whoever has attained a single drop of this ocean, the armies of the world and the cannons of the nations have never been, nor are they now, capable of holding back. The All-Knowing God bears witness and speaks this exalted word: “Blessed is everyone who hears and responds.”

116 And this is what was sent down for Siyyid ʿAlí-Riḍá. His words, sanctified and exalted be He:

117 In My name, manifest from the Most Exalted Horizon.

118 A Tablet sent down by the Wronged One in the Most Great Prison for the one mentioned by one of My loved ones who has arisen to serve My mighty Cause. O servants of the All-Merciful, listen to the call of Him who summons you to the Unique, the All-Aware. The Day of Return has come, yet most people are heedless; the promised return has come, yet most of the people are asleep. They have cast the commandments of God behind them. Truly, they are among those who have suffered the greatest loss. Thank God for having enabled you to acknowledge what My Most Exalted Pen has spoken, and remember Him by night and day. Your Lord is indeed the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. The Promised One has come, the Hidden One has appeared, and the Tongue of Grandeur has proclaimed: “Sovereignty belongs to God, the Lord of the worlds.” Now that you have attained My verses and drunk the choice wine of My utterance from the hands of My bounty, say: “Praise be to You, O Desire of the world, and glory be to You, O Beloved of those who know You.” End.

119 The sprays of the ocean of meanings and utterance have encompassed the world. Were every atom to offer praise with the tongues of all who came before and all who are to come, it would assuredly never have been, nor ever be, adequate to this grace or worthy of this bounty. Praise be to God, the friends of this age have attained that for which multitudes among earlier and later generations were sorrowful and grief-stricken in their longing to attain it. This greatest joy has been the portion of the people of the Most Great Scene. This transient one asks and hopes of the everlasting God that He will deprive no one on earth of this enduring bounty and heavenly table. He is indeed the One who hears and answers, and He is the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful.

120 And your mention of Áqá Muḥammad ʿAlí Simsár, upon him be the glory of God, was presented before the face of the Lord of Eternity. This is what was sent down for him from the heaven of the loving care of our Lord, the Ever-Forgiving, the All-Generous. His words, exalted be His utterance and mighty His proof:

121 He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

122 O Muḥammad before ʿAlí, the True One, exalted be His glory, has appeared, guided everyone to the straight path, and summoned them to the Most Great Announcement promised in the books and scriptures, earlier and later. Selfish desires kept some of the servants from the Dawning-Place of the light of oneness, while following the ignorant deprived others of recognizing the Lord of Names. The primary cause and reason for all this have been the learned of the age. They are the dawning-places and daysprings of self and desire. A few, however, turned their eyes away from the world and all it contains and directed themselves toward the Most Exalted Horizon. They are the treasures of the earth and its treasuries. Every soul whom the veils of the people of doubt have not held back today is mentioned and recorded in the Book of Names as one of the inhabitants of the highest Paradise. Amín mentioned a number of those who have turned toward Us, and each has received the loving care of the Wronged One. [He is indeed the Ever-Forgiving,] the All-Generous. He is indeed the All-Loving, the Merciful. End.

123 And this transient servant beseeches the True One, exalted be His glory, to assist them and the other friends to preserve what He has bestowed. He is powerful and mighty over all things. This servant’s plea is that every one of those who have turned toward Him, who have drunk the sealed wine and attained a new life, should keep the word of steadfastness before their eyes, for the devils and deceitful souls are many. The hope is that each may be aided to do what befits the Day of God.

124 You had also mentioned Áqá Siyyid Javád Pusht-Bághí, upon him be 9 66 [the glory of God]. After this matter was presented in His presence, these verses descended from the heaven of loving care. His words, whose mercy has preceded all things and whose bounty is all-embracing:

125 He is the Witness, the All-Aware.

126 O Javád, the Wronged One says: We accepted all the afflictions of the earth so that people might ascend to the highest summit and the Most Exalted Horizon. Yet when the call was raised and the fire of divine love was kindled, heedless souls arose to quench it and obstruct it, and with utter oppression and every effort they strove to extinguish the light of God. But the hand of power protected His Cause and awakened holy souls. Say: O friends, the devils are lying in wait at their watchposts. Each of you must, like a piercing meteor, prevent the devils from ascending into the heavens of the hearts. This is the first station and foremost rank that has been revealed by the Most Exalted Pen. Blessed is the one who has attained, and woe to the heedless. In every circumstance look to God and hold fast to Him. He is with those of His servants who have attained. Praise be to God, the Lord of the worlds. End.

127 This is what was sent down for the son of the one who ascended to God (Áqá Mírzá Muḥsin, Ṭ-B), upon them both be the glory of God.

128 In My name, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

129 O Ḥusayn-ʿAlī, listen to the call: there is no God but Me, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. I created all creation to recognize My own Self and attain My Day, yet when I came with the banners of the signs, they turned away because they followed every heedless outcast. Say: O people of Bahā, put aside what you have been forbidden in the Book, and embrace what you have been commanded by God, the Master of the promised Day. Sanctify yourselves from all that your desires command you to do. Thus We commanded you before in My inscribed Tablet. Blessed is the one who has cast aside vain imaginings, holding fast to the mountain of God, the Almighty, the All-Loving. Blessed is the one who has clung to the hem of My robe and acted on what was commanded by the True One, the Knower of all that is hidden. Remind those with you on My behalf. Say: Fear God and do not be among those who do not know. The Guide has come and the path has appeared, yet most people are unaware. Say: By God, the Day has appeared, and the One who spoke on Sinai speaks upon the throne of revelation. Blessed is the one who has heard and attained, and woe to every heedless one who is veiled. Glory from Us be upon your father, who ascended to My horizon and attained My grace, My mercy, and My loving care, which have encompassed the unseen and the seen; and upon you and those with you who have not broken God’s covenant and pledge, and who have testified to what God testified when He came with manifest sovereignty. End.

130 The mention you made of the brothers from Alif and Rā—Naṣru’llāh, Ḥusayn, and Muḥammad ʿAlī, upon them be the glory of God—was submitted in the presence of the Lord of Revelation. This is what was sent down for them from the heaven of the grace of our Lord, the Almighty, the All-Bestowing. His words—glorious is His utterance and exalted His station:

131 He is the Ruler in truth, and He is the All-Wise Ordainer.

132 O servants of the All-Merciful, the Desire of all existence remembers you in this Most Great Prison and counsels you to do what brings greatness and nobility. The power of extinction has seized the world, and its effects are evident and manifest at every moment. The friends who are associated with God and known by His name must, through the power and strength of the one Beloved, undertake deeds from which everyone endowed with a sense of smell may perceive the fragrance of holiness and purity. Today is the day for service and pure deeds. We ask God to sanctify everyone from the stain of unacceptable deeds and enable them to attain true repose. Praise the Beloved, who has illumined you with the rays of the sun of utterance. Amīn has mentioned you, and We have remembered you as a grace from Us. I am the Most Bountiful, the All-Generous. End.

133 And this is what was sent down for the loved ones of God in Dāl and Hā. His words—exalted is His glory and mighty His utterance:

134 He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.

135 O people of God, from the direction of the Prison the Wronged One reminds you of what will draw you near to God, the Lord of the worlds. The ocean of utterance has surged and the fragrance of the All-Merciful has spread, yet most people are heedless. The sweet waters of mercy have flowed among creation, yet most people deny them. They have turned away from the Most Exalted Horizon, turning instead toward every ignorant one who is far removed. Blessed is the one who has shattered the idols of desire and believed in God, the One, the All-Informed.

136 We remember the one who turned toward the Most Exalted Horizon and crossed land and sea until he arrived in a valley where the call of God, the Almighty, the All-Praised, was raised. He came, stood at the door, and attained the privilege of listening to the utterance of his Lord, the Almighty, the All-Beautiful. We testify that he reached his destination, attained the meeting, and drank the choice wine of the bounty of his Lord, the Compassionate, the All-Generous.

137 And We remember his son, who is named Badīʿ, and counsel him to do what befits a human being. I am the All-Knowing Counsellor. O Badīʿ, your Lord remembers you in such a way that your remembrance will endure as long as creation endures. Your Lord is indeed the Ever-Forgiving, the All-Merciful. Glory be upon you, upon those with you, and upon those who have attained this lofty station.

138 And We remember ʿAlī-Akbar, who perceived the fragrance of revelation and turned toward the One from whom every ignorant doubter turned away. And We remember his brother, who is named Mūsā, and give him the glad news of My mercy, which has preceded all who are in the heavens and the earths. We counsel everyone to do what will exalt the Cause of God among His servants and what befits this mighty and wondrous Day.

139 Amīn has mentioned you, and We have remembered you so that you may thank your Lord, the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent. O people of Dāl and Hā, rejoice that God has raised up from among you His name, who was imprisoned in His path, bore hardships out of love for His beauty, and spoke His praise when the people were manifestly veiled. And We remember My servants and My handmaidens there, give them the glad news of My loving care and grace, and counsel them to act with pure intent and to observe what the All-Merciful has sent down in His clear Book. Glory be upon them, men and women, and upon everyone who is steadfast and upright. End.

140 Praise be to God, everyone who has turned toward Him has attained his reward—indeed, more than that—and everyone who is steadfast is adorned with his recompense. Most of the friends remain in their own homes and gatherings, while God—exalted is His glory—in prison, despite the sorrows that have befallen Him and the emergence of displeasing affairs, is occupied night and day in remembering His loved ones: a remembrance that extinction will never touch and change will never overtake. In the words of the renowned Shaykh, upon him be God’s mercy and forgiveness: “Whose hand and tongue can ever offer the thanks He is due?”

141 I swear by the Sun of Truth, if the whole world were to voice thanks and praise for as long as the earthly and heavenly kingdoms endure, they would not be, nor have they ever been, worthy of a single letter flowing from the Most Exalted Pen. If the station of divine remembrance were revealed, everyone would voice what this mortal has said. The call is raised, but ears are few. The rider is present, but the field is narrow. One day the veils will be torn away and the hidden stations will become manifest and plain.

142 What you wrote concerning Khān Sarkār Sīn Rā, upon him be the glory of God and His loving care, and your mention of what was placed in his saddlebag were submitted before the face of the Lord of Eternity. His words—exalted is His glory:

143 In My name, the Witness, the All-Knowing.

144 O Amīn, We were with him. Your Lord hears and sees, and He is the All-Knowing, the All-Informed. He has always arisen to serve the Cause and has attained a remembrance that will remain and endure as long as the earthly and heavenly kingdoms endure. Today, from the murmur of the water of life and the rustling of the Tree of Utterance, these exalted, blessed words were heard: “Blessed is the one who has attained, discovered, recognized, and arisen to serve the Cause of God, the Lord of the worlds.” Every created thing has testified to his faithfulness to the pledge and covenant. This station is very great. From this station We send him and those with him the greeting of God’s greatness. If you see him, remember him on My behalf and give him the glad news of My loving care, of what has flowed from My Pen, and of what My tongue has testified in this inaccessible station. Glory be upon him and upon everyone who is patient, steadfast, and upright. End.

145 Truly, at this point the breezes of the verses carried this servant beyond mention, thought, and imagination. The verses of God are being sent down and flowing forth at this moment in such a manner that a hundred like this mortal have not been, and never will be, capable of recounting or recording them. I speak truthfully, and God is sufficient as a witness. And how much joy arose on seeing that the gaze of loving care was directed toward Khān, upon him be the glory of God. This servant conveys to him greetings, the salutation of God’s greatness, and remembrance, and at every moment asks God—exalted is His glory—to assist him. He is worthy of everyone’s love. Should God will, He will reveal what has been ordained for him. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the Omnipotent.

146 Your mention of arriving in Sīrjān and meeting Ḥājī Siyyid Ḥā Sīn, upon him be the glory of God alone, attained the honor of being heard before the face of the Lord of Names. His words—exalted is His glory: Some of the friends should travel through the villages and, with the utmost wisdom, engage in refining heedless souls, so that perhaps they may not remain deprived in the days of God. Today the greatest grace is manifest. Everyone must engage in guiding souls.

147 Blessed is My Afnān, who was remembered in My court and attained My presence when the light was shining from the horizon of Zawrāʾ. For Ḥaydar-ʿAlī, upon him be My glory and loving care, mentioned his teaching by proxy and likewise the visit. This undertaking is truly assisted and has attained God’s acceptance. End.

148 The aforementioned Ḥājī was favored with mention by God, exalted be His glory. Convey greetings of praise to him on behalf of this lowly one. He should endeavor to spread the Word of God in that land. How many souls have never heard a word of outward learning, yet, set ablaze by the fire of love, have opened their lips and been aided in promoting the Cause and refining souls. Today the Holy Spirit grants assistance. All the friends must treat all the world with love and friendship. In this way love will arise and hearts will become receptive. That is the time to impart the Word, but with joy and gladness. The matter rests in the hands of God, our Lord, the All-Merciful.

149 You had mentioned the land of Shīn and the friends of God, upon them be the glory of God. Each was favored with divine loving care, and a most exalted, most holy Tablet was revealed especially for Ḥājī Shukrullāh, upon him be the glory of God, and given to my beloved Āqā Mīrzā Ḥusayn, upon him be the glory of God and His loving care, to deliver. At this moment the Tongue of Eternity speaks these blessed words—His words, exalted be His glory:

150 “O Abū al-Ḥasan! O Amīn! You have mentioned the friends of God. Praise be to God, you have been aided. Through the grace and loving care of God, you have drunk time and again from the ocean of reunion, tasted the Kawthar of the All-Merciful’s utterance, beheld the Most Exalted Horizon, and been aided in speaking well of the faithful, the chosen ones, and the friends of God. We ask God to assist you always and to confirm you in whatever serves to honor His Cause, exalted be His glory. By the life of God! If someone were to give away the treasures of the world, or all souls were to be sacrificed, and this diminished the honor of the Cause of God by even an atom, it would not be permissible, nor has it ever been. In all circumstances, keep your gaze fixed on honoring the Cause of God, exalting the Word of God, and elevating its station. This is what We have enjoined upon you before, in Our presence, and on this night in which the Tongue of Grandeur proclaims: Sovereignty belongs to God, the Lord of the worlds.” End.

151 This lowly one sends a hundred thousand greetings of peace and praise to you, my beloved, and to the friends of God, and asks God, exalted be His glory, to aid everyone in supporting His Cause and to grant them success. He is indeed powerful over all things. Glory, exaltation, and praise rest upon you, upon those who are with you, and upon those who listen to your words concerning the Cause of God, the Lord of the worlds.

152 On another matter, this lowly one has repeatedly received from the spiritual friend Āqā Muḥammad Ṭāhir, upon him be the glory of God, and some of the friends, that which has brought him joy, for they speak in remembrance of God, exalted be His glory, and are adorned with devotion, humility, reverence, and readiness to serve for the sake of God, our Lord and the Lord of all who are in the heavens and on earth. I convey greetings of praise and peace to everyone, and hope that, God willing, all may receive His special loving care.

153 The mother of Āqā Muḥammad Ṭāhir, upon them both be the glory of God, has attained the honor of His presence in these days when the palace is the seat of the Throne. He does what He wills and ordains what He desires, and He is the All-Powerful, the Almighty. The queens of the world, despite seeking and waiting, did not attain it, while this honored lady, through the wondrous gifts of divine grace, has attained what most of the people of the world died longing for.

154 Likewise, in these days a letter arrived from Ḥājī Āqā Muḥammad (ʿAlāqih), upon him be the glory of God. It was presented in the most exalted, most holy court, and he was favored with mention by God, exalted be His glory. Blessed is he! It is hoped that in the future, too, he will send that which is like the spirit of inner meanings to the bodies of the sincere. Praise be to God, the Lord of the worlds, and glory rest upon those who are near to Him and the sincere.

155 As commanded, the revealed Tablets were also copied out separately and sent, in this lowly one’s handwriting except for one most exalted, most holy Tablet intended for the physician. It would be most welcome if someone with a good hand there could copy the Tablets sent and exercise the utmost care in checking and collating them. They should also be kept in a safe place, for the divine verses are scattered in various places. It would therefore be welcome and acceptable for whatever arrives in that land, and in every land, to be collected.

156 The servant, on 2 Jumādā al-Ūlā, in the year [1302].
