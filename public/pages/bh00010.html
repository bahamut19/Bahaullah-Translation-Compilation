1 In the name of our Lord, the Most Holy, the Most Great, the Most Exalted, the All-Glorious.

2 Praise be to God, who is peerless in love and unique in affection. He is indeed the Lord of all servants, the Master of creation, who speaks whatever He wills in the beginning and the return. May the fragrance wafting from the garment of God, the light shining from the horizon of His loving care, and the peace manifest and radiant from the kingdom of His grace rest upon those who have arisen to exalt His Word and make His Cause manifest, and have acted as they were commanded in His Book. These are servants through whom the commandments and laws have gained currency among humanity and the rule of sanctity has been established among its peoples. Through them the banners have been raised and the signs have appeared. The Concourse on high will surely bless them, and beyond them, the Lord of Names and Creator of heaven.

3 To continue: from the horizon of the heaven of your affection there has shone forth that which has stirred the winds of love. It is indeed a letter in which the vessel of your innate nature has overflowed. When I perceived its fragrance and recognized your condition in the Cause of God, my Lord and your Lord and the Lord of all who are in the heavens and on earth, I set out for the praised station where the throne of the Lord of existence, the Sovereign of the unseen and the seen, is established. I entered His presence and presented what the nightingale of your sincerity had sung upon the branch of faithfulness. Then the face of the Lord of Names turned toward me. By God, He spoke words that made the mountains take flight, yet nothing was imprinted upon the mirror of my heart except in the measure of my affliction, lowliness, poverty, and need. O beloved of my heart, how can a drop encompass the ocean of utterance? By the life of my Beloved and yours, I speak only the truth. I tell you truly, and that Beloved of the heart bears witness and testimony, for through the grace of God He has been and remains aware.

4 He said—exalted be His grandeur and great His majesty:

5 In the name of the Wronged One of the world.

6 O ʿAlī, praise be to God, the sight of the world is illumined by the kohl of the wisdom and utterance of the loved ones of God. Although the dark clouds of the people’s vain imaginings have prevented this light from appearing and becoming manifest, the winds of His will shall assuredly disperse these clouds. He is powerful and mighty, and His power, though concealed today, is secretly engaged in conquering the cities of hearts. The utterance of the All-Merciful is a mighty key that unlocks, a sun shining and radiant, and an all-encompassing ocean. It unlocks, sheds light, and bestows life. Blessed is the discerning one whom doubt and uncertainty, vain imaginings and conjectures, and the power and wrath of the people of the world have not kept from the loftiest summit. Through one word the world is thrown into turmoil, and through another it becomes still and assured; through one deed it is set ablaze, and through another it becomes coolness and peace. Thus, in the scriptures and tablets, the Most Exalted Pen has numbered spiritual character, pleasing deeds, and a good word among the mighty hosts of God. Beyond all these considerations, if people hold fast to fairness, they themselves will attain the lights of certitude and behold the signs, proofs, and testimonies of God—exalted be His glory—as clearly as the sun, and the throne of God’s power will also be seen, manifest and resplendent, amidst the world.

7 O Akbar, convey greetings of praise to the friends and tell them on behalf of the Wronged One: You must be distinguished from the other people of the world and at all times keep distinction in view. Know your own worth and recognize the station of the loved ones of God who have arisen to serve from the beginning of the Cause. The Most Exalted Pen has mentioned everyone and adorned them with robes of loving care, yet from the tablets of steadfast, unwavering souls the fragrance of distinction is diffused. Blessed is the one who has perceived it and is among those who act.

8 O you who look toward God, I testify that you drank the choice wine of My love in the earliest days of My revelation, acknowledged what God acknowledged, held fast to it, spread His writings, and stood ready to serve Him on a day when feet faltered. Convey greetings of praise on My behalf to My loved ones in Ṭāʾ and those around it, and give them the glad tidings of the loving care of God, the Lord of the worlds.

9 We counsel them to observe pure godliness and to do whatever exalts the Cause of God, the All-Knowing, the All-Aware. Say: Have mercy on yourselves, on the Tree of the Cause, and on the blood shed to exalt the Word of God, which all must obey. Fear God, O people, and be fair-minded. Do not squander your deeds and what you have endured for this mighty Announcement. You may well take pride before all who dwell on earth, for you have attained what no one else in creation has attained. To this bears witness He whose mention the Mother Book has voiced, the Almighty, the Wondrous. Do not make yourselves the playthings of the ignorant of the earth. Every deed attributed to you should shine like the sun amidst the world. Thus God admonishes you with true admonition, as a mercy from Him. He loves you and desires for you what befits your stations before God, the Most Exalted, the Most Great. Beware of doing anything that would hold back mercy in the heaven of His will. Thus counsels you One who is more compassionate toward you than you are toward yourselves. Your Lord, the All-Merciful, is indeed the All-Knowing, the All-Wise. May the glory shining from the horizon of My loving care and the light gleaming from the heaven of My mercy rest upon you and upon those who are with you and love you in the path of God, the Lord of this blessed and manifest Day. End.

10 God willing, all the friends of God will be adorned with the garment of sanctity. They should conduct themselves in such a way that, were they to arise in the assembly of the world to admonish and counsel its people and speak with the utmost eloquence, they would neither be inwardly ashamed nor give anyone on earth grounds for objection. Now, night and day, they should devise ways to guide a soul and give it a taste of the choice wine of recognition. These are not days for divided thought, faltering will, impure intent, inconsistent deeds, or harsh character. All deeds, actions, and qualities should be seen as a single point; that is, the focus of them all should be known, so that they may point to the true Object of recognition and reflect Him. All have been and remain beneath the gaze of His loving care. There is no one who has not attained the crown of remembrance from God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting.

He continually says that, if they recognize the Summoner and taste the sweetness of the All-Merciful’s utterance, they will count the world as nothing and direct their gaze toward whatever exalts the Cause, cleaving and holding fast to it.

12 In any case, this servant conveys to each one greetings of peace and praise, in self-effacement and nothingness, and with the utmost helplessness and supplication begs these mines of the jewels of divine love to keep their lofty stations in view and act as befits them. The plea and the helplessness are this servant’s; acceptance rests with the loved ones of God, the Almighty, the Beloved.

13 The mention you had made of the two devoted souls, the honored Āqā Siyyid Muḥammad and Ustād Muḥammad-Qulī—may the glory of God rest upon them both—and likewise of their work in the path of God, was presented in the most exalted, most holy, most inaccessible Court. For each of them the sun of loving care, the moon of tenderness, the star of compassion, and the exalted Word appeared and shone forth. His words—exalted and glorified be He:

14 In My name, the Summoner in the world.

Rejoice and be among the thankful. At this moment We remember you once again as a bounty from Our presence. Your Lord is indeed the Almighty, the All-Bountiful.

What you have done in My path has attained My good pleasure and the ornament of My acceptance. Your Lord is indeed the All-Knowing, the All-Wise.

17 Blessed are you and your work, and whoever has acted as commanded in My clear Book. Through your arising to serve Me and your work out of love for Me, the Concourse on high has proclaimed: “Blessed is the one who has been faithful to the Covenant of God on this blessed and wondrous Day.” May the glory, shining, manifest, radiant, and resplendent, rest upon you and upon every steadfast servant.

18 And this is what was sent down from the dominion of grace for the honored Ustād Muḥammad-Qulī, may the glory of God rest upon him. His words—exalted be His majesty:

19 In My name, the Guardian over the outward and the inward.

20 O Muḥammad-Qulī, the Wronged One remembers you from where He dwells, reminds you of His covenant, and sends down for you what you have done while holding fast to His Book. Blessed are you for having remained faithful and done what you were commanded out of love for Him. How many a servant has clung to the paths of delusion, turning away from His path, and how many a servant has cast the world behind him, turning toward the lights of His face! Rejoice that He has enabled you to do what draws you closer to Him in every circumstance. Happy are you for having turned to God and perceived the fragrance of His garment. We have remembered you before and afterward. Your Lord rewards whoever holds fast to the cord of His loving care. May the glory shining from the horizon of the heaven of grace rest upon you and upon whoever follows His command. End of quotation.

21 Blessed and happy are they both, for, as God bears witness, the fragrance of sincerity wafts from them, and with joy and gladness they have done what they were commanded. God willing, may you preserve this most exalted station in the name of the True One, exalted be His glory. This servant, who is passing away, asks and hopes that the exalted True One will grant them supreme steadfastness. He has power over all things. Conveying greetings and salutations to them depends upon the will and kindness of that beloved friend.

22 What you wrote concerning the honored leaf Ḥāʾ-Vāv, may the glory of God rest upon her, was presented. He said: “Excellent is the kindness shown to her.” God willing, may they be assisted in performing good deeds and whatever reflects pure wisdom. God willing, may the relatives receive the loving care of the True One and be adorned with supreme steadfastness, so that the clamors of calamity may not keep them from the path of divine unity.

23 O ʿAlī-Akbar, upon you be My glory, My mercy, and My loving care. Consider, then speak of a woman who returned to her abode. She exchanged light for fire and sold the King of humanity for a piece of diamond. Despite all her love, devotion, and claims to recognition, the calamity deceived her with a diamond flower. She has turned away from the True One and toward another. Such is the condition of the world and its people. From the beginning of the Cause she has turned away and has never had, nor does she now have, any understanding, for she has not been with Us. The meaningless deference and humility of the gentlemen of Qāf induced those souls to profess belief. He knows what is within the breasts and what is within the hearts, and He is the All-Knowing, the All-Informed.

24 If a single leaf among the leaves reflects upon her actions, deeds, and conduct, she will speak and say, “I am free of her and of everything she possesses,” and will shield her face from hers. You know that the Most Exalted Pen had refrained from mentioning those souls. Yet, in view of the oppression, transgression, and flame of selfish desire, these words have flowed and descended from the Most Exalted Pen, and this too is so that they may recognize her and become acquainted with her actions and deeds. May God protect us and you from her and others like her. End of quotation.

25 This passing soul asks and hopes that the True One, exalted be His glory, will guide the friends in that land to the straight path of trustworthiness, integrity, truthfulness, and purity, so that all may rest at ease beneath the shelter of the True One and His loving care.

26 On one occasion this exalted utterance was heard from the tongue of the Lord of Names. He said: “O servant who is present, everything has had and has an originating principle. The saying that ordinary people have repeated and still repeat, ‘Without leaven, dough remains unleavened,’ is entirely sound; it is the saying of a wise and discerning person. Today the companions of God are the leaven of the peoples of the world. All should draw upon their trustworthiness, truthfulness, steadfastness, deeds, and character. Ask the True One that they may perceive the fragrance of awareness and hold fast to what will elevate their standing and stations.” End of quotation.

27 Some of the dissenters among the people of the Bayān have objected that, despite the claim to truth, His companions practice wickedness and indecency, whereas the companions of the Primal Point practice piety and are adorned with trustworthiness and integrity. This statement departs from the path of truth and honesty, for, as this servant knows, ever since the Ancient Beauty arrived in Iraq, verses and utterances were continually revealed and dispatched in the utmost secrecy, all enjoining what God willed and forbidding what He prohibited in the Book.

28 Some time ago the Tongue of Grandeur spoke these exalted words: “From Iraq, the Land of Mystery, and the Land of the Prison, We continually counseled the people of the Bayān and commanded what befits the Cause of God. Still, rebellion and corruption have not ceased. Most remain defiled and have not attained purity and sanctity.” End of quotation.

29 That beloved friend is himself aware of the atrocious deeds that appeared among this group. Truly, one cannot describe them. No one had control over his own wife, children, possessions, or property. Most were rebellious, insubordinate, and tyrannical, yet considered themselves among the sincere companions of God. A few were seen to be free of such conduct and suspicions, and that only at the beginning of the Cause. Afterward, you yourself witnessed what they did and what happened, to such an extent that the Most Exalted One—may the life of all else be sacrificed for Him—expressed astonishment. Outwardly, however, He said nothing, for there was no supporter or helper. In His last days He wrote a Tablet in which He complained most strongly of those souls and expressed dissatisfaction with the majority. It was, however, kept concealed, since wisdom did not permit its disclosure.

30 People are weak. As was witnessed, despite the radiance of the Sun of Truth at high noon and the appearance of the Tree beyond which there is no passing on the right side of the White Spot, each person, following his own desire, presented a delusion in the guise of certainty. In any event, were we to discuss these matters, sheets and volumes would not suffice.

31 In this Most Great Revelation, however, even the enemies have testified and continue to testify to purity, sanctity, loftiness, and exaltation. One of the divines of Yazd had said: “At the beginning of the turmoil, the deeds of this community were beyond description. Now they are seen gathering in assemblies, reciting verses and prayers, and displaying the utmost trustworthiness and integrity. Evidently this is at the command of the new leader.” Consider how many souls in this Revelation freely drank the cup of martyrdom, and how many sacrificed themselves by their own hands, although the True One did not approve. Even now there are souls who are pure, sanctified, and free from every fault. Our Lord, the All-Knowing, is indeed the Witness, the All-Informed.

32 This servant will relate one account of trustworthiness and fidelity to a pledge, so that perhaps some may take heed and know with certainty that beneath His canopies God has servants possessed of great trustworthiness, good deeds, and spiritual qualities. In Alexandria, several of the friends of God were engaged in earning a livelihood in accordance with the command, and in this Most Great Revelation earning a livelihood has been accounted worship. We ask Him to enable everyone to fulfill what He has willed, and

33 The account is this: the honored Āqā Shaykh ʿAlī, son of the honored Ḥājī ʿAbd al-Raḥīm, may the glory of God rest upon them both, went to Khartoum to keep company with the beloved of the heart, the honored Ḥājī Mīrzā Ḥaydar ʿAlī, and the spiritual friend, the honored Ḥāʾ and Sīn, may the glory of God rest upon them both. The aforementioned Shaykh resolved to go and set out for the sake of God.

The two aforementioned names, may the praise of God rest upon them both, turned toward this region and attained their desire, while the honored Shaykh remained in that land earning a livelihood.

Owing to the unsettled state of affairs, however, this was prolonged until the honored Shaykh, may the glory of God rest upon him, ascended. After his ascension, the aforementioned Ḥājī ʿAbd Allāh attended to the settling of his affairs.

The two most exalted names, the honored Muḥammad and ʿAlī, may the glory of God rest upon them both, wrote from Alexandria that whatever was rightfully his would reach him. Some merchants of Cairo who had embraced the Cause told the aforementioned agent, “This sum will reach you, for these gentlemen’s word is true and beyond doubt or question.”

The unrest spread until it reached these lands. Were it not for God’s grace and mercy, the earth would have swallowed up those upon it, for, in view of the people’s heedlessness and rebellion, they deserved what befell them.

38 In short, within a few hours Alexandria was seen turned upside down, as this mortal one has previously mentioned in some letters, and as has also been revealed by the Most Exalted Pen. The friends in Alexandria and Cairo sent a petition to the Most Holy Court asking what they should do. They also sent a petition to his honor Ismu’llāh M-H, upon him be the most resplendent of all glory, who was then staying in this land. The instruction was given that the friends of Alif and Sīn should act as circumstances required and turn toward the Most Holy Presence, while the friends in Cairo should remain where they were and be at ease.

Owing to the disturbances in Egypt, all those regions were in turmoil and agitation, and the honored Ḥājī ʿAbd Allāh was besieged there until the order to leave arrived. Through God’s grace and favor, a sum became available. He took it and set out for Cairo, carrying fifty British pounds, which he handed over.

The honored Āqā Siyyid ʿAlī, may the glory of God rest upon him, has written an account of the love of the friends in Cairo such that the birds of love in the hearts of the faithful stir and take flight. Love for the sake of God truly bestows real life and brings the dead to life.

42 Their fulfillment of this pledge is mentioned and recorded before the All-Sufficient, the Most Exalted, as a sovereign among deeds. I ask God, exalted be His glory, to confirm the friends in that land and in other lands in trustworthiness and truthfulness.

Nevertheless, since it is the seat of the Throne, the loftiness and sublimity of the friends’ stations remain established among the people to a certain degree. Another defect has arisen in this affair: anyone who proves exceedingly malicious eventually even lays claim to divinity. God willing, may the hand of power preserve His servants and enable them to attain what He loves and approves.

44 In every circumstance, all one’s limbs and members should proclaim the words, “There is no power or strength except through God.” At this moment I was present before the Throne, and the Desire of the unseen and the seen spoke these words: “Write to the honored ʿAlī before Akbar, upon him be My glory, My mercy, and My loving-kindness: O ʿAlī, by My command instruct My loved ones in what will exalt their stations. Say: O friends of the All-Merciful throughout the lands! Conquer the cities of hearts with the armies of deeds and character. This is what the Lord of the world has decreed upon His establishment on the Most Great Throne. Blessed is the servant who is enraptured by My call and acts according to what he has been commanded in My perspicuous Book.” End of quotation.

45 Countless firm utterances of this kind were heard from the tongue of the Lord of creation. Blessed are those who act.

46 Concerning what you wrote about the beloved of the heart, the honored Ḥājī Mīrzā Ḥaydar ʿAlī, may the glory of God rest upon him, and your mention of his services in the path of God: after this was presented in the Most Holy Court, these words repeatedly flowed from the tongue of grandeur: “Upon him be the glory of God. Upon him be the glory of God.”

Your Lord is indeed the Compassionate, the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful, the Generous.” End of quotation.

48 This mortal one submits that it would not be without benefit if a copy of this letter were made, corrected, and sent to various regions, for it is adorned with the verses and words of God, exalted be His glory, which truly set the world and its peoples aflame. Perhaps they will taste its sweetness and arise to do what God loves. Perhaps the fragrance of good deeds and noble character will spread abroad and awaken the heedless. The command is in His hand, and I am only a servant who turns to Him.

Thus has the ocean of My utterance surged and the fragrance of My loving-kindness spread. When you have seen and perceived, arise and say: “I testify that You accepted the world’s afflictions for the exaltation of the Cause of God, and bore calamities out of love for God, the Creator of heaven and Lord of all names, until You accepted the Most Great Prison for the deliverance of the nations. I ask You by the Most Great Name, through which You have subdued the kingdom of names, not to disappoint me in what is with You or deprive me of what You have ordained for Your trusted and chosen ones. O Lord, You see me turning toward You and holding fast to Your cord. I ask You to strengthen me in steadfastness in this Cause, through which the feet of most of Your creatures have slipped. There is no god but You, the Most Exalted, the Compassionate, the Ever-Forgiving, the Generous.” End.

148 God willing, may they be strengthened to carry out what they were commanded by the Tongue of Grandeur, and drink from the cups of loving-kindness so deeply that the opposition of the oppressors cannot hold them back. In truth, if a person receives no share in these days and remains deprived of the outpourings of the All-Bountiful, what can give that person contentment and joy, and what can occupy them? It is a great pity for these precious times to pass while a person remains heedless. God willing, all the friends should devote their efforts to service. First, let all arise with the utmost purity, sanctity, fairness, and consultation. Whatever they observe to be a cause of division, let them first attend to remedying it, and then engage in service with joy, gladness, and wisdom. Their affairs and those of the whole world will be set right; statements like this have repeatedly been heard from the Tongue of Eternity. A hundred thousand blessings and gladness be upon those souls who arise to do what brings about the exaltation of the Cause and the well-being of the people of the world. In truth, that exaltation is itself well-being, but ignorant people are heedless of this matter. God willing, you, beloved, and this servant pray that they may pass from the left hand of ignorance to the right hand of knowledge.

149 What you wrote concerning the sister of the King of Martyrs—may the souls of all others be sacrificed for him—was presented before the Throne. This is what the Tongue of Grandeur uttered: “O ʿAlī, the gaze of loving-kindness has rested, and will continue to rest, upon her and the relatives of the Twin Lights. We have instructed the Name of God, Mahdī, to give full directions to Ṣādiq, upon him be My glory, concerning her rights. It has likewise been written by the Most Exalted Pen: ‘If the sister has any entitlement, you must discharge it.’ Let conduct accord with truth and justice. From this station We send her Our greeting and give her the glad tidings of My grace and mercy.” End.

150 As for their journey toward Ṣād and the action of one of the leaves in accordance with what God loves and approves, this matter attained the honor of being heard by the Lord of Names. He said: “O servant in attendance! Blessed is she, and again, blessed is she! It is fitting that there descend upon her from the heaven of My loving-kindness that which will perpetuate her remembrance as a recompense for her deed, and that My Most Exalted Pen write to her and give her the glad tidings that what she has done has been accepted. We command the Branch to write to My handmaiden and My leaf what has descended from the heaven of her Creator’s will and appeared from the horizon of her Maker’s loving-kindness, that it may be a treasure and an honor for her in every world among the worlds of her Lord.” End.

151 The radiance of the sun of loving-kindness has illumined the worlds of the unseen and the seen. O beloved of my heart, observe how dearly a worthy deed is loved by God. Were anyone to give away oceans of elixir and the treasures of the world, they would assuredly not attain such a remembrance. A deed was performed and adorned with acceptance, in such a manner that there appeared from the dawning-place of the All-Merciful’s utterance that whose fragrances will never cease for as long as the earthly and heavenly kingdoms endure. Convey to that leaf greetings of praise and peace on behalf of this servant. Upon her be the glory of her Lord; upon her be the glory of her Lord. And this is what has been sent down for her from the heaven of the loving-kindness of our Lord, the Ever-Forgiving, the All-Bountiful.

152 In My name, the Manifest, the Witnessed.

153 O My leaf, upon you be My glory! Rejoice in what has been sent down for you from the Most Exalted Pen. It has remembered you and given you glad tidings of what you have done in the path of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting. Give thanks, hold fast to the hem of loving-kindness, and say: “Praise be to You, O God of existence and Lord of the unseen and the seen, for enabling me to perform a deed that has been adorned with Your acceptance and embellished with Your good pleasure. By Your life, O Beloved of my heart, nothing created in Your heaven and on Your earth can equal what You have bestowed upon me. You are indeed the Bestower, the All-Bountiful.” End.

154 May God, exalted be His glory, assist everyone to do that from which the fragrances of His good pleasure spread. One righteous deed today is equal to a hundred thousand deeds—indeed, I ask God’s forgiveness for setting such a limit—and likewise with an evil deed. On one occasion the Tongue of Grandeur spoke these exalted words—His words, glorified and exalted be He: “O servant in attendance! God’s favors have emboldened people to a degree that it is not pleasing to mention. Those who have turned toward Him must be made aware. Today, because the sun of Revelation is shining and the radiance of the lights of His countenance has encompassed the world, most unseemly deeds have been and are being concealed. He is indeed the Concealer, the All-Wise.”

155 “Today is a day of joy and a day of supreme gladness. The ocean of loving-kindness is surging, the breeze of bounty is blowing, and the sun of grace is shining. Yet now it is seen that reprehensible deeds have reached such a point that their dust is close to reaching the hem of God, exalted be His glory, and tearing the veil of sanctity. Everything that has befallen Us from the beginning of the Cause until now, and that We have accepted, has been for the exaltation of the Word of God and the advancement of souls who are assured, steadfast, content, and well-pleasing to Him. Now, through the deeds of some, this has been and is being cast to the winds, unless God, exalted be His glory, preserves it through His consummate power, as He has done.”

156 “O servant in attendance! Write to ʿAlī, upon him be My glory and My loving-kindness: He must admonish all the friends in that land and acquaint them with what will benefit them, so that they may perhaps turn back from what has escaped them and hold fast to God, and that whatever has caused harm and brought humiliation upon souls may be cleansed and purified by the water of return. He is indeed the Ever-Relenting, the Most Bountiful, the All-Generous.” End.

157 These statements have repeatedly flowed from the tongue of the Revealer of verses. At times this servant would enter and behold the Ancient Beauty in such distress and sorrow that this mortal frame would tremble. It is clear and well known what has been and remains the cause of the Ancient Beauty’s sorrow; it needs no mention. Today the pure deeds of the friends of God proclaim the Cause among His servants. Statements of this kind have repeatedly been revealed in the divine Tablets, that they may perhaps prove beneficial and awaken people. Otherwise, He is indeed independent of all the worlds.

158 Some time ago, certain souls began to complain outwardly. Nevertheless, no answer was sent down from the Most Holy Court. He said: “My concealment has preceded disclosure, and My patience has preceded haste.” End. Yet there is a fear that our transgressions may lift the veil and rend the covering. I cling to the hem of His loving-kindness and ask Him to assist His loved ones to do what He loves and approves, to keep them from what harms them, and to guide them to what benefits them. He is indeed the All-Powerful, the All-Protecting, the All-Compassionate, the Ever-Forgiving.

159 As commanded, convey greetings of praise on behalf of God to the emigrants of the land of Ṣād, upon them be the glory of God. God willing, may they be illumined by the light of unity and arise to serve the Cause, so that the effect of that light may shine forth and become manifest throughout the horizons. All have come to some understanding of God’s good pleasure; let them hold fast to it and act in accordance with it. This is the means of salvation in this world and the next. Your emigration and afflictions have always been under His regard; God willing, may they remain preserved. End.

160 Concerning what you wrote about Āqā Mīrzā Āqā Bābā, upon him be the glory of God: he has been remembered in the Most Holy Court and has attained the lights of the loving-kindness of the Sun of Truth. Blessed is he! In these days Āqā Mīrzā Aḥmad, upon him be 669 [the glory of God], a native or resident of Sh-Ah, has sent this servant a letter on the advice of Mīrzā Ḥ-Yā, upon him be the glory of God and His loving-kindness. Several names were mentioned in it, including that of Āqā Mīrzā Āqā Bābā, upon him be 669 [the glory of God]. A most exalted Tablet was revealed specifically for each one and sent. God willing, they will receive them. In truth, the matter is as you, beloved, have written, for Mīrzā’s journey to those regions became the cause of the exaltation of the Word. “This suffices him, by the Lord of the worlds!” Since these words were heard from the tongue of the Beloved, they have been mentioned; otherwise, this servant has never been, nor is he now, worthy to recount his services.

161 What you wrote concerning the leaf, the wife of Mīrzā Ḥā and Sīn, upon them both be the glory of God and His loving-kindness, caused sorrow. All the inhabitants of the tabernacle of purity were visibly and manifestly filled with the deepest sorrow, and the matter was also mentioned in the Most Holy Court. Convey this mortal one’s condolences to Mīrzā. God willing, this servant himself will also offer words expressive of his weakness and lowliness.

162 Your mention of the ascension of Ḥājī Mīrzā Ḥasan of Herat, upon him be the glory of God, was presented in the Most Exalted, Most Holy Court. These lofty words were revealed especially for him, out of pure grace and loving-kindness—His words, exalted be His glory, exalted be His remembrance, and exalted be His praise:

163 He is the One Who remembers, the Helper.

164 O Ḥasan! The Wronged One visits you from His most exalted station, which God has made the center around which circle the inhabitants of the loftiest summit, and remembers you with a remembrance through which the fragrance of loving-kindness has spread among all people. Blessed are you, and blessed are your ascension, your turning toward Him, and your entry into the companionship on high and the all-glorious station. I bear witness that you turned toward the ultimate Goal and the most exalted Horizon when the rustling of the Lote-Tree of the Utmost Boundary rose between earth and heaven, that you bore witness to what the Tongue of Grandeur bore witness to, and that you accepted what was sent down from Him and appeared from His presence. Blessed are you for having attained forgiveness from the All-Merciful, and because the Concourse on High perceived from you the fragrance of My love. Blessed is whoever remembers you with that by which the Wronged One has remembered you in this inaccessible station. You belonged to God and have returned to the ocean of His mercy. This is nothing but His mighty, well-beloved grace. End.

165 Praise be to God, he has been honored with this supreme favor. Blessed are the souls who attain this greatest bounty and recognize its station. Steadfast, pure, assured souls have received and continue to receive their share from the heaven of the loving-kindness of the All-Bountiful, and without interruption it descends and flows upon them, whether in life or in death.

166 Concerning what you wrote about the ascension of the honored, exalted, righteous Āqā Sayyid Abū Ṭālib, upon him be the most glorious of all glory: some time ago, the honored Name of God, Zayn, upon him be the most glorious of all glory, sent from Ḥadbā to the Most Holy Court a mention of him, as well as certain matters related by his wife, upon her be 669 [the glory of God]. A detailed answer was revealed and sent, and a remembrance of him was also revealed in that blessed Tablet. To Him belong praise and gratitude in all circumstances. May the glory shining and gleaming from the horizon of our Lord’s loving-kindness rest upon you and upon those with you, both men and women.

167 I submit that two most exalted, most holy Tablets were revealed long ago from the heaven of the divine will specifically for the sons of the exalted and righteous Dhabīḥ, upon him be the glory of God and the ocean of His mercy. They were not sent during this time, and their dispatch was delayed until these days, when they were sent, so that perhaps, through divine favors, they may become mindful and arise to do what is fitting and what God loves. Every soul’s service is mentioned and regarded in the Most Holy Court, even if it is no greater than a needle’s eye.

168 The exalted Dhabīḥ, upon him be the glory of God, attained the honor of turning toward God and believing in the earliest days. He has always been, and continues to be, remembered in the Most Holy Court. After hearing the call, he did not hesitate for even an instant. He with whom is a clear Book bears witness to this. Care has therefore been taken of them in every circumstance and, God willing, will continue to be taken. This humble servant asks God, exalted be His glory, to sustain them. You too, dear friend, should watch over them with kindness. They are at the beginning of life; the winds of youth blow in a different way.

169 Another matter to submit: the honored lady Khadīja Bīgum, upon her be the glory of God, has sent letters to this servant several times. In view of the upheavals and warfare in the surrounding regions, this servant sent no reply until this occasion, when he sent a letter written several months earlier for you, dear friend, to deliver.

170 Likewise, this humble servant wrote and sent a reply to the letter that Āqā Muḥammad Karīm ʿ–Ṭ, upon him be the glory of God, had sent to His Honor the Name of Generosity, upon him be the glory of God, the All-Glorious. God willing, may he drink at all times from the cups of salvation and act as befits the days of God. News reached us from the surrounding regions that he has acted according to what God has revealed in the Book and has appointed an agent specifically for teaching the Faith. Blessed is he, and may joy be his! Whoever acts according to what God has willed will assuredly bear fruit and produce an effect in this world and the next, and will be of service in both worlds.

171 Further, convey repeated greetings of praise and peace to the friends in that land, and give ʿAlī Pāshā Khān, upon him be the glory of God, the glad news that his carpet arrived. One day it was spread out, and the Ancient Beauty went into the garden, and it was honored and blessed by the footsteps of the Desired One—may the soul of whoever dwells in the realm of divinity be a sacrifice to His footsteps. He summoned Ḥājī Ghulām-ʿAlī Musāfir, upon him be the glory of God, and said, “If you see Pāshā, upon him be My glory, give him the glad news that what he sent has been accepted. May joy and delight be his!” God willing, may they be so enkindled that their light is manifest and evident. This humble servant asks God, exalted be His glory, to aid His friends and chosen ones in recognizing what has been ordained for them. For if they become aware of it, the flood of the world will be powerless to extinguish the fire of their love, and darkness will not overtake its light.

172 Among the new favors is that wondrous, exalted Tablets have been revealed specifically for the names mentioned, both in the handwriting of this humble servant, as recorded in the letter, and in the blessed handwriting of His Holiness the Most Great Branch of God—may my soul, my essence, and my being be a sacrifice to the dust of His footsteps. In truth, the recipients of the Tablets have become possessors of two stations, two Tablets, and two remembrances. With all our limbs and members we should say, “Praise be to You, O ancient Bestower, and Yours is loving-kindness, O Desire of all the worlds.” May light and glory from the horizon of the loving-kindness of our Lord, the King of Names, rest upon you, upon those with you, and upon the friends and loved ones of God.

173 The servant, on the 13th of the month of Ṣafar, 1300.
