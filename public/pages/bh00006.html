Lawh-i-Sarraj (Tablet to Sarraj)

BH00006

Translated by GPT 6.1 Sol on 15 Qudrat 183

1 He is the Answerer.

2 Your letter was presented before the Throne and reached the seat of “He is not known through anything other than Himself,” and its contents came under the gaze of God. You had asked how a letter of the highest heaven can be changed into one of the lowest abyss, or affirmation revert to negation, or a ripe fruit be deprived of its sweetness, or a mirror remain deprived of the radiance of the lights of the Sun of inner meanings. Excellent indeed is what you have asked, and you are among those who inquire.

3 Your question was most welcome, for today every soul who is unable to comprehend the difficulties of divine questions must seek and hope to receive from the river of divine knowledge and the Euphrates of eternal wisdom, that perhaps they may drink a sprinkling of it and find repose upon the carpet of tranquillity and certitude. We ask God to raise you to a station where you behold the Ancient Beauty with your own eyes and detach yourself from the sight of the peoples of the world, hear His melodies with your own ears and detach yourself from the hearing of all created beings, and recognize His Self with your own heart and detach yourself from the hearts of all who are in the heavens and on earth; and to purify you from the defilement of the world and all that is in it, so that you will pass by nothing without hearing it declare, “There is no God but Him, and the countenance of the Most Exalted is indeed His glory in the Concourse on high, His radiance between earth and heaven, and His majesty to those in the kingdom of command and creation.” Thus does everything speak, if you are among those who listen.

4 For today God has enjoined upon every soul to examine and reflect upon His Cause with their own eyes, ears, and heart, so that, through the wondrous mercy of the All-Merciful and the outpourings of the All-Glorious, they may be illumined by the radiance of the Sun of inner meanings and attain its grace. The reason all people have been deprived of meeting God and occupied with anything other than Him is that they have contented themselves with mere fancy and been satisfied with what they have heard from others like themselves. They have walked the deadly paths of imitation and been deprived of the ways of detachment. The Cause of God has always been as manifest and radiant as the luminous dawn. That some have remained veiled from perceiving it is because they have defiled their ears and hearts with the pollution of people’s words. Otherwise, if they looked to the fundamental standard of the knowledge of God, they would never be deprived of the path of guidance.

5 Now consider for yourself: the matter admits of only two possibilities. Either the people of the Bayān acknowledge divine power, or they do not. If they do not acknowledge it, We have nothing to say to such souls, for they are reckoned among the religious communities of the past who considered God’s hand to be fettered, as the Lord of Glory has related in His exalted words: “God’s hand is fettered.” But if they acknowledge the wonders of divine power, then dwelling on questions of this kind is futile and will be of no avail. For powerlessness is a condition of creation, while that Eternal Essence has always been established upon the throne of power and might. If He so wills, with a word He can raise all who are on earth to the heavens of His Cause, and with another word return them to the lowest rank of creation. No one has the right to say “Why?” or “Whereby?” Whoever says this has disbelieved in God, turned away from His power, made war upon His Self, and contested His sovereignty, and is numbered among those who associate partners with God in the tablets of inviolable glory.

6 Likewise, He has the power to raise up the Manifestation of His own Self among creation whenever He wills. At the time of His appearance, proof and evidence must be sought from His exalted Manifestation Himself. If He produces that proof which has always existed among people, further hesitation is unwarranted. Indeed, if all who are in the heavens and on earth hesitate for less than an instant, they are reckoned among the people of the fire, even if they all claim sainthood. He exalts whomever He wills and abases whomever He wishes. He is not questioned about what He does.

7 Now consider fairly: if a person appears invested with all the expressions of divine power and, moreover, produces that proof by which people’s religion has always been substantiated and the Cause of God established among His creatures, and yet someone turns away from such a manifestation, which is the very Manifestation of God, and, not content with turning away, rises up to kill Him, what judgment applies to such a person? Say: His judgment rests with God. He decrees whatever He wills, just as He has decreed in truth, but the people do not perceive. Has such a deed ever been permissible in any age or religious community? No, by His own Self, the All-Merciful, the Compassionate!

8 If they say that this Manifestation must not appear, as those who associate partners with God have said, then God’s power and will become dependent upon and subject to the will of His creatures. Exalted, immeasurably exalted, is He above that! Thus, at the Revelation of the year sixty, all people, learned and ignorant alike, were deprived of God through such meaningless, spurious words, and returned from the highest heaven of eternity to the lowest abyss of extinction, while imagining themselves established upon the loftiest seat of faith. How evil is what they have imagined concerning themselves! In the Mother of Tablets they are recorded among those who are deluded.

9 In short, turn your gaze away from everything but God and look to Him and to whatever appears from Him, for all besides Him has been and will remain sheer nothingness. If today all who are in the heavens and on earth were to become letters of the Bayān, who are a hundred thousand degrees greater and mightier than the letters of the Qurʾān, and were to hesitate in this Cause for less than an instant, they would be reckoned before God among those who turn away and numbered among the letters of negation.

10 God, glorified and exalted is He, bears no relation, connection, resemblance, or likeness to anyone, and all have been and will be honored and exalted according to their relation to the recognition of Him. The All-Glorious Beauty is established upon the throne of the All-Merciful, and the rays of the light of the sun of His grace have shone and appeared equally upon all things. All who dwell in the realm of existence stand on a single plane before His grace, and no atom has any distinction or superiority over another except through its precedence in recognizing God and meeting Him. Blessed, then, is the one who has recognized Him through Himself and detached himself from everything but Him.

11 O ʿAlī, hear the call of God and enter a realm that has always been and will forever remain sanctified above names, so that no name may deprive you of the beauty of the One named, the Sovereign through the will of whose Pen the kingdom of names was created. By God, besides whom there is no other god, the purpose of these words is that perhaps you and a few others may rend the veil and enter the tabernacle of the Beloved’s holiness, which is sanctified above the suppositions and imaginings of His servants. Otherwise, He is indeed exalted above the turning of His creatures toward Him or away from Him, and sanctified above all the worlds.

12 When the sun shines, is it fitting for anyone to ask how the light of the stars can be taken away, while observing that the sunlight has effaced their brightness? Indeed, in this condition the stars seek the darkness of night and turn away from the light of day, for the worth and radiance of the stars are seen at night, and through the shining of the orb of day they vanish and disappear. Glorified is He above comparison and likenesses! For the orb of His beauty has always been radiant, and no one has been with Him. All besides Him have been created in earthly abodes through the will pertaining to contingent existence, and to Him they will return. He, glorified and exalted is He, in His inaccessible seat and His lofty abode, has ever been and will forever remain sanctified above all. It is most strange that people are astonished and bewildered by the change and alteration of names, although at every moment they all observe with their outward eyes the change and alteration of the manifestations of names and their dawning-places. Yet they have remained so veiled by imaginary veils and words that associate partners with God that they have become heedless of what they see with their own eyes.

13 O questioner, do not regard the divine names and attributes as imaginary. Know that all things created between earth and heaven have been and will remain manifestations of the names and dawning-places of the attributes of God, exalted is His station. It is only that the human being has been created greater in rank and loftier in station than all else. If you ascend into the heaven of “You see no disparity in the creation of the All-Merciful,” you will see neither disparity nor flaw in the creation of the All-Merciful and the dawning-places of His glorious handiwork. Blessed, then, is the one who has soared in this atmosphere, where the wings of the doubters have never flown.

14 Now consider all these manifestations of the divine names—trees, boughs, branches, and fruits, as well as roses, blossoms, and everything that grows upon the face of the earth—with what freshness, delicacy, and verdure they first appear, and how, after a time, they are all stripped of their delicate robes and return to the earth. How many ripe fruits undergo such a change that people recoil from their smell! In short, all undergo ascent and decline, change and transformation, except the universal Manifestations of God, who subsist and endure through themselves and for themselves.

15 I swear by the Sun of the morning of inner meanings that the divine Tongue speaks in keeping with the condition and capacity of the people, for most people have not reached maturity. Otherwise, He would open a door of knowledge before His servants such that all who are in the heavens and on earth, through the outpouring of His Pen, would find themselves independent of the knowledge of all besides Him and would be established upon thrones of tranquility. In view of the people’s lack of readiness, the gems of divine knowledge and the mysteries of eternal wisdom have remained guarded and concealed in the heaven of God’s will, and until now not a word of them has been revealed. Hereafter, the matter rests in His hand: He does whatever He wills and is not questioned about what He has willed. He is the All-Knowing, the All-Aware.

16 If you will listen to this servant, cast away the wings of limitation and imitation, and soar on the wings of detachment in this holy atmosphere of divine unity, so that you may find yourself sanctified from fanciful doubts and dubious allusions, and see yourself illumined by the lights of certitude. Listen with the ear of your soul to the words of the All-Merciful, that perhaps the heart may be cleansed of the obscuring dust of imaginings that has enveloped all who dwell in the world in these days, and may turn toward the Most Great Vision.

17 When you attain this most holy, most inaccessible, most pure station, you will perceive that the purpose of all that was revealed in the Bayān from the heaven of the will of My previous Manifestation has been and will remain this Revelation. By Him in the grasp of whose power is the soul of Ḥusayn, nothing greater than this Cause has been revealed in the Bayān.

18 Examine it with clear and penetrating vision, so that you may understand the intent of the holy words of the Lord. In all His pages, tablets, missives, scriptures, writings, and books, He has counseled all people and taken a covenant from them all, lest at the time of the Revelation they cling to anything that has been created and remain veiled from the Manifestation Himself. For on that Day nothing is of any avail except by His permission.

19 He says to Vaḥīd-i Akbar: “Await Him whose countenance God will call to your remembrance, for you were created only to meet Him. He is the One who has made all things dependent upon His command. Beware, beware lest in the days of His appearance you allow the Vāḥid of the Bayān to veil you, for that Vāḥid is a creation in His sight. Beware, beware lest you be veiled by the words revealed in the Bayān, for they are His own words in the temple of His previous Manifestation.”

20 Now reflect on these words of the Sovereign of names and attributes. With these firm verses and conclusive words, does any room remain for anyone to turn away? No, by Him who has caused the Spirit to speak within My breast—unless one should turn away from God altogether. The Vāḥid of the Bayān are the first creation, and all besides them were created beneath their shadow.

21 He likewise says to Vaḥīd-i Akbar: “Perhaps He will come to you about the loftiness of whose mention and the exaltation of whose Cause you have asked. Those who are in the Bayān read these words, yet they pay no heed to His appearance, nor do they believe in God, who created them through a Revelation before Him, and they lie asleep.”

22 Thus it is now seen that all recite the Book of God and write by night and day, yet they have not understood a single word of the Book. Rather, their purpose in promoting it has been and will remain nothing but to secure and establish their own leadership. Thus testifies the Tongue of God, the King, the Almighty, the All-Knowing.

23 He also says: “From the beginning of that Cause until before nine is completed, the realities of creation have not appeared. All that you have seen is from the stage of the drop of seed to that which We clothed with flesh. Then be patient until you witness the other creation. Say: ‘Blessed, then, is God, the best of creators!’”

24 He likewise says to ʿAẓīm: “This is what We promised you before We answered you. Be patient until nine has elapsed from the Bayān. Then say: ‘Blessed, then, is God, the best of originators!’”

25 He also says: “Be sure to observe the difference between the Qāʾim and the Qayyūm. Then, in the year nine, you will attain all good.”

26 Now reflect a little on these words. Reflection on the difference between the Qāʾim and the Qayyūm is also necessary, for the sorrows that have befallen this servant leave Him unable to expound the words of God. By God, besides whom there is no other god, the proclamation of the Cause of God has compelled this servant to write this Tablet, that perhaps a few lamps may be kindled in the niche of contingent existence and may arise, through the power of God, to aid this wronged and solitary One.

27 Now all things weep over My suffering and over what has befallen Me at the hands of those who were created by My word. Beyond all this, matters have reached such a pass that, although the holy verses of the Lord pour down like a torrent of rain from the heaven of His will without pause or cessation, and besides these there are signs of power and divine manifestations that have encompassed the world to such a degree that the peoples of earlier religions have acknowledged and confessed them, I must nevertheless argue by proofs and establish by evidence a Cause that has always been sanctified above proof, so that perhaps a few may ascend to the heaven of direct witness.

28 In God’s knowledge there is no injustice greater than this: that the Ancient Beauty should prove His own truth by something other than Himself, when He is manifest and radiant as the sun at the zenith of the everlasting heaven. Those who have wronged the very Self of God will soon know to what place they will return. Inescapable afflictions and decreed ordeals have descended in such measure that no one but God can number them. At every moment, the spears of hatred strike the Temple of eternity from the direction of His enemies. The Manifestations of the Self of God came to Me in the forenoon, weeping and lamenting. They said: “Alas for the Joseph of God, the All-Protecting, the Self-Subsisting! His servants have cast Him into the pit.” Then within themselves they cry out for help.

29 Say: O people of the Bayān! Do you slay the Primal Point while reading His verses every evening and morning? By God, you have done what the peoples of former times did not do, and honored servants bear witness to this, if you deny it. Do you slay God with the swords of self and desire, then sit in your seats occupying yourselves with His remembrance and citing His verses as proof? Thus has every community treated God when He appeared in the Manifestation of Himself in every age. Thus they acted, and such was their practice. Say: Today the Pen of God, the All-Protecting, the Almighty, the Self-Subsisting, will not move to record the names of these people. The gaze of God will not turn toward them, nor will the holy breezes of the Beloved of this day reach them.

30 If the servants turn away from these holy divine verses and these manifestations of eternal glory, by what testimony and proof will they establish their own religion? Say: O people of the Bayān! Look upon the Most Great Vision with God’s own sight, for He cannot be seen through any sight other than His own. Thus My previous Manifestation says: “Beware! Look upon Him with His own eye, for whoever looks upon Him through the eye of anyone besides Him will never know Him.” After giving His servants countless counsels concerning this Cause, He says: “O My God, You know that I have not fallen short in counseling these creatures or in making provision for them to turn toward God, their Lord, and to believe in God, their Maker”—to the end of His words, may my soul be sacrificed for Him.

31 I swear by the Ancient Beauty that this utterance, which has flowed from the Pen of the All-Merciful, has set the heart of all things ablaze. Everyone endowed with sight perceives in these words the lamentation and yearning of the Manifestation of the names and attributes. Yet it only increases the wrongdoers in delusion and loss. O ʿAlī, open the ear of your soul and listen to the words of the All-Merciful revealed in the Qayyūm-i Asmā, where He says: “O Qurrat al-ʿAyn! Do not stretch out Your hand over the Cause, for the people are intoxicated by the mystery. Yours will be the return after this cycle, through the Most Great Truth. Then disclose, upon the Most Great Mount, a mystery of the mystery to the measure of a needle’s eye, so that the dwellers of the Mount in Sinai will surely die at the dawning of a sprinkling of that all-protecting, crimson Light, by the permission of God, the All-Wise. He is God, who has indeed been Your Guardian in truth and according to truth.”

32 And now the return is manifest, while the dwellers of the Mount are nonexistent and lost, as can be seen. Although He says that the dwellers of the Mount become dead, nothing, and nonexistent, they nevertheless express surprise that the fruit of the Tree of Bliss should become colocynth—as indeed they have asked this very question—or that the forms of the highest heaven should be transformed into those of the nethermost prison. The Cause of God has never been, and will never be, confined by limits. If all the mirrors of existing things face the sun in the heaven of eternity for a moment, the sun’s lights appear, shine, are imprinted, and are reflected in them all; the instant they turn away, those lights are withdrawn from them all. Look, then, at the sun and then at the mirrors, so that you may find a way to what the Spirit imparts to you.

33 The exalted Ibn-i Nabīl has written tablets in proof of the Cause of God through what God has inspired in his heart. At the outset he has adduced this verse, revealed from the heaven of the will of My previous Manifestation. His words—exalted be His remembrance—are: “Say: O God, You are indeed the God of gods. You will surely bestow divinity on whomever You will, and You will surely strip divinity from whomever You will”—and so forth. Likewise: “Say: O God, You are indeed the Lord of the heavens and the earth. You will surely bestow lordship on whomever You will, and You will surely strip lordship from whomever You will”—and so forth. Although the Sovereign of existence has stated so explicitly that He bestows divinity and lordship on any soul He wishes and takes them from whomever He wills, is the mighty God who takes away the station of divinity and lordship, the highest of stations, unable to strip the robe of His name from a person, or to turn something sweet into something bitter? Glory be to God, far above what His servants imagine concerning His power! Exalted is He above what they describe! Now observe in what atmosphere the bird of power and grandeur is soaring, and in what places the people have come to a halt. What meaning have they given to the verse, “He has power over all things”? And what have they understood from “He does whatever He wills” and “He is not questioned about what He has willed”?

34 O servants! Turn from the frailest of houses and take hold of a strong support, and turn from the darkness of ignorance and unknowing toward the luminous dawn of divine knowledge. Would that the people of the Bayān would ponder the blessed verse cited by Ibn-i Nabīl, so that they might leave the paths of illusion and enter the path of certainty. O ʿAlī! Drink a cup of this sweet water of life concealed in the darkness of the words of the Sovereign of names and attributes, so that you may be purified and sanctified from the troubles of the days, the doubts of humanity, the allusions of the heedless, and the arguments of the malicious, and so that the gates of infinite divine knowledge may be opened before your heart. Then you may become certain that the Sovereign of eternity has the power, in one hour, to honor and exalt all things with the robes of the most beautiful names, and in another hour to take them from them all. I complain to God of these servants, for they look upon Me through what they possess, not through what I possess, and they measure God’s own Self against themselves and His words against their words. By Him whose Self is My own! If all who are in the heavens and on earth were today to detach themselves and turn toward God, He would assuredly teach them such wonders of His knowledge as would make them independent of all the worlds.

35 In this year of hardships, some of the servants have asked questions of this kind, and the servant present before the Throne has written and sent firm, healing, and sufficient answers. It is strange that you have not seen them. In this land, too, some have cast doubts, hoping to veil holy souls behind the veils of earlier words. Yet they are unaware that a soul who has soared in the spacious atmosphere of divine holiness and gleaned from the harvests of infinite divine knowledge will neither be held back nor deprived by fanciful doubts. Say: Whoever has reached the Most Great Ocean will never turn toward a mirage on a plain, nor drink of scalding water. Although it is not fitting that the Most Exalted Pen should be sullied by mentioning the words of those possessed of hatred, or move to write them, there is nevertheless no escape from doing so, in order to convey the divine messages, so that perhaps an ignorant person may enter the ocean of knowledge, or one lost in the valley of heedlessness and forgetfulness may, through the grace of the All-Merciful, advance. He indeed guides whomever He wills to the path of Bahā, and He has power over all things.

36 But this discourse and exposition are for souls who truly seek the way of guidance; otherwise, even Salsabīl will not purify one afflicted with malice and hatred. Thus today most of the people of the Bayān have seen with their own eyes and heard with their own ears all the manifestations of power, the divine signs, and the revealed verses, yet they have arisen in such hatred that it cannot be described—except those whose eyes God has opened, whom He has strengthened in His Cause, brought out of the darkness of illusion, and guided to His mighty and praiseworthy path. They have clung to things that have never had any standing before God, and they are so heedless that they have bowed down before a stone and turned away from the Most Great Vision and the most manifest, most luminous Beauty. How many an object of worship is worth less than a hair of its worshipers! O ʿAlī! Say: Today you and the one you worship are on an equal footing before God. Whoever among you believes is safe from the Most Great Terror, and whoever turns away has departed from the path of God. This is indeed the truth, and beyond truth there is nothing but error.

37 Beware, beware, O people of the Bayān! Do not disbelieve in God, do not wage war against the Manifestation of His own Self, and do not dispute with Him who has come to you from the Dayspring of the Cause with manifest authority. Do you imagine that the Cause of God can be obstructed by those who turn away, or that the light of the sun of everlasting glory can remain concealed within the coverings of darkened souls? No, by Him who has spoken within My breast, raised Me up in truth, and sent Me to all the worlds!

38 Among the doubts that some of those who associate partners with God have raised in this land is this: Can gold become copper? Say: Yes, by My Lord! But the knowledge of it is with Us. We teach whomever We will through knowledge from Our presence. Whoever is in doubt should ask God, his Lord, to enable him to witness it and become one of those who are certain. The very fact that copper attains the rank of gold is a clear proof that gold can return to its former state, if only they understood. All metals attain the weight, form, and substance of one another, but the knowledge of this is with Us in a hidden Book.

39 We say: The knowledge of those who turn away has not risen to the point of understanding that gold can become copper; they have not even understood that it becomes dust. Yet this much has been evident to everyone endowed with understanding: all things emerge from dust and return to dust. Dust is lower in worth and price than copper, for it is reckoned among bodies, while copper is reckoned among metallic bodies. This is plain and evident. If people had been found worthy and mature, some of the hidden divine sciences would certainly have been mentioned here. But what has been decreed has been decreed. It is evident to everyone endowed with sight that God—exalted be His remembrance—has always had, and will always have, power over all things. Certainly, if He wishes, He transforms gold into copper by the mere act of willing. It is in created things that this powerlessness exists and is seen. 299 People, throughout all the pages of the Bayān I gave everyone the glad tidings of this holy and eternal Revelation, and said that when this Luminary of supreme glory arose, you must not become occupied with anything created between the heavens and the earth and remain veiled from the Beauty of Eternity.

300 I also expressly said: beware, people, lest at the time of the Revelation you be veiled by the Vāḥid of the Bayān. It was known that the Vāḥid of the Bayān is reckoned before God as the first of the creation of the Bayān, and that I have given it precedence over all.

301 I also stated in an explicit text: beware, people, lest at the time of the Revelation you be veiled by what has been revealed in the Bayān. Yet now, people, because of a description revealed in the Bayān—when you do not know what is intended or whom it concerns, for no one has ever been aware of My intention—you have turned away from the One who brought that description into being, revealed it, manifested it, and fulfilled it, through a single word from whom such temples have been and continue to be created. If only you had been content with this! Instead, you have issued a decree for His death. By God, at every moment you have tormented this Essence of the All-Merciful like a serpent. You did not believe and did not recognize God; why, then, did you consent to His death and injury?

302 I swear by My Beauty that you have not drunk from the cup of fairness or tasted the ocean of My good pleasure. You have not set foot on the right side of justice or passed through the quarter of those endowed with insight. You have counted error as guidance itself and regarded sheer idolatry as the essence of divine unity.”

303 All of you have seen with your own outward eyes the person whom He protected for twenty years and who is still alive. Though He had power over him and knew what lay hidden within him, He sheltered him within His merciful protection. Nevertheless, that person arose to kill Him. When his intention and deed became known among the emigrants, he occupied himself with fabricated accusations solely to conceal his own vile deeds and cast doubt into minds prone to imagining things. He attributed his own acts and deeds to the Essence of Eternity, hoping thereby to draw the servants from the right hand of certitude to the left hand of delusion.

304 You have denied what you have seen with your own eyes and perceived, and through fabricated accusations you have remained veiled from the Countenance of Oneness. I swear by the beauty of My transcendent holiness that what they have written about this manifest Beauty is proof to all of the falsehood of those who utter it and confirmation of this manifest Cause. For they have made allegations whose falsehood is more evident than the sun in the midst of heaven, since you have all associated with this Beauty of most exalted glory and have perceived according to your own capacity.

305 Nevertheless, because of these fabricated words you have turned away from the Revealer of the verses of Oneness. Even if we concede that they are true, have you not known that the True One does whatever He wills, and have you not become certain that He decrees whatever He desires?

306 Apart from all these considerations, this company who have emigrated with God on this journey have perceived most of these matters with their own eyes and hearts, and testify that the True One—exalted be His glory—has been and will remain distinguished in every respect from all besides Him. Nevertheless, you have deemed these souls liars, while you have regarded, and still regard, as truthful those who have never been aware of the matter and never will be, and who walk the paths of delusion and conjecture. Shame on you, you company of the deluded!

307 I swear by My Beauty that God’s conclusive proof has been fully established for all, and His perfect Word has dawned from the horizon of truth. Today there is no refuge but His refuge, and no sheltering shade is to be seen but His. Listen to the call of your Beloved, hold fast to what you have seen with your own eyes, and loose your hold on the handle of delusion.

308 If you look with the eye of truth, by God, the inhabitants of the Concourse on high are mourning and lamenting, and all the maidens of the heavenly chambers are sighing and wailing. The leaves of the Lote-Tree of the Utmost Boundary have withered through the oppression of these oppressors. By God, the True One, the breezes of the All-Merciful’s mercy have been cut off from the realm of contingent being, and the radiance of the lights of the All-Glorious Countenance has been withheld from the inhabitants of creation.

309 By God, the True One, you have committed such oppression that all things have been severed from their own life. Today no creature remains—whether among the inhabitants of the Concourse on high, the dwellers in the cities of eternity, or those abiding in the ocean of names—who has not exchanged all the delights of joy for sorrow and put on a garment of black. The entire company of the cherubim and the realities of the Prophets and Messengers are lamenting in the chambers of established glory, while you, heedless ones of the earth, are joyful and wander through the land of the abyss in utter delight.

310 You have slain the essence of religion, yet imagine yourselves seated on the thrones of religion and certitude. By God, O people, nothing like this Revelation has ever appeared, nor has the eye of contingent being ever beheld its like. Hear My call, and do not be among those punctilious ones who were found among the people of the Qur’an: so scrupulous were they that they took precautions over the blood of a gnat, yet issued rulings authorizing the shedding of the blood of God.

311 They were occupied with remembrance, yet when the verses of the Sovereign of Remembrance were recited to that company, they cried out, “Leave aside these remembrances, and do not distract us from the remembrance of God!” Such were the deeds and actions witnessed from that company. And you, people of the Bayān, are committing and carrying out the very essence of those deeds in these days, while you have reckoned, and still reckon, yourselves to be of the True One.

312 Thus I call God to witness, and His angels, His Prophets, His Messengers, those who circle around His throne, and all that He has created in the heavens and on earth, that I have not fallen short in conveying My message to you. I have delivered to you the messages of God at the time of My manifestation, at the time of My ascent, and at this time when I have manifested Myself from the All-Glorious Horizon. I have imparted to you wisdom and utterance, made known to you the beauty of the All-Merciful, and completed the testimony for you, the evidence against you, and the proof among you. No admonition remains that I have not conveyed to you.

313 Thus, O my God, You know that I have not fallen short in Your Cause, and that I have conveyed to these people what You commanded Me before the creation of the heavens and the earth. I have explained to them the ways of Your justice and shown them the paths of Your good pleasure.

314 Thus, O my God, have mercy on these people, and do not make them among those who turned away from Me, denied My right, and disputed My verses until they shed My blood and dismembered My limbs. O my God, strengthen them in Your Cause, then aid them with Your assistance. Do not deprive them of these breezes that have blown from this paradise which You have created at the heart of the gardens of heaven, and do not withhold from them the fragrances You have sent forth from the horizon of Your name, the All-Merciful.

315 Thus, O my God, bring into being within their breasts, from the light of Your words, the fire of attraction to You, that through sheer power it may turn them toward the right hand of the throne of Your mercy. Then kindle within their hearts, O my God, the torches of love and longing for You, that through them the veils that have barred them from the court of Your nearness and Your presence may be burned away.

316 Then take their hands, O my God, with the hands of power and might. Rescue them from the depths of delusion and desire, and bring them to the abode that You have sanctified beyond the allusions of all that has been created between earth and heaven. Then impart to them the word through which the hearts of those who know You are drawn to the heaven of the glory of Your loving-kindness, and the hearts of those brought near to You are drawn to the atmosphere of the holiness of Your favors.

317 Then make them, O my Beloved, among those whom all who are in the heavens and on earth have not prevented from turning toward Your loving care, remaining steadfast in Your Cause, acknowledging Your presence, and attaining certainty of meeting You. You are indeed the Ever-Forgiving, the Merciful, the Bestower, the Almighty, the Munificent, the Helper, the All-Bountiful.
