The Kitab-i-Iqan (The Book of Certitude)

BH00002

Translated on November 11, 2026 with GPT 6.1 Sol

1 In the name of our Lord, the Exalted, the Most Exalted.

2 This chapter explains that people will never reach the shore of the ocean of recognition except through utter detachment from all who are in the heavens and on earth. Sanctify yourselves, people of the earth, so that you may reach the station God has ordained for you and enter a pavilion that God has raised in the heaven of utterance.

3 The essence of this chapter is that those who tread the path of faith and seek the cups of certitude must cleanse and sanctify themselves from all contingent concerns: their ears from listening to sayings, their hearts from conjectures concerning the veils of glory, their spirits from attachment to outward means, and their eyes from attending to perishable words. They must pursue their path trusting in God and turning to Him, so that they may become receptive to the radiant revelations of the suns of divine knowledge and recognition, and a place where the outpourings of the infinite Unseen may appear. For if a person wishes to make the sayings, deeds, and actions of others, whether learned or ignorant, the measure of recognition of God and His chosen ones, that person will never enter the paradise of recognition of the Lord of Might, nor attain the wellsprings of the knowledge and wisdom of the Sovereign of Oneness, nor ever reach the abiding home, nor receive sustenance from the cup of nearness and good pleasure.

4 Consider past ages: how many people, both high and low, were always awaiting the manifestations of divine Oneness in holy human temples. At every moment and in every season they watched and waited, offering prayers and supplications that perhaps the breeze of divine mercy would begin to blow and the promised Beauty would step from the pavilion of the unseen into the realm of manifestation. Yet when the gates of grace were opened, the cloud of bounty was raised, and the Sun of the unseen appeared above the horizon of power, they all denied Him and shunned meeting Him, although meeting Him is the very meeting with God. The details of this are recorded and set down in all the heavenly books.

5 Now reflect a little: what caused people to object after all their seeking and hopes? They objected so fiercely that speech and expression, spoken account and written record, are all powerless and inadequate to describe it. Not one of the holy Manifestations or Dawning-Places of Oneness appeared without being afflicted by the objections, denials, and disputations of the people. As He says: “Alas for the servants! No messenger comes to them without their mocking him.” And elsewhere He says: “Every nation plotted against its messenger to seize him, and they disputed with falsehood to refute the truth with it.”

6 Likewise, the revealed words that have descended from the cloud of eternal power and the heaven of divine might are beyond people’s capacity to count or encompass. For those endowed with hearts and vision, the Surah of Hud suffices. Reflect a little on that blessed surah and ponder it with your original, unspoiled nature, so that you may gain some awareness of the wondrous affairs of the Prophets and the rejection and denial voiced by the words of negation. Perhaps you may enable people to soar from the abode of heedlessness of the self to the nest of unity and divine recognition, and may drink the pure water of everlasting wisdom and be nourished by the fruits of the tree of knowledge of the Lord of Glory. This is the portion of detached souls from the holy, enduring table sent down from heaven.

7 If you become aware of the trials of the Prophets and the reasons and causes of people’s objections to those Suns of divine Being, you will gain insight into many matters. Moreover, the more you consider people’s objections to the Dawning-Places of the suns of the attributes of Oneness, the firmer and more steadfast you will become in your religion and in the Cause of God. For this reason, some accounts of the Prophets are briefly mentioned in these Tablets, so that it may become clear and evident that in every age and era people inflicted upon the Manifestations of power and Dawning-Places of might things that the pen is ashamed and abashed to recount. Perhaps these accounts will keep some people from being troubled by the rejection and objections of the learned and ignorant of the earth, and will instead increase their certitude and assurance.

8 Among the Prophets was Noah, who lamented for nine hundred and fifty years and called people to the blessed valley of the spirit, yet no one answered Him. Each day they inflicted such harm and suffering upon that blessed Being that they felt certain He would perish. What mockery, ridicule, and taunts were directed against Him! As He says: “Whenever a company of His people passed by Him, they mocked Him. He said: ‘If you mock us, we will indeed mock you as you mock us. Soon you will know.’” After long periods, He several times promised His companions that help would be sent down at an appointed time. Each time a change in the decree occurred, and some of those few companions turned away because of the appearance of that change. The details are recorded in most of the well-known books and have surely come, or will come, to your attention. At last, only forty souls, or seventy-two souls, remained with Him, as is mentioned in the books and traditions. Finally, from the depths of His soul He raised the cry: “My Lord, do not leave a single unbeliever dwelling on the earth.”

9 Now some reflection is needed: why did those people object and keep their distance in this way throughout that period, and why did they not exchange the garment of negation for the honor of attaining the robes of affirmation? And why did a change occur in the divine promises, causing some who had turned toward Him to turn away? Much reflection is needed, so that you may become aware of the mysteries of unseen affairs, catch the spiritual fragrance of the garden of truth, and acknowledge that divine tests have always existed among His servants and always will, so that light may be distinguished from darkness, truthfulness from falsehood, truth from error, guidance from misguidance, felicity from misery, and the thorn from the flower. As He has said: “Alif. Lam. Mim. Do people suppose they will be left to say, ‘We believe,’ without being tested?”

10 After Noah, the beauty of Hud shone from the dawning-place of creation. For nearly seven hundred years or more, according to differing accounts, He called people to the paradise of nearness to the Lord of Glory. How many afflictions rained upon Him like a downpour! His repeated calls only increased their rejection, and the intensity of His efforts only intensified their disregard. “Their unbelief increases the unbelievers in nothing but loss.”

11 After Him, the person of Salih stepped forth from the unseen, spiritual paradise and called people to the waters of everlasting nearness. For a hundred years or more, He enjoined the divine commandments and forbade what was prohibited. It bore no fruit, and no effect appeared. Several times He withdrew from sight, even though that eternal Beauty called people to nothing but the city of Oneness. As He says: “And to Thamud We sent their brother Salih. He said: ‘My people, worship God; you have no god other than Him’”—and so on to the end of the passage. “They said: ‘Salih, before this you were someone in whom we placed our hopes. Do you forbid us to worship what our fathers worship? We are indeed in troubling doubt about what you call us to.’” Nothing availed, until, at a single cry, they all returned to the fire.

12 After Him, the beauty of the Friend unveiled Himself, the banner of guidance was raised, and He called the people of the earth to the light of piety. However earnestly He counseled them, it bore no fruit but envy and yielded nothing but heedlessness, except among those who detached themselves wholly for God and ascended on the wings of certitude to a station God has raised beyond comprehension. The details of His story are well known: how many enemies surrounded Him, until the fire of envy and rejection was kindled. After the episode of the fire, they expelled that divine Lamp from the city, as is mentioned in all the epistles and books.

13 Afterward, His time came to an end, until the turn of Moses arrived. With the staff of command and the white hand of knowledge, He came forth from the Paran of divine love; with the serpent of power and eternal majesty, He appeared from the Sinai of light in the arena of revelation. He called all who dwell in the realm of creation to the kingdom of everlasting life and the fruits of the tree of faithfulness. You have heard what objections Pharaoh and his chiefs raised, and how many stones of suspicion were hurled by idolatrous souls at that goodly Tree. It reached the point where Pharaoh and his chiefs set themselves to quench and extinguish the fire of that divine Lote-Tree with the water of denial and rejection, unaware that the fire of divine wisdom cannot be quenched by material water, nor can the lamp of divine power be extinguished by contrary winds. Rather, in this station water causes it to blaze and wind preserves it, if you look with penetrating sight and walk in the way of God’s good pleasure. How fine were the words spoken by the believer of Pharaoh’s household, whose story the Lord of Glory recounts to His Beloved: “A believing man from Pharaoh’s household, who concealed his faith, said: ‘Would you kill a man for saying, “My Lord is God,” when he has brought you clear proofs from your Lord? If he is lying, his lie will be held against him; but if he is truthful, some of what he threatens you with will befall you. God does not guide anyone who is a transgressor and a liar.’” In the end, matters reached such a point that they put this very believer to death with the utmost torment. Indeed, God’s curse rests upon the unjust.

14 Now reflect a little on these matters: what caused such differences that whenever a Manifestation of the True One appeared in the contingent world from the horizon of the placeless, such corruption, turmoil, oppression, and upheaval became manifest throughout the world? Yet all the Prophets, at the time of Their appearance, gave people glad tidings of the Prophet to come and mentioned a sign of the next revelation, as is recorded in all the books. Given that people were seeking and awaiting the holy Manifestations, and that the signs were mentioned in the books, why should such things happen in the world—that in every age and era all the Prophets and chosen Ones should suffer such oppression, coercion, and aggression? As He says: “Whenever a Messenger came to you with what your souls did not desire, did you grow arrogant, calling some impostors and killing others?” He says that whenever, in any age or era, a Messenger came to you from the Lord with something contrary to your own desires, you grew arrogant and did not attain certainty; some of those Prophets you called impostors, and others you killed.

15 Consider, then, what caused these actions, that they should treat the Countenances of the beauty of the All-Glorious in this way. Whatever caused those people to turn away and close their eyes in those times has now caused these people to be deceived as well. If we say that the divine proofs were not complete and perfect, and therefore gave rise to people’s objections, this is blatant unbelief. For it is utterly incompatible with the bounty of the All-Bountiful and far removed from His all-embracing mercy that He should choose a soul from among all His servants to guide His creation, yet fail to bestow on Him sufficient and adequate proof, and then punish His creatures for not turning toward Him. Rather, the generosity of the Sovereign of existence has always encompassed all contingent beings through the appearance of the Manifestations of His own Self. There is not a moment when His grace is cut off or the rains of mercy are withheld from the cloud of His loving care. These occurrences arise, then, only from narrow-minded souls who walk in the valley of pride and arrogance and roam the deserts of remoteness, following their own suppositions and whatever they have heard from their religious scholars. Thus they do nothing but turn away and seek no outcome but to close their eyes. It is clear to everyone with discernment that if, at the appearance of each of the Manifestations of the Sun of Truth, these people had cleansed and sanctified their eyes, ears, and hearts from whatever they had seen, heard, and understood, they would certainly not have been deprived of the divine Beauty or barred from the sanctuary of nearness to and reunion with the holy Daysprings. But in every age they weighed the proof against their own knowledge, which they had heard from their religious scholars; and because it did not accord with their feeble minds, such unworthy acts appeared from them in the world of manifestation.

16 At all times, it has been the religious scholars of the age, in whose capable hands lay the reins of the people, who have turned them away and kept them from the shore of the ocean of oneness. Some did so out of love of leadership, and others through lack of knowledge and understanding. Thus all the Prophets drank the life-giving waters of martyrdom with the sanction and permission of the religious scholars of Their age, and soared to the highest horizon of glory. What oppression the leaders and religious scholars of each age inflicted upon the Sovereigns of existence and the Gems of all desire! They contented themselves with these few fleeting days and were deprived of the imperishable kingdom. They denied their eyes the sight of the radiance of the Beloved’s beauty and deprived their ears of the wondrous melodies of the Dove of the Desired One. For this reason, the circumstances of the religious scholars of every age are mentioned in all the heavenly books. As He says: “People of the Book, why do you disbelieve in God’s signs when you yourselves bear witness?” And likewise He says: “People of the Book, why do you clothe truth in falsehood and conceal the truth when you know?” Elsewhere He says: “Say: People of the Book, why do you turn others away from the path of God?” It is clear that the people of the Book who turned people away from the straight path were the religious scholars of that age. The names and particulars of them all are recorded in the books, and this can be understood from most of the verses and traditions, if you look with the eye of God.

17 Reflect, then, with the eye of divine insight on the horizons of heavenly knowledge and the inner realities of the perfect, eternal Words, so that all the mysteries of spiritual wisdom may appear from behind the pavilion of grace and bounty, free of the veils of glory. All the people’s objections and disputes have arisen from a lack of understanding and recognition. For example, they did not understand the statements that the Countenances of the beauty of the True One made concerning the signs of the next revelation, nor did they grasp their true meaning. Thus they raised the standard of corruption and unfurled the banners of discord. It is clear that none but the eternal Temples can understand the interpretation of the words of the eternal Doves, and none but the ears of the people of everlasting life can hear the melodies of the spiritual Dove. The Copt of oppression can never have a share of the wine of the Israelite of justice, nor can the Pharaoh of unbelief gain any knowledge of the white hand of Moses. As He says: “None knows its interpretation except God and those firmly grounded in knowledge.” Nevertheless, they sought the interpretation of the Book from those veiled from the truth and did not take knowledge from its source.

18 For example, when the days of Moses had passed and the lights of Jesus shone from the dawn of the spirit and encompassed the world, all the Jews objected: “The person promised in the Torah must promote and complete the laws of the Torah. Yet this young man of Nazareth, who calls himself the Messiah of God, has abrogated the laws of divorce and the Sabbath, which are among the greatest laws of Moses. Moreover, the signs of the revelation have not yet appeared.” Thus the Jews are still awaiting the revelation mentioned in the Torah. How many Manifestations of the holiness of oneness and Daysprings of eternal light have appeared in creation since Moses! Yet the Jews have remained, and still remain, concealed behind satanic veils of self and false, selfish suppositions, waiting to see when their imagined figure will appear with the stated signs as they themselves have understood them. Thus God seized them for their sin, took from them the spirit of faith, and tormented them with a fire in the abyss of hell. This was due only to the Jews’ failure to understand the passages recorded in the Torah concerning the signs of the next revelation. Since they did not grasp their true meaning, and such things did not occur outwardly either, they were deprived of the beauty of Jesus and did not attain the presence of God, and they remained among those who wait. All peoples have always clung, and continue to cling, to these same fabrications of unworthy thoughts, depriving themselves of any share in the pure and gentle flowing springs.

19 To unveil these mysteries, some of the sayings of the Prophets were mentioned in the wondrous melodies of Hijaz in tablets previously written for one of the friends. Now, at your request, We mention them again in these pages in the sweet strains of Iraq, that they may perhaps guide those thirsting in the deserts of remoteness to the ocean of nearness, and bring those lost in the wilderness of separation and estrangement to the tents of nearness and reunion. May the cloud of error be lifted and the world-illumining sun of guidance rise from the horizon of the soul. In God I place My trust, and from Him I seek help, that perhaps there may flow from this Pen what will bring the hearts of people to life, so that all may rise from their beds of heedlessness and hear the melodies of the doves of paradise from a tree planted by the hands of power, with God’s permission, in the garden of oneness.

20 It has been known and clear to those endowed with knowledge that, when the fire of the love of Jesus had burned away the veils of Jewish restrictions and His authority had, to some extent, outwardly prevailed, that unseen Beauty one day spoke of separation to some of His spiritual companions, kindled the fire of longing, and said, “I am going away and will come again.” Elsewhere He said, “I am going away, and another will come to say what I have not said and complete what I have said.” These two statements are, in truth, one, if you behold the Manifestations of oneness with the eye of God.

21 If viewed with the eye of spiritual insight, the Book of Jesus and His Cause were, in truth, confirmed in the age of the Seal as well. With regard to His name, He Himself declared, “I am Jesus,” and He also affirmed that the works, accounts, and Book of Jesus were from God. From this standpoint, no difference is seen between their persons, nor is any distinction discerned between their Books, for both arose through the command of God, both spoke in remembrance of God, and the Books of both conveyed the commandments of God. This is why Jesus Himself said, “I am going away and will return.” It is like the sun: if the sun of today says, “I am the sun of the previous day,” it speaks the truth; and if, with regard to the limitations of each day, it says, “I am other than that sun,” it also speaks the truth. Likewise, consider the days: if it is said that they are all one thing, this is correct and true; and if it is said that, within the limits of their names and distinctive features, they are different from one another, this too is true. As you see, although they are one thing, a different name, different properties, and different features are nevertheless discerned in each that are not discerned in another. Through this same explanation and principle, comprehend the stations of distinction, difference, and unity of the holy Manifestations, so that you may recognize and understand the allusions in the words of that Originator of names and attributes concerning the stations of unity and distinction, and fully find the answer to your question about that eternal Beauty designating Himself in each station by a particular name and distinctive description.

22 Afterward, His companions and disciples asked what the sign of His return and appearance would be, and when this would take place. On several occasions they put this question to that incomparable Countenance, and on each occasion He mentioned a sign, as recorded in the four Gospels.

23 I, this wronged one, will mention one passage and, for the sake of God, bestow upon the servants of God the hidden blessings of the treasured Lote Tree, so that mortal beings may not be deprived of its everlasting fruits. Perhaps they may attain a sprinkling from the unfailing rivers of the Lord of Glory that have flowed in Baghdad, the Abode of Peace, without My asking any recompense or reward. “We feed you only for the sake of God; We desire from you neither recompense nor thanks.” This is the food through which spirits and radiant hearts attain everlasting life, and this is that very heavenly table of which He says, “Our Lord, send down to us a table from heaven.” This table will never be withheld from its people, nor will it be exhausted. At every moment it grows from the tree of grace and descends from the heavens of mercy and justice, as He has said: “As a parable: a good word is like a good tree, its root firm and its branches in heaven; it yields its fruit at all times.”

24 It is a pity for a person to deny himself this gracious gift and deprive himself of this lasting blessing and enduring life. Appreciate, then, the value of this spiritual table, so that through the wondrous favors of that true Sun, dead bodies may perhaps find fresh life and withered spirits attain boundless joy. My brother, we must make an effort, while the days remain, to taste from the cups of eternity. The life-giving breeze will not always blow from the Egypt of the Beloved; the rivers of exposition will not always flow; nor will the gates of paradise remain forever open. A time will come when the nightingales of paradise will fly from the holy rose garden to their divine nests, and you will no longer hear the nightingale’s song or behold the rose’s beauty. So, while the eternal Dove is singing in rapture and the divine springtime displays its splendor and adornment, seize the opportunity and do not deprive the ear of your heart of its call. This is this servant’s counsel to you and to the loved ones of God. Whoever wishes, let him accept it, and whoever wishes, let him turn away. God is indeed independent of him and of all that is witnessed and seen.

25 These are the melodies that Jesus, son of Mary, uttered in majestic tones in the paradise of the Gospel concerning the signs of the next revelation. In the first book, attributed to Matthew, when asked about the signs of the next revelation, He answered: “Immediately after the distress of those days, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the earth will be shaken. Then the signs of the Son of Man will appear in heaven, and all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the Son of Man coming upon the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. And He will send His angels with the sound of the great trumpet.” Here ends the quotation. Its translation into Persian is this: After the hardship and tribulation that encompass all people, the sun will be prevented from shedding its radiance—that is, it will grow dark—and the moon will cease to give light, and the stars of heaven will descend to earth, and the foundations of the earth will be shaken. At that time the signs of the Son of Man will appear in heaven; that is, the promised Beauty and pure Essence of being will come from the realm of the unseen into the visible world after the appearance of these signs. He also says that at that time all the tribes dwelling on earth will lament and mourn, and people will see that Beauty of oneness coming from heaven, riding upon a cloud, with power, grandeur, and great bounty, and He will send His angels with the sound of the great trumpet. Here ends the quotation. These same statements are mentioned in the other three books, attributed to Luke, Mark, and John. Since they have been discussed in detail in the Arabic Tablets, We have not undertaken to mention them in these pages and have confined Ourselves to one of them.

26 Because the scholars of the Gospel did not understand the meanings of these statements or the purpose enshrined in these words, and clung to their outward sense, they were barred from the stream of Muḥammad’s outpouring grace and the cloud of Aḥmad’s bounty. The ignorant among that community likewise clung to their own scholars and remained deprived of beholding the beauty of the King of Glory, because such signs as have been mentioned did not appear at the dawning of the Sun of Aḥmad. Thus centuries passed and ages came to an end, and that Essence of the Spirit returned to the eternal seat of His sovereignty. Another blast was sounded in the divine trumpet by the spiritual breath, and dead souls were raised from the graves of heedlessness and error to the land of guidance and the abode of favor. Yet that people still wait for these signs to appear and that promised Figure to come into being, so that they may support Him, spend their wealth in His path, and sacrifice their lives for His sake. Other religious communities, likewise, have through these same suppositions remained far from the Kawthar of the meanings of the Creator’s boundless mercy, and are occupied with their own imaginings.

27 Beyond this passage, there is another statement in the Gospel, where He says, “Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will not pass away.” Its meaning in Persian is this: Heaven and earth may pass away and cease to exist, but My words will never pass away and will always remain enduring and established among people. This is why the people of the Gospel say that the law of the Gospel will never be abrogated, and that whenever the promised Countenance appears with all the signs, He must confirm and establish the law exalted in the Gospel, so that no religion but this religion remains in the whole world. They regard this as an established and indisputable matter. So firmly do they believe it that, even if someone were raised up with all the promised signs but pronounced a ruling contrary to the law apparent in the Gospel, they would certainly neither acknowledge nor accept Him, but would instead declare Him an unbeliever and ridicule Him, as was witnessed at the dawning of the Sun of Muḥammad. Now, if in every revelation they had asked the Manifestations of oneness, with complete humility, about the meanings of these words revealed in the scriptures—words whose meaning all people have failed to attain and have thus remained veiled from the ultimate goal and the Lote Tree of the farthest limit—they would certainly have been guided by the rays of the Sun of guidance and become acquainted with the mysteries of knowledge and wisdom.

28 Now I, this servant, will mention a sprinkling of the meanings of these words, so that those endowed with insight and a sound nature may, through their meaning, become acquainted with all the allusions in the divine words and the intimations in the utterances of the holy Manifestations. Thus may the overwhelming majesty of the words not bar them from the ocean of names and attributes, nor veil them from the Lamp of oneness, which is the seat of the revelation of the divine Essence.

29 His words, “After the distress of those days,” mean the time when people are afflicted with hardship and distress. This is when the signs of the Sun of Truth and the fruits of the Tree of knowledge and wisdom disappear from among the people, the reins of humanity fall into the hands of the ignorant, the doors of divine unity and recognition—which are the primary purpose of human creation—are closed, knowledge turns into conjecture, and guidance gives way to wretchedness. Today, as can be seen, the reins of every group have fallen into the hands of an ignorant person, and they lead people in whatever direction they wish. Among them, nothing remains of the One they worship but a name, and nothing of the One they seek but a word. The winds of desire and self have so prevailed that they have extinguished the lamps of reason and heart within them. Although the doors of divine knowledge have been opened by the keys of heavenly power, and the gems of the existence of created things have been illumined and guided by the light of knowledge and the outpourings of holiness, so that in every thing a door of knowledge has opened and in every atom signs of the Sun have become visible, yet, despite all these revelations of knowledge encompassing the world, they still consider the door of knowledge closed and the rains of mercy cut off. They have clung to conjecture and remained far from the firm, surest handle of knowledge. From what can be understood of them, it seems that they have no natural inclination toward knowledge or its door, nor do they even contemplate its appearance, for in conjecture and supposition they have found ways to earn their bread, whereas in the appearance of the Manifestation of knowledge they have found nothing but the sacrifice of life. Thus they inevitably flee from the latter and cling to the former. Although they regard the divine decree as one, a ruling issues from every corner and a command appears in every place. No two people are found to agree on one ruling, for they seek no god but desire and wish for no path but error. They have regarded leadership as the ultimate attainment of their goal, and counted pride and arrogance as the highest degree of reaching the Beloved. They give precedence to selfish deceits over divine decrees; they have abandoned submission and contentment and occupied themselves with scheming and hypocrisy. With all their strength and power, they preserve these positions, lest their grandeur suffer any diminution or their prestige any harm. If an eye is illumined by the kohl of divine knowledge, it sees a number of beasts preying upon the dead bodies of the souls of God’s servants.

30 Now, what distress and hardship could be greater than the conditions described, when anyone wishing to seek truth or knowledge does not know whom to approach or whom to ask, so greatly have opinions diverged and paths multiplied? This hardship and distress are among the conditions of every revelation: until they occur, the Sun of Truth does not appear, for the dawn of the revelation of guidance rises after the night of error. Thus the traditions and reported sayings contain all these themes: that unbelief will overtake the world, that darkness will encompass it, and similar statements, as already mentioned. Because these traditions are well known, and for brevity, I, this servant, have refrained from quoting their wording.

31 Now, if they understand this distress to mean that the world will become constricted, or imagine other things of their own devising, these will never be witnessed, and they will certainly say that this condition has not been fulfilled, as they have said and continue to say. Rather, distress means being deprived of divine knowledge and an understanding of the heavenly words: in the days when the Sun and its mirrors have set, people fall into hardship and difficulty and do not know to whom they should turn, as already mentioned. Thus We teach you something of the interpretation of the traditions and impart to you some of the mysteries of wisdom, so that you may discover what is intended and be among those who have drunk from the cup of knowledge and recognition.

32 And His words, “The sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven.” The sun and moon mentioned in the words of the Prophets do not refer solely to this visible sun and moon that we observe. Rather, they have intended many meanings by sun and moon, each meaning suited to its particular context. For example, one meaning of the sun is the Suns of Truth, who rise from the dawning-place of eternity and impart grace to all created things. These Suns of Truth are the universal Manifestations of God in the realms of His attributes and names. Just as, by the command of the true Object of worship, the visible sun nurtures visible things—fruits, trees, colors, produce, minerals, and all else that is seen in the material world—so the trees of divine unity, the fruits of singleness, the leaves of detachment, the flowers of knowledge and certitude, and the fragrant plants of wisdom and utterance appear through the care and nurture of the spiritual Suns. Thus, when these Suns shine, the world is renewed, the rivers of life flow, the seas of beneficence surge, the clouds of grace rise, and the breezes of bounty blow over the forms of created beings. It is through the heat of these divine Suns and spiritual fires that the warmth of divine love is kindled within the world’s foundations, and through the grace of these sanctified Spirits that the spirit of everlasting life is bestowed upon the bodies of the perishable dead. In truth, this visible sun is but a sign of the radiance of that spiritual Sun, a Sun for which no counterpart, likeness, equal, or rival can be found. All things subsist through its existence, appear through its grace, and return to it. From it all things have appeared, and to the treasuries of its command they have returned; from it all created things have begun, and to the repositories of its decree they have gone back.

33 When, in explanation and discourse, particular names and attributes are assigned to them, as you have heard and continue to hear, this is only for the understanding of weak and limited minds. Otherwise, they have always been sanctified above every name and will forever remain exalted beyond every description. The essences of names have no access to their court of holiness, and the subtleties of attributes have no path into the kingdom of their glory. Glory be to God, that His chosen Ones should be known through anything other than their own beings, or His holy Ones described by anything other than their own selves! Exalted is He above all that His servants say in describing them, and exalted is He above all that they understand.

34 These sanctified Lights have often been called suns in the words of the infallible Ones. Among these is the Prayer of Lamentation, which says, “Where are the rising suns? Where are the shining moons? Where are the radiant stars?” It is thus clear that, in their primary meaning, the sun, moon, and stars refer to the Prophets, the holy Ones, and their companions, through the light of whose knowledge the worlds of the unseen and the seen are bright and illumined.

35 In another context, the sun, moon, and stars refer to the religious scholars of the previous revelation who are present at the time of the next revelation and hold in their hands the reins of the people’s religion. If, when the new Sun appears, they become illumined by its radiance, they will be accepted, radiant, and bright. Otherwise, the judgment of darkness applies to them, even though they may outwardly be guides. For all these conditions—unbelief and faith, guidance and error, blessedness and wretchedness, light and darkness—depend upon acknowledging that divine, spiritual Sun. Any religious scholar upon whom the Source of recognition pronounces the judgment of faith on the day of loss and beneficence is truly entitled to the designations of knowledge, divine approval, light, and faith. Otherwise, the judgment of ignorance, negation, unbelief, and oppression applies to him.

36 It is evident to everyone with insight that, just as the light of a star fades before the shining of the visible sun, so the sun of outward knowledge, wisdom, and understanding fades and grows dark before the rising of the Sun of Truth, the spiritual Sun.

37 These scholars are called suns because of their high standing, fame, and renown, like the acknowledged religious scholars of the age, who are renowned throughout the lands and accepted among the people. If they reflect the divine Sun, they are counted among the exalted suns; otherwise, they belong to the suns of Sijjin, as He says, “The sun and the moon are subject to a reckoning.” You have surely heard the meaning of the sun and moon in the verse just mentioned; there is no need to repeat it. Anyone who is of the same nature as this sun and moon—that is, in turning toward falsehood and away from truth—will certainly have come forth from that reckoning and will return to it.

38 So, seeker, we must cling to the surest handle, that perhaps we may turn from the night of error to the light of guidance, flee the shadow of negation and enter the shelter of affirmation, and be freed from the fire of reckoning and illumined by the light of the beauty of the All-Bountiful. Peace. Thus We give you some of the fruits of the Tree of knowledge, so that you may surely be among those who rejoice in the paradise of God’s wisdom.

39 In another sense, the terms sun, moon, and stars refer to the teachings and laws established in every religious dispensation, such as prayer and fasting, which, in the dispensation of the Quran, are the most firmly established and greatest of all laws after the concealment of the beauty of Muhammad. The traditions and reports attest to this, and because it is so well known, there is no need to cite them. Indeed, in every age the law of prayer has been firmly established and observed, as, from the lights shining from the Sun of Muhammad,

40 it is related that the law of prayer was revealed to all the prophets in every age, except that in each age, according to the needs of the time, it was given a particular form and new observances. And since in every subsequent revelation the observances, customs, and teachings that were exalted, firmly established, radiant, clear, and enduring in the previous revelation are abrogated, they have therefore been referred to figuratively as the sun and moon: “so that He may test you as to which of you is best in deeds.”

41 In a tradition, too, the terms sun and moon have been applied to fasting and prayer, as He says: “Fasting is radiance, and prayer is light.” One day, however, I was sitting in a place when a well-known religious scholar arrived and, in the course of conversation, cited this tradition. He said, “Since fasting produces heat in the body, it is described as radiance, which is the sun; and since night prayer calls for coolness, it is described as light, which is the moon.” I saw that this poor man had not attained a single drop from the ocean of meanings or a single ember from the fire of the Tree of divine wisdom. After a while, with the utmost courtesy, I remarked, “Sir, what you have said about the meaning of this tradition is commonly repeated on people’s tongues and lips. Yet it seems that another meaning may also be understood from it.” He asked for an explanation. I said that the Seal of the Prophets and Lord of the Chosen Ones had likened the religion established in the Quran to heaven because of its loftiness, elevation, grandeur, and encompassing of all religions. Just as in the visible heaven two supremely great and steadfast pillars have been established—the two luminaries called the sun and the moon—so in the heaven of religion two luminaries have been ordained, namely fasting and prayer: “Islam is a heaven; fasting is its sun, and prayer its moon.”

42 This, then, is what is meant by the allusions in the words of the Manifestations of God. The application of the terms sun and moon, in these instances, to the stations mentioned has thus been verified and established through revealed verses and transmitted reports. It follows that the darkening of the sun and moon and the falling of the stars refer to the waywardness of the religious scholars and the abrogation of the laws established in a religious dispensation, which the Manifestation of that revelation foretells through these allusions. None but the righteous have a share of this cup, and none but the good have a portion of it: “The righteous will drink from a cup whose mixture is camphor.”

43 It is certain that in every subsequent revelation the sun of the teachings, laws, commands, and prohibitions established in the previous revelation grows dark—that sun and moon of knowledge and commandments beneath whose shelter the people of that age were illumined and guided. That is, its authority and influence come to an end. Now consider: had the followers of the Gospel understood what was meant by the sun and moon, or had they asked the Manifestation of divine knowledge without objection or obstinacy, their meanings would certainly have become clear, and they would not have become so afflicted and entangled in the darkness of self and desire. Indeed, because they did not acquire knowledge from its source and mine, they have perished in the deadly valley of unbelief and error. They still have not realized that all the signs have appeared, that the promised Sun has shone from the horizon of revelation, and that the sun and moon of the former teachings, laws, and knowledge have grown dark and set.

44 Now, with the eye of certain knowledge and the two wings of certain vision, set foot on the path of certain truth: “Say: God! Then leave them to play in their idle discourse.” Thus you may be counted among those companions of whom He says, “Those who say, ‘Our Lord is God,’ and then remain steadfast—the angels descend upon them,” and may behold all these mysteries with your own eyes.

45 My brother, take the step of the spirit, so that in an instant you may cross the far-stretching deserts of remoteness and separation, enter the paradise of nearness and reunion, and in a single breath attain the presence of the divine Souls. With bodily steps these stages can never be traversed, nor can the goal be attained. Peace be upon the one who follows the Truth through truth and stands, in the name of God, on the path of the Cause upon the shore of recognition.

46 This is the meaning of the blessed verse in which He says, “No! I swear by the Lord of the places of rising and setting.” For each of these suns has a place of rising and setting. Since the commentators did not discover the reality of these suns, they were at a loss to interpret this blessed verse. Some said that because the sun rises each day from a point different from that of the previous day, the plural form was used. Others wrote that the four seasons are intended: because in each season the sun rises in one place and sets in another, the expressions “places of rising” and “places of setting” were used. Such is the extent of people’s knowledge; yet what ignorance and faults they attribute to the Gems of knowledge and the Subtleties of wisdom!

47 Likewise, from these clear, firm, conclusive, and unambiguous statements, understand the splitting of heaven, which is one of the signs of the Hour and the Resurrection. Thus He says, “When heaven is split apart.” What is meant is the heaven of religions, which is raised in every revelation and split apart by the next—that is, it is annulled and abrogated. By God, if considered rightly, the splitting of this heaven is greater than the splitting of the visible sky. Reflect a little: a religion has been established for years; everyone has grown and developed under its shelter and has long been trained by its radiant laws. From their fathers and forefathers they have heard of nothing else, so that their eyes have perceived nothing but the sway of its authority, and their ears have heard nothing but its laws. Then a Soul appears and, through divine strength and power, breaks apart and severs all these things—indeed, negates them all. Now consider whether this is greater, or what this ignorant rabble has imagined the splitting of heaven to be.

48 Consider also the hardship and bitterness endured by those radiant Figures who, with no outward helper or supporter, establish the laws of God in the face of all the peoples of the earth. Despite all the suffering inflicted on those blessed, tender, and sensitive Beings, they remain patient with consummate strength and endure with supreme ascendancy.

49 Likewise, understand the meaning of the transformation of the earth: upon whatever hearts the cloud of mercy from that heaven bestowed its spring rain of bounty, the soil of those hearts was transformed into the earth of knowledge and wisdom. What fragrant herbs of divine unity have sprung up in the gardens of their hearts, and what anemones of the realities of knowledge and wisdom have grown from their illumined breasts! If the earth of their hearts had not been transformed, how could men who have not learned a single letter, seen a teacher, or set foot in any school utter words and knowledge that no one can comprehend? It is as though they have been fashioned from the dust of eternal knowledge and kneaded with the water of divinely bestowed wisdom. Thus He says, “Knowledge is a light that God casts into the heart of whomever He wills.” This kind of knowledge has always been, and remains, praiseworthy—not the limited learning produced by veiled and clouded minds, which people sometimes steal from one another and use to vaunt themselves over others.

50 If only people’s hearts were cleansed and sanctified from the imprints of these limitations and dark words, so that they might receive the shining rays of the sun of knowledge and meanings, and the gems of the mysteries of divinely bestowed wisdom. Now consider: if these barren lands of existence were not transformed, how could they become the place where the mysteries of divine oneness appear and the gems of the divine Essence emerge? Thus He says, “On the day when the earth is changed into another earth.”

51 Through the breezes of the bounty of that Sovereign of existence, the outward earth, too, has been transformed, if you would but reflect on the mysteries of revelation.

52 Further, grasp the meaning of this verse, in which He says: “The whole earth will be in His grasp on the Day of Resurrection, and the heavens will be rolled up in His right hand. Glory be to Him, and exalted is He above the partners they associate with Him.” Its meaning is that on the Day of Resurrection the whole earth is held in His hand, and heaven is rolled up in His right hand. Now, a little fairness is needed: if the intended meaning is what people have understood, what merit would follow from it? Moreover, it is certain that no hand visible to the outward eye and performing such acts can be attributed to the Essence of the exalted God. Indeed, to affirm such a thing is sheer unbelief and utter falsehood. And if you say that it is the Manifestations of His Cause who are charged with this task at the Resurrection, this too is most implausible and pointless. Rather, by the earth is meant the earth of understanding and knowledge, and by the heavens, the heavens of religions. Now consider how He gathered into the grasp of His power and might the earth of knowledge and understanding that had previously been spread out, spread a new and exalted earth within the hearts of His servants, and caused fresh herbs, wondrous flowers, and lofty trees to spring from radiant hearts.

53 Likewise, consider how the heavens of the religions raised up in the past were rolled up in the right hand of power, how the heaven of the Bayán was raised by the command of God, and how it was adorned with the sun, moon, and stars of new and wondrous ordinances. These are the mysteries of the words, unveiled and made manifest, so that you may perceive the dawn of meanings, extinguish the lamps of conjecture, fancy, doubt, and misgiving through the power of trust in God and detachment, and kindle the new lamp of knowledge and certainty in the niche of your inmost heart.

54 The purpose of all these symbolic words and enigmatic allusions that issue from the sources of divine authority is, as has been mentioned, to test His servants, so that the fertile and radiant soil of hearts may be distinguished from barren and perishable ground. This has always been God’s way among His servants, as is recorded in the Books.

55 Likewise, consider the verse concerning the direction of prayer. After the Sun of Muḥammad’s prophethood had migrated from its dawning-place in Baṭḥá to Yathrib, He would turn toward Jerusalem at the time of prayer, until the Jews uttered certain unseemly remarks that are not fitting to mention here and would prolong the discussion. He became deeply distressed and gazed toward heaven in thought and perplexity. Then Gabriel descended and recited this verse: “We have indeed seen your face turning toward heaven; We will surely turn you toward a direction of prayer that will please you.” Then one day, while He and a group of His companions were performing the obligatory noon prayer and had completed two units of prayer, Gabriel descended and said: “Turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque.” In the midst of prayer, He turned away from Jerusalem and faced the Kaaba. Immediately, turmoil and agitation arose among His companions, to such a degree that some broke off their prayers and turned away. This trial was only to test His servants; otherwise, that true Sovereign could have left the direction of prayer entirely unchanged, retained Jerusalem in that age as well, and not stripped it of this robe of acceptance.

56 Thus, in the time of most of the prophets raised up as Messengers after Moses, such as David and Jesus, and other great prophets who came between these two, the ordinance concerning the direction of prayer was never changed. All these Messengers, on behalf of the Lord of the worlds, commanded people to turn in that same direction. All lands stand in the same relation to that true Sovereign, except any land He singles out for a particular purpose when His Manifestations appear. As He says: “To God belong the East and the West; wherever you turn, there is the face of God.” These things being established, why was it changed, causing His servants distress and alarm and His companions turmoil and agitation? Indeed, such things, which fill every soul with dismay, occur only so that all may be put to the touchstone of God’s testing, and the truthful may be distinguished and separated from the false. Thus, after the disagreement among the people, He says: “We appointed the direction of prayer that you formerly observed only to know who follows the Messenger and who turns back on his heels.” Its meaning is this: We did not change and set aside the direction of prayer, namely Jerusalem, except to know who follows you and who turns back on his heels—that is, who turns away, refuses to obey, invalidates his prayer, and flees. “Like startled wild asses fleeing from a lion.”

57 If you reflect a little on this very subject and explanation, you will see the gates of meanings and elucidation opened and behold all knowledge and its mysteries unveiled. These things are only for the education of souls and their deliverance from the cage of self and desire. Otherwise, that true Sovereign has from eternity been, in His Essence, independent of the recognition of created beings, and will forever remain, in His own Being, independent of the worship of contingent beings. A single breeze from His self-sufficiency would honor the entire world with robes of sufficiency, and a single drop from the ocean of His generosity would confer everlasting life upon all existence. But since the purpose is to distinguish truth from falsehood and the sun from the shadow, the tests sent down by the Lord of Might pour forth at every moment like abundant rain.

58 If people were to reflect a little on the prophets of the past and their appearance, the matter would become very easy for the inhabitants of these lands. They would no longer remain veiled by deeds and words contrary to self and desire, but would burn away every veil with the fire of the Tree of recognition and rest upon the throne of tranquillity and assurance. For example, Moses, son of ʿImrán, who was one of the exalted prophets and the bearer of a Book, was passing through the marketplace one day early in His life, before His prophetic mission. Two men were disputing with one another. One of them sought Moses’ help, and He assisted him and killed his opponent, as is recorded in the Book. To recount the details would delay and interrupt the purpose of this discussion. News of this spread through the city, and fear overcame Him, as the text of the Book states. Then He was informed that “the chiefs are plotting to kill you,” and He left the city and took up residence in Midian in the service of Shuʿayb. On His return, He entered the blessed valley, the wilderness of Sinai, and beheld the revelation of the Sovereign of Oneness from the Tree that is neither of the East nor of the West. He heard the life-giving spiritual call from the divinely kindled fire and was charged with guiding the people of Pharaoh, so that He might deliver them from the valley of self and desire and lead them into the heart-cheering expanses of spirit and guidance, and, through the flowing waters of detachment, bring all who dwell in creation from the bewilderment of remoteness to the Abode of Peace of nearness. When He entered Pharaoh’s dwelling and proclaimed what He had been charged to convey, Pharaoh spoke insolently and said: “Was it not you who killed a man and became one of the unbelievers?” Thus the Lord of Grandeur has reported Pharaoh’s words to Moses: “You did that deed of yours, and you are one of the unbelievers.” He said: “I did it then, when I was among those who had gone astray. So I fled from you when I feared you; then my Lord bestowed wisdom upon me and made me one of the Messengers.”

59 Now reflect on the divine trials and the wonders of His tests: a person known to have killed a man, who Himself acknowledges His wrongdoing, as is mentioned in the verse, and who, to outward appearances, had been brought up for thirty years or less in Pharaoh’s house and had grown up on his food and sustenance—suddenly God chose Him from among His servants and charged Him with the task of supreme guidance. Yet that mighty Sovereign could have prevented Moses from killing, so that He would not be known among His servants by this reputation, causing dismay in their hearts and leading souls to shun Him.

60 Likewise, consider Mary’s condition: that exalted Countenance, overwhelmed by the gravity of the matter and by bewilderment, wished for nonexistence. This is understood from the blessed verse stating that, after the birth of Jesus, Mary lamented and uttered these words: “If only I had died before this and been utterly forgotten.” Its translation is: “If only I had died before this matter came to pass and been among the forgotten.” By God, hearts melt and souls dissolve upon hearing these words. This anguish and sorrow arose only from the taunts of enemies and the objections of the people of unbelief and wickedness. Reflect, then: what answer could Mary give the people? How could she make it clear to them that a child whose father was unknown was of the Holy Spirit? So that veiled Lady of eternity took the child and returned home. As soon as her people caught sight of her, they said: “O sister of Aaron, your father was not an evil man, nor was your mother unchaste.” Its meaning is: “O sister of Aaron, your father was not a wicked man, and your mother was not a woman of loose morals.”

61 Now consider this supreme ordeal and this greatest test. Beyond all this, He bestowed prophethood upon that very Essence of the Spirit who was known among the people as fatherless, and made Him His proof to all the inhabitants of the heavens and the earth.

62 Now observe how greatly the affairs of the Manifestations of Revelation, brought forth by the Sovereign of creation, run counter to the desires and inclinations of His servants. When you become acquainted with these gems of mystery, you will understand the purpose of that Beloved and regard the words and deeds of that mighty King as alike, so that whatever is seen in His deeds may also be discerned in His words, and whatever is discerned in His words may be seen in His deeds. Thus these deeds and words are outwardly a punishment for the wicked, but inwardly a mercy for the righteous. If viewed with the eye of the heart, the words sent down from the heaven of His will and the events brought forth from the kingdom of His power will be seen as one and understood in the same way, as has been mentioned.

63 Now consider, my brother: if such things were to appear in this age and such a story were to unfold, what would people do? I swear by the One who nurtures all existence and sends down the words: at once, without a word, they would pronounce a verdict of unbelief and order an execution. How would they listen to anyone saying that Jesus had appeared through the breath of the Holy Spirit, or that Moses had been commissioned by an irrevocable command? Though you cry out a hundred thousand times, no one would listen to the assertion that a fatherless one had been sent as a Messenger, or that a murderer had brought from the tree of fire the words, “Truly, I am God.”

64 If the eye of fairness is opened, all these statements make it evident that the Manifestation of all these things, and the culmination of them all, is manifest today. Although nothing like these events has occurred in this Revelation, people have nevertheless clung to the idle suppositions of rejected souls. What accusations they have made, and what afflictions they have inflicted, the like of which has never appeared in creation!

65 God is Most Great! When the discourse reached this point, a spiritual fragrance wafted from the everlasting Dawn, and the morning breeze blew from the city of eternal Sheba. Its intimation brought fresh glad tidings to the soul and boundless joy to the spirit. It spread out a new array and brought countless, limitless gifts from that traceless Friend—gifts whose delicate form the robe of mention is far too short to clothe, and whose luminous stature the mantle of expression cannot encompass. Without words it discloses the mysteries of meaning, and without a tongue it speaks the secrets of exposition. It teaches the nightingales on the branches of separation and estrangement to lament and cry out, and instructs them in the ways and customs of love and loving, and the secret of surrendering the heart. It teaches the wondrous flowers of the paradise of nearness and reunion the art of captivating hearts and the manners of enchantment. It bestows the secrets of realities upon the anemones in the garden of love and deposits the subtleties and refinements of its mysteries in the breasts of lovers. Such favor has it shown at this hour that the Holy Spirit yearns for it intensely. It has given the drop the waves of a sea and bestowed upon the atom the splendor of a sun. Its favors have reached such a height that the dung beetle has sought the musk pod, and the bat has made its home before the sun. With the breath of life it has raised the dead from the graves of the body, given the ignorant a seat of honor in knowledge, and assigned the unjust a place on the heights of justice.

66 And the world of being has become pregnant with all these favors. When will the effect of this unseen grace appear in this earthly realm, bring those who have collapsed from thirst to the Beloved’s crystal-clear Kawthar, and admit those lost in the wilderness of remoteness and nonexistence to the pavilion of nearness and of the Loved One’s existence? In the soil of whose hearts will these seeds of holiness sprout, and from the gardens of whose souls will the anemones of unseen realities blossom? In any event, the tree of love is not so ablaze upon the Sinai of affection that the waters of words could quench or exhaust it. Oceans cannot slake the thirst of this fish, and this fiery salamander will make its home nowhere but in the fire of the Friend’s face. So, my brother, kindle the lamp of the spirit within the niche of the heart with the oil of wisdom, and guard it with the glass of understanding, so that the breaths of souls who associate partners with God may neither extinguish it nor keep it from shining. Thus have We illumined the horizon of the heaven of utterance with the lights of the suns of wisdom and spiritual knowledge, so that your heart may be reassured by them and you may be among those who have flown on the wings of certitude in the atmosphere of the love of their Lord, the All-Merciful.

67 And His words, “Then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven.” He says that after the eclipse of the sun of divine knowledge, the falling of the stars of established ordinances, the eclipse of the moon of learning that nurtures His servants, the disappearance of the banners of guidance and salvation, and the darkening of the dawn of truth and righteousness, the sign of the Son of Man will appear in heaven. By heaven is meant the visible heaven: as the appearance of that Orb of the heavens of justice and the sailing of the Ark of guidance upon the sea of grandeur draw near, a star appears in the visible sky, announcing to the inhabitants of the heavens the appearance of that Most Great Luminary. Likewise, a star appears in the heaven of inner meaning, announcing to the people of the earth that most steadfast and most noble Dawn. These two signs in the outward and inward heavens have appeared before the advent of every Prophet, as people have heard.

68 Among them was the Friend of the All-Merciful. Before His appearance, Nimrod had a dream and summoned the soothsayers, who foretold the rising of a star in heaven. Likewise, a person appeared on earth who gave the people glad tidings of His advent.

69 After Him came the story of the One who conversed with God. The soothsayers of that time informed Pharaoh that a star had risen in heaven, indicating the conception of one at whose hands he and his people would perish. Likewise, a learned man appeared who, by night, brought glad tidings, comfort, and reassurance to the children of Israel, as is recorded in the books. If these matters were recounted in detail, this treatise would become a book. Moreover, I do not wish to recount stories of past events. God bears witness that even these statements are made only out of the fullness of My love for you, so that perhaps some of the poor of the earth may reach the shore of wealth, or a group of the ignorant may approach the ocean of knowledge, or those thirsting for understanding may attain the Salsabil of wisdom. Otherwise, I, this servant, consider engagement in such discussions a grave sin and regard it as a great transgression.

70 Likewise, when the advent of Jesus drew near, several of the Magi, having learned of the appearance of the star of Jesus in heaven, followed that star until they entered the city that was the seat of Herod’s rule. In those days, sovereignty over those lands was in his grasp.

71 They said, “Where is the newborn King of the Jews? The one God is witness that, if you reflect even a little, beyond all these established points and proofs already mentioned, the very rejection, abuse, and curses directed by the people of the earth against these horsemen in the field of submission and detachment constitute the greatest proof and the mightiest testimony to their truth. And whenever you reflect upon the objections of all the people—scholars, learned men, and the ignorant—you will become firmer, more deeply rooted, and more steadfast in this Cause, for the Mines of innate knowledge and the Sources of eternal decrees foretold all that has occurred.

267 Although I, this servant, had not intended to cite the traditions of the past, out of affection for you I will mention a few accounts appropriate to this subject, even though there is truly no need, for what has been stated suffices for the whole earth and all who dwell upon it. Indeed, all the scriptures and their mysteries have been set forth in this brief account, so that anyone who reflects a little will perceive, from what has been stated, all the mysteries of the divine words and the acts proceeding from that true Sovereign. Since, however, not everyone is of the same disposition or station, I will cite a few traditions, so that they may bring steadfastness to wavering souls and assurance to troubled minds, and so that the proof of God may be full and complete for the high and low among His servants.

268 Among these traditions is the saying: “When the banner of the Truth appears, the people of the East and the West will curse it.” Now one must drink a little of the wine of detachment, take one’s seat upon the heights of inaccessibility, and bear in mind that “An hour’s reflection is better than seventy years of worship.” What, after all, causes this shameful act—that all people, despite professing love for God and seeking Him, should curse the people of truth after the Manifestation appears, as this tradition indicates? It is clear that the cause is the abrogation of the rules, customs, habits, and practices within whose bounds all people have confined themselves. Otherwise, if the Beauty of the All-Merciful were to follow those same customs and practices and confirm people in their pursuits, why should all this discord and turmoil appear in the world? Confirming and substantiating this noble tradition are His words, exalted is He: “On the day when the caller calls to a thing unknown.”

269 In short, since the Herald of divine unity calls people from beyond the veils of holiness to complete detachment from all they possess, and since this divine call runs counter to their desires, all these trials and tests occur. Consider the state of the people: they make no mention of these firmly established traditions, all of which have been fulfilled, but cling to traditions whose authenticity or falsity is unknown, asking why they have not been fulfilled. Yet even things they had never conceived have appeared and become manifest, and the signs and tokens of the Truth shine like the sun in the midst of heaven. Nevertheless, His servants remain bewildered in the wilderness of ignorance and unknowing. Despite the many verses of the Qur’an and authenticated traditions, all pointing to a new law, a new ordinance, and a wondrous Cause, they still expect the promised Countenance to rule according to the law of the Qur’an, just as the Jews and Christians say the same thing.

270 Among the words indicating a new law and a wondrous Cause are these passages from the Prayer of Lamentation: “Where is the One held in reserve to renew the obligations and practices? And where is the One chosen to restore the faith and the law?” And in the visitation prayer it is said: “Peace be upon the new Truth.” Abu ’Abd Allah was asked about the conduct of the Mahdi: “What will his conduct be?” He replied, peace be upon him: “He will do as the Messenger of God did, blessings be upon him, and will demolish what came before him, just as the Messenger of God demolished the order of the age of ignorance.”

271 Now consider how, despite such traditions, they advance all manner of arguments against any change in the ordinances, even though the purpose of every Revelation is to bring about change and transformation in the foundations of the world, secretly and openly, outwardly and inwardly. For if the affairs of the earth were in no way to change, the appearance of the universal Manifestations would be futile. Moreover, in the ’Avalim, which is among the well-known and authoritative books, it is said: “A youth will appear from the Banu Hashim, possessing a Book and new ordinances,” until it says: “And most of his enemies will be the religious scholars.” Elsewhere it records that Sadiq ibn Muhammad said: “A youth will indeed appear from the Banu Hashim and call upon the people to pledge allegiance to him. He possesses a new Book, and will receive the people’s allegiance on the basis of a new Book, severe upon the Arabs. If you hear anything of him, hasten to him.” How well they have followed the counsel of the Imams of the Faith and the lamps of certitude! Although it says that if you hear that a youth has appeared from the Banu Hashim, calling people to a new divine Book and wondrous ordinances from the Lord, you should hasten to him, they nevertheless all pronounced that Lord of all creation an unbeliever and outside the faith. They approached that Hashimite Light and divine Manifestation only with drawn swords and hearts full of hatred. Consider, too, how explicitly the enmity of the religious scholars is mentioned in the books. Despite all these manifest and demonstrative traditions and clear, established allusions, all people have turned away from the pure essence of knowledge and utterance and have turned toward the manifestations of error and rebellion. Despite these recorded traditions and revealed words, they say whatever their own selves desire. If the Essence of Truth makes a statement contrary to the selfish desires of this group, they immediately declare Him an unbeliever and say: “This contradicts the words of the Imams of the Faith and the manifest lights, and no such command or ordinance has been issued in the firmly established law.” Just so have such useless words proceeded, and continue to proceed, from these mortal frames today.

272 Now consider this tradition, and how all these events were foretold. In the Arba’in it is stated: “A youth will appear from the Banu Hashim, possessing new ordinances. He will summon the people, but no one will answer him, and most of his enemies will be the religious scholars. When he decrees a thing, they will not obey him, but will say: ‘This is contrary to what we have received from the Imams of the Faith.’” And so the tradition continues. Today they all repeat these very words, unaware that He is seated upon the throne of “He does whatever He wills” and established upon the seat of “He ordains whatever He desires.”

273 No understanding can anticipate the manner of His appearance, and no knowledge can encompass the scope of His Cause. Every utterance depends upon His confirmation, and all affairs stand in need of His command. All besides Him are created by His command and exist by His decree. He is the Revealer of the divine mysteries and the Expounder of the wisdom of the unseen, eternal Realm. Thus, in the Bihar al-Anvar, the ‘Avalim, and the Yanbu’, it is recorded that Sadiq ibn Muhammad said: “Knowledge consists of twenty-seven letters. All that the Messengers have brought is two letters, and to this day the people have known no more than these two letters. When our Qa’im arises, he will bring forth the remaining twenty-five letters.” Now consider: he has specified that knowledge consists of twenty-seven letters, and all the Prophets, from Adam to the Seal, have expounded two of its letters and have been sent forth with these two letters. He says that the Qa’im will reveal all these twenty-five letters. From this statement, consider His worth and rank: His worth is greater than that of all the Prophets, and His Cause is higher and more exalted than the knowledge and understanding of all the holy ones. Yet these ignorant rabble measure by their own deficient reason, learning, and understanding a Cause of which the Prophets, the holy ones, and the chosen ones had no knowledge, or which, by God’s irrevocable command, they did not disclose. If it does not conform to their measure, they reject it. “Or do you suppose that most of them hear or understand? They are only like cattle—indeed, they are further astray.”

274 How do they interpret this tradition, which explicitly foretells the revelation of hidden matters and wondrous new things in His days? These wondrous things cause such discord among the people that all the religious scholars and jurists decree the death of Him and His companions, and all the people of the earth rise in opposition. Thus, in the Kafi, in the tradition of Jabir concerning the Tablet of Fatimih, it is said in description of the Qa’im: “Upon him rests the perfection of Moses, the splendor of Jesus, and the patience of Job. His friends will be abased in his time, and their heads will be exchanged as gifts, just as the heads of the Turks and the Daylamites are exchanged as gifts. They will be killed and burned, and will live in fear, terror, and dread. The earth will be stained with their blood, and wailing and lamentation will spread among their women. These are truly My friends.” Now consider how not a word of this tradition has remained unfulfilled. In most places their noble blood was shed; in every town they were taken captive and paraded through provinces and cities, and some were burned. Yet no one reflected that, if the promised Qa’im were to be sent forth and appear with the former law and ordinances, why should these traditions have been related? And why should all this discord arise, to the point that people deem it obligatory to kill these companions and regard tormenting these holy souls as a means of ascending to the heights of nearness to God?

275 Consider, moreover, how all these events that have occurred and deeds that have befallen them were mentioned in the traditions of the past. Thus, in the Rawdih of the Kafi, concerning Zawra, it is said: “And in the Rawdat al-Kafi, Mu’awiyah ibn Wahb relates from Abu ’Abd Allah: He said, ‘Do you know Zawra?’ I replied, ‘May I be your sacrifice! They say it is Baghdad.’ He said, ‘No.’ Then he said, ‘Have you entered Rayy?’ I replied, ‘Yes.’ He said, ‘Have you been to the livestock market?’ I replied, ‘Yes.’ He said, ‘Have you seen the black mountain to the right of the road? That is Zawra. Eighty men from the descendants of such a one will be killed there, each of them fit for the caliphate.’ I asked, ‘Who will kill them?’ He said, ‘The sons of the Persians will kill them.’”

276 Such is the fate and condition of that exalted One’s companions, foretold in the past. Now consider that, according to this tradition, Zawra is the land of Rayy. These companions were put to death there with the cruelest torments, and all these holy souls were martyred by the Persians, as stated in the tradition, as people have heard, and as is clear and evident to the whole world. Why, then, do these worms of the earth not reflect on these traditions, all of which have become manifest like the sun in the midst of heaven, and turn toward the Truth? Instead, clinging to certain traditions whose meaning they have not understood, they have turned away from the manifestation of the Truth and the Beauty of God and chosen their abode in hell. These things result only from the aversion of the jurists of this age and the religious scholars of this time. Thus Ṣādiq ibn Muḥammad says: “The jurists of that time are the worst jurists beneath the shadow of heaven. From them has arisen discord, and to them it will return.”

277 I implore the jurists and religious scholars of the Bayān not to act in this way, and not to inflict upon the divine Essence, the heavenly Light, the eternal Being, the Beginning and End of the unseen Manifestations, in the time of Mustaghāth, what was inflicted in this cycle. Let them not cling to their reason, understanding, and learning, or dispute with that Manifestation of infinite divine knowledge. Yet, despite all these admonitions, it is seen that a one-eyed man, one of the leaders of the people, will arise in the fiercest opposition. Likewise, in every city they will arise to deny that holy Beauty, and the companions of that Sovereign of existence and Essence of all desire will flee into the mountains and deserts and conceal themselves from the oppressors, while some will put their trust in God and, with complete detachment, lay down their lives. It is as though one can already see a man so renowned and celebrated for his perfect asceticism and piety that all people consider obedience to him obligatory and submission to his command necessary, arising to wage war against that Root of the divine Tree and opposing Him with the utmost effort and exertion. Such is the way of the people.

278 We hope, then, that the people of the Bayān will be educated, soar in the atmosphere of the spirit, and dwell in its expanse; that they will distinguish the Truth from all else and recognize the disguises of falsehood with the eye of insight. Yet in these days a breath of envy has blown such that, I swear by the One who nurtures all existence, unseen and seen, from the beginning of the world’s existence—though it has no beginning—until now, such malice, envy, and hatred have never appeared, nor will they ever appear again. Thus a group who have never caught the fragrance of fairness have raised the banners of hypocrisy and united in opposition to this servant. From every direction a spear is raised, and from every side an arrow flies, although I have never boasted of anything before anyone or sought superiority over any soul. To every person I was a most loving companion and an exceedingly forbearing and generous friend. Among the poor I was as one of the poor, and before the learned and the eminent I showed complete submission and acquiescence. Nevertheless, by God, besides whom there is no god, all the trials, hardships, and afflictions inflicted by enemies and the people of the Book are sheer nothingness and utter nonexistence beside what has been inflicted by the friends.

279 But what can I say? Were the world of existence fair, it could not bear this account. When this servant first arrived in this land and gained some knowledge of the events that would later arise, I chose to depart before they occurred and withdrew into the wilderness of separation. For two years I lived alone in the deserts of estrangement. Springs flowed from My eyes, and seas of blood poured from My heart. How many nights I had no food, and how many days My body found no rest! Yet amid these descending afflictions and successive calamities, by Him in whose hand is My soul, perfect joy was present and the utmost gladness evident, for I knew nothing of anyone’s harm or benefit, health or illness. I was occupied with Myself and oblivious to all else, unaware that the snare of divine decree is wider than imagination and the arrow of His destiny beyond all human devising. There is no escape from His snare, and no recourse before His will but acquiescence. I swear by God that in this departure I had no thought of returning, and in this journey no hope of reunion. My sole purpose was to avoid becoming a cause of disagreement among the loved ones, a source of turmoil among the companions, a cause of harm to anyone, or a reason for sorrow to any heart. Beyond what I have mentioned, I had no other thought or purpose, although everyone devised an explanation and imagined something according to their own desires. At last, the command to return issued from the Source of authority, and I had no choice but to submit and return.

280 The pen is powerless to recount what was witnessed after My return. For two years now, the enemies have exerted the utmost effort and diligence to destroy this mortal servant, as everyone has come to know. Yet not one of the friends has offered support or shown any thought of assistance. Instead of help, sorrows have rained down continually and in succession, in word and deed, like a torrential downpour. With complete acquiescence, this servant stands ready, life in hand, so that perhaps, through divine loving-kindness and heavenly grace, this widely mentioned letter may be sacrificed and lay down its life in the path of the Point and the Most Exalted Word. Were it not for this thought, by Him at whose command the Spirit has spoken, I would not remain in this city for a moment. God is sufficient as a witness. I close with these words: “There is no power or strength except through God. We belong to God, and to Him we return.”

281 Those endowed with discernment, who have drunk the wine of love and have not taken a single step to gratify the self, see the evidences, proofs, and testimonies, all of which point to this wondrous Cause and exalted divine Revelation, as clearer than the sun in the fourth heaven. Now consider how people have turned away from the divine Beauty and toward their own selfish desires. Despite all these compelling verses and firm indications in the Greater Weight, the divine trust among His servants, and these clear traditions, more explicit than any explanation or exposition, they have ignored and turned away from them all. Clinging instead to the outward sense of a few traditions that they have not found to accord with their own understanding and whose meaning they have not grasped, they have remained deprived of, and without hope of attaining, the flowing wine of the Lord of Majesty and the unfailing pure waters of the everlasting Beauty.

282 Consider that even the year of the appearance of that Being of light has been mentioned in the traditions. Yet they have not perceived it, nor have they detached themselves from selfish desire for even a moment. In the tradition of Mufaḍḍal, he asked Ṣādiq: “What, my master, of his appearance?” He replied, peace be upon him: “In the year sixty his Cause will appear and his name will be exalted.”

283 One can only marvel at these people: how, despite these clear and evident indications, have they shunned the Truth? For example, the sorrow, imprisonment, and afflictions that befell that Quintessence of the divine creation were mentioned in the traditions of the past. In the Biḥār it is stated: “In our Qāʾim are four signs from four prophets: Moses, Jesus, Joseph, and Muḥammad. The sign from Moses is fear and expectation; the sign from Jesus is what they said concerning him; the sign from Joseph is imprisonment and concealment of his faith; and the sign from Muḥammad is that he will appear with writings like the Qurʾān.” Despite this firmly established tradition, which mentions all these events just as they occurred, no one has taken heed, nor do I suppose they will take heed hereafter, “except those whom your Lord wills.” “God indeed causes whomever He wills to hear, but I cannot cause those in the graves to hear.”

284 It is known to you that the birds of divine Being and the doves of eternity have two kinds of utterance. One is outwardly expressed, without allusion, veil, or concealment. They have spoken and continue to speak in this way so that it may be a guiding lamp and a light showing the way, leading wayfarers to the heights of holiness and drawing seekers into the court of intimacy, as has been mentioned concerning the explicit traditions and clear verses. They have also spoken and continue to speak in veiled and concealed utterances, so that what the malicious have hidden in their hearts may be revealed and their true natures made evident. Thus Ṣādiq ibn Muḥammad says: “By God, they will surely be tested! By God, they will surely be sifted!” This is the divine balance and heavenly touchstone by which He tests His servants. None can grasp the meanings of these utterances except assured hearts, souls pleasing to God, and detached minds. In utterances of this kind, the intended meanings have never been, and are not, the outward meanings that people understand. Thus he says: “Every knowledge has seventy aspects, and only one is known among the people. When the Qāʾim arises, he will spread the remaining aspects among the people.” He also said: “We speak a word and intend by it seventy-one meanings, and for each of them we have an explanation.”

285 In any case, these matters are mentioned so that people will not be disturbed by certain traditions and utterances whose effects have not appeared in the earthly realm, but will attribute this to their own lack of understanding, rather than to the failure of the meanings of the tradition to be fulfilled. For these servants do not know what the Imams of the Faith intended, as may be gathered from the tradition. Therefore, people should not deprive themselves of the outpourings of grace because of such expressions, but should ask those who possess this knowledge, so that the hidden mysteries may become manifest and clear, without a veil.

286 Yet not a single person among the people of the earth is seen to seek the Truth, so as to turn to the Manifestations of Oneness concerning abstruse questions. All dwell in the land of forgetfulness and follow the people of rebellion and defiance. But God deals with them as they themselves act, and forgets them as they forgot the meeting with Him in His days. Thus has He passed judgment upon those who disbelieved, and He passes judgment upon those who denied His signs.

287 I conclude with His words, exalted is He: “Whoever turns away from the remembrance of the All-Merciful, We assign him a devil to be his companion.” And: “Whoever turns away from My remembrance will indeed have a life of hardship.”
