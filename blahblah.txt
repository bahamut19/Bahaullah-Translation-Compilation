---
title: "Skyway"
---

It was another cold night up here. Lonely, too. I couldn't hear the sound of anything familiar, no dog barking, no person talking, not even the sounds of engines on the ground or in the sky. Sometimes there was a rustling in the grass. I was also trying not to rustle the grass. The grass has been my bed these past several nights, but it is not home. I do not know whose home I am in. What I do know is it was another cold night up here. 

The fog is briskly floating by against my short, young fur. It is really hard to stay dry, but at least I am not left thirsty. I am hungry, though. My stomach growls as the color of the fog turns from black to a dark gray. It is a start of a new day. How many days do I have left? How many days will this hunger grow? How many days will I remain lonely? I have to push these thoughts to the back of my mind. It is time to get up, stretch my paws, and walk alongside the nearby road and hope. You might not think a dog has a soul, but I remember God. I remember who left me here and I remember who used to feed me and give me shelter. If it was not God, then who was it?

As I walk for awhile, a gray car approaches. I try my best not to look sad, and I do my best to prance a little. To seem cute and safe. The car slows down but drives by. I lower my head and tuck my tail back between my tired legs, trying to hold back the whimper of not being seen. Do I even exist to these gods anymore? 

I continue a little more, but I am scared to go too far. I do not know where I am and where I last was is the only safe place I am assured of. Again, I hear no sound of anything familiar. No dog barking. No person talking. No more engines. The fog is starting to turn white and be distant. I look beyond the short trees and again, I see no houses. I see only more trees descending down from where I am. I see more mountains. I am so lost. 

My floppy brown and white ears hear another engine on the ground. I turn around and again, try my best to prance and look my best. It is the same gray car! It pulls over on the side of the road and two people step out. There is a man and a woman. The man talks to me. I don't know what he is saying, but he reminds me of my last God. He reminds me of the God who would feed me and play with me when I was smaller. He also reminds me of the same God who hit me when I barked, or yelled at me if I did anything wrong. Thinking about my last God, I am so scared. Will this one beat me, too?

I watch from a distance, uncertain what to do. The two talk. 

"Maybe he wants to come home with us."

"Look at his ribs, he is so skinny."

"He is younger than I thought, maybe just a year old."

I don't know all they are saying, I am just watching. They sound nice and caring. But so was my last God. I watch as he grabs some bottles and opens the top. He pours water out into a small cup and sets it on the side of the road. He steps back. I sniff it, but I am not thirsty. The cold fog gave me all the water I need. It is my stomach that hurts. 

They watch me and I watch them. One turns to the car and pulls out a bag. He pours some chips next to the water and steps back. Once I know there is space, I sniff them. It's the first food I have had in days and I eat them quickly, crunching every chip and licking every single crumb off the pavement. I look back at them. 

The man has the bag and sits down. I can tell he wants me to come to him. He is trying to trick me with the food. Will he use my desperation against me? Or is he wanting to help me? I want help, but the memories of the past haunt my every breath. My heart is racing. I just stare blankly at him, begging him to give me the food. Just let me have it? Why do you want to torture me?

"Can we just grab him?"

"We need his consent first. Even if we want to help, we shouldn't kidnap him."

He gets up and pours more chips on the pavement next to the water. The woman takes the bag and he steps away and to the side of the car.

"I wish we had better food for him." 

Two other cars drive by and they ignore us. I trust her a little more than him. Still, she is a stranger and she is with this man who reminds me of all the pain I am in, reminds me of the desperation I feel. What if she is pretending to be nice to hurt me? I take a chip from her hand, but my legs are shaking so much from fear. I feel like I will collapse. If I collapse, with they try to take me? The bag is emptied and the man walks back up. He grabs another bag and opens it. Instead of giving its contents to me, he opens up the side of the car, and places it in the car. He walks away. I go near but I do not dare to get too close. This must be a trick. I don't trust this man at all.

"You know how we said Americans treat their pets with so much kindness and love? They are also capable of the most cruelty."

"Yeah, this dog has gone through a lot in the first year. Who imagines leaving this dog in the most isolated and difficult place to live? We are a mile high and there is no one."

"What can we do to get him to trust us?"

The two are talking a lot more now. They probably hope I get into their car, but I will not be tricked. I step back to where I was and look at them. 

"Maybe if we name him?"

"Skyway seems appropriate, given we are at the highest part of the Cherohala Skyway."

Look! I knew I can't trust them. They think they can own me. They think they can make me their property. 

"It hurts so much to see he was hurt so much, he can't tell the difference between help and harm."

"Did you see his neck, the red around it? What is that on his back?"

"It looks like it might be ticks. I can't get close enough to tell."

Just give me more food! I won't be with you! I will not speak to you. I will not do any tricks for you. I will not lick you. I will not let you pet me. I would rather be alone up here forever than to ever feel the pain I felt before. I am free, you Gods! I am free of you!

The people take the bag from the back of the car and empty it onto the grass along the road. 

"We are going to have to let him be and trust God will be merciful to him."

Their eyes are red. I have never seen this expression from a person before. They step back from the chips. There are a lot of chips! I bury my face down into them and eat them as quickly as I can. When I look up, the car is there no more. My stomach hurts less. My heart beat slows down. It is not as cold as it was as the sun appears over the clouds.

I think of the name Skyway. I guess it is fitting. Up here, I am closer to heaven than the god who hurt me. The way to the sky? Maybe that iss my next home. I'll be Skyway today.
